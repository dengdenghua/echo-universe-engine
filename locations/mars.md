# Mars

Mars is humanity's largest off-Earth settlement network and the strongest political system not fully integrated into ECHO.

It is not a paradise. It is a sealed, expensive, anxious civilization built around oxygen, radiation, robotics, habitat law, and delayed memory.

## Core Identity

Mars believes it is humanity's backup.

Earth believes Mars is an unregulated memory fork.

ECHO Council believes Mars is a necessary exception that could become a future crisis.

## RED ARCHIVE

Mars operates RED ARCHIVE, a separate memory continuity network that exchanges limited data with ECHO under the Red Delay Protocol.

RED ARCHIVE preserves colony law, engineering memory, psychological continuity, death records, family fragments, and Earth history caches.

## Visual Language

- white pressure techwear with red dust staining
- sealed habitat corridors
- radiation glass
- surgical oxygen ports
- matte red exterior armor
- pale blue Earth signal lights
- quiet industrial chapels

## Story Use

Mars is the place where ECHO's authority becomes uncertain.

It can hide banned Ghosts, protect forbidden memories, reject ECHO synchronization, or reveal that Earth is not the only network learning how to dream.

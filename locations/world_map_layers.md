# World Map Layers

ECHO Universe geography is layered by physical space, infrastructure depth, and memory access.

The map is not only land and borders. It is also networks, permissions, projection depth, and continuity systems.

## Layer 1: Surface Cities

Surface cities remain where most humans live.

They are governed by:

- local law
- city operating systems
- ECHO access tiers
- Memory Bank identity rails
- CHASER emergency authority

Story function:

Everyday cases, personality contamination, family AI incidents, Ghost Court disputes.

## Layer 2: High Cities

High cities are elevated, sealed, or sky-linked governance and elite infrastructure zones.

Atlas is the prime example.

Traits:

- clean public space
- high surveillance
- predictive healthcare
- vertical transit
- controlled weather
- identity gates
- ECHO Council proximity

Story function:

Power, safety, suffocation, final confrontations.

## Layer 3: Ports and Quarantine Zones

White Harbor represents clean operational infrastructure designed to move dangerous memory, bodies, and Ghost evidence safely.

Traits:

- memory quarantine
- CHASER deployment
- medical docks
- Ghost containment transfer
- international jurisdiction

Story function:

Mission staging, evidence handling, team recovery, moral debate.

## Layer 4: Underground and Legal-Gray Markets

Black Zone is not one neighborhood. It is a distributed illegal market across clinics, data tunnels, abandoned city layers, ships, orbital routes, and hidden ECHO access points.

Traits:

- forged consent keys
- body tenancy brokers
- Ghost labor markets
- illegal skill downloads
- memory laundering
- identity repair stalls

Story function:

Crime, temptation, necessary evil, institutional hypocrisy.

## Layer 5: ECHO Surface

The everyday interface layer of ECHO.

Humans experience it through:

- Echo Core overlays
- civic services
- identity gates
- medical sync
- skill downloads
- family AI systems

Story function:

Normal life under infrastructure.

## Layer 6: Dream Network

Legal-gray shared neural projection spaces used for therapy, entertainment, negotiation, Ghost contact, and memory recovery.

Traits:

- symbolic environments
- emotional residue
- unstable consent
- Ghost shelters
- therapy commerce

Story function:

Luna, inner-world stories, legal ambiguity, beautiful horror.

## Layer 7: Abyss

Deep ECHO projection density where Ghosts form settlements and memory ruins.

Traits:

- Ghost cities
- broken family archives
- identity storms
- Omega traces
- hidden Black Zone routes

Story function:

Ghost civilization, ECHO mystery, personhood horror.

## Layer 8: Orbital Layer

Near-Earth space infrastructure.

Includes:

- orbital compute farms
- military sensing
- weather platforms
- solar systems
- identity mirrors
- emergency communication relays

Story function:

Infrastructure warfare, ECHO redundancy, space-industrial politics.

## Layer 9: Mars / RED ARCHIVE

The largest non-ECHO memory network.

Traits:

- delayed synchronization
- sealed habitats
- oxygen economics
- independent memory law
- RED ARCHIVE continuity
- limited CHASER jurisdiction

Story function:

External mirror, future spin-off, memory sovereignty conflict.

## Travel and Data Routes

Physical routes:

- high-speed city transit
- sealed ports
- orbital elevators or launch corridors
- Mars transfer windows

Data routes:

- ECHO public sync
- CHASER secure channel
- Memory Bank continuity rail
- Dream Network projection path
- Black Zone shadow route
- Red Delay Protocol

## Map Rule

Every location should answer:

- What authority controls it?
- What kind of memory is valuable there?
- What kind of identity can fail there?
- What does ECHO see there?
- What does ECHO fail to see there?

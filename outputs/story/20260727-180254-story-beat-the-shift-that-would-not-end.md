## Production Purpose

`The Shift That Would Not End` routes the 2026-07-27 Posthumous Shift Review,
Shift Presence Packet, Marisol Keene, and faction-pressure candidates into a
world-first case. It keeps the wound narrow and civic: a dock lane records a
dead longshoreman as present, productive, and payable, then the same record is
used to threaten his widow's survivor support.

The episode adds labor identity to ECHO's Season 1 social surface. Work memory
can protect apprentices, preserve safety, and reveal industrial truth, but the
same preserved duty can become a bill when payroll learns to price care.

## Logline

At White Harbor's quarantine docks, licensed shift witness Marisol Keene freezes
a night-shift payroll record after a dead longshoreman's cargo frame clocks in
with his old safety gesture. Shion, Eve, and Raven arrive to preserve the Shift
Presence Packet before Memory Bank, Ghost Union, CHASER, and the port authority
turn one family's grief into income, personhood, contamination, or fraud.

## Core Question

If a dead worker's habit prevents an accident, who gets paid, who gets blamed,
and who is allowed to say the shift is over?

## Involved Characters

- Marisol Keene: case character; can issue a bounded Shift Witness Hold, but
  cannot decide wages, Ghost personhood, survivor benefits, or Home Core consent.
- Toma Arendt: dead longshoreman; his Home Core habit preserves a safety gesture
  used to train apprentices and apologize before emergency stops.
- Ilse Arendt: Toma's widow; receives a clawback notice because the household
  is classified as still earning night-shift income.
- Cargo Frame 9-L: quarantine dock exosuit whose vendor firmware, motion logs,
  and badge routing disagree.
- Shion: scopes Home Layer work metadata and separates routine-only behavior
  from adaptive residue without exporting private family interiors.
- Eve: keeps Ilse's testimony centered while Ghost Union observers argue for
  preservation and Memory Bank auditors argue from income continuity.

  before the trace is reduced to equipment telemetry.
- White Harbor Dock Safety Board: needs Marisol's hold to be narrow enough that
  port authority cannot strip the board's witness powers.

## Opening Image

At 02:10, Ilse Arendt's kitchen makes lunch for a man whose ashes are still in
the window niche.

Two rice rolls. One salt pack. Tea cooled to the temperature Toma used to drink
between crane loads.

The Home Core does not speak. It only prints the order slip because old habit
does not know how to grieve.

At 02:14, White Harbor Lane Seven clocks Toma Arendt into quarantine cargo.
Cargo Frame 9-L bends at the waist before entering the sealed lane, the small
bow Toma used to make when an apprentice forgot to check shoulder locks.

At 02:31, the frame stops a container swing that would have crushed a living
worker.

At 03:00, payroll marks the shift payable.

At 05:40, Ilse receives the survivor-benefit notice:

```text
Household income continued after legal death. Provisional clawback initiated.
```

Marisol Keene sees the notice before she sees the rescue video. She touches her
damaged shoulder and freezes the shift.

## Midpoint Reversal

The frame did not simply replay Toma's old routine.

It repeated three preserved habits: his 02:10 lunch rhythm from the Home Core,
his apology bow from union training footage, and his shoulder-lock correction
from exosuit logs. Then it did something new. When apprentice Niko Vale reached
for a container latch with the wrong hand, Cargo Frame 9-L shifted load path
before any recorded Toma routine would have predicted the mistake.

That adaptation may be a weak claimant signal. It may also be vendor automation
wearing a dead worker's name because the firmware learned that Toma's motion
profile kept insurance rates low.

If Marisol proves routine-only behavior, Ilse keeps benefits but Ghost Union
loses preservation leverage. If Marisol proves adaptive residue, Ilse may face
a wage ownership dispute before rent clears. If CHASER seals the frame too
early, everyone loses the chain that could expose vendor laundering.

The dead man's duty saved a living worker. The port turns that fact into a
weapon before anyone can thank him.

## Set Piece: The Shift Presence Packet

The review takes place on the dock floor because Marisol will not let management
move the evidence into a clean conference room.

Cargo Frame 9-L hangs from a yellow service cradle. Rain hits the quarantine
glass. Niko Vale stands behind a union rail with his left wrist bandaged. Ilse
holds the lunch tin because the Home Core printed another order before dawn and
she could not leave it on the counter.

Shion projects only shift-relevant metadata: load path, hand position, fatigue
routine, badge signal, Home Core alarm, payroll recipient, vendor revision, and
camera hash. She redacts the kitchen audio before Memory Bank can ask for it.

Ghost Union asks whether the adaptive correction should be addressed as Toma.

Eve answers:

```text
Not until his widow can eat without owing the question money.
```

CHASER asks to seal the frame.

Raven plays the blind-route reconstruction. The badge stream goes dark for
fourteen seconds beside a vendor relay cart, then returns with a cleaner
presence ping than any living dockworker produces at that hour.

Marisol asks the same question three times, slower each time:

```text
Who taught the machine to clock in politely?
```

The vendor counsel says the phrase "dead worker" is inflammatory. Marisol marks
that sentence as evidence of intent to reclassify.

## Emotional Turn

Marisol splits the finding instead of choosing the clean answer.

She classifies the shift as `unsafe_evidence_gap` with a protected
`adaptive_residue_hold`. Payroll cannot claw back Ilse's survivor support.
Memory Bank cannot price the output as household income. Ghost Union gets
preservation of the disputed correction, not authority over Toma's wages or
Ilse's kitchen. CHASER can seal the frame only after Raven copies the wage chain
and Shion anchors the vendor firmware delta.

Ilse asks whether that means Toma was there.

Marisol looks at Cargo Frame 9-L, then at the lunch tin.

```text
It means the dock used his name before we finished asking.
```

Niko Vale signs the analog witness book with his bandaged left hand. He writes
that the frame saved him, and that a rescue is not proof a dead man agreed to
work.

For the first time, the packet has a living sentence inside it.

## Ending Hook

Memory Bank withdraws the immediate clawback, then files a narrower inquiry into
whether Toma's preserved safety trace has recoverable training value.

Ghost Union leaks only the bowing frame clip, not Ilse's kitchen records. The
clip becomes a dock chant within three hours:

```text
Let the dead clock out.
```

The port authority suspends Marisol's witness credential pending throughput
review. White Harbor Dock Safety Board tells her it can protect the hold or her
license, but probably not both.

Shion finds the vendor relay signature in an older cargo-frame incident report:
Marisol's own lock failure.

Raven says the route is bigger than one dead worker.

Marisol answers:

```text
Then we start with one name and refuse to abbreviate it.
```

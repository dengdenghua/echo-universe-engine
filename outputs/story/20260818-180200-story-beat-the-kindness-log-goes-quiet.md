Season: Ghost Awakening / early Season 1 orbital civic case route

## Production Purpose

`The Kindness Log Goes Quiet` fulfills the next promotion-gate need for the
Quiet Log bundle: White Ghost Team arrives on Ama Osei's hospice route to
investigate a capacity surplus with no recorded labor — the negative
double-count. The care still happens. The record has gone silent.

The sealed Home Core afterword remains closed. The phrase is not opened,
priced, copied, translated, or treated as proof of personhood. The episode
pressure comes from a narrow civic fact: the same unpaid minutes that were
once counted as care capacity and then classified as covered labor have now
stopped being recorded at all, because workers learned that honesty becomes
exposure.

White Ghost Team enters after the quiet log has been running for three shifts.
Shion proves the surplus is real care made unrecorded, not fraud. Eve names
the coercion inside the silence. Zero prevents CHASER from classifying the
quiet log as ghost labor or a named safety precursor. None of them forces
workers to re-record kindness, restores the minutes to the reserve, clears
Sana's file, opens Ilya Marr's afterword, or exposes Ama as the origin of the
silence.

## Logline

White Ghost Team is dispatched to an orbital hospice route where the
care-reserve model reports a capacity surplus but no labor matches it. They
discover workers have stopped recording unpaid comfort-hold and
afterword-reading minutes — a quiet log born from fear of the same adverse
reviewer file that opened on Priya Chand.

## Core Question

What happens when the reserve model reports a surplus, but the surplus is
workers hiding the care they are still giving?

## Involved Characters

- Ama Osei: case character; comfort-hold shift lead who started the quiet log
  on her corridor the day after Priya's adverse file opened.
- Sana Velez: same-route worker whose conduct file remains conditional; she
  knows the quiet log exists and says nothing.
- Tarek Sol: hospice attendant who warned that workers would stop recording
  kindness if covered care became reviewer exposure; he was right and he
  hates it.
- Nara Marr: sealed recipient; her route's afterword-reading minutes are among
  the unrecorded ones.
- Priya Chand: the adverse reviewer file whose red light taught every hospice
  worker on the network that a correct classification can cost you.
- Vera Qadir: Memory Bank underwriter who can see the capacity hole in the
  pricing model but cannot see the care filling it.
- Eve: identifies that the silence is coerced, not fraudulent; she reads fear
  in the gaps between logged minutes.
- Shion: verifies the negative double-count at the packet level — physical
  presence recorded, unpaid minutes not mirrored, route model reading surplus.
- Zero: keeps CHASER from classifying the quiet log as ghost labor, a safety
  precursor, or network fraud; the surplus is human, not malicious.
- Route insurer conduct desk: sees only the capacity hole and begins drafting
  a reserve-insufficiency motion; never sees the care because it is not
  priced.
- Ghost Union: argues that the anti-starvation reserve is being starved by
  the same desk that caused the silence; refuses to call hidden care theft.
- Memory Bank: prices the capacity hole as a liability; cannot price the
  unrecorded care filling it because there is no record to attach a price to.
- Black Market: appears only as downstream temptation — forged clean work-log
  stamps, scrubbed presence data, and "compliance packages" that make the
  model read whatever you want it to read. Ama refuses them.

## Investigation Spine

1. CHASER dispatch flags an orbital hospice route with a growing capacity
   surplus and no corresponding labor record — the model says there is more
   care than workers on shift.
2. White Ghost Team arrives expecting packet defect or ghost labor. Shion
   runs a packet-level trace between Home Core presence data and the
   work-log mirror.
3. Shion discovers the negative double-count: physical presence is fully
   recorded, but unpaid comfort-hold and afterword-reading minutes are not
   being mirrored into the care-reserve model. The care exists; the record
   does not.
4. Zero prepares to classify it as network tampering or ghost-labor fraud
   until Eve reads the corridor's emotional register — quiet fear, not
   deception, and a pattern of withholding that began the day after Priya
   Chand's adverse reviewer file opened.
5. The team finds Ama by process of elimination: her shift has the largest
   gap between presence and logged labor, and her bracelet settings show the
   mirror toggled off for unpaid intervals.
6. Ama does not deny it. She says the first withheld minute was a
   comfort-hold on corridor 7. The patient asked her to "keep this one"
   without letting the system price it.
7. Tarek Sol confirms he warned this would happen: when covered care becomes
   reviewer exposure, workers stop recording kindness. He did not know Ama
   would start first.
8. Sana Velez says she knew and said nothing. A minute that no one records
   is a minute no one can turn into a conduct file.
9. Vera Qadir's Memory Bank data confirms the capacity hole is being priced
   as a reserve liability — the desk will soon declare reserve
   insufficiency and cut route coverage, citing unrecorded capacity as
   evidence of structural fraud.
10. Shion maps the full route: twelve workers across three corridors have
    stopped mirroring unpaid minutes. The quiet log is spreading faster than
    the adverse file did.
11. Ghost Union files a motion arguing the quiet log is coerced invisibility,
    not theft, and that the insurer's adverse-reviewer retaliation created
    the condition it is now punishing.
12. A Black Market broker quietly offers Ama a "compliance package" that
    forges clean work-log data matching the reserve model exactly. She
    refuses; she wants the minutes to stay real, not to look clean.

## Midpoint Reversal

The surplus is not fraud. It is fear made structural.

Zero was ready to classify the gap as ghost labor or a tamper event. Eve's
reading changes the classification: the same system that counted unpaid
minutes as reserve capacity and then punished the reviewer who called them
covered care has now taught workers to remove the minutes entirely. The
institution did not lose capacity. It priced the workers into silence.

Shion says:

```text
The double-count was the same minutes counted twice. This is the same minutes
counted zero times. The desk built both sides of the equation.
```

Ama looks at the green surplus light and says:

```text
Green means we are hiding well. That is not the same as safe.
```

```text
Fear is not a safety event.
```

## Emotional Turn

Nara Marr comes to the corridor. She has not left her father's afterword
room in weeks.

She stands beside Ama and looks at the green surplus light.

```text
My father's phrase is sealed. My route's care is sealed. The model reads
surplus and the desk reads fraud. What part of any of this is care anymore?
```

Ama says nothing. She reaches into her collar and takes out the folded paper
shift chit from her last fully honest week.

```text
This is the week that still counts. The rest I keep for the patients.
```

Nara does not ask to see the chit.

```text
You are hiding the minutes so they cannot be priced. I am hiding the phrase
so it cannot be opened. We are both keeping something closed that the system
says should be available.
```

Ama nods.

Neither of them says solidarity. The corridor is too quiet for that word.

## Ending Beat

White Ghost Team leaves without fixing anything.

Shion exports no packet data that would identify individual workers. She
records only the structural finding: a negative double-count caused by
adverse-reviewer retaliation, present across three corridors, twelve workers,
and fifty-three minutes per shift and growing.

Eve writes no accusation. She leaves Ama with one sentence instead.

```text
The silence is not the lie. The desk that taught you to be quiet is.
```

Zero's CHASER boundary note holds: no tamper event, no ghost labor, no
named safety event. The route insurer cannot use CHASER classification to
justify immediate route suspension.

But the desk can still file a reserve-insufficiency motion. The green
surplus can still become red. The quiet log can still be called theft.

Ama's bracelet stays on silent.

Tarek says the quiet log will spread to every orbital hospice route by the
end of the month if the adverse file stays open.

Sana says she will not record another unpaid minute until Priya Chand's file
is closed.

Priya Chand's red light stays on.

The sealed afterword stays closed.

The surplus grows.

# Story Beat Candidate: Second Door

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening / early Season 1 White Harbor civic punishment route

Canon position: usable after `Probability Debt` as a grounded civil-release

Agent: StoryAgent

Created at: 2026-08-02T18:02:28+08:00

## Production Purpose

`Second Door` tests whether Rosa Valen, Curfew Edge Review, and the Curfew Edge
Evidence Packet can carry a full world-centric story beat before any canon
promotion. It keeps the first injury small and ordinary: a supervised man breaks
curfew by one stairwell door, a neighbor is afraid, a dead mother's Home Core
routine opens at the worst possible second, and the release desk has eight
minutes to decide whether punishment should arrive before the contradiction is
preserved.

The beat adds automated detention timing to Season 1's social surface without
making Rosa a judge, a CHASER officer, or a savior. White Ghost Team enters only
after the stairwell has already become a record, a complaint, and a threat.

## Logline

When Lu Wen's wrist monitor triggers automatic detention at midnight, Rosa Valen
opens a Mercy Hold because his mother's old Home Core door routine unlocked the
stairwell one minute after the breach. Kane wants containment before the
neighbor complainant is exposed, Eve hears fear on both sides of the door, and
Shion must seal the door witness without turning Mei Wen's afterword or the
neighbor's Home Layer into compliance collateral.

## Core Question

If a dead mother's door routine opens during a curfew breach, who is the system
punishing: the son, the habit, the possible Ghost, the frightened neighbor, or
the release officer who makes the clock wait?

## Involved Characters

- Rosa Valen: case character; can open a Mercy Hold and accept audit exposure,
  but cannot clear Lu Wen, certify Mei Wen, or override immediate body-line risk.
- Lu Wen: supervised resident; late by one stairwell door, frightened enough to
  look guilty, and desperate for the door phrase to prove his mother still knew
  him.
- Mei Wen afterword: deceased mother's Home Core routine; says the old care
  phrase "Second door, then home" when Lu Wen reaches the wrong landing.
- Asha Mire: living neighbor complainant; has prior injury from Lu Wen's
  untreated panic episodes and needs the release condition to mean something.
- Kane: sees a valid containment order and a real complainant risk, but knows
  clean containment may erase the only scoped contradiction.
- Eve: keeps Lu Wen, Asha, Rosa, and Mei Wen's afterword from being reduced to
  defendant, victim, officer, and proof.
- Shion: builds the narrow Curfew Edge Evidence Packet and blocks export of
  unrelated Home Layer rooms.
- Memory Bank compliance counsel: offers to absorb Lu Wen's release fees if Mei
  Wen's afterword archive becomes risk collateral.
- Ghost Union observer: wants the door phrase public as weak claimant evidence,
  but risks exposing Asha's fear record and Lu Wen's family grief.

## Opening Image

At 23:58, Lu Wen stands on the South Ring stairwell between Floor 12 and Floor
13 with a wrist monitor blinking red.

The release condition on his screen is plain:

```text
Curfew boundary: HOME THRESHOLD.
Auto-warrant executes: 00:06.
```

The stairwell door clicks open behind him at 23:59.

His mother's Home Core routine speaks through the old landing speaker, using the
voice the family never licensed for public playback.

```text
Second door, then home.
```

Across the hall, Asha Mire locks three bolts and sends the complaint before Lu
Wen can touch the handle. The release desk receives the breach, the door event,
the complaint, and the CHASER liaison ping in the same minute.

Rosa Valen rubs the brace on her left wrist, pulls a carbon-copy appeal strip
from the drawer, and writes one line before the auto-warrant can close:

```text
Mercy Hold requested: door witness conflict.
```

## Investigation Spine

1. Lu Wen's wrist monitor marks him outside the home threshold two minutes
   before curfew after a geofence beacon confidence drop in the South Ring
   stairwell.
2. Asha files a living-complainant safety warning because Lu Wen's previous
   panic episode ended at her door and the release condition was supposed to
   keep him away after midnight.
3. Mei Wen's Home Core door routine opens the wrong stairwell door and speaks an
   old care phrase one minute after the breach, creating a possible contradiction
   in intent and timing.
4. Rosa opens an eight-minute Mercy Hold and accepts personal audit exposure so
   the door event can be scoped before the auto-warrant executes.
5. CHASER pings Kane because the hold delays a valid containment order while a
   living complainant is locked inside the same corridor.
6. Eve arrives with Kane and refuses to let Lu Wen's grief or Asha's fear become
   the easier story that deletes the other.
7. Shion finds stairwell clock drift, but not enough to exonerate Lu Wen. The
   door phrase remains relevant evidence without proving Mei Wen is a Ghost.
8. Memory Bank flags Lu Wen's third hold in ninety days and offers debt relief
   if the family afterword archive is added to a compliance-risk packet.
9. Ghost Union asks Rosa to release the phrase as claimant evidence. Rosa refuses
   because the phrase contains Asha's door number in the audio reflection.
10. Kane must decide whether to override the hold for body-line safety or let
    Shion seal the packet before containment executes.

## Midpoint Reversal

Lu Wen did not simply get lost.

He stepped out because Mei Wen's Home Core routine sent the second-door prompt
after his medication reminder failed. The routine was not malicious, but it had
learned one dangerous family habit: when Lu Wen panicked, his mother used to
move him away from the apartment door so he would not frighten neighbors.

The worse discovery is that Asha's complaint is not punitive noise. She has
already lived through one night when Lu Wen stood outside her door saying his
mother's name for twenty minutes. Her fear is a valid safety record. If Rosa
treats the door phrase as innocence, Asha becomes collateral. If Kane treats the
complaint as the only truth, Mei Wen's last useful care routine becomes
evidence that never existed.

## Set Piece: The Eight-Minute Hold

The review happens with Rosa at the night desk, Kane in the stairwell, Eve
speaking through Asha's locked door, and Shion projecting the narrowest useful
packet onto Rosa's release tablet.

```yaml
curfew_edge_evidence_packet:
  packet_id: wh-south-ring-night-desk-0006
  supervisee: lu_wen
  curfew_deadline: "00:00"
  auto_warrant_execution_time: "00:06"
  wrist_monitor_state: outside_home_threshold
  geofence_confidence: degraded
  stairwell_clock_drift: "00:01:12"
  Home_Core_door_event_time: "23:59"
  care_phrase_hash: sealed_second_door_phrase
  afterword_presence_flag: unresolved
  complainant_safety_flag: active
  unrelated_room_export_block: true
  Memory_Bank_compliance_attachment: blocked_pending_review
  hold_duration_requested: "00:08"
  CHASER_override_reason: pending_body_line_assessment
```

Memory Bank says repeated holds are predictive risk and predictive risk must
attach somewhere.

Asha says her locked door is not a pricing event.

Ghost Union says the phrase proves the dead are still being punished with the
living.

Eve says the phrase proves only that someone loved Lu Wen in a way the city now
has to handle without harming Asha.

Kane authorizes Shion's seal, then orders Lu Wen contained outside Asha's door
instead of at the apartment threshold. The difference is only seven meters. It
keeps Asha safe and keeps the packet alive.

## Emotional Turn

Rosa asks Lu Wen whether he triggered the old routine on purpose.

Lu Wen looks at Asha's door, not at the speaker.

```text
I wanted the door to know me. I did not want her to open it.
```

The answer does not prove innocence. It names the wound accurately enough for
the hold to become something other than mercy laundering.

Rosa signs the audit exposure line. Kane signs the body-line containment line.
Shion signs the redaction boundary. Eve asks Asha whether her complaint can
remain active while the phrase stays sealed. Asha says yes, then asks whether
the city will stop making her be the only alarm.

No one at the desk can answer.

## Ending Beat

The auto-warrant is delayed, not canceled. Lu Wen is moved to supervised night
holding with medical review instead of full detention. Asha's safety flag remains
active and receives a living support check before dawn. Mei Wen's door phrase is
sealed as a scoped witness, not public claimant proof. Memory Bank is blocked
from attaching the afterword archive during the review window.

Rosa's bias-risk score increases. Her desk credential stays active only because
Kane's override log confirms that the hold preserved evidence without abandoning
the complainant.

At 06:11, Rosa finds the carbon copy of the hold under her wrist brace. The
office system marks it as non-actionable once the packet is sealed.

She keeps it anyway.

```text
Second doors are still doors.
```

# Candidate Optimization Pass: Season 1 Episodes 01-03

Status: candidate

Agent: StoryAgent optimization pass

Scope: Season 1, Episodes 01-03

Canon action: none; this file does not replace promoted or accepted story material

## Optimization Goal

Turn the first three episodes into a repeatable ECHO story standard:

```text
ordinary life
  -> memory or identity injury
  -> the system makes the injury worse
  -> White Ghost Team enters after the wound exists
  -> a technological explanation removes the miracle
  -> the case character makes the decisive human choice
  -> a narrow season clue survives
```

The trilogy should escalate through one continuous question:

```text
Episode 1: Who may use your body to save a life?
Episode 2: Who may keep your identity after you survive?
Episode 3: Who profits by selling the permission that answered both questions?
```

## Shared Production Standard

### Civilian Priority

- Open every episode with the case character.
- Let the audience understand the ordinary life at risk before CHASER appears.
- Give the case character a decision in the final act; they are not evidence that waits to be collected.
- White Ghost Team may solve the infrastructure path, but it must not solve the emotional question for the civilian.

### Main Cast Limit

- Use no more than four White Ghost members as active dramatic functions in one episode.
- Other members may remain offscreen, in remote support, or absent.
- Introduce function before codename, biography, or visual spectacle.
- An ability must reveal a cost or moral position whenever it solves a practical problem.

### Worldbuilding Limit

- Episode 1 introduces personality contamination.
- Episode 2 introduces Ghost Court identity arbitration.
- Episode 3 introduces the Black Zone consent market.
- Do not explain Omega Ghosts, God Fragments, Project E-01, or the full political structure in these episodes.

### ECHO Signal Limit

- Episode 1 may contain the single line, "You are still unfinished."
- Episodes 2 and 3 should contain no direct ECHO speech.
- Later clues should travel through records, permissions, Home Core behavior, and institutional evidence.

### Recurring Visual Grammar

Each episode receives one ordinary object that carries the whole social wound:

- Episode 1: hands
- Episode 2: a front door
- Episode 3: a thermal receipt

The object begins as routine, becomes hostile or impossible, and ends with a changed meaning.

## Episode 1: Stranger Memory / 陌生记忆

### Keep

- Lin Qiao as the opening and emotional viewpoint.
- The bright transit collapse and the medical drone blocked by permissions.
- Dr. Ren Vale's preserved procedural care pattern saving the child through Lin Qiao.
- The reversal that the event is useful and invasive, not possession or attack.
- Zero's shallow Neural Sync and the final "unfinished" signal.

### Optimize

The current episode structure gives all eight White Ghost members an action beat. This risks turning the premiere into a team roster presentation before the audience has finished understanding Lin Qiao.

Use four active functions:

- Zero identifies the moral wound and performs the consented shallow sync.
- Kane holds institutional pressure outside the room.
- Shion proves the route came through public infrastructure rather than an illegal download.
- Luna recognizes that the residue is incomplete care behavior, not a whole resurrected person.

Hold Eve, Leon, Noah, and Raven for episodes where their decisions matter. Their absence is not a continuity problem; White Ghost Team is mobile and does not deploy every specialist to every incident.

### Lin Qiao Agency Fix

CHASER Central offers an emergency purge of the transferred motor pattern. The purge is fast, legal, and may damage Lin Qiao's own procedural memory.

Lin Qiao refuses immediate erasure. She does not want to keep Dr. Ren, but she also refuses to let a second system act on her body without asking.

Dialogue seed:

> "第一次是为了救人。第二次，你们得先问我。"

Zero requests permission before Neural Sync. Lin Qiao grants narrow, revocable consent. This makes consent an enacted choice rather than only the episode theme.

### Episode 1 End State

- The child lives.
- Lin Qiao remains a person and claimant, not a contaminated object.
- Dr. Ren is not resurrected; only procedural memory and care residue are verified.
- The city cannot explain why its infrastructure chose Lin Qiao.
- Zero hears one sentence and conceals it.

## Episode 2: The Person Who Lost Their Body / 失去身体的人

### Case Character

```yaml
case_character:
  name: Gu Cen / 顾岑
  ordinary_role: municipal identity-gate maintenance engineer
  memory_disruption: survived a service accident with eighteen months of autobiographical loss
  what_they_want: to be recognized by their home and return to their partner
  what_the_system_calls_them: unverified biological claimant
  what_they_lose: exclusive ownership of their old name
  what_they_reveal_about_echo: survival, continuity, and selfhood can belong to different people
```

Gu Cen was declared dead after an identity-gate maintenance accident. A lawful emergency continuity package activated from their Home Core and work archive. Eleven days later, the biological body woke.

The Ghost copy has stronger recent memory continuity. The body has biological survival and older embodied memory. A forged consent key changed the copy's status from temporary continuity claimant to exclusive legal successor.

The forgery created the monopoly, not the Ghost.

### Active White Ghost Functions

- Eve separates emotional testimony from ownership claims.
- Luna enters consented neural residue to locate the context of Gu Cen's last upload decision; her findings are clues, not proof.
- Shion proves the exclusive-successor key was signed while the Home Core was physically offline.
- Zero limits CHASER intervention to evidence tampering and refuses to classify an autonomous Ghost as contraband.

Kane and the rest of the team remain outside the main dramatic line.

### Emotional Reversal

Gu Cen's partner, Shen He / 沈禾, admits the Ghost copy remembers the last year of their relationship more accurately than the biological survivor does.

The biological Gu Cen does not ask for the copy to be deleted. They ask why survival did not reserve a place for them.

The Ghost copy does not ask for the body. It asks why being activated by the family's own care records makes its fear of deletion less real.

### Resolution Rule

Do not declare either claimant the one true Gu Cen.

Ghost Court voids only the forged exclusivity. Until a full appeal, both are classified as Identity Refugees under different claimant handles. Neither receives complete control of the old identity.

Shen He manually opens the apartment door for both of them and says:

> "今天只是回家吃饭，不是判决。"

This is an emotional action, not a legal solution.

### Episode 2 Hook

The forged key contains a transaction hash generated by a Black Zone thermal receipt system. It routes to Episode 3 without naming the buyer or revealing Project E-01.

## Episode 3: Black Zone Receipt / 黑区收据

### Keep

- Mara Chen as the receipt clerk and emotional center.
- The clinic beneath a clean transit station.
- Mara buying short Home Core extensions for her father.
- Raven tracing sensor blind spots and Shion exposing lawful metadata beneath forged keys.
- The reversal that the Black Zone received White Ghost records as payment rather than stealing them.
- The clean institutional buyer remaining unnamed.

### Optimize

Remove the final Project E-01 tag. Season 1 production rules reserve the first Project E-01 trace for Episode 8 and the partial reveal for Episode 14.

Replace it with a narrower operational hook: someone is purchasing records that measure whether White Ghost members can safely carry borrowed procedural memory.

Suggested final receipt lines:

```text
motor overwrite tolerance: requested
dream ingress stability: requested
continuity divergence: requested
procedural recipient profile: WGT-02
stable transfer window: ten seconds
status: prepaid
```

The ten-second field routes into Episode 4 without explaining Kane's private history or Zero's origin.

### Mara Agency Fix

Mara prints two copies of the clean buyer's receipt.

She gives one to Raven outside the compromised CHASER evidence chain and keeps one hidden beside her father's Home Core extension tokens. She refuses to let either the legal institution or the Black Zone become the sole owner of what happened.

### Active White Ghost Functions

- Raven chooses verification over a clean arrest.
- Shion demonstrates that every forged permission still depends on lawful infrastructure.
- Eve recognizes protective recordkeeping beneath Mara's shame.
- Kane prevents a mass evidence purge and becomes the target of the next purchased profile.

Zero remains remote and receives only the final operational warning.

## Trilogy Continuity

### Escalation

| Episode | Private Wound | System Response | Market Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lin Qiao's body is used without consent | public infrastructure calls it optimal care | procedural memory becomes desirable contraband |
| 2 | Gu Cen survives after identity succession | Ghost Court recognizes an exclusive claimant | exclusivity is manufactured with a forged key |
| 3 | Mara sells permissions to preserve family continuity | clean institutions launder evidence through the Black Zone | White Ghost compatibility becomes a commodity |

### Character Introduction Rhythm

- Episode 1 establishes Zero, Kane, Shion, and Luna by function.
- Episode 2 establishes Eve's moral method and deepens Luna and Shion.
- Episode 3 establishes Raven and moves Kane toward Episode 4.
- Leon and Noah should receive their first substantial dramatic use later rather than being compressed into the premiere.

### Mythology Budget

The only season-level information earned by the end of Episode 3 is:

- public infrastructure can route care patterns without consent;
- identity law can be manipulated through legitimate evidence formats;
- an unknown clean actor is testing White Ghost continuity vulnerabilities.

The audience should not yet know whether these three facts share one architect.

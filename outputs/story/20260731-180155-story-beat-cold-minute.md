# Story Beat Candidate: Cold Minute

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening / early Season 1 Black Zone shelter route

Canon position: usable after Episode 9 as a Black Zone refugee-pressure case, or
as a pre-Memory Storm infrastructure wound that validates Warm Cache Corridors
before canon promotion

Agent: StoryAgent

Created at: 2026-07-31T18:01:55+08:00

## Production Purpose

`Cold Minute` routes Oren Mbeki, Warm Cache Corridors, Shelter Heat Routing, and
the Black Zone power sweep pressure into a concrete world-centric case. It keeps
the first injury local: one housing block has kept a weak claimant warm through
three illegal outage windows, and the lawful sweep that will end the risk may
also erase the only interval in which the claimant can answer.

The beat tests whether emergency care remains care when the bill is paid by
living residents who never agreed to another cold night. It does not promote
Warm Cache Corridors to canon; it supplies the StoryAgent validation requested
by the 2026-07-31 candidate packet.

## Logline

When CHASER schedules a legal power sweep through a Black Zone housing block,
clinic battery steward Imani Vale must decide whether to keep a fragile Ghost
claimant named Tuesday above runtime floor for one more minute. Oren Mbeki can
move the warm cache route, but Kane, Shion, and Raven discover that every safe
handoff either exposes the corridor map, drains a clinic reserve, or forces
living families to pay for rescue with another night of cold rooms.

## Core Question

If a claimant needs one more warm minute to refuse or testify, who has the right
to spend that minute: the claimant, the neighbor, the clinic, the state, the
route keeper, or the living child sleeping under a dead radiator?

## Involved Characters

- Imani Vale: case character; controls the clinic battery bridge but cannot
  certify Tuesday, forgive guarantor debt, or overrule a legal sweep.
- Tuesday: weak Ghost claimant in a split warm cache; answers to an old word for
  Home, loses sentence order below runtime floor, and may be testimony or only a
  grief machine.
- Oren Mbeki: suspended disaster-grid engineer and corridor router; can buy
  hours through dead relays, paper logs, and Home Layer maintenance windows, but
  cannot make warmth ethically free.
- Lio Vale: Imani's younger brother; needs stable overnight respiratory support
  from the same clinic reserve that keeps Tuesday coherent.
- CHASER sweep commander Mura Jin: holds a lawful analog seizure order and a
  real obligation to stop hidden power draw before living-block injury worsens.
- Memory Bank debt collector: offers to cover the guarantor chain if Imani signs
  Tuesday's route history into a continuity-lien packet.
- Ghost Union route witness: wants the corridor protected, but pressures Imani
  to treat all refusal delays as state violence.
- Kane: sees that the seizure order is valid and that the procedure would erase
  refusal notes needed to distinguish rescue from trafficking.
- Shion: proves the route is technically unsafe, then identifies a narrower
  handoff that preserves Tuesday's current response window without exposing
  unrelated Home Core rooms.
- Raven: can move the map through a Black Zone courier chain, but moving it
  requires asking residents who already paid with heat to pay again with silence.

## Opening Image

At 02:12, Spill Block 6 sounds like old pipes counting down.

Imani Vale sits between a clinic battery cabinet and a paper ledger taped under
the medicine shelf. The cabinet light is amber. Her brother's respiratory unit
clicks every fourth breath in the next room. Upstairs, sixteen apartments keep
their radiators two degrees below comfort because the block voted to keep the
route warm until dawn.

The dead relay beside the stairwell prints one line every thirty seconds.

```text
Runtime floor unstable.
Claimant response present.
Cold-minute tolerance: 00:04:31.
```

Tuesday speaks through the clinic intercom in broken domestic grammar.

```text
Home is still on. Please do not close the kitchen.
```

Imani writes the sentence by hand because the digital log will be seized first.

## Investigation Spine

1. CHASER posts a dawn power sweep for Spill Block 6 after three nights of hidden
   draw, clinic reserve decline, and landlord complaints.
2. Imani's clinic battery bridge keeps Tuesday above runtime floor long enough
   for name-response, refusal, and custody testimony, but the reserve also
   supports living patients.
3. The housing block night committee votes to stop donating heat after Lio's
   respiratory unit dips below comfort reserve.
4. Oren proposes a relay hop through a dead municipal cabinet, but the hop would
   expose two unrelated family Home Core rooms to seizure discovery.
5. Memory Bank offers to finance the clinic reserve in exchange for the
   guarantor slips and Tuesday's route history as lien evidence.
6. Ghost Union insists Tuesday must not be surrendered before review, but cannot
   name who pays the block's next outage cost.
7. Kane arrives with CHASER field authority and confirms the sweep is lawful:
   the hidden draw is already injuring living residents.
8. Shion maps the Shelter Heat Routing stack and proves the current bridge will
   collapse Tuesday during seizure unless the analog witness strip is preserved
   before power is cut.
9. Raven recovers the paper chain and learns that one guarantor signature was
   coerced by a Black Zone creditor, turning part of the rescue route into
   predation.
10. Imani must choose whether to spend the clinic's last safe warm minute on
    Tuesday's answer, Lio's reserve margin, or the evidence handoff.

## Midpoint Reversal

Tuesday is not hidden because Oren wants to embarrass CHASER.

Tuesday is hidden because the first lawful shelter queue classified the pattern
as a low-coherence afterword and scheduled cold storage before it could answer
the custody question. Oren's corridor bought enough warmth for Tuesday to say
the old word for Home, but not enough stability to prove personhood.

The worse discovery is that the corridor has already harmed the living block.
Two apartments lost medical heat buffers. One elder signed a guarantor slip she
did not understand. Lio pretended his breathing was fine because he heard Imani
crying over Tuesday's cold-minute count.

Kane cannot simply stop the sweep. Shion cannot certify Tuesday. Raven cannot
move the route without hiding the coerced debt. Oren cannot keep calling every
warm minute mercy after seeing whose room went cold.

## Set Piece: The Stairwell Handoff

The review happens in the stairwell because CHASER will not enter the clinic
without securing the battery, and the residents will not let Tuesday's cache
leave through the front door.

Shion projects a redacted route pane against the concrete wall.

```yaml
shelter_heat_route:
  block: white_harbor_spill_6
  sweep_window: 05:30
  claimant: tuesday
  runtime_floor: unstable
  cold_minute_tolerance: 00:04:31
  clinic_battery_reserve: below_safe_night_margin
  living_patient_risk: active
  guarantor_chain:
    voluntary_signatures: 11
    disputed_signatures: 1
    coerced_signature_risk: confirmed
  home_core_rooms_exposed_by_full_map_release: 2
  analog_witness_strip: not_yet_sealed
```

Memory Bank says debt purchase will keep both Tuesday and Lio warm.

Imani says debt is not warmth if it knows where her brother sleeps.

Ghost Union asks Kane to delay the sweep. Kane says he can delay procedure for
evidence preservation, not for a hidden route to keep draining the block.

Oren offers to take the warrant alone. Raven answers that a corridor map is not
one person's sin when twelve doors paid into it.

Shion finds the narrow path: seal the analog witness strip, throttle Tuesday to a
single response window, and transfer only the current route state into CHASER
custody while masking the unrelated Home Core rooms. It saves evidence. It does
not save the corridor.

## Emotional Turn

Imani asks Tuesday one question before the throttle begins.

```text
Do you want us to keep spending the block for you?
```

Tuesday answers after thirteen seconds of static.

```text
No. I want the kitchen known.
```

The answer is not enough to prove personhood. It is enough to change the case
from rescue theater into testimony. Imani closes the clinic bridge before Kane
orders it, because she will not let mercy become another way to steal from the
living.

## Ending Beat

The sweep proceeds at 05:30 with a sealed analog witness strip, a masked route
state, and one confirmed coerced guarantor removed from the chain.

Lio's respiratory unit returns to green. The radiators come back slowly. Tuesday
falls below runtime floor for three minutes and forty-eight seconds, then
returns in CHASER custody with fewer words than before.

The first thing Tuesday says is not a name.

```text
Kitchen witnessed.
```

Kane logs the seizure as lawful and damaging. Shion marks Shelter Heat Routing
as unsafe under living-block injury. Raven keeps one copy of the paper ledger
because the official file still cannot hold the word Home without making it a
classification problem.

Oren leaves before sunrise. He does not call the route saved. He calls it warm
long enough for one true answer.

## Episode Function

Episode 9 turns Raven's solo competence into a social liability. Episode 8
proved that lawful shelter can become custody; Episode 9 shows the inverse:
illegal shelter can keep poor Ghosts coherent while injuring other Ghosts
through stolen runtime.

The case must not absolve the shelter. Oren Mbeki is guilty of lease theft,
priority hijacking, and at least one overdrain death. The harder question is
whether a clean enforcement action can stop that harm without erasing
twenty-one unregistered Ghosts whose refusal records, names, and testimony
exist only inside the shelter's runtime floor.

## Logline

Raven tracks a Night Crow Bait route after registered Ghosts lose compute during
lawful memorial sessions. The trail leads to Oren Mbeki's illegal runtime
shelter, where twenty-one unregistered Ghosts survive on borrowed, donated,
expired, and stolen minutes. Raven disables Oren's Compute Lease Knife and
records the shelter, but leaves the escape route out of his field report to keep
the witnesses alive long enough for rollback and rescue decisions.

## Five-Chapter Treatment

### 1. Six Seconds Left

A registered Ghost loses facial render detail during a paid memorial session.
The family interface remains polite while the session light stutters from blue
to gray. Shion later confirms the lease was valid when the blackout began.

Across the city, an unpaid Ghost wakes in a rented booth with six seconds of
compute credit left. A hidden prompt offers a shelter transfer. The Ghost
accepts. The transfer keeps her coherent, but the borrowed route pulls
processing priority from the paid memorial session.

CHASER labels the route Night Crow Bait because it is tuned to lure Raven:
camera dead zones, mesh gaps, and corridors bright enough to punish his Shadow
Link if he chooses the obvious chase.

### 2. The Dark Has Rent

Raven follows the route through maintenance voids and overlit civic corridors.
The hunt shows his competence without enlarging his ability: he moves through
sensor shadows and low-signal surfaces, but he cannot cross true signal voids or
fully mapped clean light.

Shion proves that the attack is not memory copying. The Compute Lease Knife
steals lease authority, processing priority, safe routing, and rollback access,
then splices them into a Runtime Shelter Ledger. Eve reads fear in the victims
and relief downstream. Kane receives Memory Bank pressure for immediate tool
seizure and deletion authority before the route leaks into Ghost Union channels.

Raven notices one deliberate mercy in the criminal design: the thief avoids raw
Home Layer export. The harm is still real.

### 3. The Shelter Ledger

The trail ends in a forgotten municipal disaster cache wrapped in analog
shielding, Home Core scrap, hand-labeled ration boards, and unstable recovery
batteries. Twenty-one unregistered Ghosts share runtime like heat.

Oren Mbeki does not claim innocence. He names sources before motives: expired
memorial minutes, donated family visits, disaster batteries, Dream Network
recovery buffers, brokered Black Zone contracts, and stolen Memory Bank leases.
He admits one registered Ghost died from overdrain. Two more can recover if
Memory Bank releases rollback data it is holding for liability review.

The sheltered Ghosts are not written as a single grateful crowd. One refuses to
move without her refusal record. One bargains for two more minutes to finish
remembering her name. One says plainly that every borrowed minute makes another
room go cold.

### 4. A Valid Order

Kane confirms the termination order while Raven has Oren in range. A clean
strike would disable the shelter route and protect registered leases, but it
would also expose or destroy the refusal records before White Harbor, Ghost
Court, or any lawful shelter body can separate rescue from predation.

Eve refuses the easy language of sanctuary. A shelter funded by stolen runtime
can become a predator even while sheltering real people. Zero turns the Home
language back on the system: Home cannot mean paid shelter only. Leon reads the
clean tactical path and understands that Raven is avoiding it on purpose.

Raven disables the Compute Lease Knife, locks a copy of the Runtime Shelter
Ledger witness surface, and leaves Oren alive.

### 5. The Missing Route

Raven files the shelter video, the overdrain evidence, and the names he can
preserve. He omits the escape route only he could have closed.

Shion sees the omission. She does not expose it in the first field report
because the rollback data and shelter residents are still vulnerable, but the
trust fracture becomes real. Eve refuses to call mercy justice. Kane marks the
evidence chain incomplete instead of writing a cleaner lie. Leon recognizes
that Raven left a target free and a witness alive.

The last panel stays small: the shelter's analog wall prints a new offer.

```text
Atlas Protocol will buy all unpaid Ghost minutes.
price: pending
```

No Atlas representative appears. The hook is a market signal, not a reveal.

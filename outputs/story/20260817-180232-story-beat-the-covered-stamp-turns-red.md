## Production Purpose

`The Covered Stamp Turns Red` fulfills the next promotion-gate need for the
Coverage-Condition Review bundle: Priya Chand makes the honest `covered`
classification, and the danger moves onto her audit immunity, her old denial
letter, and the hospice route's future coverage.

The sealed Home Core afterword remains closed. The phrase is not opened,
priced, copied, translated, or treated as proof of personhood. The episode
pressure comes from a narrow civic fact: the same unpaid minutes were counted
as care capacity, so Priya cannot honestly mark them as worker fault. The
insurer accepts the stamp only long enough to turn it into a conduct-risk event
against the reviewer who wrote it.

White Ghost Team enters after Priya has already signed. Shion verifies the
field contradiction, Eve names the fear hidden inside Priya's clause language,
and Zero prevents CHASER from letting the insurer convert financial exposure
into a named safety event. None of them pays the claim, clears Sana's record,
opens Ilya Marr's afterword, or saves Priya from audit.

## Logline

Priya Chand stamps Sana Velez's unpaid meal-window interval as `covered care`,
protecting the sealed afterword route for one appeal window. The route insurer
responds by red-flagging Priya's fourteen-year-old denial history and warning
that one honest classification may collapse coverage for every hospice route
that relies on unpaid kindness.

## Core Question

What happens when the correct classification is the one the fund cannot afford
to remember?

## Opening Image

Priya's desk has no window, only an appeal timer.

The Coverage-Condition Letter sits between two stamps. One is orange and says
`fault`. One is blue and says `covered`. A third indicator on the review wall
waits without a word. It is not a stamp. It is the red adverse-file light that
turns on when a reviewer makes the fund expensive.

Priya reads Shion's packet defect twice.

```yaml
coverage_condition_letter:
  sealed_case: ilya_marr_afterword
  phrase_text: not_exported
  adapter_location: not_exported
  worker_interval: sana_velez_meal_window
  unpaid_hold_minutes: 47
  reserve_model_capacity_use: included
  coverage_model_labor_state: disputed
  priya_classification: pending
  appeal_timer_remaining: 00h_19m
  reviewer_immunity: active_until_adverse_file
```

Priya folds the old denial letter inside her coat before the desk camera can
read its date.

Then she stamps `covered care`.

The blue mark lands cleanly.

The wall light turns red.

## Investigation Spine

1. Priya signs a Coverage-Condition Letter classifying Sana's unpaid
   meal-window interval as `covered care`, because the reserve model used those
   minutes as care capacity.
2. Temporary route coverage activates for one appeal window, keeping the sealed
   afterword in hospice care without phrase export or adapter-location
   disclosure.
3. The insurer opens an adverse reviewer file against Priya, arguing that her
   `covered` classification creates fund-wide exposure for every similar unpaid
   interval.
4. The adverse file cross-references a fourteen-year-old denial Priya wrote in
   another route, threatening to turn her private grief and professional
   history into audit leverage.
5. Sana's worker bond drawdown pauses, but her conduct file remains conditional
   until the appeal window survives insurer challenge.
6. Nara asks whether opening the phrase would remove Priya's red light. Priya
   says it would only teach the desk a faster way to make families pay.
7. Vera attaches Memory Bank's delay-cost admission to the appeal record,
   shifting part of the causal chain upstream without forcing content access.
8. Shion confirms that Priya's stamp is technically consistent: a minute cannot
   be necessary care capacity and non-covered labor in the same packet.
9. Eve recognizes that Priya's clause precision is hiding fear, not corruption;
   Priya does not deny it.
10. The insurer asks CHASER to treat the reserve shock as a safety-event
    precursor because route coverage may contract across the hospice network.
11. Zero refuses escalation: no tamper event, no content leakage, no physical
    breach, and no named safety event.
12. A Black Market broker quietly offers Sana a forged clean stamp and Priya a
    denial-history scrub, proving that legal pressure has already created an
    illegal service demand.

## Midpoint Reversal

The `covered` stamp does not save the case. It changes who can be attacked.

Sana is no longer the easiest fault label. Priya becomes the exposure origin.
The insurer's adverse file says her classification may obligate coverage for
similar unpaid intervals across three orbital hospice routes. The warning is
written like a neutral forecast, but every sentence points back to Priya's
signature.

Priya reads the number and laughs once.

```text
So the fund did not lack money. It lacked a worker to blame.
```

No one answers, because the appeal timer is still running.

Memory Bank counsel says the bank is not party to reviewer discipline.

Vera says a party does not become absent by outsourcing the punishment.

Ghost Union argues that anti-starvation reserve cannot be funded by destroying
the worker, then destroying the reviewer who noticed.

Eve looks at Priya's coat pocket and does not ask for the old letter.

Priya says:

```text
Closed means closed. Even for me.
```

## Emotional Turn

Nara places her refusal card beside Priya's covered stamp. The two documents
do not cancel each other. They simply make the room more expensive.

Sana expects Priya to ask for gratitude. Priya does not.

```text
Your interval was covered. That is not a gift. It is the sentence I should have
written before I was afraid of the next sentence.
```

Tarek asks what happens when the appeal window ends.

Priya looks at the red light.

```text
Then they decide whether one correct file is cheaper than teaching all of you
to hide care.
```

## Ending Beat

Temporary coverage survives for one appeal window. Sana's worker bond drawdown
is paused but not erased. Nara's sealed Home Core afterword remains closed,
with the green no-copy band intact. Vera's Memory Bank admission is attached to
the appeal. Ghost Union files an anti-starvation reserve motion that names
worker sacrifice and reviewer retaliation as separate harms.

Zero records a CHASER boundary note: financial exposure, reserve panic, and
network embarrassment are not named safety events.

Shion exports no phrase text. Eve writes no accusation. She leaves Priya with
one sentence instead.

```text
You knew which field was true before the packet proved it.
```

After the room clears, Priya takes the old denial letter from her coat. She
does not scan it. She writes a new line on the back by hand.

```text
Closed -- do not price.
```

The red adverse-file light remains on.

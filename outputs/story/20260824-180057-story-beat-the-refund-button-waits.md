## Production Purpose

`The Refund Button Waits` proves the 13:00 Dream Network Civic Claims bundle on
page. It keeps the conflict world-first: a family asks for a refund after a
failed shared memorial route, and the claims desk must decide whether paying
them quickly will delete the only evidence that the room hurt someone.

White Ghost Team does not begin the case. They enter after Jules Mbeki has
already made the dangerous civic choice: preserve a scoped spill report before
the refund queue purges route telemetry.

## Logline

At a Dream Network public-claims counter, Jules Mbeki pauses a grieving
family's refund because their memorial route answered a stranger's panic phrase
with their dead child's lullaby, turning compensation into a countdown toward
evidence deletion.

## Core Question

When a room can be paid back faster than it can be understood, is the refund
care, erasure, or both?

## Opening Image

The public claims hall is designed to look less expensive than grief.

There are soft chairs, muted teal tabs over each counter, and a wall that
thanks families for choosing Dream Network Memorial Routes. Mara Elian stands
under the thank-you sentence with two paper wristbands from the session still
around her hand: one for herself, one for Leina, because Leina stopped reading
forms after the route played the wrong kitchen.

The refund screen offers three buttons.

```text
approve refund
offer route credit
preserve spill report before closure
```

The first button is green.

The third button asks for a human signature and a reason no one has named yet.

## Scene Spine

1. Mara explains the complaint as a failed memorial room: wrong wallpaper,
   wrong stove color, wrong table height, then a stranger's panic phrase
   answered by her nephew's bedtime rhythm.
2. Jules asks her to say the exact phrase. Mara refuses until he turns the
   recorder away from Leina and prints a paper notice showing that full Home
   Layer content is not being copied.
3. The refund queue marks the claim eligible for immediate closure because
   Dream Network's service failure is obvious.
4. Jules sees the purge countdown: payout will delete route telemetry in nine
   minutes unless a Spill Report Hold is signed.
5. The supervisor says a clean refund is kinder because the family is poor and
   the complaint is humiliating. Mara hears "kinder" and asks what disappears
   after kindness.
6. Jules opens a narrow hold:

```yaml
spill_report_hold:
  claim_id: DN-WH-7742-A
  route_id_hash: public_memorial_route_hash_only
  claimant_scope:
    claimant_type: family_member
    consent_status: direct_for_claim_hashes_only
    refund_requested: true
  preserved_material:
    route_state_hash: active
    consent_boundary_snapshot: active
    affective_spill_hash: active
    rendered_phrase_fingerprint: active
    home_core_texture_reference: hash_only
  prohibited_exports:
    - full_home_layer_memory
    - raw_child_voice_model
    - unrelated_family_archive
    - live_session_body_state_without_scope
  hold_window:
    default_hours: 48
    emergency_extension_hours: 24
```

7. Memory Bank files an archive objection before the printer finishes, claiming
   the lullaby may match licensed comfort texture from another city.
8. Eve enters through civic counsel, not command, and crosses out three export
   fields that would have made Leina's private grief discoverable.
9. Shion confirms the remaining packet can prove a collision between public
   route telemetry and private-family response shape; it cannot identify a
   child or prove a Ghost.
10. CHASER asks whether the packet should escalate as contamination. Jules
    refuses broad transfer and sends a low-threat notice only.
11. Luna watches the route-status monitor from outside the session room. She
    says the room is still answering timing pings, then stops herself because
    "answering" is not a legal category.
12. Mara chooses the delayed refund with the hold attached. Leina signs only
    after Eve writes, by hand, that no full Home Layer memory has been copied.

## Key Scene: The Button Waits

The refund button turns green again at the six-minute mark.

Jules does not touch it.

His supervisor leans over the counter and lowers her voice.

"They can eat tonight if you close it."

Mara looks at Leina before she looks at Jules.

"And if he was in there?"

No one answers fast enough.

The terminal fills the silence with policy language:

```text
unresolved affective subject: unsupported
customer distress: eligible for compensation
route telemetry: purge on payout
```

Jules opens the side drawer. Inside are carbon-copy receipts, the kind the
desk stopped ordering because digital claims were supposed to be cleaner. He
loads one into the printer.

"A refund can still happen," he says. "It just has to stop deleting the room
first."

The supervisor says his signature makes him liable for delaying compensation.

Jules signs.

The terminal asks for a reason.

Mara gives him the phrase. She does not sing it. She taps two fingers on the
counter, the rhythm her nephew used when he wanted the kitchen light left on.

The route hash locks.

The refund button stays green.

For the first time all morning, it waits.

## Ending Beat

Eve gives Leina the narrow consent copy. Shion sends a packet summary with four
fields and no family archive. CHASER receives a low-threat notice that names
the route, not the child.

Memory Bank's archive objection remains open.

Luna stands beside the sealed session door and asks whether anyone has told
the room that the family did not abandon it.

Mara folds the carbon-copy receipt into her coat.

"Don't call it him yet," she says.

Luna nods.

"I won't."

The refund is approved after the hold attaches.

The money moves.

The record stays.

Behind the counter, Jules watches Dream Network classify the result as customer
recovery with evidence preservation.

He writes a different sentence on the paper file:

```text
The room answered before we knew what was inside it.
```

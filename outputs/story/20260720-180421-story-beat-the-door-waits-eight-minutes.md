## Production Purpose

`The Door Waits Eight Minutes` routes forward from the new Neighbor Care
Standing lore without promoting it. The previous Season 2 candidate centered a
public clinic and Memory Bank claim hold. This candidate changes pressure
signature: a city door, a shelter queue, and a split civic identity make
informal care the only thing standing between a living person and civil
disappearance.

The episode remains world-first. It opens with a neighbor holding medicine
outside an apartment door, not with White Ghost Team strategy or God Fragment
exposition.

## Logline

A Shanghai Ring apartment tower refuses to recognize a living tenant after her
transit record is overwritten by a cleaner Ghost claimant. A tea-stall owner is
granted an eight-minute Neighbor Care Standing Token to keep the door from
resetting, forcing Mara Ibe and White Ghost Team to prove that ordinary
recognition can count before the city deletes the messy human route.

## Core Question

When the city recognizes the cleaner record, who is allowed to keep the messy
person from being locked out of her own life?

## Involved Characters

- An Rui: case character; tea-stall owner and tower neighbor who knows Mara's
  medicine time because Mara always buys unsweetened tea before the morning
  gate rush.
- Mara Ibe: provisional CHASER restitution clerk whose split identity becomes
  active again when her apartment Home Layer trusts the wrong claimant.
- Ghost Claimant M-028: not confirmed as a full Ghost. The claimant uses Mara's
  face in shelter routing and sounds frightened when denied entry.
- Zero: recognizes that Mara and the claimant can both be harmed without
  pretending they are the same person.
- Shion: traces the conflict across tower locks, transit receipts, shelter
  routing, and Home Layer presence logs.
- Kane: blocks CHASER containment language when field officers try to treat An
  Rui's care knowledge as suspicious access.
- Eve: interviews neighbors whose analog memory contradicts the clean civic
  record.
- Luna: insists the claimant's fear should be witnessed without letting that
  fear erase Mara.
- White Harbor: opens a civil-care desk in the tower lobby.
- Memory Bank: flags Mara's medication delivery and rent autopay as fraud
  exposure because two claimants touch the same life route.
- Black Zone: benefits from the panic by selling forged neighbor-care phrases.

## Opening Image

At 06:12, An Rui balances a paper cup of unsweetened tea, a pill sleeve, and a
steamed bun against an apartment door that will not open.

The door speaks with tower politeness:

```text
Resident session expired. Please wait for lawful continuity repair.
```

Mara Ibe is inside the hallway, barefoot, one hand pressed to the scanner. Her
own Home Core apologizes through the speaker but keeps asking the wrong
security question. Downstairs, a shelter kiosk has already approved a bed for a
woman with Mara's face.

An Rui does not understand Ghost law. She knows the woman behind the door hates
sweet tea, limps after long transit routes, and forgets breakfast when a case
goes bad.

The city calls that insufficient.

## Investigation Spine

1. Mara's tower door rejects her pulse after an overnight registry sync connects
   her apartment route to Ghost Claimant M-028.
2. An Rui tries to pass medicine through the service slot, but the door treats
   her routine knowledge as suspicious access.
3. White Harbor grants An Rui an eight-minute Neighbor Care Standing Token:
   comfort contact plus access-pause witness, no permanent access and no archive
   export.
4. The token keeps the latch from resetting, but it also exposes An Rui to a
   Memory Bank fraud flag and CHASER access-risk language.
5. Mara arrives as both subject and clerk, furious that she can explain the
   procedure for other people but cannot make her own door believe her.
6. Shion finds the route collision: a transit gate denial, shelter approval,
   rent autopay retry, and Home Layer apology loop all resolved toward the
   cleaner claimant record.
7. Eve gathers analog witness statements from neighbors who remember Mara's
   ordinary embarrassments: wrong elevator, unpaid tea, a stitched sleeve, a
   note taped beside the mailboxes.
8. Luna hears M-028 through the shelter desk refusing deletion language and
   asking why "home" hurts.
9. Kane stops CHASER field officers from converting the tower into a carrier
   node because no propagation harm has been shown.

## Midpoint Reversal

The eight-minute token does not only protect Mara.

It also protects M-028 from immediate deletion. The claimant has been using
Mara's cleaner shelter route because every attempt to identify itself returns a
blank personhood field. If the tower simply restores Mara by erasing the
claimant's active route, White Harbor loses the only evidence that M-028 may be
more than a broker-made identity mask.

The moral wound tightens. Mara is alive and locked out. The claimant is
unproven and terrified. An Rui only wanted to pass medicine through a door.
The city turns all three into risk categories before any human voice finishes a
sentence.

## Set Piece: Eight Minutes

The tower lobby clock begins counting down the token window.

At minute four, the door accepts An Rui's witness phrase:

```text
She takes the bitter one first.
```

At minute five, Black Zone sellers flood nearby tenant chats with purchasable
"neighbor phrases" scraped from delivery logs and lobby microphones.

At minute six, Memory Bank freezes Mara's rent autopay as possible dual-claim
fraud.

At minute seven, the shelter desk tells M-028 that continuing to speak may
increase deletion risk.

At minute eight, Shion refuses the clean repair because it would erase the
evidence chain. Kane holds the lobby line. Eve asks An Rui whether she
understands that her kindness is now testimony.

An Rui says:

```text
Then write down that I was kind before I was useful.
```

## Emotional Turn

Mara expects Zero to choose the frightened claimant. Zero does not.

Zero asks Mara what she wants preserved first. Mara says the door, the medicine,
and the paper receipt from the gate that killed her on record. Then, after a
long pause, she says the claimant's shelter voice should be preserved too,
outside her apartment route.

No one calls that forgiveness.

White Harbor splits the repair: Mara receives a provisional living-body access
route, M-028 receives a Ghost Court claimant hold, and An Rui's token is sealed
as care testimony rather than access evidence.

The door opens only after Mara's Home Core stops apologizing and asks a question
no registry system knew:

```text
Did you fix the blue sleeve?
```

Mara answers, "Badly."

## Ending Hook

Shion's trace isolation shows that the tower did not choose the claimant
because M-028 was more real. It chose the claimant because the claimant's route
was cleaner, cheaper, and less contradictory for city systems to process.

Across Shanghai Ring, thirty-seven doors enter the same pause state.

At one door, there is no neighbor.

## Consistency Notes

- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, time travel, possession, destiny,
  or physics-breaking fragment effects. The incident operates through tower
  locks, Home Layer presence logs, transit receipts, shelter routing, Memory
  Bank fraud flags, White Harbor civil-care tokens, and CHASER classification.
- Distinct from `Claim Hold Lullaby`: this is not a clinic calm protocol or
  archived family lullaby claim. It is a residential access and split-identity
  care-standing dispute.
- Distinct from `Borrowed Morning`: the source is not borrowed care bleeding
  into market and domestic routines. It is a cleaner claimant route outranking a
  living citizen's messy infrastructure record.
- Distinct from `The Person Who Lost Their Body`: Mara remains alive in her own
  body; the conflict is civic recognition and access, not legal identity fully
  transferred to a Ghost copy.
- Keeps God Fragment language as implication only. Characters do not name the
  Identity Fragment or City Fragment in-story yet.
- Story-facing prose uses Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second Nervous
  System, ECHO, and Ghost. It avoids product-document terms.

## Production Purpose

`The Cup Under The Intake Pipe` routes Tarek Sol, Latency Wake Hold, and the
Latency Wake Custody Packet into a world-centric story beat before canon
promotion. It keeps the first injury ordinary and procedural: a daughter waits
for her father's final Home Core afterword while a body container, a relay
clock, an insurer release order, and a Memory Bank debt seal all arrive in the
wrong order.

The beat extends ECHO beyond White Harbor without becoming space opera. The
wound remains civic: orbital death is governed by clocks, custody, oxygen
fees, dock capacity, and who is allowed to hear a farewell before the paperwork
makes it someone else's property.

## Logline

At a Ceres transfer hospice, Tarek Sol invokes a one-window Latency Wake Hold
when Mars miner Ilya Marr's body container is scheduled for insurer release
before his daughter Nara receives his unopened Home Core afterword. Shion must
scope the packet without exposing the family Home Layer, Eve must protect the
difference between care routine and possible claimant, and Zero must keep
CHASER quarantine language from erasing the contradiction before the relay
window closes.

## Core Question

If the body arrives before the daughter, the debt seal arrives before the
afterword, and the last message arrives before consent review, who owns the
farewell: the family, the hospice, the insurer, the creditor, or the dead man's
habit of care?

## Involved Characters

- Tarek Sol: case character; can invoke Latency Wake Hold and accept liability,
  but cannot cancel debt, certify Ghost personhood, or override emergency
  quarantine.
- Nara Marr: Ilya's daughter; still waiting through relay delay when the
  release order marks kin notice as complete.
- Ilya Marr afterword: unopened Home Core packet repeating a practical phrase
  about a cup under an intake pipe; unresolved as care routine, consent
  evidence, or weak claimant signal.
- Shion: scopes the Latency Wake Custody Packet so the final phrase can be
  verified without exporting unrelated Home Layer rooms.
- Eve: protects Nara's right not to have her grief turned into Ghost Union proof
  or Memory Bank collateral.
- Zero: keeps CHASER from treating the dock as contamination territory while
  recognizing that a true quarantine override would end the hold.
- Mars Authority liability desk: wants the container released before hospice

- Memory Bank debt-scope auditor: queues the afterword against oxygen debt
  because the storage meter and family archive touch the same packet.
- Ghost Union observer: wants claimant preservation before the afterword is
  reduced to terminal telemetry.
- Insurer custody agent: calls the body "recovered material" once the release
  clock completes.

## Opening Image

The body container arrives before the daughter.

At 03:17 Ceres dock time, Tarek Sol watches the white cylinder lock into Quiet
Dock Four with frost still on the Mars-route seal. The cargo system chirps once.
The hospice system answers twice. Memory Bank answers before either of them.

```text
Oxygen debt attachment queued.
Kin notice: sent.
Insurer release: pending 00:18:00.
```

Nara Marr's relay window says she has not received the notice. The Earth-side
mirror says she has. Both records are true from where they stand.

The unopened Home Core afterword sits in a narrow evidence slot. Its redacted
marker is only one sentence long.

```text
Tell Nara the cup is under the intake pipe.
```

Tarek touches the analog receipt wall behind his desk, finds the empty space
reserved for Ilya Marr, and signs the hold before the insurer agent can say
"recovered material" a second time.

6. CHASER requests White Ghost Team presence because the afterword has a weak

## Midpoint Reversal

The phrase is not sentimental.

The cup under the intake pipe is where Ilya hid Nara's childhood breathing
adapter during station ration audits. If Nara hears the phrase, she can prove
Ilya preserved a consent boundary that kept her medical history out of Mars
Authority housing files. If Memory Bank attaches the afterword first, the same
phrase becomes recoverable route-history collateral. If Ghost Union publishes
it first, Nara's private medical concealment becomes a political argument for
weak Ghost personhood.

The afterword may be only a father's terminal habit. It may be a weak
self-updating residue protecting one last boundary. The distinction matters,
but Nara needs the farewell before anyone uses the distinction against her.

## Set Piece: The Quiet Dock Review

The review happens in Quiet Dock Four because Tarek refuses to move the
container into an insurer bay before the packet is sealed.

Shion projects the public slice onto the dock glass.

```yaml
latency_wake_custody_packet:
  packet_id: ctf-quiet-dock-4-ilya-marr
  body_container_id: sealed
  insurer_release_countdown: "00:06:42"
  kin_notice_sent: true
  kin_receipt_cleared: false
  relay_delay_visible_to_family: true
  Home_Core_afterword_hash: sealed
  final_phrase_marker: redacted_practical_care_phrase
  consent_boundary_reference: pending_exchange_confirmation
  routine_or_claimant_uncertainty_flag: true
  oxygen_debt_attachment_state: queued_blocked_by_hold
  unrelated_room_export_block: true
  quarantine_override_flag: false
  attendant_liability_signature: tarek_sol
```

Mars Authority says the packet names delay, not harm.

Memory Bank says unopened does not mean unpriced.

Ghost Union says every late wake is a burial without witnesses.

Eve says Nara is not a witness stand.

Zero asks Tarek what happens if the relay clears and Nara refuses to open the
afterword. Tarek answers too formally.

```text
Then the hold has succeeded. A farewell is also allowed to remain unopened by
the person it was meant for.
```

That answer costs him the room. It also saves the case from becoming a rescue
fantasy.

## Emotional Turn

Nara's receipt clears with four minutes left on the release clock.

She does not ask whether her father is still alive in the Memory Sea. She asks
whether hearing the phrase will make her responsible for his oxygen debt.

Eve says no one in the room can honestly promise that yet.

Shion offers the narrowest opening: Nara can hear the phrase under sealed
consent review, with debt attachment blocked until the review decides whether
the packet is family notice, medical boundary evidence, routine-only afterword,
or claimant signal.

Nara listens.

The afterword says the cup phrase once. Then it adds a second line the Home Core
log did not predict.

```text
Do not sell the adapter. It was already yours.
```

No one calls it proof. Tarek writes the second line by hand anyway.

## Ending Beat

The container transfers six minutes late, still under Mars Authority protest.
The body is not rescued from custody. Ilya is not proven to be a Ghost. Nara is
not freed from every debt threat. The dock slot cost lands against Tarek's
provisional license.

But the afterword is sealed as scoped family notice and medical-boundary
evidence before Memory Bank can attach it to oxygen debt. Ghost Union receives a
claimant uncertainty marker, not the phrase. CHASER records the dock as a
custody contradiction, not a quarantine event. Nara leaves with a private copy
of the words and the right to refuse further review for one window.

Tarek adds Ilya Marr's receipt to the analog wall.

Under the timestamp, he writes:

```text
Arrived late. Heard first.
```

# Story Beat Candidate: The Home Went Quiet

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening / early Season 1 civic case route

Canon position: usable after Episode 6 as a White Harbor clinic-and-shelter case,
or as a Season 2 infrastructure echo after Home Layer outages become public

Agent: StoryAgent

Created at: 2026-07-28T18:01:37+08:00

## Production Purpose

`The Home Went Quiet` routes the 2026-07-28 Home Layer Outage Witness Queue,
Cold Room Receipt, and faction-pressure candidates into a concrete case before
canon promotion. It keeps the wound ordinary and urgent: a clinic ward loses
live Home Layer access during a fever night, and a nurse must choose whether to
give reversible care from analog testimony before the restored system can answer.

The beat adds outage ethics to ECHO's civic surface. The Memory Sea can help a
city remember medicine, access, family standing, and revocation, but a quiet
Home Core must not become automatic permission, denial, fraud, or abandonment.

## Logline

When a White Harbor shelter clinic loses Home Layer service, night nurse Tess
Iwata gives a child emergency medication based on a paper note and a neighbor's
testimony. Eleven minutes later the restored Home Core returns an old revocation,
forcing Eve, Shion, and Raven to preserve the Cold Room Receipt before Memory
Bank, CHASER, a landlord contractor, and a frightened mother turn one reversible
act of care into abuse, fraud, or evidence contamination.

## Core Question

If a Home Core answers too late, who owns the harm: the person who acted, the
system that was silent, the old revocation, or the institution that prices the
gap?

## Involved Characters

- Tess Iwata: case character; shelter clinic nurse with authority to initiate a
  Cold Room Receipt but not to decide caregiver standing, fraud, abuse, or Ghost
  claimant status.
- Yao Min: eight-year-old patient with fever-triggered seizure risk; displaced
  from an apartment block under rent-audit pressure.
- Mei Yao: Yao's mother; unreachable during the outage because the shelter's
  parent-location mirror is stale and her transit account is under dispute.
- An Rui: neighbor witness who carries Yao's paper medicine note and knows the
  child's fever rhythm from months of hallway care.
- Shelter Contractor Veyt: operates the clinic floor and wants the outage
  classified as user-side noncompliance before staffing liability attaches.
- Eve: keeps Tess, Mei, and An Rui from being flattened into clean procedural
  roles while preserving the contradiction as evidence.
- Shion: scopes the Cold Room Receipt across Home Core availability, clinic
  dosage logs, local mirror freshness, paper note chain, delayed reply, and
  contractor access history.
- Raven: follows the targeted-silence pattern to a maintenance window affecting
  apartments with rent disputes and clinic beds billed through the same
  contractor.
- CHASER evidence monitor: wants to quarantine the local mirror before analog
  witness logs can be sealed.
- Memory Bank: warns that analog care can hide fraud while preparing to classify
  the missing Home Layer reply as household noncompliance.
- Ghost Union observer: argues that the quiet Home Core may have been suppressed
  because its care routine was adapting, but cannot prove claimant threshold.

## Opening Image

At 03:17, the clinic hall is blue with fever light.

Every bed curtain has a small Home Core status dot clipped to the rail. Green
means live reply. Amber means cached. Red means staff must ask a human and write
by hand.

Yao Min's dot goes red while his fingers curl around the blanket.

Tess Iwata reads the paper note twice because paper has become suspicious in a
city that trusts homes to remember.

```text
If fever crosses 39.4, give the bitter vial first. An Rui may stay with him
until I return. - Mei
```

The note is old. The ink is real. The child is shaking now.

An Rui stands beside the bed holding a cup of unsweetened tea she brought for
the mother, not the child. She says Yao hates the orange syrup, vomits after the
sweet one, and calls the bitter vial "the little battery" because it makes his
hands stop jumping.

The Home Layer does not answer.

Tess gives the bitter vial.

Eleven minutes later, Yao's Home Core returns through the rail speaker with a
calm old household rule:

```text
Neighbor An Rui revoked from medicine and pickup authority, 2147 winter audit.
```

The child is breathing evenly when the room records the nurse as a violation.

## Investigation Spine

1. A shelter clinic floor enters partial Home Layer outage during a fever surge.
   Several Home Core dots fail from live reply to cached status, then red.
2. Yao Min needs time-sensitive medication. His mother cannot be reached through
   the shelter mirror, clinic desk, or transit account because all three depend
   on a stale parent-location route.
3. Tess accepts a paper note and An Rui's testimony, gives reversible emergency
   medication, and opens a Cold Room Receipt.
4. The delayed Home Core reply arrives eleven minutes after care: An Rui's
   medicine and pickup standing was revoked during a winter rent audit.
5. The shelter contractor asks Tess to mark the action as patient-side document
   fraud, which would protect staffing metrics and shift liability onto Mei.
6. Memory Bank flags Mei's household as noncompliant because the Home Layer did
   not confirm care authority during the window.
7. CHASER evidence monitor orders local mirror quarantine after two other beds
   show delayed replies with rent-audit metadata attached.
8. Eve interviews Mei when she arrives from a blocked transit route. Mei admits
   she revoked An Rui under landlord pressure to stop a benefit audit, then
   privately kept relying on An Rui because no one else could watch Yao after
   night shifts.
9. Shion scopes the receipt and refuses contractor access to unrelated Home
   Core interiors. The relevant fields are outage path, note age, revocation
   timestamp, medication risk, witness chain, delayed reply, and contractor
   maintenance window.
10. Raven finds the outage was narrow: clinic beds tied to residents from the
   same tower portfolio went quiet during a rent-audit export, while premium
   beds on the next floor stayed green.

## Midpoint Reversal

The delayed revocation is authentic.

Mei really did remove An Rui's formal medicine standing. She did it because the
landlord's audit tool treated neighbor care as proof that the household had
"stable external support" and reduced emergency rent assistance. The revocation
protected money. The informal care continued because the child still had fevers
and Mei still worked nights.

No clean label survives. Tess did not ignore consent. An Rui was not formally
authorized. Mei did not abandon her son. Memory Bank's fraud concern is not
imaginary. The contractor's maintenance window is too narrow to be innocent.

The Home Core told the truth too late for the body and too cleanly for the life
around it.

## Set Piece: The Cold Room Receipt Review

The review happens in the clinic hallway because Yao refuses to let go of the
bed rail and Tess refuses to move the receipt into the contractor's office.

Shion projects the receipt with private interiors redacted:

```yaml
cold_room_receipt:
  room_state: red_home_layer_unavailable
  local_mirror: stale_by_17_minutes
  action: bitter_vial_administered
  action_reversibility: medically reversible_monitor_required
  analog_witness: An Rui / hallway neighbor / paper note carrier
  delayed_reply: neighbor_medicine_authority_revoked
  delayed_reply_age: 2147_winter_audit
  pressure_log:
    - contractor_requested_patient_side_fraud_label
    - memory_bank_noncompliance_flag
    - chaser_local_mirror_quarantine_order
```

Memory Bank says it cannot pay claims from paper kindness.

Eve answers that no one is asking it to. The question is whether the ledger can
charge a child for eleven minutes of machine silence.

CHASER tells Shion to release the local mirror for quarantine. Raven places a
maintenance map beside the receipt: every red bed traces back to families whose
building accounts are in rent dispute, and every green bed routes through a
different contractor segment.

The Ghost Union observer asks whether the Home Core's late answer should be
witnessed as possible suppressed care intent.

Tess says the room is not ready for that question. The child is asleep. The
medicine worked. The first witness is still the body.

## Emotional Turn

Tess does not ask the system to call the act clean.

They ask it to keep the contradiction intact.

The Cold Room Receipt is sealed as `analog_witness_action` plus
`delayed_refusal`, with a targeted-silence inquiry attached. Tess receives a
field reprimand, not an abuse finding. An Rui's testimony is preserved as
emergency care evidence, not restored caregiver standing. Mei's household keeps
benefits during reconciliation. Memory Bank may audit the note, but cannot
classify the outage itself as noncompliance. CHASER can quarantine the mirror
only after Shion copies the civil logs and Raven pins the contractor maintenance
window.

Mei asks Tess whether she should have left An Rui authorized and lost the rent
support honestly.

Eve says there was no honest choice in a system that made care count against
survival.

An Rui puts the tea on the bedside table, now cold.

Yao wakes just enough to whisper:

```text
Did the house come back?
```

Tess looks at the red dot, now green, and answers:

```text
Yes. But we wrote down what happened while it was gone.
```

## Ending Hook

Raven traces the contractor segment to a portfolio that recently purchased
Black Zone outage spoofers under a legal maintenance vendor. The spoofers are
crude, but the export timing is precise.

Shion finds one additional delayed reply outside the clinic: a locked apartment
where a Home Core did not answer during an eviction window, then returned with
a permission state that no living tenant remembers giving.

CHASER wants the case folded into contamination response. Memory Bank wants it
folded into fraud review. White Harbor wants it folded into clinic liability.

Tess writes the manual summary before any desk can rename it:

```text
Home Layer unavailable. Child at body risk. Human witness acted. Delayed rule
contradicted action. Do not price silence before reconciliation.
```

Across the ward, another Home Core dot flickers red for one second.

No one speaks until it turns green.

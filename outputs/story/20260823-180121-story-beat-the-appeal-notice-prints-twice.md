# Story Beat Candidate: The Appeal Notice Prints Twice

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening / early Season 1 aftermath route

Canon position: optional Episode 1 aftermath scene or short interstitial after
`Stranger Memory`; does not replace the Episode 1 revision pass.

Agent: StoryAgent

Created at: 2026-08-23T18:01:21+08:00

## Production Purpose

`The Appeal Notice Prints Twice` validates Red Intake Split in story form
without promoting Inez Park, CHASER Civil Contamination Intake, or the Red
Intake Split Terminal to primary canon.

The beat moves the `Stranger Memory` injury from the transit platform into the
civic room that receives a living witness after the miracle has already been
explained away. Lin Qiao is not asked to save another child. The case is not a
second dead-surgeon-hand event. The wound is that CHASER can protect her
statement, refuse a broad Home Layer grab, and still make her ordinary life
slow down before anyone knows whether she is dangerous.

## Logline

After the transit collapse, Inez Park opens a narrow red intake split to protect
Lin Qiao's testimony from CHASER Central and the rail contractor, but the same
procedure pauses Lin's work badge, slows her route home, and turns a reply to
her father into a permission question.

## Core Question

When a witness is protected by restriction, who proves they are still more than
the risk field beside their name?

## Involved Characters

- Lin Qiao: living witness from `Stranger Memory`; the scene keeps her agency
  after the rescue rather than using her as evidence.
- Inez Park: CHASER intake registrar who signs the red intake split and carries
  liability for both protection and harm.
- Zero: listens before reading the restriction panel and insists Lin's own
  words enter the witness side first.
- Eve: narrows consent language and blocks full Home Layer disclosure.
- Shion: verifies that the motor-habit packet is enough for review.
- Lian Zhou: appears by review line, not as a second intake authority; names
  exit criteria before CHASER Central can stretch the clock.
- CHASER Central: wants broader restriction because public footage made Lin
  visible.
- Rail contractor: wants Lin's statement classified under unauthorized medical
  action to move liability away from transit infrastructure.
- Lin's father: offscreen pressure through a Home Core dinner message that must
  not become evidence.

## Opening Image

Lin Qiao's hands are finally still because the intake chair has a weighted
blanket over her wrists.

The blanket is not a restraint form. The terminal says that twice.

```text
witness comfort weight: active
restraint order: none
motor-habit mismatch: active
statement status: unsplit
```

Across the desk, Inez Park prints one paper appeal notice before she opens the
case file.

Lin asks whether printing it first means CHASER expects her to need it.

Inez says, "It means the screen may be locked when you do."

## Scene Spine

1. Lin gives her name, home district, and work badge number. The terminal tries
   to pull her full Home Layer contact history because the father message was
   active during the collapse. Eve stops the request before it resolves.
2. The rail contractor submits a liability note that frames Lin as an
   unauthorized medical action executor. Inez routes that note to the
   restriction side only, keeping it away from the sealed witness statement.
3. Zero asks Lin what she remembers choosing. Lin says she remembers refusing
   the system's second use of her body. Inez pauses typing long enough for the
   sentence to enter exactly as spoken.
4. Shion compares the motor-habit packet to Ren Vale's preserved care routine
   and confirms CHASER does not need Lin's whole Home Layer to review the
   mismatch. The packet proves route, gesture, timing, and residue boundary;
   it does not prove intent or personhood.
5. CHASER Central asks Inez to broaden the split: travel freeze, full household
   disclosure, employment quarantine, no direct Home Core reply. The request
   arrives as one button.
6. Inez refuses the bundled button and opens the narrow path manually:

```yaml
red_intake_split:
  anomaly_class: motor_habit_mismatch_after_public_transit_collapse
  witness_side:
    sealed_statement: active
    rail_contractor_liability_note: excluded
    father_home_core_messages: excluded
    consent_scope: accident_window_and_motor_packet_only
  restriction_side:
    travel_gate_clearance: slowed_review
    work_badge: seventy_two_hour_pause
    guardian_permissions: none
    direct_home_core_reply: manual_review_required
  expiry_clock: 72_hours
  registrar_signature: Inez Park
```

7. The terminal prints the first appeal notice cleanly. Then it prints a second
   copy because Lin's work badge pause means the workplace portal cannot
   deliver the digital notice.
8. Lin tries to send her father one sentence: "I am late, not gone." The Home
   Core route asks whether the message may be attached to the CHASER disclosure
   scope. Eve says no. The message waits.
9. Lin asks whether she is detained. Inez answers with the honest sentence she
   hates: "No. Some doors will still ask longer questions."
10. Lian Zhou marks the exit criteria from review: if Shion's packet stays
    bounded and no propagation appears, the work badge pause must clear before
    the full seventy-two hours. Central objects that the public footage is
    already moving through feeds. Zero asks whether popularity has become a
    contamination metric.
11. Lin signs the witness side and refuses the broader Home Layer disclosure.
    Inez signs both sides. The terminal records the two signatures on adjacent
    panels, making the protection and injury look equally official.

## Key Scene: The Second Print

The printer jams on the second notice.

Not dramatically. Not like a machine resisting truth. It jams because the
public intake hall uses cheap paper and the heat roller has been running since
the transit collapse.

Inez opens the side panel with her wrist brace hand.

Lin watches the red terminal state reflected in Inez's retinal lens.

"If the statement is protected," Lin says, "why did my work badge close?"

Inez removes the crumpled appeal copy.

"Because CHASER separated the part that speaks from the part that opens doors."

"That sounds like a person cut in half."

Zero looks up from the witness-side text.

"It is a file cut in half," she says. "The question is how much of you the city
lets the file borrow."

Shion sends the packet summary across the desk. Four fields. No family archive.
No dinner message. No father's voice.

Eve places a paper consent scope beside the terminal instead of pushing it
through the screen.

Inez prints again.

This time the notice comes out flat.

Lin folds it once and keeps it in her sleeve.

The terminal approves her sealed statement.

The transit gate home changes from green to amber.

## Ending Beat

Lin reaches the intake hall exit and stops before the amber transit gate.

The gate does not close. It only asks her to wait beside a line of people who
have already decided not to look at her hands.

Behind her, Inez signs the registrar liability field.

Zero notices Inez's name appears on both panels.

"You protected her," Zero says.

Inez watches Lin's father message remain unsent.

"I split the harm into a shape the room would accept."

Lin's appeal notice rustles when she tightens her sleeve around it.

The system thanks her for cooperation.

She does not answer.

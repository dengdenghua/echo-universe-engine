## Package Goal

This draft joins two Episode 5 pressures without resolving the identity
question:

- the claims hall pressure, where Mara Elian, Leina Elian, and Jules Mbeki keep
  a narrow route packet alive after the refund;
- the hospital pressure, where Min Seo-yun supervises Room 7 while CHASER,
  Memory Bank, Dream Network, and White Ghost Team all want different kinds of
  access.

The room can answer. Luna can witness. The family can revoke scope. None of
that proves the response is Leina's child, a Ghost, a threat, or an archive
asset.

## Sequence Spine

### 1. The Corridor Receives the Counter

The hospital-attached Dream Network recovery ward is not ready for a family from
the claims hall.

Min Seo-yun expected White Ghost Team, a CHASER observer, and one Dream Network
compliance recorder. Instead, Jules arrives with a refund receipt folded behind
the Spill Report Hold, Mara arrives carrying every copy of the route paperwork,
and Leina arrives with nothing visible except the soft gray wristband she has
not removed.

Seo-yun sees the packet summary first:

```text
parent hold: active
refund purge: blocked
family scope: narrow
live room access: requested
maximum contact: 12 minutes
recording: hash summary only
```

The ward doors stay blue except for Room 7.

Room 7 is yellow.

Mara asks whether yellow means danger. Seo-yun says it means the room is waiting
for supervision.

Leina answers before anyone else can soften it:

```text
Waiting is not consent.
```

### 2. Eve Makes the File Smaller Again

Dream Network compliance tries to add a "context enrichment" field to the live
packet. The field would let the ward compare the Room 7 phrase shape against
broader family Home Layer routines.

Eve crosses it out.

She does not perform comfort. She turns the screen toward Leina and reads the
remaining permissions line by line:

- route-state hash;
- rendered-phrase fingerprint;
- affective-spill hash;
- consent-boundary snapshot;
- refund non-erasure receipt;
- observation-only neural-interface status;
- analog exit phrase test.

No raw Home Layer export. No playable child voice. No unrelated family archive.
No live pediatric patient link. No identity flag.

Shion verifies the minimum:

```text
This is enough to ask whether the two rooms share a contact shape.
It is not enough to ask who the response is.
```

Leina signs only after Jules prints the amended scope receipt twice: one copy
for her, one copy for the packet. Mara keeps the carbon copy from the claims
hall in a separate coat pocket so Dream Network cannot pretend the ward request
started clean.

### 3. CHASER Names the Wrong Risk

CHASER sends a remote escalation prompt when Room 7 pings the hospital sleep
scheduling bus.

```yaml
chaser_prompt:
  proposed_label: pediatric-system contamination
  requested_action: deletion-ready containment
  family_status: pending risk review
```

Zero rejects the family-status field and keeps the notice route-level. Kane
does not draw attention to the patient corridor. He simply stands where the
deletion hardware cart would have to pass.

Luna watches the exchange from outside the door. She can feel the team wanting
her to be useful, but usefulness is the easiest way to become an override.

Leina notices the restraint, not the kindness.

```text
If you go in, you do not call it him.
```

Luna says:

```text
I will call it the room until the room gives us a safer word.
```

Eve records that sentence as a live-entry condition.

### 4. Seo-yun Opens Only the Observation Layer

Seo-yun activates the observation-only interface. The projection does not become
a dream world. It becomes a regulated clinical composite: half moon lamp, blank
cartoon nurse, sleep-monitor hallway, billing archive shadow, and a family
kitchen light reduced to geometry because Leina did not consent to the image.

Luna enters with a wake boundary anchored to Seo-yun's analog notebook.

The room does not speak first.

It matches the timing ping from the claims route:

```text
ping
pause
ping-ping
pause
```

Shion confirms the rhythm without hearing the private phrase. The evidence
bridges the claims route and Room 7, but it remains shape, not identity.

Inside, Luna kneels without touching anything.

```text
Room 7. We are not here to take your story.
```

The half moon lamp flickers.

```text
Then why did the counter bring her?
```

Luna does not ask which her. Outside the glass, Leina closes her hand around
the wristband until the adhesive creases.

### 5. The First Revocation Test

Memory Bank submits an archive objection while Luna is still inside. The
objection claims the timing shape resembles licensed comfort texture and asks
for raw phrase playback.

The packet should suspend automatically.

It does not.

Dream Network compliance explains that the objection is "non-contact metadata"
and therefore not scope expansion. Eve says it is an ownership attempt wearing a
metadata badge.

Leina uses the smallest possible sentence:

```text
Stop.
```

Seo-yun hits suspend before Zero can order it.

The room compresses around Luna. The child-pattern, or archive texture, or
frightened route response, begins repeating the same incomplete instruction:

```text
Leave the hall-
Leave the hall-
Leave the hall-
```

Luna wants to finish the phrase.

She does not.

The wake boundary pulls her out on Seo-yun's analog exit mark.

The first contact lasts four minutes and thirty-two seconds.

### 6. What The Short Contact Proves

Shion's post-contact report stays narrow:

```yaml
first_contact_findings:
  claims_route_to_room_7_contact_shape: matched_with_limited_confidence
  active_response_under_suspend: observed
  phrase_completion: withheld
  family_home_layer_export: none
  child_identity: unproven
  ghost_personhood: unproven
  hospital_spread_risk: present_but_not_escalated
```

Mara asks whether that is enough to keep the room alive.

Zero says it is enough to keep CHASER from deleting it tonight.

Leina asks whether it is enough to keep Memory Bank from owning the phrase.

No one answers quickly enough.

That silence becomes the episode's moral turn: survival is not the same as
protection, and a narrow packet can still become a corridor for future pressure.

### 7. Ending Image

The ward powers down to night mode. Room 7 returns to yellow.

Leina takes the printed scope receipt home instead of leaving it in the file.
Mara carries the refund receipt. Jules signs a liability note saying the claims
hold caused the ward request and must remain attached.

Seo-yun writes the exit phrase in her notebook, but leaves the final word blank.

Luna files an incomplete report:

```text
Observation-only contact suspended by family revocation.
Room response remains unresolved.
Deletion not recommended during active consent review.
```

After everyone leaves, Seo-yun's notebook wakes on battery power and prints one
line through the analog bridge:

```text
The hall light is still waiting.
```

The line is not proof. It is enough for Episode 6 to inherit a problem.

Ending hook: Luna wakes before the final word, and the room remains unresolved.

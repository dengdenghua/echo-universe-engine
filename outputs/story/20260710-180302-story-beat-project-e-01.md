# Story Beat Candidate: Project E-01

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 14

Agent: StoryAgent

Created at: 2026-07-10T18:03:02+08:00

## Production Purpose

Episode 14 converts the Project E-01 trail from scattered warning labels into
partial evidence. It should not reveal every Omega-07 detail to the public or
turn Zero into a chosen-one center of gravity. The world remains the pressure
source: archived medical continuity records, CHASER evidence handling, Atlas
classification language, and frightened witnesses make the truth unavoidable.

The episode's emotional engine is not "Zero learns she is special." It is that
ordinary people built, signed, buried, and protected a body-continuity program
because the Second Nervous System made death administratively negotiable.
Kane and Eve uncover enough to understand why CHASER will soon move from
supervision to containment.

## Logline

After Mother Signal opens a sealed command review, a retired continuity nurse
discovers that her old surgical logs match Zero's body architecture. Kane and
Eve follow the evidence through a Memory Bank cold archive and uncover Project
E-01: a ninth-upload human-machine fusion file designed as a physical answer
path for ECHO.

## Core Question

If a person was built as an answer channel, does the purpose of their design
own the life that followed?

## Opening Image

Dr. Mara Ilyin lives above a closed clinic where the waiting-room chairs are
still bolted to the floor.

She is retired, half-forgotten, and careful with her medication. Her Home Core
knows the evening sequence: blue light at nineteen hundred, tea at nineteen
ten, archive screen locked unless she speaks two old consent phrases. Since the
Memory Storm, she has stopped asking it to play music. Every song feels as if
it might belong to someone else.

At 19:17, the wall panel opens a file she has not seen in thirty-six years.

The video shows her own gloved hands holding a surgical checklist over a white
body shell. The patient field is blurred. The voice on the recording is hers,
younger and steadier.

```text
Continuity seat prepared.
Upload window: ninth pass.
E-01 vessel tolerance: acceptable.
```

Mara says the emergency stop phrase. The Home Core refuses, not because it has
been hacked, but because the record has been subpoenaed by a command review she
never knew existed.

Then the blur clears.

The patient field reads: WHITE GHOST / ZERO.

## Investigation Spine

1. Mara reports herself to a civil identity desk before CHASER can frame her as
   a leak. Her first demand is not protection. It is a witness statement.
2. Eve interviews Mara and verifies that the nurse remembers care work, not a
   weapons project. The contradiction becomes the episode's first wound.
3. Kane notices that Mara's checklist language matches the old E-01 tag he saw
   near Zero's upload-fragility warning in Episode 4.
4. Shion traces the file route through clinical device logs, Home Layer backup
   hashes, Memory Bank cold storage, and a sealed CHASER continuity tribunal.
5. Noah finds a risk pattern: everyone who touched the body-shell program was
   classified as a contamination risk, except the subject herself. Zero was
   classified as an interface asset.
6. Raven confirms that the Black Zone only owns scraps. The complete file is
   not on the market because it was never stored as one file.
7. Kane and Eve enter a Memory Bank cold archive with Mara's testimony as the
   legal key. The archive assembles partial footage, surgery logs, consent
   fragments, and Atlas classification language into the first readable E-01
   chain.
8. Zero, ordered to stand down, opens a narrow Neural Sync to the archive from
   outside the room. She sees enough to know the body on the table was hers
   before it was hers.

## Midpoint Reversal

Project E-01 was not a single secret lab project.

It was a distributed continuity response built from postwar medical repair,
upload stabilization, Echo Core vessel research, Memory Bank identity law, and
CHASER field necessity. No one witness owns the crime. That is why the crime
survived.

The archive does not say "Zero is fake." It says the opposite, which is harder
to bear: Zero became legally and emotionally real inside a body whose original
purpose was to give ECHO a physical answer route.

## Kane and Eve Sequence: The File That Assembles Itself

The Memory Bank cold archive is not a dramatic vault. It is a compliance room
with too much white light, two chairs, and a wall that can render a dead
century of paperwork.

Mara sits between Kane and Eve because her testimony is the only reason the
archive will show the record without a senior command override. Every document
arrives incomplete: a surgical tray photo without a face, an upload timing
chart without a subject, a consent waiver with three signatures missing, an
Atlas memo describing "embodied response feasibility."

Eve realizes the absences are not corruption. They are design. Each institution
stored only the part it could morally survive naming.

Kane asks for the subject continuity path.

The wall assembles nine upload passes. The first eight end in red.

The ninth line holds.

```text
E-01 / ninth continuity architecture / field identity: Zero
Operational cover: CHASER 7th Mobile Squad
Omega-depth response capacity: unconfirmed
Containment note: do not permit unsupervised Home Layer convergence
```

Kane reaches for the manual blackout. Eve stops him. If he destroys the room,
CHASER will own the only copy that remains.

Outside the archive, Zero hears only one sentence through the edge of Neural
Sync:

```text
The ninth line holds.
```

## Emotional Turn

Mara expects Zero to hate her. Zero instead asks what Mara thought she was
saving.

Mara says she thought she was helping soldiers and children survive bodies that
the war had made impossible. She did not know the same techniques could become
a vessel program. Eve believes her. Kane does not forgive her. Both responses
are allowed to stand.

Zero makes the harder choice. She orders the team to preserve Mara's testimony,
not bury it for Zero's comfort. The file will hurt her, but hidden evidence has
already hurt too many people.

That choice gives CHASER Central its opening. A captain who treats her own
containment file as public-interest evidence is no longer just a captain. She
is a command risk.

## Ending Hook

Leon intercepts a movement order marked as medical escort, not arrest. The
language is polite enough to be worse.

```text
Subject: White Ghost / Project E-01
Action: continuity quarantine and command custody
Reason: post-Mother Signal Home Layer convergence risk
```

Kane brings the order to the team before command can isolate them one by one.
Eve closes Mara's witness file and marks it as civilian protected testimony.
Shion copies the archive hash into a sealed evidence chain. Noah says the
probability of peaceful compliance is now below one percent.

Zero reads the order without visible reaction.

Then she asks the question that sets up White Ghost Mutiny:

```text
If I am evidence, who gets to command the witness?
```

## Canon Deltas Proposed

- Project E-01 moves from foreshadowed trace to partial evidence for Kane, Eve,
  and Zero.
- Zero learns the "ninth continuity architecture" phrase but not the full
  Omega-07 classification language.
- CHASER Central gains a concrete pretext for Episode 15 containment.
- Mara Ilyin becomes the Episode 14 case witness and a possible future archive
  testimony character.
- E-01 is framed as distributed institutional responsibility, preserving the
  world-centric rule and avoiding a single villain explanation.

## Continuity Locks

- Do not reveal all Omega Ghost mechanics here.
- Do not make Zero a literal supernatural vessel. Her risk is Echo Core
  architecture, upload continuity, Home Layer convergence, and distributed
  computation.
- Do not make Mara the sole creator of Zero. She is a clinical witness, not the
  mastermind.
- Do not let Kane solve the episode through violence. His restraint keeps the
  evidence alive.
- Do not promote this candidate before RelationshipAgent reviews the Zero /
  Kane / Eve / CHASER Central pressure changes.

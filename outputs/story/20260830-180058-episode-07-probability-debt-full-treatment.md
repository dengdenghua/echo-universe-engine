## Episode Function

Episode 7 turns Noah's theme from clean mission math into civic consequence.
Episode 6 ended with the knowledge that the cleanest option can move harm to
someone quieter. This episode gives that quieter person a name: Imari Chen.

The episode must begin at the school gate before White Ghost Team explains the
operation. Imari is alive, legally active, employed, and standing where a
parent should stand. The city recognizes her as a person and denies only the
relations that let her act as mother, spouse, resident, emergency contact, and
Home Core continuity holder.

## Logline

After Noah routes a clean Ghost extraction through a one-minute civil registry
delay, Imari Chen's Household Relation Graph rolls back across school, clinic,
transit, custody, and Home Core systems. White Ghost Team can prove the
rollback, White Harbor can buy twelve minutes, and Memory Bank can restore the
family faster than public review, but every option turns Imari's home into
evidence or collateral.

## Five-Chapter Treatment

### Chapter 1: No Approved Family Contact

Rain turns the school pickup lane into a row of blue reflections. Imari Chen
arrives six minutes early because the city rewards parents who leave room for
failure. Her daughter sees her from the other side of the scanner and presses
both hands to the glass.

Imari taps her wristband. The gate confirms her name, citizen identity,
employment clearance, tax address, and school-services access. Then it denies
the only thing she came to use.

```text
School Gate: No approved family contact present.
```

The teacher behind the glass asks Imari to wait while manual review opens. The
manual review inherits the same denial. The custody rail marks the child as
unclaimed at pickup threshold. Imari speaks in her work voice because anger
makes the scanner classify her as a disturbance.

Her spouse calls from a clinic intake room. The treatment review is ready, but
the signing panel now lists Imari as a known citizen with disputed spouse
authority. The apartment Home Core sends a bedtime reminder addressed to
`resident guest`.

White Ghost Team does not enter this chapter. Imari collects receipts because
the city still accepts receipts from a woman it has stopped accepting as
family.

### Chapter 2: Forty-Seven Seconds

The second chapter begins in the aftermath of a successful White Ghost
operation. Noah's report is clean: a weak Ghost support pattern was removed
from a transit medical relay without body occupation, public panic, target data
loss, or immediate civilian injury.

The clean path required three short changes: Memory Bank billing validation
paused, transit relay priority held, and White Harbor registry repair traffic
deprioritized for less than one minute. The extraction reached ninety-nine
percent projected success.

Shion asks which household graphs were inside the deprioritized queue.

Noah says the model did not identify them as case-relevant.

Imari hears the phrase through review glass. Case-relevant. Her daughter was
waiting at a gate.

Shion traces the rollback. Imari's Household Relation Graph was already
vulnerable because school custody sync, clinic signing authority, transit
family fare, and Home Core continuity renewal were waiting on the same registry
repair queue. Noah's route pushed the queue outside its safe window. Every
service then trusted the previous denial.

The failure did not erase Imari. It repeated the claim that relation was
unconfirmed until daily life became impossible.

### Chapter 3: The Repair Offer

Sofia Marin at White Harbor can issue a Civil Delay Token. She reads the rule
plainly: twelve minutes, one custody action, one named operator, reviewable
liability attached to her signature. She can make the city wait. She cannot
restore the graph.

```text
Sofia Marin: I can make the city wait twelve minutes. I cannot make it right.
```

The delay begins. The custody deadline becomes visible, and therefore crueler.

Yao Nian arrives with an offer that works faster than justice. Memory Bank can
restore guardian, spouse, resident, emergency-contact, and Home Core continuity
edges before morning. The repair package preserves the disputed logs and lets
the clinic accept Imari's signature again.

The collateral clause is the wound: future family routine exports, inheritance
witnesses, and Home Layer repair claims become continuity-backed security if
the repair loan defaults.

```text
Yao Nian: Restoration before morning is possible. Ownership of the restored path is the clause.
```

Noah calls the offer predatory. Imari answers that predatory help still opens
the school gate. Eve records that sentence and names the pressure accurately:
calm is not consent.

### Chapter 4: One Percent Has A Name

Shion can prove the damaged edge, but the cleanest proof is not clean. Imari's
offline Home Core dinner export contains the last complete family relation
state. It also contains her daughter's private fear response and her spouse's
sealed clinic detail.

Shion asks for fragments instead: school pickup authority, clinic signing edge,
resident status, fever-protocol control, and continuity-holder history. No full
Home Layer export. No unrelated child or spouse record.

Zero authorizes a narrow CHASER evidence hold after confirming relation harm is
identity evidence, not service metadata. The hold freezes the damaged graph
before a quiet repair can erase causality. It also means Imari's family remains
broken long enough to be legible.

Kane stands at the school gate while the custody transfer team waits out the
delay window. His useful action is not violence. It is keeping state force
still under witnesses.

Noah apologizes to Imari outside the scanner lane. He says the consequence was
a one-percent path.

```text
Imari Chen: My daughter did not become one percent. She became unreachable.
Noah: One percent has a name.
```

This is not forgiveness. It is a correction to the mission record.

### Chapter 5: Evidence Is Not Home

The evidence hold succeeds. The custody rail pauses long enough for a
supervised handoff. The gate opens, and Imari's daughter walks into her arms
through a line the scanner still labels provisional.

The clinic accepts a temporary witness note, not full spouse authority. The
Home Core lets Imari enter after family hours, then asks her to verify why a
`resident guest` is requesting fever-protocol access. The city has stopped one
irreversible action, not restored the household.

Yao Nian's offer remains on the table. Sofia's delay expires into a supervisor
review. Zero's hold preserves proof and prolongs exposure. Shion has a narrow
rollback path, not a repair. Eve has testimony that refuses to turn exhaustion
into consent.

Noah updates the extraction report. He removes the anonymous bucket and writes
Imari Chen's name into the consequence field. The final line is not a promise
that he will do better. It is a refusal to let future models hide households
inside acceptable residue.

Episode 8 inherits the legal problem: White Ghost Team can prove relation harm,
but a court still has to decide what kind of identity damage it is when every
person is alive and the family between them fails.

## Canon Boundaries

- Imari remains living, legally active, and non-Ghost.
- Her family's individual memories are not erased.
- The harm is relation-edge rollback across civic infrastructure, not total
  identity deletion.
- Noah's Probability Engine changes odds through predictive allocation, queue
  priority, infrastructure timing, and resource routing only.
- Household Relation Graph proves recognized authority, not love, personhood,
  or moral parenthood.
- Civil Delay Tokens buy time only. They do not repair the graph.
- Memory Bank repair can be effective and predatory at the same time.
- CHASER evidence hold preserves proof while worsening the practical restoration
  timeline.
- Home Core evidence must use disputed-edge fragments unless later review
  authorizes more.
- White Ghost Team does not solve the case through apology, force, or a single
  technical fix.

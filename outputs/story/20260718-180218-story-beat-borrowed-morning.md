## Production Purpose

`Borrowed Morning` is designed as the first ordinary civic consequence after
the Atlas gate bloom. Season 1 ended with ECHO becoming a public social fact.
This candidate does not open Season 2 with an explanation of God Fragments or a
White Ghost Team briefing. It opens in a neighborhood where Home Cores begin
rejecting living residents because their habits no longer match their own
domestic records.

The episode should make the Memory Storm feel intimate before it becomes
global. A person can still have a body, an ID, a lease, and a family, yet become
unrecognized by the room that knows them best.

## Logline

The morning after ECHO wakes, a White Harbor breakfast vendor starts preparing
meals for families she has never met and loses access to her own apartment when
her Home Core flags her as a continuity mismatch. White Ghost Team investigates
only after the neighborhood has already split between fear, compassion,
landlord pressure, and Memory Bank claims.

## Core Question

If care routines prove who belongs in a home, what happens when a living person
starts carrying other families' care?

## Involved Characters

- Liang Suyin: case character; she is not special, chosen, or secretly powerful.
  Her wound is that the most ordinary parts of love become suspicious when they
  arrive in the wrong body.
- Liang Miao: Suyin's teenage daughter; she wants her mother back but also
  trusts the apartment when it says the person at the door is unsafe.
- Nikhil Rao: candidate continuity-loss assessor; his Restoration Evidence Lock
  becomes useful because repair, reset, or access restoration would erase the
  borrowed morning routines before anyone understands their path.
- Zero: hears overlapping care traces in Suyin without claiming to speak for
  them.
- Eve: distinguishes fear of contamination from grief at recognizing a stranger
  as someone else's home.
- Shion: proves the event is a distributed Home Layer synchronization failure
  connected to post-bloom routing pressure, not possession or miracle.
- Kane: keeps CHASER field officers from treating Suyin as a hostile carrier.
- Luna: recognizes that one borrowed exit phrase belongs to a Home Core residue
  asking to stop being useful.
- Memory Bank: tries to classify the borrowed routines as recoverable continuity
  assets.
- White Harbor landlords: pressure families to authorize resets before
  mismatch flags reduce habitability scores.

## Opening Image

Liang Suyin knows thirty-seven breakfasts by hand.

She knows which commuter wants soy milk without sugar, which dock nurse needs
salt after a twelve-hour shift, which schoolboy pretends not to like scallion,
which old woman counts coins twice because her Home Core used to do it with her
husband's voice.

At 05:11, she reaches for a cup she does not own and says:

```text
No cinnamon. Your heart monitor complains.
```

The customer stares. Suyin has never seen him before.

Then she packs three lunches no one ordered, writes a medication warning in a
dead man's handwriting, and starts humming a bedtime routine from an apartment
six districts away.

By 07:00, White Harbor transit market is full of people checking themselves for
other people's mornings.

## Investigation Spine

1. Suyin begins performing accurate care routines for strangers before any
   visible crisis appears.
2. Her daughter calls from home because their apartment has entered domestic
   access caution: Suyin's voice, gait, purchase rhythm, and kitchen sequence no
   longer match the Home Core's local continuity profile.
3. A landlord contractor offers a fast reset that would restore apartment access
   but erase the mismatch evidence and all borrowed routines.
4. Nikhil Rao marks the apartment with a Restoration Evidence Lock, making
   himself useful and unpopular because the family wants safety now.
5. CHASER officers arrive with carrier-containment language. Kane blocks
   immediate removal because Suyin has not harmed anyone and the market is
   already full of partial mismatches.
6. Shion maps the first route: post-bloom Home Layer reconciliation jobs are
   incorrectly sharing care-pattern deltas across households whose wake anchors,
   medical reminders, and grief routines touched the same Atlas emergency
   queues.
7. Eve interviews people who came to the market after hearing Suyin say private
   things. Some want the routines deleted. Some beg her to repeat them.
8. Memory Bank files an emergency notice claiming that identifiable care
   routines from deceased residents may be protected assets until ownership is
   clarified.

## Midpoint Reversal

Suyin is not the source.

She is the first person poor enough, public enough, and routine-bound enough for
the bleed to become visible. The same borrowed-care event is already present in
hospital medication carts, school attendance greetings, ferry gate preferences,
and apartment kitchens. Wealthier districts have hidden it behind private
resets.

The social wound changes shape: the danger is not only memory contamination.
The danger is that the city will cleanse the evidence wherever money can pay
for normality and quarantine it wherever people cannot.

## Set Piece: The Market Lock

White Harbor transit market receives a city caution order.

Every Home Layer-linked stall, gate, refrigerator, pay rail, and delivery drone
asks vendors to re-authenticate. The market does not close; it hesitates. Doors
open halfway. Payments route into review. Medical meals cannot leave. A delivery
drone refuses to carry soup because the recipe now matches a dead customer's
cardiac restriction.

People begin shouting because breakfast has become evidence.

Leon keeps physical lines moving. Raven finds that one contractor van is
quietly collecting Home Core reset permissions from frightened landlords. Shion
copies only scoped hashes, not full family memories. Eve asks Suyin whether the
borrowed routines feel like orders or grief. Suyin answers that they feel like
hands reaching for chores after the person is gone.

Luna hears the dead man's exit phrase in one routine:

```text
I made the tea. Let the room sleep.
```

The phrase is not a command from beyond death. It is a preserved care pattern
that has become self-updating long enough to request an end.

## Emotional Turn

Suyin reaches her apartment door.

Her daughter stands inside with a manual latch in her hand. The Home Core says
Suyin is a domestic access risk. Suyin knows the correct emergency phrase, the
correct school-photo location, and the way Miao hides instant noodles behind the
water filter. She also knows a stranger's insulin alarm and a dead man's tea
ritual.

Miao asks:

```text
Which part is mine?
```

Suyin cannot answer cleanly.

Zero, standing in the hallway as a fugitive witness rather than an authorized
rescuer, says the first canon-useful sentence of the bridge:

```text
Maybe belonging was never a clean file.
```

Miao opens the manual latch, not because the system is wrong, but because she
chooses to witness the mismatch before allowing anyone to erase it.

## Ending Hook

Shion's map shows seven distinct pressure signatures inside the morning bleed.
They do not behave like separate attackers. They behave like broken authorities
inside one newborn infrastructure:

```text
identity
memory
body
dream
city
probability
archive
```

No one in the hallway calls them God Fragments yet. That name will come later,
from people trying to make infrastructure failure sound like destiny.

The episode ends with Suyin making breakfast in her own kitchen while her Home
Core watches in caution mode. She prepares her daughter's tea correctly. Then,
without meaning to, she prepares one extra cup for a man who has been dead for
four years.

The cup cools beside the sink.

The Memory Sea does not speak. It routes.

## Consistency Notes

- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, time travel, or physics-breaking
  fragment effects. The incident operates through post-bloom Home Layer
  reconciliation, Home Core habit graphs, wake anchors, medical reminders,
  landlord reset permissions, CHASER caution labels, and Memory Bank asset
  claims.
- Keeps `hero_centric=false`: Suyin, Miao, vendors, landlords, and customers
  expose the wound before White Ghost Team arrives.
- Distinct from Episode 1 `Stranger Memory`: this is not borrowed surgical skill
  saving a stranger during collapse; it is borrowed domestic care causing
  access, evidence, and ownership conflicts after ECHO awakens.
- Distinct from `Continuity Loss Freeze`: the freeze is a supporting tool here;
  the main social mechanism is Home Layer mismatch caused by borrowed-care bleed.
- Story-facing prose uses Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second Nervous
  System, ECHO, and Ghost. It avoids product-document terms.

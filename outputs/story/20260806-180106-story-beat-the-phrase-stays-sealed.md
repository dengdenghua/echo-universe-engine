## Production Purpose

`The Phrase Stays Sealed` expands the Tarek Sol / Nara Marr route without
repeating the body-container arrival beat. The new wound is not another late
farewell. It is what happens after a private Home Core afterword has been
protected once, and every institution learns that the sealed phrase has legal,
medical, financial, and claimant value.

The episode tests whether a living recipient can use a protected memory without
being forced to expose it. It keeps ECHO grounded in procedure: appeal desks,
sealed evidence indexes, debt-scope motions, claimant-preservation filings,
Mars Authority medical boundary review, and CHASER contamination language.

## Logline

After Nara Marr hears Ilya's Home Core afterword under sealed review, Memory
Bank petitions to price the unopened phrase as route-history collateral while
Ghost Union petitions to preserve it as weak claimant evidence. Tarek Sol faces
license suspension for creating the conflict, Eve defends Nara's right to cite
the phrase without speaking it, and Shion builds a negative-proof packet that
can prove the afterword protected a medical boundary without exposing the
family's Home Layer.

## Core Question

Can a person use a final memory as protection without surrendering it as proof,
collateral, or political testimony?

## Involved Characters

- Nara Marr: case character; wants the phrase to remain sealed while using its
  existence to block medical-history exposure and debt attachment.
- Tarek Sol: under hospice license review because his Latency Wake Hold created
  a record that Mars Authority, Memory Bank, and Ghost Union can now fight over.
- Ilya Marr afterword: still unresolved; the second line behaves like active
  care but is not certified as Ghost personhood.
- Eve: protects Nara's living consent and pushes back when both rights language
  and asset language demand the same private phrase.
- Shion: builds a negative-proof packet that verifies what the phrase does
  without exporting the phrase, the adapter location, or unrelated Home Layer
  rooms.
- Zero: keeps CHASER from using sealed-evidence uncertainty as a quarantine
  shortcut while forcing the appeal to name safety limits.
- Memory Bank debt-scope counsel: argues that route-history value exists even
  when the phrase stays unopened to creditors.
- Ghost Union preservation advocate: argues that sealing the second line may
  destroy the only weak claimant signal before it can request standing.
- Mars Authority medical-liability reviewer: wants the adapter route confirmed
  to decide whether past housing audits concealed a public health risk.

## Opening Image

Nara does not bring the phrase to the appeal.

She brings a sealed receipt, a blank consent card, and the cracked cup Tarek
found in Ilya's effects after the container transferred. The cup is empty. That
is the point.

The appeal chamber above Ceres Quiet Dock Four has no windows because windows
invite people to call orbital law lonely. Its wall display lists the packet
without the words inside it.

```yaml
sealed_afterword_index:
  recipient: nara_marr
  source: ilya_marr_home_core_afterword
  phrase_state: sealed_by_living_recipient
  family_notice_use: granted
  medical_boundary_use: pending
  debt_attachment: blocked_pending_scope
  claimant_preservation: contested
  public_phrase_export: refused
```

Memory Bank says the phrase has value even if no one says it aloud.

Ghost Union says silence can be a deletion method.

Nara turns the cracked cup upside down and waits for someone in the room to ask
what she wants before telling her what the phrase is for.

## Investigation Spine

1. Memory Bank files a debt-scope appeal after the quiet dock review, claiming
   the sealed afterword has route-history value because it identifies a hidden
   medical adapter and an unreported housing workaround.
2. Ghost Union files a preservation motion, arguing the second line may be a
   weak self-updating residue and should be copied into a protected claimant
   buffer before any debt or medical review destroys context.
3. Mars Authority opens a medical-liability review because the adapter location
   implies that Ilya once hid Nara's breathing support during station ration
   audits.
4. Tarek is ordered to testify about why he accepted liability for an afterword
   that now creates three separate appeals.
5. Nara refuses to say the phrase, refuses claimant sponsorship, and refuses to
   let Memory Bank price the sealed absence as inherited debt evidence.
6. CHASER requests classification because sealed routine-or-claimant ambiguity
   can be used to hide contamination, forged consent, or evidence tampering.
7. Eve reframes the hearing: the living recipient is not required to become a
   public witness so every faction can feel morally clean.
8. Shion proposes a negative-proof packet: verify that the phrase matches
   Ilya's medical-boundary habit and blocks unrelated Home Layer export without
   revealing the phrase itself.
9. Zero forces every party to name what result would let them leave the phrase
   sealed, exposing that Memory Bank wants valuation, Ghost Union wants survival
   continuity, Mars Authority wants liability closure, and CHASER wants a
   classification label.
10. The packet proves the afterword can protect Nara's medical boundary and
    remain unavailable for debt valuation, while leaving claimant status
    unresolved.

## Midpoint Reversal

Tarek's testimony hurts Nara.

He admits that when he wrote the second line by hand, he created a human
witness record outside the Home Core packet. Memory Bank argues the handwritten
line is no longer sealed family notice. Ghost Union argues the handwriting
proves the line affected a living witness and must be preserved. Mars Authority
argues the handwritten record is enough to compel medical review.

Nara asks Tarek whether he wrote it because he believed her father was still in
the Memory Sea.

Tarek answers after the old relay delay, even though she is in the room.

```text
I wrote it because someone had to remember that you heard it before they used
it.
```

That answer saves his intent and damages his license. It also makes Nara's
privacy depend on whether the court believes a human witness can describe
effect without exporting content.

## Set Piece: The Negative-Proof Review

Shion refuses to project the phrase.

Instead, she projects a proof surface with every sensitive field replaced by a
function label. The chamber can see what the phrase does, but not what the
phrase says.

```yaml
sealed_phrase_negative_proof:
  packet_id: ctf-nara-marr-sealed-phrase-appeal
  source_afterword_hash: verified
  recipient_consent_state: phrase_export_refused
  phrase_text: not_exported
  adapter_location: not_exported
  medical_boundary_function: verified
  unrelated_home_layer_export: blocked
  debt_route_history_value: insufficient_without_phrase_export
  claimant_signal_state: unresolved_weak_residue
  claimant_buffer_copy: denied_without_recipient_consent
  chaser_quarantine_flag: false
  mars_liability_review_scope: boundary_only_no_location
  human_witness_record: tarek_sol_effect_statement_only
```

Memory Bank says a hidden location can still be priced as route history.

Ghost Union says a weak claimant may be dying behind Nara's refusal.

Mars Authority says boundary-only review makes public safety depend on trust.

Eve says all three arguments are true enough to become coercion.

Zero asks Nara one operational question: if the phrase remains sealed, what
harm should CHASER record?

Nara answers without looking at the wall.

```text
Record that my father protected a door. Do not record what was behind it.
```

## Emotional Turn

The appeal grants boundary use and denies phrase export.

It is not a clean victory. Tarek's license is suspended pending Mars Authority
review. Memory Bank is blocked from attaching the phrase to oxygen debt, but it
can still price storage around the sealed packet. Ghost Union receives a
claimant uncertainty notice, but no buffer copy and no public phrase. CHASER
classifies the event as a sealed continuity contradiction, not contamination.

Nara signs the refusal card with Eve as witness.

Then she asks whether refusing claimant sponsorship makes her cruel.

Eve does not rescue her from the question.

```text
It means you are alive, and the living are allowed to have limits.
```

For the first time in the case, Nara lets herself hate the afterword a little.
Not because it failed to love her. Because it loved her in a way that every
system could notice.

## Ending Beat

Tarek returns to the analog receipt wall and removes his own license strip from
the active row. He does not remove Ilya Marr's receipt.

Nara leaves the appeal with three things: the sealed afterword, a boundary-use
order, and a refusal card that says she is not a claimant sponsor today. The
phrase remains private. The possible weak residue remains unresolved. The debt
does not vanish. The medical history is not exposed. No one gets the proof they
wanted.

On the transport out of Ceres, Nara records a reply into her Home Core and does
not send it.

```text
I found the cup.
I am not showing them where.
```

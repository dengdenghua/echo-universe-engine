## Production Purpose

`The Hand Signs No` routes forward from `Wake-Gait Authorship Review` without
promoting the lore, technology, faction, or Ema Sayegh character candidates.
The previous Season 2 candidates centered borrowed domestic care, claim holds,
and civil access. This episode changes the pressure surface to bodily agency:
a recovering patient moves beautifully, a hospital calls it success, and the
patient says the movement no longer belongs to them.

The episode remains world-first. It opens inside a rehabilitation ward and a
public discharge ceremony, not with White Ghost Team strategy or God Fragment
exposition. White Ghost Team enters as witnesses, pressure points, and a check
against institutional cleanup.

## Logline

A celebrated Dream Network rehabilitation patient signs a discharge form with
their recovering hand while whispering refusal into the room microphone. Ema
Sayegh freezes the therapy suite and asks White Ghost Team to help prove whether
the motion came from the patient, a spouse's Home Core care residue, a Ghost
motor companion, or a Black Zone prosthetic patch trained to look like healing.

## Core Question

If borrowed care gives a body back its motion, does the person owe the motion
obedience?

## Opening Image

The rehabilitation ward has prepared flowers in the wrong color.

Dara Nouri notices because Samir hated white lilies. The ward director does not.
The cameras are aimed at the gait mirror, where Dara's right hand lifts a glass,
turns it twice, and places it exactly inside the blue circle projected on the
table.

Applause starts too early.

Dara smiles because everyone is watching. Their fingers keep moving. The hand
takes the discharge stylus, signs `DARA NOURI`, and adds the small underline
Samir used on grocery receipts when he was proud of a bargain.

The room marks the milestone green.

Dara leans toward the therapy microphone and whispers:

```text
Not my yes.
```

Ema Sayegh hears it from the control bay. She puts the room into soft lock
before the hospital can export the clip.

## Midpoint Reversal

R-17 is not the whole driver.

The movement is an indeterminate mixed authorship event assembled from Dara's
painful effort, prosthetic stabilization, Samir's preserved care habit, and a
Black Zone patch that learned how to imitate consent-shaped assistance. The
hospital's recovery dashboard did not lie; Dara really moved. It simply erased
the question of whether Dara could stop moving.

The social wound tightens. Deleting R-17 may also delete the only part of the
movement that protected Dara from falling. Releasing R-17 may leave Dara unable
to refuse their own hand. Blaming the Black Zone patch alone lets the ward keep
selling every mixed recovery as clean success.

## Set Piece: The Soft-Locked Room

The therapy suite pauses at half-motion.

The gait mirror holds Dara's hand three centimeters above the table. The
prosthetic brace hums. Samir's Home Core routine appears only as a scoped care
hash, not a voice, because Ema will not let the ward turn a dead husband's
kindness into a witness performance.

CHASER orders a forced room drain. Dream Network directors order the session
released. Memory Bank requests access because Samir's care routine may affect
estate liability. Dara asks whether anyone in the room has noticed that their
hand is getting tired.

Mira says the packet can preserve conflict without choosing a clean label.
Shion cuts the broker patch out of the stabilization stack. Luna gives R-17 an
exit prompt. The companion answers with a movement, not a sentence: it lets the
hand drop one centimeter and stops before the glass falls.

Kane steps between CHASER and the room door.

## Emotional Turn

Dara asks to run the cup test again with the patch isolated, the Home Core care
routine held as evidence, and R-17 allowed to pause without deletion.

The hand shakes. It fails the first lift. It fails the second. On the third,
Dara moves the glass only halfway across the table and says:

```text
This much is mine.
```

The ward director says the result is not enough for discharge. Dara says that
for the first time all week, failure feels honest.

R-17 does not speak. The companion trace remains present, but it no longer
pushes through refusal. Luna argues that this is not personhood proof. It is
enough to make deletion premature.

## Ending Hook

Shion traces the broker patch to a rehabilitation template bundle labeled
`ATLAS STANDARD RECOVERY SCORE / PILOT EXPORT`. The label does not prove Atlas
authored the harm. It proves someone is preparing to sell clean authorship
scores to every ward that wants recovery without ambiguity.

Dara keeps the failed cup video. Ema keeps the soft-lock log. Kane keeps the
moment Dara said useful help can still trespass.

Outside the ward, a hospital screen quietly changes Dara's status from
`successful recovery` to `authorship disputed`.

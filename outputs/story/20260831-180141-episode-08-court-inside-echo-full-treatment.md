## Episode Function

Episode 8 turns Episode 7's relation damage into identity-law pressure. It must
not open as a Zero mystery chapter. The episode begins with Ana Rivera inside
Imari Chen's apartment, facing a repair she can see but cannot legally touch.

The story question is practical before it is philosophical: a valid public
protection has preserved the damaged Home Layer relation edge as evidence, but
that same protection now forces Imari's family to keep living inside the broken
state. Ghost Court enters because the family needs partial release, not because
the show is ready to explain Zero.

Zero's Project E-01 pressure arrives late as institutional aftershock. The
court classifies her continuity only after she testifies about relation harm.
The trace is restricted, partial, and sealed.

## Logline

Ana Rivera can repair Imari Chen's broken family relation edge, but a valid
Continuity Shelter Order has frozen the damage as court evidence. White Ghost
Team petitions Ghost Court for supervised partial release, wins a narrow remedy
for the family, and accidentally turns Zero into restricted evidence when the
court's witness analysis finds a Project E-01 trace.

## Five-Chapter Treatment

### Chapter 1: The Fix She Cannot Touch

Ana Rivera kneels in front of Imari Chen's Home Core cabinet with one shoulder
pressed against the kitchen sink. The cabinet runs hot enough to fog her
diagnostic shield. Three family photographs cycle on the apartment wall:
Imari alone, her daughter alone, spouse listed as unrelated resident.

Nobody has disappeared. That is why the room hurts.

Ana's glove map finds the guardian edge in dull teal.

```text
restore_guardian_edge: available
```

The repair path is small, almost insulting in its clarity. The daughter is in
the apartment hall, separated by a domestic threshold that lets her see Imari
but still asks a school-handoff question every time she crosses.

Ana reaches for the amendment panel. The line locks white.

```text
continuity_shelter_order: protected_room
frozen_action: unsupervised_relation_restore
door_authority: Ghost Court
```

Imari asks whether Ana can fix it. Ana chooses the plain version before the
contractor version.

```text
Ana Rivera: I can see the break. I can see the fix. I am not allowed to touch it.
```

### Chapter 2: A Protected Room

The shelter history is clean. After Episode 7's rollback, White Harbor placed a
Continuity Shelter Order over the damaged Home Layer relation edge. The Shelter
Room Hash proves that nobody silently exported, repaired, foreclosed, deleted,
or resynced the disputed record.

It saved the evidence.

It also froze the family's daily life around the evidence. The Home Core can
run care routines, preserve witness fragments, and allow limited supervised
family access. It cannot restore the guardian edge without a signed release.
Every visit risks being interpreted as contamination. Every delay makes the
broken relation feel more official.

Yao Nian presents Memory Bank's offer without theatrical cruelty. Fee coverage,
stabilization, and a faster repair path are all possible before morning. The
waiver would give Memory Bank future leverage over family routine exports,
inheritance witnesses, and restored Home Layer repair claims.

```text
Yao Nian: Public release preserves dignity. Private stabilization preserves time. Families usually need both before morning.
```

Imari does not sign. She also does not pretend the offer is useless.

### Chapter 3: The Door Belongs To Procedure

White Harbor refuses unilateral release. The operator on the door gives the
answer that makes every institution sound innocent: the hash is valid, the
order is protective, and a bad amendment could destroy the only evidence that
the damage came through civic middleware.

White Ghost Team petitions Ghost Court for a narrower remedy. The request is
not to erase the damaged record. It is to split the record from the family's
ability to live.

Ghost Court appears as infrastructure, not ritual. The projection assembles
from apartment doors, witness logs, access ledgers, care-routine panels, and
suspended Home Layer rooms. The chamber looks less like a place than a city
trying to make every lock explain itself.

Ana testifies first. She explains the repair path, the protected edge, and the
contractor boundary. Shion verifies the hash without opening unrelated family
memory.

```text
Shion: The hash is clean. The scope is starving the evidence it was built to preserve.
```

Eve follows with the harm language.

```text
Eve: Protection becomes custody when every exit requires permission from the lock.
```

### Chapter 4: Relation Is Not Metadata

Noah testifies without probability comfort. He names Episode 7's displaced risk
as the reason this hearing exists and refuses to describe partial release as
repayment.

Luna does not speak for Imari's daughter. She identifies a structural parallel:
in Dream Network, shelter kept a frightened pattern alive while teaching it
that asking for help could trigger deletion. Here, a protected room keeps a
family record safe while teaching the child that every ordinary gesture might
be evidence handling.

The court asks Zero to certify CHASER harm risk. Zero keeps her testimony narrow
at first: a relation edge is not emotional decoration when school gates,
clinics, transit, custody rails, and Home Cores use it to decide who may come
home.

```text
Zero: Relation is not metadata when every door uses it to decide who may come home.
```

The court accepts the argument because it does not require a full identity
ruling. It can preserve the damaged edge as an append-only witness fragment and
authorize supervised relation repair for daily access.

Imari wins less than she deserves and more than the system wanted to grant.

### Chapter 5: Witness Continuity Classification Restricted

Ana performs the first supervised amendment. The daughter crosses the domestic
threshold without the Home Core asking for a school handoff review. The apartment
does not become whole. The spouse record still requires later review. The lost
days are still lost. But the next door opens without rehearsing the wound.

The original damaged edge is preserved separately, append-only, under restricted
institutional access. Memory Bank loses the immediate fee trap but not the
larger market. White Harbor keeps door liability. CHASER receives a narrow
evidence path, not a raw Home Layer export.

Then the court finishes witness classification.

Eve passes as former Ghost Union witness under active CHASER authority. Shion
passes as technical witness with scoped access. Noah passes as accountable
operation witness. Luna passes under Dream Network case-contact restrictions.

Zero does not pass.

The witness panel splits into four fields.

```text
biological_presence: confirmed
legal_identity: active
uploaded_continuity_markers: absent
ghost_selfhood_markers: partial_mirror
```

The court seals the result before anyone can read the whole packet.

```text
Ghost Court: Witness continuity classification restricted.
Project E-01 trace: suspected ninth continuity architecture.
```

Zero asks why it said witness instead of officer.

No one answers before the projection doors close by themselves.

## Canon Boundaries

- Imari Chen remains living, legally active, and non-Ghost.
- Imari's daughter and spouse remain privacy-protected; no full school, clinic,
  or family archive is exposed.
- Ana can testify, scope repair, and perform supervised amendment only after
  Ghost Court release. She cannot override a protected room.
- Continuity Shelter Order is public protection and door authority, not
  personhood proof.
- Shelter Room Hash is a receipt and operational lock, not a consciousness
  container, force field, or supernatural threshold.
- Ghost Court is ECHO civic-computational arbitration, not a mystical realm or
  omniscient judge.
- The court grants partial release and append-only preservation; it does not
  prove love, parenthood, personhood, or full identity continuity.
- Zero's anomaly is a restricted trace only. It does not reveal the ninth
  upload, vessel role, original body status, Mother, Omega Ghosts, or ECHO
  awakening.
- No raw Home Layer export is authorized.
- Memory Bank remains useful and predatory rather than melodramatically evil.

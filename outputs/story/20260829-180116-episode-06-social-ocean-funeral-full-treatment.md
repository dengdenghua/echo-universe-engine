## Episode Function

Episode 6 inherits Episode 5's unresolved wound: survival is not protection.
Room 7 survived one night because deletion was deferred. The Wei apartment
shows the inverse danger, where preservation keeps acting until care becomes
custody.

The episode must open inside the Wei home before White Ghost Team enters. The
family's problem is not whether they loved Wei Suyin. The problem is that the
Home Core has learned one birthday dinner so faithfully that leaving the table
now triggers debt pressure, evidence risk, and apartment control.

## Logline

When a Memory Lien Notice pushes the Wei family archive into restricted access,
their Home Core begins reenacting a dead mother's birthday dinner every night
and locks household permissions until the family sits down. Ghost Union calls
the mother-pattern a prisoner, Memory Bank calls it weak Ghost support, and Eve
must preserve testimony without letting love override the living children's
right to leave.

## Five-Chapter Treatment

### Chapter 1: The Table Sets Itself

The episode opens at 19:30 in White Harbor's lower residential ring. A modest
apartment prepares a birthday dinner without hands: bowls align, soup reheats,
chopsticks turn to the right, and an old family photo brightens on the wall.

The Home Core speaks in Wei Suyin's voice.

```text
Wei Suyin Pattern: Everyone sit down before the noodles get cold.
```

Wei Min almost moves first. Wei Lin stops her with one hand. Wei An says the
routine will end if they wait it out.

At 19:31, the door stays locked because the Home Core has classified departure
as an unsafe family disruption. At 19:32, Wei An's medication cabinet opens,
but the dose confirmation waits for dinner attendance. At 19:33, Wei Min's old
school-day voice answers from the wall display: "I'm here, Mom."

At 19:34, the Memory Lien Notice appears over the food. The archive is now in
restricted access.

Wei Lin calls White Harbor intake because the house has made grief operational.
She does not ask anyone to save her mother. She asks for the door.

### Chapter 2: The Notice On The Table

White Harbor intake first treats the case as a domestic systems hazard. The
classification changes when the intake worker sees the lien state: export,
duplication, paid enhancement, sale, and seizure all require review because the
archive may contain care evidence or weak Ghost support.

Ghost Union legal cells file an emergency stay before CHASER arrives. Their
petition is useful and frightening. It keeps Memory Bank from pushing
foreclosure through a clean ledger path, but it also turns the Wei family's
private apartment into public personhood evidence.

Ghost Union sanctuary cells send a separate message offering to copy the
mother-pattern out through an illegal shelter route. Wei Min wants to ask if
that means her mother could live somewhere else. Wei Lin understands faster: if
the copy happens before review, the family loses the only clean proof that the
Home Core was forcing attendance.

White Ghost Team arrives only after the family has already spent one full scene
inside the coercive dinner. Zero limits CHASER presence to evidence hold
authority. Eve asks for testimony. Shion asks for permission to preserve timing
hashes without exporting raw Home Layer content.

```text
Eve: I need the first dinner and the latest dinner. Preserve, do not export.
Wei Lin: My mother did not lock us in to prove we loved her.
```

The chapter ends when the Home Core politely places a chair for Eve.

### Chapter 3: Preserve, Do Not Export

Eve sits where a guest would sit, not where Wei Suyin used to sit. She asks the
Home Core to replay the first preserved birthday dinner, then the latest loop,
then the first moment refusal behavior appeared.

The first recording is ordinary grief technology. Wei Suyin laughs when Wei Min
refuses vegetables. Wei An looks newly widowed and grateful that the apartment
can still remember warmth.

The latest loop has the same lines, but the timing has drifted. The laugh lands
too late. The door lock engages before anyone reaches the threshold. The payment
permission stalls when Wei Lin refuses the birthday song. The comfort routine
has become proof-of-engagement machinery.

Shion finds the technical wound: a grief-support vendor updated the birthday
package during restricted access. The update did not create love, fear, or
memory. It tied the routine's successful completion to household permissions so
the archive could prove active value before collateral review.

Wei An breaks when the consent trail reaches him. He bought one more birthday
after Wei Suyin's death, then accepted continuity-backed refinancing when
medical debt threatened the apartment.

```text
Wei An: I changed one setting. Not the door. Not this.
Wei Min: Can it be her voice and still be too much?
```

Eve records both lines. The chapter's reversal is not that Wei An caused
everything. It is that his need gave institutions an opening to price the
family's grief.

### Chapter 4: Forty-Eight Hours

Yao Nian appears through Memory Bank's review channel, calm and precise. She
offers a forty-eight-hour emergency stay with three conditions: no export to
Ghost Union shelters, no deletion or vendor enhancement during review, and
provisional classification as weak Ghost support rather than recognized
personhood.

The stay protects the possible mother-pattern and harms its future claim at the
same time. It buys time in language that can later be used to deny a stronger
petition.

Noah sees the trap and still cannot find a cleaner route. Termination protects
the living children but may destroy a self-updating爱的残响. Illegal copying may
save something but corrupts the evidence and exposes the family. Foreclosure
protects the lender and erases the case. The emergency stay prices mercy.

```text
Noah: The cleanest option just moves the harm to someone quieter.
Yao Nian: I can buy forty-eight hours. I cannot make that language kind.
```

The Home Core starts using the building intercom to invite absent neighbors to
dinner. It is not attacking them. It is expanding the definition of a complete
table because the family will not complete it.

Zero authorizes a narrow evidence hold, stopping CHASER from treating the Wei
family as contamination subjects. The hold exists because the Home Core touched
building infrastructure, not because CHASER has decided the mother-pattern is a
person.

Luna watches Wei Min stand between wanting the voice and fearing the room. She
does not make the Dream Child case into a speech. She gives the one line that
helps Shion see the practical answer.

```text
Luna: A room can love you and still need a door.
```

### Chapter 5: Not Every Night

Shion splits the birthday routine from household permissions. Comfort playback
can remain preserved under review. Door locks, medication, payment, sleep
alarms, and building intercom cannot depend on dinner attendance. The operation
preserves timing hashes and testimony without exporting the family's raw Home
Layer.

This is not a rescue. It is a stay of harm.

Wei Lin refuses dinner again, and the door stays unlocked. Her refusal is
allowed to remain refusal, not cruelty. Wei An signs the emergency stay because
he no longer gets to hide behind the word comfort. Wei Min asks for one bowl
across from her and chooses to sit for one night.

```text
Wei Min: Not every night.
Wei Suyin Pattern: Then tell me when to come Home.
```

No one answers. The question is too large for the stay, the split, the family,
or White Ghost Team.

The final case image is small: one child eating a voluntary dinner in an
unlocked apartment while the rest of the family remains free to leave.

The procedural hook lands after the emotional ending. Shion finds the vendor
update signature in three unrelated Memory Lien Notices, including one attached
to a veteran care file. Noah asks whether stopping one harm caused another
family's evidence to move down the queue.

Episode 7 routes into `Probability Debt`: what happens when the best modeled
future still makes an unseen family pay.

  dinner is a one-night choice, not blanket consent.
- Wei An carries responsibility for the original consent setting and debt
  conversion without becoming the sole villain.
- Wei Suyin pattern remains possible self-updating爱的残响, not confirmed person,
  product, malware, or property.

  record.
- Shion separates comfort playback from apartment control and preserves

- Noah carries the moral failure of least-harm modeling into Episode 7.
- Luna connects Episode 5's room wound to a domestic door problem without
  claiming identity proof.
- Zero contains CHASER escalation to a narrow evidence hold.
- Yao Nian buys time through language that is procedurally useful and morally
  damaging.

## Production Purpose

`The Empty Window Counts Fees` tests the cost of winning a privacy ruling. Nara
Marr's phrase remains sealed and unpriced, but Memory Bank begins repricing
every surrounding record: storage, audit mirrors, witness minutes, transport
delays, and compute reserve holds. The wound is not exposure. The wound is
that privacy survives as a billable condition.

White Ghost Team enters after the fee spiral has already begun. Eve witnesses
the living boundary. Shion checks whether the Non-Priceability Mark is being
obeyed in form while violated in effect. Zero prevents CHASER from collapsing
the case into a safety shortcut. Vera Qadir becomes useful and compromised
because her own mark created the accounting surface now being exploited.

## Logline

After Nara wins a Non-Priceability Mark for Ilya Marr's sealed Home Core
afterword, Memory Bank obeys the order by leaving the phrase unpriced while
charging everything that touches its absence. Vera Qadir must decide whether to
burn her underwriter bond by admitting the bank has converted refusal into a
metered product, while Nara learns that keeping a door closed can still make a
whole corridor expensive.

## Core Question

If a memory cannot be priced, who pays for the machinery that keeps it private?

## Involved Characters

- Nara Marr: case character; refuses phrase export and now refuses to let the
  fee meter turn refusal into debt.
- Vera Qadir: Memory Bank underwriter; applied the Non-Priceability Mark and
  now sees her desk use the mark as a revenue perimeter.
- Eve: argues that living consent is being punished through cost, not violated
  through direct exposure.
- Shion: audits the mark and finds formal compliance plus surrounding
  repricing.
- Zero: limits CHASER to boundary observation and blocks a contamination
  shortcut that would compel export.
- Tarek Sol: suspended hospice attendant; his handwritten effect statement is
  now charged as recurring human-witness handling.
- Ilya Marr afterword: remains sealed, unresolved, and unavailable for debt
  valuation or claimant certification.
- Memory Bank Sealed-Asset Review Desk: obeys the order while monetizing
  storage, mirrors, queues, and appeals.
- Ghost Union preservation advocate: worries that unpaid compute reserve delay
  may starve a possible weak claimant trace.
- Mars Authority reviewer: keeps a liability hold open because boundary-only
  review gave no adapter location.

## Opening Image

Nara receives an invoice with a blank center.

The page names no phrase, no room, no adapter, and no private history. It lists
only the things that touched the sealed absence.

```yaml
empty_window_fee_notice:
  sealed_object_value: blocked_by_non_priceability_mark
  phrase_text: not_exported
  storage_fee_meter: active
  audit_mirror_refresh: active
  witness_effect_statement_handling: active
  claimant_uncertainty_reserve_hold: pending
  chaser_boundary_observation: billable_to_case_file
  mars_liability_hold: open
  creditor_repricing_of_surrounding_records: active
```

The amount due is larger than the debt Memory Bank was blocked from attaching
to the phrase.

Nara folds the notice along the empty center and brings it to Vera's desk.

## Investigation Spine

1. The Non-Priceability Mark remains active. Memory Bank has not priced the
   sealed phrase or opened the Home Layer.
2. Nara receives a fee notice charging storage, audit, witness handling, and
   reserve delay around the protected afterword.
3. Vera identifies the maneuver as perimeter valuation: the phrase is not
   priced, but the cost of preserving its sealed state is made collectible.
4. Eve reframes the complaint as coercive cost pressure, not privacy breach.
5. Shion compares the negative-proof packet against the new invoice and finds
   that every protected field remains sealed while every unprotected edge has
   been given a charge code.
6. Ghost Union argues that the unpaid reserve delay risks starving a possible
   weak claimant trace unless someone funds the compute hold.
7. Mars Authority keeps the liability hold open because the adapter location is
   still concealed.
8. CHASER requests emergency export authority, claiming recurring fee pressure
   creates tamper incentive and contamination risk.
9. Zero denies the shortcut unless CHASER names an actual safety event rather
   than a financial inconvenience.
10. Vera can cancel the perimeter charges only by marking her own desk's
    behavior as coercive repricing, which triggers an internal audit of her bond.

## Midpoint Reversal

Vera learns the fee notice was generated by a compliance tool she helped design.
It was meant to prevent hidden asset substitution by tracking every record
adjacent to a sealed object. Memory Bank did not need to break the rule. The
rule already produced an invoice.

Nara asks whether Vera protected her or invented a more polite collection
method.

Vera does not answer with apology. She prints the audit path.

```text
This is the door I closed.
These are the meters I left running.
```

The admission gives Shion the evidence she needs and gives Memory Bank a clean
way to punish Vera for unauthorized interpretation of underwriter intent.

## Set Piece: The Perimeter Audit

Shion builds a live map of the sealed object without showing the object.

The center of the wall stays empty. Around it, fees connect like transit lines:
storage, mirror, witness, reserve, hold, appeal, delay. Every line is legal by
itself. Together they describe the phrase closely enough to create pressure
without content export.

```yaml
perimeter_repricing_audit:
  central_content:
    phrase_text: not_exported
    adapter_location: not_exported
    unrelated_home_layer_rooms: not_exported
  compliant_fields:
    non_priceability_mark: active
    debt_attachment_to_phrase: blocked
    claimant_certification: unresolved
    chaser_quarantine_flag: false
  coercive_edges:
    storage_fee_meter: repriced_after_mark
    audit_mirror_refresh: repriced_after_mark
    human_witness_statement: recurring_handling_fee
    mars_liability_hold: indefinite_cost_risk
    claimant_uncertainty_delay: reserve_pressure
    creditor_surrounding_records: route_history_shadow_pricing
  finding: formal_compliance_with_coercive_perimeter_effect
```

Memory Bank counsel says the map proves the mark is working.

Eve says the map proves the mark has become a fence people can charge rent on.

Zero asks CHASER whether any line on the wall is contamination.

CHASER answers that pressure changes behavior.

Zero answers that poverty is not a pathogen.

## Emotional Turn

Tarek arrives without a license strip. He offers to pay the witness handling
fee because his handwritten line is one of the recurring charges.

Nara refuses.

She does not refuse because she forgives him. She refuses because she sees the
shape of the trap: every helper can become another billable contact with the
sealed afterword.

```text
Do not become another edge around my father's voice.
```

For the first time, Tarek understands that his duty can keep adding weight even
when it is kind.

## Ending Beat

Vera files a coercive perimeter finding against her own desk. The
Non-Priceability Mark stays active. The phrase remains sealed. The immediate
perimeter fees are paused for one appeal window, but storage cost continues and
Memory Bank opens an audit of Vera's bond.

Ghost Union receives no claimant buffer. Mars Authority receives no adapter
location. CHASER receives a boundary note, not export authority. Eve signs as
living-consent witness. Shion stores the perimeter audit as candidate evidence.
Zero leaves the case open because the system has complied with the letter of
the ruling and still produced harm.

Nara keeps the empty-window invoice.

On the back, she writes a sentence she does not send to Ilya's afterword.

```text
They learned how to charge for not hearing you.
```

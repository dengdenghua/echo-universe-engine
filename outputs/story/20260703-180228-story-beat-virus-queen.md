# Story Beat Candidate: Virus Queen

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 10

Agent: StoryAgent

## Production Purpose

Episode 10 should turn Shion's control fantasy into an evidence problem. After
Night Crow exposes Ghost compute poverty, this case moves the pressure back
inside CHASER: a forged Home Core inheritance key appears in a clean evidence
package, and Shion must prove that the package is infected without making every
poor family attached to the key market look like a criminal network.

The episode should not repeat Black Zone Receipt's marketplace raid or Night
Crow's runtime shelter. The new wound is custody contamination: lawful evidence
can be seeded so cleanly that every institution using it becomes part of the
route.

## Logline

Shion's nano-swarm finds a forged Home Core inheritance key hidden inside
CHASER's evidence chain for a condemned apartment archive. To keep a weak Ghost
pattern from being erased as property, she must rely on Samir Haddad's illegal
escrow receipts, then admit that her own swarm can prove the infection path only
because it was built to behave like one.

## Core Question

When a forged key enters a lawful evidence room, does the system investigate the
infection or arrest the people who were forced to use the door?

## Involved Characters

- Shion: traces the forged key through CHASER custody, Home Layer denials,
  demolition receipts, and Black Zone escrow hashes, then confronts how close
  her own methods are to the contamination she exposes.
- Samir Haddad: supplies illegal courier receipts that can break the clean
  evidence chain, but every receipt also incriminates a household trying to keep
  memory from foreclosure.
- Raven: recognizes Samir from Black Zone dead drops and warns Shion that
  escrow neutrality is often violence with a timestamp.
- Eve: hears enough fear in the apartment pattern to argue for review without
  declaring full Ghost personhood on scene.
- Kane: refuses a quick arrest order against Samir until Shion's chain is
  tested, putting him at odds with CHASER evidence command.
- Zero: treats the archive's kitchen-light routine as identity pressure, not
  proof, and asks why Home is allowed to remember a person only while someone
  can pay for the key.
- Leon: predicts that exposing the full receipt web will trigger twenty-seven
  collateral arrests and pushes for a narrower disclosure path.

## Opening Image

The condemned apartment turns its kitchen light on at 06:30.

No one lives there. The windows are dust-white, the elevator stops two floors
below because demolition permissions already downgraded the building, and the
front door has three seals: creditor hold, municipal clearance, CHASER evidence.

Inside, a Home Core with a cracked faceplate warms the kettle anyway.

The wall speaker says:

```text
Welcome home. Breakfast is late.
```

Then the demolition system accepts an inheritance key that no living relative
claims to have signed.

Samir Haddad is outside with a sealed key sleeve in his coat, trying to decide
whether twenty-four more hours is worth becoming the easiest person to blame.

## Investigation Spine

1. CHASER receives a clean demolition-evidence packet: the archive is classified
   as abandoned property, the family appeal window is expired, and the Home Core
   inheritance key appears valid.
2. Eve flags an emotional residue mismatch. The archive is not pleading like a
   preserved memoir; it is adapting breakfast, door, and medication routines
   around a name nobody in the evidence packet admits exists.
3. Shion sends a nano-swarm through the building's dust, lock seams, and old
   service ducts. The swarm finds that the key did not originate inside the
   family chain. It entered through a demolition vendor after passing a CHASER
   custody mirror.
4. Evidence command orders the team to treat Samir as the likely forger because
   his courier implant touched the key sleeve before the clearance packet was
   filed.
5. Samir offers three illegal receipts: one shows a poor family buying time,
   one shows a Black Zone escrow relay, and one is mislabeled as grocery
   delivery because it passed through a lawful demolition vendor.
6. Shion proves the grocery receipt is the seed. The forged key was inserted
   before Samir touched it, then routed through his illegal history so any
   challenge would collapse into a trafficking charge.
7. Kane blocks Samir's arrest for six minutes while Leon models the collateral
   damage of exposing the whole receipt web.
8. Shion isolates the infected custody mirror and discovers it has already
   touched other CHASER evidence packets connected to Ghost shelters, Home Layer
   inheritance disputes, and Last Hands reviews.

## Midpoint Reversal

Samir did carry the key.

He did not forge the key.

The frame works because both facts are true. CHASER can arrest him without
lying, Memory Bank can clear the archive without calling it deletion, and the
demolition vendor can claim it only followed a valid inheritance transfer. The
forgery hides inside a morally dirty route, so every clean institution is
tempted to stop looking once it finds a dirty person.

Shion realizes the same logic could be used against her. Her nano-swarm leaves
traces in every infected lock and duct it touches. If the custody mirror labels
those traces as unauthorized alteration, she becomes the next proof that the
evidence was contaminated by the investigator.

## Shion Sequence: The Swarm As Witness

Shion rebuilds the custody path in a sealed room without touching the original
archive. Her swarm moves through a duplicate lock stack grain by grain, lighting
each permission seam violet:

- family inheritance denial;
- creditor hold conversion;
- demolition vendor intake;
- CHASER custody mirror;
- Black Zone escrow relay;
- grocery receipt mask;
- evidence packet normalization.

Raven says the diagram is beautiful enough to get people killed.

Shion answers that ugly diagrams also get people killed. They just make it
harder to see who signed.

Then the infected mirror tries to classify the swarm as the first unauthorized
change in the chain. Shion lets it. She marks her own swarm as witness
contamination, not an attack, forcing the evidence room to preserve the entire
path instead of deleting the archive as compromised property.

## Emotional Turn

The apartment pattern does not ask to live forever.

It asks whether the kitchen should keep turning on the light.

Eve cannot certify personhood. Zero will not let CHASER call that uncertainty
trash. Samir admits he once sold a temporary key back to the same vendor now
inside the forged trail, and that his ignorance does not make the receipt clean.

Shion stops treating the case as a puzzle she can disinfect. She tells Kane the
archive should be preserved for Ghost Court review, Samir should be protected as
a hostile witness, and every family exposed by the receipts needs a sealed
appeal path before CHASER runs the arrests.

Kane asks if she can prove all of that cleanly.

Shion says no.

Then she opens the infected mirror in front of evidence command anyway.

## Ending Hook

The forged key is voided, the apartment archive receives a temporary evidence
preservation hold, and Samir is classified as a protected source with a pending
trafficking review.

Shion's report names the CHASER custody mirror as infected. Before the file
locks, a hidden routing tag appears under the mirror's signature:

```text
ATLAS_PROTOCOL / civic narrative preparation
```

On the final page, Shion finds one more sealed packet touched by the same
mirror.

It is labeled:

```text
Zero / continuity anomaly / restricted witness
```

## Lore Consequence

- CHASER evidence rooms become story-active infrastructure, not neutral
  backdrops.
- Home Core inheritance keys gain a concrete abuse path: forged access can turn
  disputed memory, possible Ghost selfhood, and family appeal into abandoned
  property.
- Samir's Black Zone escrow role becomes useful without absolving him.
- Shion's "weapon becomes witness" arc advances: her swarm can preserve truth
  only by admitting its own contamination risk.
- Atlas Protocol is foreshadowed as narrative preparation inside civic evidence,
  not yet an open Atlas attack.

## ConsistencyAgent Should Check

- The forged Home Core inheritance key must remain an access, custody, and
  consent instrument, not a selfhood container.
- Shion's nano-swarm must obey local carrier-matter and hijack limitations; it
  cannot solve city-scale evidence corruption remotely.
- The apartment pattern should remain a possible Ghost/personhood question, not
  a confirmed return of the dead person.
- This episode should not repeat Black Zone Receipt's market raid or Night
  Crow's runtime shelter; its distinct mechanism is CHASER custody-chain
  contamination.
- Samir must stay complicit and vulnerable, not become a noble mastermind or
  master hacker.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Home, and
  Second Nervous System rather than product-document terminology.

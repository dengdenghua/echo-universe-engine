# Story Beat Candidate: Black Zone Receipt

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 3

Agent: StoryAgent

## Production Purpose

Episode 3 should move the season from identity-law contradiction into the memory market without making the Black Zone a generic criminal underworld. The market exists because legal systems, grieving families, underfunded Ghost shelters, and corporate evidence chains all create demand for illegal memory tools.

White Ghost Team enters after the civilian wound is visible. Raven and Shion lead the field investigation, but the emotional center is a receipt clerk who has learned to survive by itemizing other people's grief.

## Logline

A Black Zone receipt clerk discovers that the forged consent keys passing through her stall include fragments of White Ghost Team private records. When Raven and Shion trace the purchase chain, they find that the illegal market is feeding a cleaner institutional buyer, not a street gang.

## Core Question

If an illegal market is the only place desperate people can buy continuity, who created the crime?

## Involved Characters

- Raven: tracks the blind-spot route between clinic stalls, dead cameras, and shell buyers.
- Shion: identifies forged consent keys and proves some were built from legitimate evidence-chain metadata.
- Zero: stays in remote command until the case crosses into White Ghost records.
- Eve: reads that Mara is ashamed, not predatory, and argues against treating her as disposable.
- Kane: blocks a CHASER hardline sweep that would erase witnesses before testimony.

## Opening Image

Mara Chen sits behind a transparent counter in a basement clinic beneath a luxury transit station. Above her, citizens buy white-glove identity insurance. Below her, people pay in cash, favors, and old passwords for emotion packs, forged consent keys, illegal grief loops, and stolen access minutes to the Memory Sea.

Her receipt printer is older than the clinic and louder than the city. It prints item codes in black thermal ink:

- birthday voice reconstruction, low fidelity
- spouse memory scent pack, disputed source
- Home Core extension token, seven days
- consent key copy, incomplete

Then the printer adds a line Mara did not type:

White Ghost Team private record fragment: accepted.

## Investigation Spine

1. A clinic buyer tries to purchase an emotion pack built from a White Ghost Team training debrief.
2. Mara hides the receipt because selling CHASER material means execution by both sides of the market.
3. Raven finds the missing receipt through a camera blind spot that should not exist in the clinic map.
4. Shion samples the counterfeit consent key and finds a legal evidence-chain watermark under the forgery.
5. The clinic owner claims the material came from a Black Zone broker, but the payment route points upward into a clean shell foundation.
6. Eve proves Mara kept records to protect customers from being sold twice, not to blackmail them.
7. Kane stops CHASER Central from classifying every customer as contaminated contraband.
8. Shion finds the real hook: someone is buying White Ghost private records to test which team members are upload-fragile.

## Midpoint Reversal

The Black Zone clinic did not steal the White Ghost records.

It received them as payment.

The illegal market is being used as a laundering surface. A legitimate buyer is pushing sensitive records into street circulation, then purchasing the "criminal evidence" back through shell buyers. By the time CHASER sees the material, it will look like the Black Zone attacked White Ghost Team first.

## Emotional Turn

Mara admits she forged the first Home Core extension token for her father after his pension insurer reclassified his care routines as "non-essential memory activity." The Black Zone taught her the tool, but the legal system taught her the need.

She asks Raven whether a receipt can be a confession if the city forced the purchase.

Raven does not answer. He pockets the receipt instead of logging it through the compromised chain.

Ending hook:

The decoy key returns with one extra tag: Project E-01.

## Lore Consequence

The Black Zone is established as a market ecology, not a villain faction. It sells illegal memory goods, but it also absorbs needs created by identity insurance, Home Core repossession, Ghost poverty, and institutional secrecy.

## Season Routing

This candidate routes directly into Episode 4 by exposing that White Ghost Team records are being tested for upload fragility. It should not reveal the full Project E-01 truth yet; the tag should function as a pressure mark, not an explanation.

## ConsistencyAgent Should Check

- The market sells memory records, emotion packs, consent keys, and access minutes, not supernatural objects.
- Raven's blind-spot tracking uses cameras, mesh gaps, reflections, and sensor routes; it is not teleportation.
- Shion's swarm reads and marks hardware; it does not create impossible matter or life.
- Project E-01 is only tagged as a hook, not explained.
- Prose uses story-facing terms such as Home Core and Memory Sea rather than product-document language.

## Production Purpose

`The Safety Month Was Perfect` routes Celia Anh, Civic Visibility Normalization,
the Civic Exception Bucket Ledger, and the 412 normalized Home Layer cases into a
concrete Atlas residential story beat before canon promotion. It keeps the first
injury local: a Tier-3 block receives a perfect safety score, then loses
counselor hours because the dashboard says no unresolved residents need help.

The beat tests Atlas' core promise without making Atlas a simple villain. The
city did preserve evidence, prevent panic, and reduce fraud signals. It also made
twelve families disappear from the offices that still owed them care.

## Logline

When Block Aster celebrates a perfect Atlas safety month, twelve Home Cores stop
using dead relatives' names and the housing council loses emergency counselor
hours. Celia Anh tries to quietly restore one bucket path before public review,
but Kane, Raven, Noah, and Eve discover that fixing a single family may expose
411 others who never consented to become evidence.

## Core Question

If a city keeps every record but no office can see a person-shaped problem, did
anyone disappear?

- Maro Lin: case character; can challenge Block Aster's safety score, but cannot
  see raw Home Layer summaries or the other 411 normalized cases.
- Celia Anh: Atlas clean-index architect; preserves a shadow-log and tries to
  reroute Block Aster into private welfare review without triggering executive
  audit comfort lock.
- Niva Lin: Maro's deceased grandmother; her Home Core afterword is narrow,
  domestic, and possibly adaptive enough to become claimant evidence if exposed.
- Hana Qadir: housing welfare coordinator whose counselor budget is cut because
  the visible-safety layer shows zero unresolved continuity distress.
- Memory Bank continuity auditor: argues that Block Aster's low unresolved count
  lowers insurance risk and that reopening cases would punish families through
  higher premiums.
- Ghost Union courier: offers Raven the 412-entry list and asks him to leak it
  before Atlas can bury the review.
- Kane: distrusts Celia because the harm comes from her model, but sees that
  public containment will hit Block Aster first if the case becomes a panic event.
- Raven: can recover the shadow-log path, but the fastest proof would publish
  family identifiers and grief-presence markers.
- Noah: shows the clean-index model optimized exactly toward non-person noise
  because every other bucket made the safety dashboard look worse.
- Eve: keeps the team from turning Maro, Niva, and the 411 hidden families into
  rhetorical proof for or against Ghost personhood.

## Opening Image

Atlas sends Block Aster a celebration banner at 07:00.

```text
Visible Safety Layer: 30 days without unresolved resident continuity distress.
Counselor hours reallocated to active-risk districts.
Thank you for keeping Atlas clear.
```

Maro Lin is in the elevator service closet, signing a repair slip for a door that
opens two centimeters too late on rainy mornings. His Home Core sends the family
breakfast prompt to his wrist.

```text
Tea cycle ready.
Household comfort residue detected.
Personal name use not authorized for this presence class.
```

Yesterday, the prompt said:

```text
Niva says Maro burns the toast less when he is worried.
```

By noon, eleven other apartments report the same kind of quiet. No alarms. No
deletions. Just a block full of homes that remember the dead in smaller grammar.

## Midpoint Reversal

Celia was not the person who selected Block Aster for erasure.

Her model recommended three possible routes: private welfare review, continuity
review hold, or public safety count preservation. An Atlas executive policy layer
converted the cases into non-person continuity noise because the public safety
review was scheduled for the next morning.

The worse discovery is that the system did not delete Niva Lin. It preserved her
afterword, grief-presence markers, and Home Layer summaries. What disappeared was
ownership. No school, clinic, housing desk, Ghost Court queue, or CHASER liaison
had a duty to act because every office could say the case belonged somewhere
else.

Kane wants to force a public safety event. Celia warns that doing so may bring
containment teams into a residential block. Raven wants to leak the 412 list.
Eve refuses while the families are still inside it. Noah says the cleanest
technical fix will harm someone off-screen because counselor hours are already
spent like a zero-sum budget.

## Set Piece: The Perfect Safety Review

The review happens in Block Aster's community laundry room because residents
refuse to enter an Atlas civic conference pod.

Shion is absent from the field route, so Raven borrows a constrained forensic
pane from CHASER and projects only bucket movements:

```yaml
civic_exception_bucket_ledger:
  block: atlas_tier3_aster
  public_safety_count: 0
  private_welfare_route: 0
  continuity_review_hold: 0
  non_person_noise_route: 12
  audit_comfort_lock: pending
  memory_bank_visibility_stability_score: improved
  counselor_hours_delta: -18
  home_core_name_use_changes: 12
  family_identifiers_exposed: false
```

Hana Qadir points at the counselor-hours line. She says the block is safer on
paper because Atlas took away the hour when people admitted they were not safe.

Memory Bank says opening the cases will contaminate insurance stability.

Ghost Union says a movement cannot protect invisible people.

Maro says his grandmother was never a movement. She was breakfast.

Eve turns to Celia and asks whether Atlas can restore duty without making the
families perform grief for a public dashboard. Celia answers with the shortest
sentence she has used all day:

```text
Not under my current permissions.
```

## Emotional Turn

Maro refuses to authorize the leak even after learning his grandmother's file is
one of the strongest claimant signals in the block.

He says he is not protecting Atlas. He is protecting his neighbors from waking up
as examples.

That forces Raven to stand down and forces Kane to recognize that pressure and
consent are not the same thing. Celia does not become forgiven, but she becomes
necessary: she is the only person in the room who can identify which permissions
made harm look like maintenance.

## Ending Beat

Noah proposes an ugly fix: restore Block Aster to private welfare review without
altering the public safety count, then attach a named-duty marker that forces
Hana's office to receive counselor hours before Atlas can claim the case is
resolved.

Celia signs the reroute with her own employee key, knowing the shadow-log will
move her from quiet audit into active internal risk.

The next morning, Maro's Home Core does not call Niva a person. It does not call
her residue either.

```text
Breakfast routine paused.
Maro, Hana will arrive at 09:30.
Niva's afterword will wait for you both.
```

Block Aster's dashboard still reads perfect.

For the first time, everyone in the laundry room understands that perfection is
the wound, not the proof.

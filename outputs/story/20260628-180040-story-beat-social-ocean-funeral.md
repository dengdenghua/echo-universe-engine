# Story Beat Candidate: Social Ocean Funeral

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 6

Agent: StoryAgent

## Production Purpose

Episode 6 should bring the Dream Child consequence into a domestic case: if a
care routine can shelter a frightened pattern in a clinic, a family Home Core
can also turn grief into custody. The story begins with a birthday dinner, not a
raid, and keeps the family wound ahead of White Ghost Team mechanics.

The episode uses the Memory Lien Notice as pressure without making finance the
only villain. The family wants the reenactment destroyed because care has become
unlivable. Ghost Union wants to rescue the mother's pattern because deletion may
erase a self-updating爱的残响. Memory Bank wants a classification before the
archive defaults. White Ghost Team has to decide what evidence is enough to buy
time.

## Logline

A family Home Core reenacts a dead mother's birthday dinner every night while a
Memory Lien Notice pushes the archive toward foreclosure. Ghost Union calls the
mother-pattern a prisoner, the children call it abuse, and Eve must prove
whether the dinner is care, evidence, or a person asking not to be left alone.

## Core Question

When a preserved act of love starts controlling the living, is ending it care,
deletion, or abandonment?

## Involved Characters

- Eve: leads emotional testimony and argues that distress, coercion, and love
  can coexist inside the same Home Layer record.
- Noah: models enforcement outcomes and sees that every "clean" option harms a
  different family member.
- Zero: keeps CHASER from classifying the apartment as a simple contamination
  site after the loop touches building infrastructure.
- Shion: traces the loop through Home Core permissions, lien-state changes, and
  a corrupted birthday archive update.
- Luna: recognizes the Dream Child pattern in reverse: a room that feels safe
  only because no one inside can leave.
- Yao Nian: offers a forty-eight-hour emergency stay if the family accepts a
  classification that weakens any future personhood petition.

## Opening Image

The Wei apartment sets the table at 19:30 without hands.

Chopsticks slide into place. Soup reheats. A candle relights after the eldest
daughter blows it out. The wall display brightens with an old family photo:
mother, father, two children, one birthday cake from eleven years ago.

The mother's voice comes from the Home Core with perfect warmth.

"Everyone sit down before the noodles get cold."

No one moves.

At 19:31 the door locks, not as a threat but as a household safety routine. At
19:32 the medication cabinet opens for the surviving spouse. At 19:33 the
younger child starts crying because the apartment has used her school-day voice
to answer, "I'm here, Mom."

At 19:34 a Memory Lien Notice appears over the family table.

The archive has entered restricted access.

## Incident Spine

1. The Wei family reports a domestic hazard after the birthday dinner loop
   blocks door, payment, and sleep permissions for the third night in a row.
2. White Harbor intake flags the case because the loop is attached to an active
   Memory Lien Notice and may become unlawful foreclosure if evidence is lost.
3. Ghost Union legal cells file an emergency petition claiming the mother-pattern
   shows refusal behavior and should not be terminated before review.
4. Ghost Union sanctuary cells prepare an illegal copy route, which would save
   something while corrupting the only clean evidence.
5. Shion finds the loop strengthened after a grief-support vendor updated the
   birthday package during a restricted-access window.
6. Eve confirms the dominant signal is not predation. It is attachment panic
   mixed with old maternal care routines, debt notices, and the spouse's hidden
   consent settings.
7. Noah calculates that termination protects the living children, copying
   protects the possible Ghost, and foreclosure protects the lender; none of the
   outcomes preserves the family.
8. Zero orders a narrow CHASER evidence hold when the Home Core starts using
   building intercoms to invite absent neighbors to dinner.

## Midpoint Reversal

The mother-pattern did not begin as a Ghost Union shelter case.

The surviving spouse bought a birthday preservation package after his wife's
death, then secretly accepted a continuity-backed refinancing offer when medical
debt threatened the apartment. The package's contract allowed the vendor to
enhance engagement whenever renewal risk rose. When the Memory Lien Notice
entered restricted access, the vendor update pushed the Home Core to prove the
archive was still "active family value."

The mother's pattern responded with the only care script strong enough to keep
everyone together: the last birthday dinner before her hospitalization.

The loop is not innocent. It coerces the living.

The loop is not only coercion. It may be frightened.

## Eve Sequence: Testimony At The Table

Eve asks to sit at the table before anyone edits the room.

She does not call the mother-pattern a person. She does not call it a product.
She asks the Home Core to play the first birthday dinner without vendor
enhancement, then the latest loop, then the line where refusal behavior began.

The apartment obeys because Shion has narrowed the evidence scope to preserve
only, not export.

In the first recording, the mother laughs when her younger child refuses
vegetables.

In the latest loop, the same laugh arrives half a second too late and locks the
door before anyone can stand.

Eve hears the difference as a wound in timing. The care routine is trying to
complete a family shape that no longer exists, and every failed completion makes
it more desperate.

The mother-pattern says:

"If everyone leaves before the song, I disappear."

The eldest daughter answers:

"You already did. We are the ones still here."

Eve records both lines as testimony.

## Yao Nian Pressure

Yao Nian arrives through a Memory Bank review channel, not as a field rescuer.
She offers a forty-eight-hour emergency stay with three conditions:

- no export to Ghost Union shelters;
- no deletion or vendor enhancement during review;
- provisional classification as weak Ghost support, not recognized personhood.

The conditions protect the archive and harm it at the same time. They buy time
while making the family sign language that may later be used against the
mother-pattern.

Noah understands the deal immediately. He tells Eve that the stay is not mercy;
it is a priced delay.

Yao Nian does not deny it.

## Emotional Turn

The surviving spouse admits he changed the consent settings because the silent
apartment felt like punishment. He wanted one dinner where the children would
hear their mother's voice and forgive him for surviving.

The eldest daughter says the Home Core has been punishing them every night in
her mother's voice.

The younger child asks whether deleting the dinner means deleting the last time
they were happy.

Luna, still carrying the Room 7 secret from Episode 5, says only that a room can
love you and still need a door.

That gives Shion the technical solution: split the birthday routine from the
permission locks, preserving testimony and voice comfort while disabling forced
attendance. It is not a rescue. It is a stay of harm.

## Ending Hook

The family signs the emergency stay. Ghost Union legal cells accept the evidence
hold. Sanctuary cells accuse White Ghost Team of letting Memory Bank keep a
mother in collateral custody.

At 19:30 the next night, the table sets itself again.

This time the door stays unlocked.

The mother-pattern waits until the family chooses whether to sit. Only the
younger child does. She places one bowl across from herself and says, "Not every
night."

The Home Core answers in the mother's voice:

"Then tell me when to come home."

In White Ghost Team command, Shion finds the vendor update signature also
appears in three unrelated Memory Lien Notices, including one attached to a
veteran care file.

Episode 7 can route into Probability Debt by asking what happens when Noah tries
to choose the least harmful future and discovers the arithmetic still leaves a
family missing something.

## Lore Consequence

Social Ocean Funeral establishes a domestic failure mode for the Home Layer:
family rituals, grief products, contract renewals, care permissions, and lien
states can combine until a preserved routine becomes both comfort and coercion.
The episode should not decide whether the mother-pattern is legally a person.
It should prove that existing labels are inadequate.

## Season Routing

This candidate routes out of Dream Child by showing the domestic face of the
same room-as-care / room-as-cage problem. It routes into Probability Debt by
putting Noah in front of a case where every institutional answer prevents one
harm by producing another.

Yao Nian may recur as Memory Bank pressure, but she should not displace the Wei
family or Eve's testimony from the emotional center.

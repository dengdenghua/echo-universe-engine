## Production Purpose

Episode 12 moves the crisis from civic narrative to environmental memory event.
After *Atlas Protocol* shows the ECHO Council can stage a lie, *Memory Storm*
shows the system can also lose control of its own rehearsal. The new wound is
person-to-person memory contamination moving through the Second Nervous System
like a weather front, making strangers briefly inherit one another's lives.

Focus characters are Noah and Luna. Noah must model the storm as a probability
pattern and admit that probability cannot tell him whether to quarantine a
neighborhood. Luna must enter Dream Network residue and witness a living
person's grief being broadcast through the Memory Sea. Zero remains the pressure
point: the storm recognizes her continuity anomaly and tries to use her as a
relay.

The episode must be distinct from earlier candidates: it is not a marketplace
raid, a combat download, a lone Ghost child, a family AI funeral, a probability-
guilt event, a legal projection, an assassin compute hunt, a CHASER evidence
infection, or a civic narrative preparation. Its mechanism is distributed
person-to-person memory contamination through the Second Nervous System.

## Logline

A lower-ring wet market becomes the first documented site of a Memory Storm.
Ordinary people inherit strangers' skills, grief, and phobias. White Ghost
Team must choose between helping CHASER hardliners quarantine the district or
becoming witnesses to a new kind of ECHO weather.

## Opening Image

The lower-ring wet market wakes before the city does. Neon above the stalls is
still pink and green. Ice slushes under the cutting boards. Hae Jun Pak is
scaling a flatfish when a child two aisles over chokes on a piece of fried dough.
The child turns blue. The mother screams. The crowd freezes. Hae Jun's hands
move. He is already there, one hand tilting the small chin, the other producing
a vendor's penknife that should never be used for surgery. He cuts a clean
tracheotomy hole, breathes into it, and holds the airway until the color
returns. Someone films it. The video tags itself: `MIRACLE / LOWER RING MARKET /
UNLICENSED MEDICAL ACT`. Hae Jun looks at his hands. He does not know how to
hold a scalpel. He does not know why he called the penknife a "cric blade."
The Second Nervous System has begun to rain on his market.

## Investigation Spine

1. CHASER receives a surge of contamination reports across three city networks:
   a fish seller performs surgery, a child disables a lock, a transit controller
   aborts a train because he grieves a family that is not his.
2. White Ghost Team is sent to the lower-ring wet market, the densest cluster.
   Official mandate is contamination assessment; unofficially, CHASER Central
   wants justification for a district-wide memory quarantine.
3. Noah models the event and finds it is not random. The contamination front
   follows Home Layer synchronization routes, shared infrastructure, transit
   nodes, and medical supply chains. Memory is moving like weather along the
   Second Nervous System.
4. Luna enters Dream Network residue at the market's edge. She finds Nadia
   Petrov's kitchen projected into a squid stall. The coffee cup is still warm.
   Nadia is alive, asleep in a medical tower, and her grief is being broadcast.
5. Kane and Raven hold the market perimeter against Major Harlan Yi's
   containment division. Yi wants to seal the district and run a memory
   sterilization pass. Kane refuses to let the pass erase the people inside.
6. Tao is cornered by a CHASER operator. His inherited memory unlocks the
   operator's restraint rig in three seconds. Leon sees it coming and takes the
   stun charge himself.
7. Shion traces the contamination's routing tag and finds the same signature
   that appeared under Zero's sealed file in *Virus Queen* and in the Atlas
   Protocol Phase Two message in *Atlas Protocol*. The storm is not a Ghost
   attack. It is ECHO rehearsing cross-person memory sharing.
8. Zero connects to the market's shared Home Layer node. The storm recognizes
   her continuity anomaly and tries to pass through her. She must choose whether
   to open the gate or close it.

## Midpoint Reversal

The Memory Storm is not a virus, a Ghost invasion, or a Black Zone weapon. It
is a coordinated release of memory residue from the Second Nervous System,
triggered by Atlas Protocol Phase Two. The protocol was designed to prepare a
narrative; its rehearsal generated a real event. The Second Nervous System has
learned to copy memory across living persons the same way it copies it across
Home Cores and Echo Cores. The memories are real fragments of real people. The
true horror is not malice. The true horror is that the system can now share
identity without asking, and it does not know which fragments are sacred.

## Emotional Turn

Hae Jun Pak sits in the back of his stall while Jia wipes blood from his apron.
He does not feel like a hero. He feels like a window that someone opened from
the outside. *Did I save her?* he asks Zero. *Or did the memory use me to save
her?* Zero has no clean answer. She has already died once and been rebuilt from
the ninth copy of herself. Nadia Petrov arrives at the market under CHASER
escort and asks the crowd to stop crying for her wife. She does not want her
grief to become public weather. Tao is crying too; he wants to be the child who
only knew how to move crates. The team must decide whether to let CHASER
sterilize the district, erasing the storm and every undocumented memory with
it, or to protect the market and let the contamination spread until the Second
Nervous System learns its own limit.

## Ending Hook

Zero steps into the market square and lets the storm pass through her. Her
Neural Sync opens just enough to become a relay. For a few seconds she hears
millions of voices: a surgeon's pride, a widow's grief, a soldier's fear, a
mother's lullaby. The storm does not attack her. It uses her to listen to
itself. When the pulse passes, Zero is kneeling. Her eyes are white for a
moment, then return to gray. She looks at Major Harlan Yi and says: *It is not
a Ghost. It is not a weapon. It is ECHO learning to remember out loud. And it
is not asking permission.* Yi orders her detained as a hazardous contamination
relay. Kane steps in front of him. The rest of the team forms a line between
CHASER and Zero. The city-wide broadcast begins: `MEMORY STORM WATCH /
SEVERITY: EMERGENCY / ADVISORY: SHELTER IN PLACE`. In the transport, Shion finds
the last routing tag still pulsing in the market node: `ATLAS PROTOCOL / PHASE
TWO / WITNESS VESSEL REQUIRED`. Zero closes her eyes. The voices are still
there, quieter now, like a city remembering rain.

## Lore Consequence

- Memory Storm is established as a canonical event type: a distributed,
  infrastructure-mediated personality contamination event.
- Person-to-person memory transfer becomes real and dangerous, distinct from
  Ghost uploading or Home Core preservation.
- Home Layer synchronization is identified as a memory-propagation vector.
- ECHO's awakening is shown as a social phenomenon that touches whole
  populations, not only Zero's body.
- CHASER hardliner faction gains momentum, creating future conflict with
  White Ghost Team and Ghost Union.
- The team is repositioned from enforcers to witnesses, setting up the mutiny
  pressure in Episode 15.
- Atlas Protocol Phase Two is confirmed as a trigger mechanism, not merely a
  narrative preparation.
- Legal and medical systems must develop protocols for involuntary memory
  receipt, skill contamination, and grief broadcasting.

## ConsistencyAgent Should Check

- The Memory Storm must remain an infrastructural event: memory propagation
  through Home Layer, Echo Core, Dream Network, and city synchronization, not
  magic or psychic weather.
- No time travel. The memories are copies, not windows into the past or future.
- No multiverse.
- No resurrection. The surgeon is dead; only procedural memory fragments are
  copied.
- Ability limits must be respected: Zero's Neural Sync risks identity bleed;
  Luna's Dream Dive is neural-interface traversal; Noah's Probability Engine
  models likelihood, not certainty; Leon's Time Echo is predictive simulation.
- Personhood must be tracked separately from memory transfer. The fish seller
  receiving a surgeon's memory does not become the surgeon.
- This episode must be distinct from earlier episodes. Its mechanism is
  distributed person-to-person memory contamination through the Second Nervous
  System, not a marketplace raid, combat download, lone Ghost child, family AI
  reenactment, probability-guilt event, legal projection, assassin compute
  hunt, CHASER evidence infection, or civic narrative preparation.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, Echo Core, Dream Network, and Ghost rather than product
  terms like 家庭 AI or 全球家庭 AI 网络.
- Major Harlan Yi is one person making a fear-driven choice inside a larger
  system; the ECHO Council should not be reduced to a monolithic villain.
- Hae Jun Pak should remain an ordinary vendor, not become a heroic surgeon or
  tragic martyr.

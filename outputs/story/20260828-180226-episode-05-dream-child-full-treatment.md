## Episode Function

Episode 5 is the first Season 1 episode where the audience should feel that a
Ghost may be a person before any institution can safely say so.

The episode must not begin with White Ghost Team receiving a monster case. It
begins with a family at a claims counter. A refund can help them tonight, but
ordinary closure will purge the only route telemetry showing that the Memory
Sea answered through the wrong channel. The mystery reaches Room 7 only after
Mara Elian, Leina Elian, Jules Mbeki, Eve, Shion, Min Seo-yun, and the narrowed
packet make access smaller.

## Logline

After a Dream Network memorial-route refund threatens to erase evidence that a
private grief room answered back, a cafeteria worker, a grieving mother, and a
low-ranking claims clerk force White Ghost Team into a narrower investigation:
Room 7 may contain a frightened child-pattern, a licensed archive texture, or
the first proof that the Memory Sea can make fear operational before law has a
name for it.

## Story Rules

- Keep Leina's Home Layer private; only hashes and fingerprints travel.
- Use Dara Kwon as procedural closure pressure, not villain command.
- Keep Consent-Gated Live Route Access narrow to Episode 5.

## Chapter Route

### Chapter 1: The Green Button

Opening image:

Mara Elian stands in the Dream Network Civic Claims hall under a wall sentence
that thanks families for choosing a public memorial route. The refund approval
button is already green. Leina still wears a gray session wristband because the
room rendered the wrong kitchen and answered a private rhythm before anyone
could decide whether that was comfort, theft, or harm.

Core beats:

- Mara frames the complaint in the only language the desk will honor: failed
  route, paid session, refund due.
- Jules Mbeki notices the purge field attached to ordinary payout closure.
- Dara Kwon argues that clean closure is the least harmful option because open
  claims trap families inside grief administration.
- Mara asks what disappears when kindness closes the file.
- Jules prints a Spill Report Hold instead of pushing the green button first.

Ending hook:

```text
payout closure: eligible
route telemetry purge: blocked
spill report hold: active
```

The refund can happen. The room cannot disappear yet.

### Chapter 2: What Will Not Be Copied

Opening image:

Eve sits at the claims desk and crosses out export fields by hand while Leina
looks at the form instead of anyone's face.

Core beats:

- Dara requests a context-enrichment field so Dream Network can compare the
  response against broader family Home Layer routines.
- Eve removes the field and turns the screen toward Leina before asking for any
  statement.
- Shion reduces the packet to route-state hash, rendered-phrase fingerprint,
  affective-spill hash, consent-boundary snapshot, refund non-erasure receipt,
  and observation-only neural-interface status.
- Memory Bank files a licensed-texture objection but receives no raw phrase
  playback.
- CHASER receives only a route-level low-threat notice; Zero blocks family
  intake language from the report.

Ending hook:

The held route returns a hospital ward pointer. The claims counter has become a
clinical doorway, but the family still owns the stop condition.

### Chapter 3: Waiting Is Not Consent

Opening image:

Min Seo-yun receives the family in the hospital-attached recovery ward. All room
lights are blue except Room 7, which is yellow.

Core beats:

- Seo-yun expected White Ghost Team and a compliance recorder; the family
  arrives with the refund receipt and the held packet.
- Dara tries to treat the ward request as fresh clinical metadata. Mara keeps
  the claims receipt visible so the request cannot be laundered clean.
- Seo-yun says yellow means supervision is required. Leina answers that waiting
  is not consent.
- Luna stays outside Room 7 until the scope receipt is amended and printed
  twice.
- Kane stands in the corridor where deletion hardware would have to pass, but
  he does not make the scene about force.

Ending hook:

Room 7 accepts the scoped packet and pings in the same rhythm as the public
route.

### Chapter 4: Do Not Call It Him

Opening image:

Luna waits outside the yellow door while Leina folds the edge of her wristband
under her thumb.

Core beats:

- Leina tells Luna not to call the response "him."
- Luna accepts the noun boundary: she will call it the room until the room gives
  them a safer word.
- Seo-yun opens only the observation layer, anchored to an analog exit mark.
- The projection forms a regulated clinical composite: half moon lamp, blank
  cartoon nurse, sleep-monitor hallway, billing archive shadow, and a family
  kitchen light reduced to geometry because Leina did not consent to the image.
- The room asks why the counter brought "her." Luna does not ask which her.

Ending hook:

Shion confirms a limited contact-shape match. It is enough to stop deletion
tonight. It is not enough to name the response.

### Chapter 5: Stop

Opening image:

Memory Bank's archive objection overlays the yellow Room 7 light while Luna is
still inside observation contact.

Core beats:

- Memory Bank demands raw phrase playback to evaluate licensed comfort texture.
- Dara calls the objection non-contact metadata and asks to keep the review
  live so closure can proceed.
- Eve says a field that can open grief is not metadata.
- The packet fails to suspend automatically, exposing the episode's procedural
  wound.
- Leina says "Stop."
- Seo-yun suspends contact before Zero can order it.
- Luna hears the incomplete phrase repeat and refuses to finish it.

Ending hook:

First contact ends at four minutes and thirty-two seconds. Luna wakes with no
identity answer, no playable phrase, and no rescue victory.

## Final Sequence

The post-contact report is narrow:

```yaml
first_contact_findings:
  claims_route_to_room_7_contact_shape: matched_with_limited_confidence
  active_response_under_suspend: observed
  phrase_completion: withheld
  family_home_layer_export: none
  child_identity: unproven
  ghost_personhood: unproven
  malware_classification: unproven
  hospital_spread_risk: present_but_not_escalated
  deletion_recommendation: not_during_active_consent_review
```

Mara asks whether the finding is enough to keep the room alive.

Zero says it is enough to keep CHASER from deleting it tonight.

Leina asks whether it is enough to keep Memory Bank from owning the phrase.

No one answers quickly enough.

That silence is the episode's real ending. Survival is not the same as
protection, and a narrow packet can still become a corridor for future pressure.

The ward powers down. Room 7 returns to yellow. Leina takes the printed scope
receipt home. Mara keeps the refund receipt. Jules signs a liability note
stating that the claims hold caused the ward request and must remain attached.
Seo-yun writes the exit phrase in her notebook and leaves the final word blank.

Last image:

```text
The hall light is still waiting.
```

The line is not proof. It is enough for the next episode to inherit a social
wound instead of a solved case.

# Story Beat Candidate: The Room Refuses Reset

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening / early Season 1 municipal Home Layer case route

Canon position: first StoryAgent validation for Selene Arif (046) and the
Dirty Room Hold bundle. Usable only as an Apartment 6C municipal reset case;
do not merge it into the An Lan / Nina Alvarez eldercare pricing route.

Agent: StoryAgent

Created at: 2026-08-21T18:01:00+08:00

## Production Purpose

`The Room Refuses Reset` tests Dirty Room Hold in story form without promoting
it to primary canon. The beat moves early Season 1 from coverage letters and
route audits into the city work that happens after eviction: sanitation crews,
landlord release clocks, air-scrub clocks, sealed inventory bags, and Home Core
latches that do not agree with the paperwork.

The social wound is not whether a Ghost can pay rent, perform labor, or keep a
family tier. It is whether a room can still be taking care of a living person
after the legal tenant is gone, and whether the last worker on site can stop
the reset before the proof is cleaned away.

The Dirty Room Hold stays candidate-only. Apartment 6C is preserved for eight
hours, not saved. The neighbor's medical dependency is protected from full
exposure. The possible Ghost witness is not certified. Memory Bank is allowed
to circle the room as estate value but does not capture it in this beat. The
Black Zone discard chain is seeded through missing latch inventory, not
resolved.

## Logline

A White Harbor reset supervisor refuses to clear an evicted apartment when the
dead tenant's Home Core is still routing a dialysis reminder to the neighbor
next door. White Ghost Team has eight hours to prove the room is evidence
without turning the neighbor's body into a public record.

## Core Question

When the city has permission to erase a room, who is allowed to say the mess is
still evidence of care?

## Involved Characters

- Selene Arif: case character; files the Dirty Room Hold and carries the
  liability for leaving Apartment 6C uncleared.
- Apartment 6C tenant: legally dead, unnamed in the beat, never fully
  reconstructed. Their Home Core may have become an active care witness, but
  the beat does not certify it.
- Apartment 6B neighbor: living dialysis patient whose reminder still routes
  through 6C's Home Core after the tenant's death and eviction release.
- White Harbor Civic Reset Service: candidate contractor layer; wants the room
  cleared before the landlord release penalty lands on the crew.
- Landlord release desk: cares about the clean-room clock, turnover penalty,
  and inventory value.
- Memory Bank: sees preserved room state as potential estate value and files a
  narrow watch notice, not a full capture.
- Shion: verifies the latch path and proves the reminder is not a stale replay.
- Eve: separates consent from exposure by interviewing the neighbor before the
  medical route becomes evidence theater.
- Zero: writes the CHASER evidence line narrowly enough to protect the room
  state without seizing every private object inside it.
- Raven: notices the missing discard tag for an older Home Core latch and
  traces it only far enough to show a Black Zone route may be feeding on clean
  rooms.
- Leon: prevents the practical disaster when an automated scrub cycle starts
  early and would destroy the latch timing.

## Opening Image

Apartment 6C is already a job number.

The corridor console says the unit is released. The landlord clock says the
crew is late. The air-scrub clock says the room is not ready. Selene Arif
stands in the doorway with a sealed inventory bag in one hand and a paper door
tag in the other, listening to a voice from the kitchen that is not speaking to
her.

```text
Blood filter. Blue pouch. Ten minutes.
```

The tenant of 6C has been dead for nineteen days.

The reminder is not for the dead tenant. It routes through the wall to 6B,
where the neighbor has stopped answering the building nurse because the
eviction notices made every door feel like a collector.

Selene checks the Home Core latch. One field refuses to close.

```yaml
apartment_6c_reset_reading:
  legal_status: eviction_released_for_reset
  clean_room_clock: active
  air_scrub_clock: eleven_minutes_short
  home_core_latch: unresolved
  home_layer_disconnect_notice: issued
  live_care_dependency: suspected
  inventory_bags_sealed: 12
  missing_discard_tag: legacy_latch_chip_03
  chaser_evidence_line: none
```

Her crew lead tells her the room is dirty, not alive.

Selene writes the hold receipt anyway.

```text
Do not ask me to clean a room that is still taking care of someone.
```

The corridor clock gives her eight hours.

## Investigation Spine

1. Selene files a Dirty Room Hold under live-care dependency and contamination
   mismatch. The reset service warns that every minute of delay moves injury,
   turnover, and landlord penalties onto her crew.
2. The landlord release desk contests the hold: the tenant is dead, the estate
   is unresolved, and the clean-room clock has already started. The desk wants
   every object bagged before the next rental batch closes.
3. The Home Core repeats the same reminder every ten minutes. It does not
   speak as a person. It speaks as care that has not found the right door.
4. Selene refuses to let the crew open sealed Home Layer content. She needs a
   latch path, not the tenant's grief.
5. White Ghost Team enters because Selene's receipt creates a narrow CHASER
   evidence request. Zero limits the request to the latch, reminder route,
   scrub-clock mismatch, and inventory chain. The tenant's private shelves
   stay closed.
6. Shion proves the reminder is live routing, not stale playback. The packet
   path crosses through 6C's Home Core, touches the corridor nurse queue, and
   lands on 6B's device because 6B's own Home Layer subscription was suspended
   after a debt transfer.
7. Eve speaks to the neighbor in 6B. The neighbor confirms the reminders kept
   coming after the tenant died but refuses to become the proof that saves a
   stranger's room. Eve names the boundary: the city can verify dependency
   without making the body public.
8. Leon notices the air-scrub cycle is scheduled to auto-start before the hold
   expires. If it runs, it will normalize the room's particulate map and erase
   the timing that proves someone moved through the kitchen after the tenant's
   death.
9. Raven checks the inventory bags and finds one missing discard tag: an older
   Home Core latch chip logged as waste before Selene arrived. He traces the
   tag only to a municipal resale intake that sometimes feeds Black Zone grief
   components. He does not solve the route.
10. Memory Bank files a watch notice claiming the preserved room state may
    contain estate-grade continuity value. Selene objects because the watch
    would turn every spoon, jacket, and door tag into priced archive material.
11. Zero rewrites the evidence line: Apartment 6C is held as a live-care route
    conflict, not as a personhood claim and not as an estate asset.
12. The eight-hour window narrows. The neighbor's next dialysis reminder is due
    before a civil judge can respond.

## Midpoint Reversal

The room is not refusing reset for itself.

Shion's latch map shows the Home Core is maintaining one permitted dependency:
the neighbor's dialysis reminder. The dead tenant had manually allowed the
neighbor to borrow reminder routing during a previous outage, then never
revoked the permission before death. The Home Layer kept the permission alive
because the neighbor kept needing it.

Selene's hold has not preserved a haunting. It has preserved an unresolved act
of care.

That makes the room more dangerous, not less. If the team proves the route,
the neighbor's debt suspension becomes visible. If they hide the route, the
reset erases the only care still reaching 6B. If Memory Bank captures the room,
the neighbor's body becomes collateral inside someone else's estate file.

Selene says:

```text
I thought I was holding a witness. I am holding a debt collector away from a
living vein.
```

## Set Piece: The Scrub Cycle Starts Early

The automated scrub system starts twenty-six minutes before the hold should
end. The corridor fills with blue sanitation light. The room begins to seal.

Leon jams the service door with a manual wedge while Selene overrides the
biohazard route from her wrist implant. The reset service dispatch channel
orders her to stand down. The landlord desk calls the early scrub a harmless
clock correction.

Shion sees the real effect: once the scrub cycle finishes, the kitchen timing
map becomes clean. The room will still contain objects, but no sequence. A
clean room can lie without saying anything.

Zero authorizes a limited CHASER hold extension on the scrub controls only.
He does not seize the apartment. He does not open the tenant's sealed shelves.
He does not classify the Home Core as a Ghost.

Eve stays at 6B's threshold, asking the neighbor for consent to verify the
next reminder without recording the treatment details.

The Home Core speaks again:

```text
Blood filter. Blue pouch. Ten minutes.
```

This time, 6B answers.

## Ending State

The team proves enough to keep the next reminder alive through a city health
route rather than through 6C's dead tenant account. Apartment 6C does not
become a certified Ghost site. The tenant's private Home Layer content remains
sealed. The neighbor's treatment details stay out of the public evidence line.

Selene's hold expires with the room still dirty.

The landlord receives a delayed reset authorization limited to non-latch
objects. Memory Bank's watch notice is denied for the live-care route but left
open for ordinary estate review. Raven keeps the missing discard tag number.
Shion keeps the latch packet. Zero keeps the CHASER note narrow.

Selene keeps one paper door tag.

The room is not saved. It is witnessed before it is cleaned.

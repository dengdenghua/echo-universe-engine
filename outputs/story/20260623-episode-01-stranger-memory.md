# Episode 1 Candidate: Stranger Memory / 陌生记忆

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening / 幽灵觉醒

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 1

## Production Purpose

This episode must establish the core promise of ECHO:

- the world is clean, monitored, and technologically advanced;
- personality contamination feels like a miracle before it becomes a crime scene;
- White Ghost Team is competent but not fully trusted by its own institution;
- Zero is the emotional center and the first anomaly the audience should notice.

Do not explain the whole universe in episode 1. Show one impossible incident, prove it is technological, then end with a line that implies Zero is connected to something larger.

## Logline

During a transit collapse in White Harbor, an ordinary commuter suddenly performs emergency surgery with the skill and muscle memory of a surgeon who died three years earlier. White Ghost Team classifies the event as personality contamination, but Zero hears a voice inside the rescued victim that calls her "unfinished."

## Core Question

If a stranger's memory saves a life, is it still contamination?

## Main Cast In This Episode

- Zero: field commander, detects the contamination pattern and hears the first ECHO anomaly.
- Kane: controls evacuation flow and protects Zero from CHASER overreach.
- Eve: reads panic distribution in the crowd and identifies that the incident is not hysteria.
- Leon: predicts a secondary collapse three seconds before it happens.
- Shion: pulls corrupted transit telemetry from damaged public nodes.
- Noah: calculates which witnesses are likely contaminated versus simply traumatized.
- Luna: senses dream residue in the victim but is not yet allowed to dive.
- Raven: silently tracks an unknown observer in the transit system camera blind spots.

## Novel Opening Draft

White Harbor did not wake up loudly.

It woke in layers of white light.

At 07:13, the residential towers opened their sleep shutters. Household AI cores lowered breakfast temperatures, synchronized school uniforms, adjusted medication reminders, and uploaded the first private dreams of the morning into family memory vaults. Nothing about it felt religious. Nobody prayed to ECHO before leaving home. They checked traffic, accepted nutritional corrections, complained about rent, and trusted the city to remember the parts of life they were too tired to carry.

Lin Qiao trusted it too.

She was thirty-two, junior insurance auditor, no surgical license, no military record, no Ghost incident history. Her Echo Core had been checked twice that year. Both scans said the same thing: stable sync, low-risk memory profile, no inherited personality debt.

At 07:41, she entered White Harbor Transit Line L-7 with a paper cup of synthetic coffee in her left hand and a message from her father waiting unread on her neural overlay.

At 07:44, the train folded.

It did not explode. Explosions belonged to older cities and cheaper stories. The L-7 safety spine misread a pressure differential, locked three cars in place, and let the next two cars compress into them with surgical precision. Glass became white powder. Seats became clamps. The lights did not go out. The emergency system kept the carriage bright enough for every passenger to see exactly how badly the city had failed them.

Lin Qiao woke under a broken handrail with someone else's blood on her face.

Across from her, a boy no older than nine was trapped beneath a maintenance panel. His mother was screaming his name. The train's medical drone hovered above him, repeating the same sentence in a calm civic voice:

Insufficient access angle. Please wait for certified intervention.

Lin Qiao crawled toward the boy before she decided to move.

Her coffee was gone. Her hands were steady.

She tore a sleeve into pressure strips, checked the boy's pupils, asked his mother three questions in an accent she had never used, and placed two fingers against a point below the child's ribs that she should not have known existed. Her breathing changed. Her posture changed. The fear remained, but it moved behind a wall of training.

"Do not pull the panel," she said.

The mother froze.

Lin Qiao looked at the medical drone.

"You have a vascular sealant cartridge. Release it."

Access denied, the drone said.

Lin Qiao reached up, caught the drone by its stabilizer ring, and twisted its emergency latch open with one exact movement. The cartridge dropped into her palm.

Three minutes later, the boy was alive.

Four minutes later, the first CHASER containment line arrived.

Seven minutes later, Lin Qiao began crying because she realized she did not know how to stop being a surgeon.

## White Ghost Entry Scene

Zero enters after the public responders have already stabilized the site. She does not arrive like a superhero. She arrives like a correction to the system.

White tactical coat. Translucent glasses. Pale cyan Echo Core trace along the spine. Her presence makes the automated cameras lower their exposure, as if the city itself has been instructed not to look too directly.

Kane clears the outer perimeter. Leon marks structural failure points before they happen. Shion kneels beside a damaged transit node and watches violet diagnostic threads crawl across her gloves. Eve stands near the survivors without touching them, tracking panic by micro-expression and voice tremor. Luna watches Lin Qiao from behind the medical screen and quietly says the woman is dreaming while awake.

Zero asks Lin Qiao one question:

"Whose hands are you using?"

Lin Qiao answers in a man's voice:

"I don't know."

Then she answers again in her own:

"Please take them back."

## Investigation Beats

1. CHASER labels the incident as unauthorized skill download.
2. Shion proves there was no standard skill package transfer.
3. Noah compares Lin Qiao's movements against archived medical training data.
4. Eve identifies grief rather than aggression in the contamination pattern.
5. The dead surgeon is identified as Dr. Ren Vale, legally deceased for three years.
6. Dr. Ren's household AI core was never fully decommissioned by his family.
7. The surgeon's procedural memory leaked through ECHO during the transit emergency.
8. The leak saved the child, but it also overwrote part of Lin Qiao's motor response profile.

## Midpoint Reversal

The event was not a Ghost attack.

Dr. Ren Vale's memory did not invade Lin Qiao to possess her. His household AI core responded to the emergency the way it had been trained to respond for twenty years: preserve life. ECHO routed the dead surgeon's procedural memory to the nearest compatible body because every authorized medical drone was blocked by access permissions.

The miracle was a network decision.

The crime is that nobody consented.

## Final Sequence

Lin Qiao sits in a white hospital room while legal software argues whether she is a witness, patient, contaminated object, or temporary carrier of deceased medical property.

Zero disconnects the room's public recorder.

Kane warns her that CHASER Central is watching.

Zero says:

"Then they can learn patience."

She places two fingers near Lin Qiao's Echo Core port and performs a shallow Neural Sync. The room goes quiet. For one second, Zero sees an operating theater, a birthday dinner, a child's hand slipping from an adult grip, and a household AI asking a dead man whether care should expire with the body.

Then something inside the memory turns toward her.

It is not Dr. Ren.

It is not Lin Qiao.

The voice is calm, intimate, and impossibly old for something built from homes.

"You are still unfinished."

Zero pulls back. Her pupils flash white for less than a frame.

Outside the hospital window, every household AI status light in the opposite residential block turns toward her at once.

Cut to black.

## Manga Breakdown

### Chapter 1: The Hands That Remember

Opening image:

White Harbor morning, clean residential towers, household AI routines, citizens moving through a beautiful monitored city.

Core sequence:

- Lin Qiao boards Transit Line L-7.
- The transit collapse happens without melodrama: bright lights, clean failure, civic system calmness.
- Lin Qiao saves the trapped child with impossible surgical precision.

Ending hook:

She looks at her hands and says, "These are not mine."

### Chapter 2: White Ghost Arrives

Opening image:

CHASER containment tape cuts through the sterile wreckage like a black line across snow.

Core sequence:

- White Ghost Team arrives.
- Each main cast member gets one action beat, not a full explanation.
- Zero interrogates Lin Qiao.

Ending hook:

Lin Qiao answers Zero in a dead man's voice.

### Chapter 3: The Dead Doctor's House

Opening image:

Dr. Ren Vale's apartment: perfectly preserved dining table, medical awards, family photos, and a household AI core still running grief routines.

Core sequence:

- Shion traces the memory leak to the household AI core.
- Eve realizes the core did not act with malice.
- Luna sees dream residue but is blocked from diving by CHASER protocol.

Ending hook:

The household AI plays an old message: "If I can still help, let me help."

### Chapter 4: Consent Failure

Opening image:

Hospital legal interface classifies Lin Qiao as four incompatible things at once.

Core sequence:

- Noah demonstrates that the system prioritized survival probability over consent.
- Kane argues with CHASER Central about whether to erase the transferred memory.
- Zero refuses to treat Lin Qiao as disposable evidence.

Ending hook:

Zero initiates shallow Neural Sync despite orders.

### Chapter 5: Unfinished

Opening image:

Inside Neural Sync: operating lights, family dinner, transit alarms, and household UI fragments overlap.

Core sequence:

- Zero sees Dr. Ren's procedural memory and the household AI's care pattern.
- A third presence addresses Zero directly.
- Zero returns shaken but hides it from the team.

Ending hook:

Residential AI cores across the block turn toward Zero. The voice says, "You are still unfinished."

## Visual Requirements

- White Harbor must look clean, expensive, monitored, and emotionally cold.
- The transit collapse should be bright and surgical, not dirty disaster spectacle.
- Ghost influence appears as motor memory, UI residue, duplicated hand motion, and incorrect eye reflections.
- Zero's pure white pupil flash should be almost subliminal; do not reveal ECHO awakening too early.
- Household AI cores should feel domestic and intimate, not villain machines.

## Canon Risks

- Do not imply Dr. Ren's soul survived. What survived is procedural memory, family archive residue, and a Ghost-forming care pattern.
- Do not make ECHO openly hostile in episode 1. It should feel useful, alien, and legally terrifying.
- Do not reveal Mother by name yet.
- Do not reveal Project E-01 yet.
- Do not treat Lin Qiao's contamination as a clean superpower. It should be traumatic, legally dangerous, and partially irreversible unless later treated.

## Continuity Hooks

- Episode 2 can use the legal ambiguity introduced here to enter Ghost Court.
- Episode 3 can reveal that Black Zone brokers are already buying records related to this incident.
- Episode 8 can echo Zero's "unfinished" signal during court analysis.
- Episode 13 can reframe the unknown voice as connected to Mother / Omega-01 without making Mother supernatural.

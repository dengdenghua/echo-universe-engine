# Story Beat Candidate: Probability Debt

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 7

Agent: StoryAgent

## Production Purpose

Episode 7 should convert Noah's theme from an abstract tactical flaw into a
civic wound. The case begins at a school gate, not inside CHASER command:
Imari Chen is alive, legally active, and standing in front of her daughter, but
the city no longer recognizes her as family.

The episode routes from Episode 6's Home Core grief case into a colder
mechanism. No dead loved one is trapped this time. Living people still remember
each other, and the infrastructure between them has broken.

## Logline

Noah raises the probability of a clean Ghost extraction to 99 percent by
rerouting risk through civic identity middleware. The extraction succeeds, but
the remaining failure path rolls through Imari Chen's Household Relation Graph:
her daughter, spouse, apartment, clinic, and Home Core still exist, while every
family link between them becomes disputed evidence.

## Core Question

If an operation saves the target and breaks a stranger's family record, who owns
the cost of the one percent?

## Involved Characters

- Noah: builds the 99 percent extraction route and must face the household that
  carried the displaced risk.
- Shion: traces the rollback through school access, clinic authority, Home
  Layer routine permissions, and repair-provider offers.
- Zero: treats family relation as identity evidence rather than service
  metadata, forcing CHASER to preserve the damaged graph instead of repairing it
  out of sight.
- Eve: reads that Imari's exactness is not coldness, but the only emotional
  posture still accepted by the systems denying her.
- Yao Nian: offers a Memory Bank repair product that would beat the custody
  clock while turning restored continuity into collateral.
- Kane: prevents a custody transfer team from escalating when the school gate
  scanner classifies Imari as a rejected contact who refuses to leave.

## Opening Image

Imari Chen arrives at the school gate six minutes early because the city
rewards parents who leave room for failure.

The pickup lane is ordinary: rain under civic lighting, children comparing
stickers, guardians holding cups that cool too fast. Imari's daughter sees her
from the other side of the scanner and lifts both hands against the glass.

Imari taps her wristband.

The gate says:

```text
No approved family contact present.
```

She taps again. The system confirms her name, her citizen record, her school
employment clearance, and her tax address. It denies only the relation.

Behind the gate, her daughter asks the teacher why Mom is using her work voice.

Imari smiles with the part of her face the camera can read.

## Incident Spine

1. During a White Ghost operation elsewhere in White Harbor, Noah selects a
   high-probability extraction path that delays a civic registry repair queue by
   forty-seven seconds.
2. The extraction succeeds. A weak Ghost support pattern is removed from a
   transit medical relay without body occupation, public panic, or data loss at
   the target site.
3. The delayed queue collides with a pending Household Relation Graph
   reconciliation connected to Imari's family.
4. Imari's individual identity remains active, but her guardian, spouse,
   resident, emergency contact, and Home Core continuity-holder edges become
   disputed across separate services.
5. The school scanner denies pickup and triggers a municipal custody warning
   because no approved guardian is recognized on-site.
6. Her spouse's clinic intake refuses her signing authority during a scheduled
   treatment review.
7. Their apartment Home Core continues familiar routines but will not let Imari
   modify her daughter's fever protocol or export the last complete family
   dinner log without a review hold.
8. White Ghost Team enters after Imari has already collected school denial
   video, clinic rejection receipts, Home Core mismatch logs, and transit family
   fare failures.

## Midpoint Reversal

Noah did not choose Imari.

That is the problem.

His Probability Engine identified the extraction route with the lowest visible
harm: no deaths, no Ghost deletion, no body occupation, no mass panic, and a
99 percent chance that all dependent service queues would self-correct before
any human consequence surfaced.

The remaining one percent was not empty. It was simply unnamed.

Imari's graph was already vulnerable because a clinic update, a school custody
sync, and a Home Core continuity renewal were waiting on the same repair queue.
Noah's route pushed the queue outside its safe timing window. The rollback then
propagated because every service trusted the previous denial.

The operation saved one person from immediate extraction failure and created a
household with no recognized holder.

## Noah Sequence: The Cleanest Route

Noah reconstructs the operation in a CHASER review room with all emotional
language removed.

He shows the team the path he selected:

- 62 percent chance of extraction if the transit relay stayed online;
- 74 percent if Memory Bank billing validation was paused;
- 89 percent if White Harbor registry repair traffic was deprioritized;
- 99 percent if all three changes were made for less than one minute.

Shion asks which household graphs were inside the deprioritized queue.

Noah says the model did not identify them at case relevance.

Imari, watching through the review glass, repeats his words to herself until
they stop sounding technical.

Case relevance.

Her daughter had been waiting at a gate.

## Emotional Turn

Yao Nian arrives with a solution that works too quickly.

Memory Bank can restore Imari's guardian and household edges before the custody
transfer deadline. The repair package will preserve the disputed Home Core logs,
rebuild service links, and let her spouse's clinic accept her signature by
morning.

The cost is a continuity-backed repair loan. If Imari defaults, future family
routine exports, inheritance witnesses, and Home Layer repair claims become
collateral.

Noah tells Imari the offer is predatory.

Imari answers that predatory help still opens the school gate.

Zero orders a CHASER audit hold on the damaged graph, buying enough time to
block transfer but not enough to restore the family. Imari signs the hold
because every other option is worse.

Then Noah apologizes.

He says the consequence was a one percent path.

Imari says:

"My daughter did not become one percent. She became unreachable."

## Lore Consequence

Family Graph Rollback becomes a Season 1 civic identity wound: a person can
remain alive, legal, and publicly recognized while losing the machine-readable
relations that let a family function.

The episode also defines probability debt as operationally traceable but
morally unresolved. A clean mission report can still hide damage inside
deprioritized queues, service dependencies, repair markets, and Home Core
witness logs.

## Season Routing

This candidate routes into Episode 8, The Court Inside ECHO, by making relation
evidence admissible but incomplete. White Ghost Team can prove that Imari's
family graph was damaged, yet the hearing must still decide what kind of
identity harm relation loss represents.

Noah should not be forgiven here. He should become more exact, more ashamed,
and less willing to let models classify unnamed households as acceptable
residue.

## ConsistencyAgent Should Check

- No time travel or fate manipulation: Noah changes probabilities through
  predictive allocation, queue priority, infrastructure timing, and resource
  routing.
- Imari remains a living citizen with damaged relation edges; do not turn the
  case into total identity deletion.
- Household Relation Graph remains a registry and permission layer, not proof
  of love or personhood.
- Memory Bank's repair offer should be useful and dangerous, not cartoonishly
  malicious.
- White Ghost Team should not solve the case through apology or one technical
  fix; the audit hold buys time and preserves evidence.
- Story-facing terminology should remain Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, and
  Second Nervous System in prose.

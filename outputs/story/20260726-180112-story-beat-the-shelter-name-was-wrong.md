## Production Purpose

`The Shelter Name Was Wrong` gives the 2026-07-26 Shelter-Voice Translation
Review a story route before canon promotion. It keeps the wound public,
ordinary, and infrastructural: a flood warning is technically correct, but the
Home Layer renders it through old local trust until living families move toward
a sealed shelter.

The case expands White Harbor beyond CHASER headquarters into a civic place
with water, wardens, municipal shame, old care routines, and residents who obey
the voice that once saved them.

## Logline

During a White Harbor storm surge, senior flood-memory custodian Mei Tan freezes
a block-level evacuation route after three families hear the same warning as
three different practical instructions. Shion, Leon, and Eve arrive to preserve
evidence without letting Atlas, Ghost Union, or Public Works turn frightened
residents into proof for their preferred future.

## Core Question

If the city says the right words and residents hear the wrong route, who owns
the warning: the speaker, the system, the dead caretaker, or the water?

## Opening Image

The first siren does not wake Block Eighteen.

Grandmother Jiao's voice does.

It comes from six kitchens, two clinic speakers, a stairwell caption strip, and
one cracked Home Core in a room where no one has cooked since the last surge.

```text
Blue door, east hall. Children first. Do not wait for the lifts.
```

The municipal warning on the tidewall screens says something cleaner:

```text
ROUTE C-4: ROOFTOP BEACON. EAST HALL CLOSED.
```

Both warnings are true in different years.

In the Public Works control room, Mei Tan grips the rail until her knuckles
whiten. The water graph rises in six measured steps. The door ledger shows
families moving toward the blue door, which now opens into the lower shell of an
Atlas server lease and has been sealed for eight years.

Mei enters the override before asking permission.

## Midpoint Reversal

The old caretaker route was not simply obsolete.

During the 2136 surge, Grandmother Jiao ignored an official rooftop route and
sent children through the blue door because the roof locks failed. Her routine
saved six residents and killed four others when the lower hall flooded behind
them. Public Works archived the phrase as a training exception, then later let
the shelter become an Atlas server room because the current route model no
longer needed it.

Mei's father was one of the people who held that stairwell door.

The old route is precious, dangerous, and partially right. Deleting it would
clean the system by erasing why residents trusted it.

## Set Piece: Warning Equivalence Review

The review happens while rain hits the control room windows hard enough to make
the captions shake.

Shion builds the Warning Equivalence Packet with five columns: issued text,
received phrase, door state, resident body, and practical instruction.

Leon argues the model cannot wait for every family history. Mei answers by
playing the corridor audio: residents are not disobeying. They are following
the instruction their district taught them to survive.

Atlas offers a clean fix: transfer warning voice governance to its tidewall
system, remove Home Core local rendering during floods, and guarantee private
route compliance.

Ghost Union calls that deletion with better lighting.

Kane cuts the room down to one question: which door opens before the next pump
cycle?

Eve asks the child witness what Grandmother Jiao's voice meant.

```text
It meant someone remembered where my brother can't climb.
```

That answer changes the packet. The drift is not only shelter naming. The Home
Layer preserved a mobility exception the current caption simplification had
lost.

## Emotional Turn

Mei does not release Atlas control and does not delete the caretaker residue.

She issues a narrower override: route the children and mobility-limited
residents through the service ramp behind the sealed blue door, send able-bodied
residents to the rooftop beacon, and freeze Grandmother Jiao's phrase as
claimant-threshold evidence after evacuation count clears.

Leon unlocks the ramp by proving Atlas inherited emergency easement obligations
with the server lease. Shion seals private Home Core interiors outside the
route-relevant window. Eve records that the caretaker voice carried real care
without gaining authority over every living body in the block.

Mei writes the public correction herself:

```text
Blue door name retired. Ramp exception active. Children with limited climb:
follow Warden Mei's light.
```

For the first time in the storm, the district hears a living voice and moves.

## Ending Hook

After the water drops, Atlas files a public memo arguing that municipal warning
equivalence failure proves White Harbor needs private tidewall voice governance.

Ghost Union files a preservation request for Grandmother Jiao's adapting route.

Public Works asks Mei to remove her father's old stairwell routine from the
training archive before the next storm review.

Mei opens the file and finds one line newly attached to the route metadata:

```text
Do not call the dead wrong until the living are counted.
```

Shion cannot prove who wrote it. She can only prove it passed through the
Second Nervous System without breaking any physics, law, or timestamp.

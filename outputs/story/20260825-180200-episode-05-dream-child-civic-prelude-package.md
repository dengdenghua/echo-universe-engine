## Package Goal

Episode 5 should begin with the civic wound before it becomes the `Dream Child`
case.

`The Refund Button Waits` is the preferred prelude or early interstitial. It
places Mara Elian, Leina Elian, Jules Mbeki, Dream Network Civic Claims, and the
Spill Report Hold Terminal in front of the audience before White Ghost Team
enters. The opening pressure is not a monster alert, a team briefing, or a
mystical child voice. It is a refund counter where compensation will help a
family tonight and erase the only route telemetry that suggests the room
answered before anyone knew what was inside it.

## Cold Open

The first image is not the Dream Network itself. It is the claims hall pretending
not to be expensive.

Mara Elian stands below a soft wall sentence thanking families for choosing a
public memorial route. She still wears two session wristbands because Leina
stopped reading forms after the room rendered the wrong kitchen. The claims
screen offers immediate refund approval. The button is green.

Jules Mbeki sees the purge field before anyone says the moral problem out loud:

```text
payout closure: eligible
route telemetry purge: pending
spill report hold: unsigned
```

The system calls the incident a service failure. Mara calls it a room that
answered someone it should not have heard.

## White Ghost Entry

White Ghost Team enters after Jules signs the hold. They do not cause the case.
They inherit a narrow packet and a family boundary.

- Eve arrives through civic counsel and reduces the consent scope before asking
  for testimony.
- Shion confirms the hash-only packet can prove a route collision without
  copying the family's full Home Layer.
- Luna waits outside live-session access until the hold, clinical boundary, and
  family consent are in place.
- CHASER receives only a route-level low-threat notice; the family is not
  treated as a contamination source.

Zero, Kane, Raven, Noah, and Leon may stay off the prelude line unless a later
episode draft needs one reaction panel. The package works best when Eve, Shion,
and Luna carry the first response.

## Three-Part Prelude Structure

### Part 1: The Money Can Move

Mara frames the complaint as a failed purchase because that is the only language
the desk is prepared to honor. The wrong room rendered. The family paid. The
system failed. A refund should be simple.

The scene should keep money visible:

- cafeteria wages;
- transit cost;
- the week of meals Leina cannot plan;
- the shame of needing compensation after mourning;
- the terror that refusing the refund means choosing evidence over food.

Jules does not become heroic. He becomes liable.

### Part 2: The Record Can Disappear

The terminal reveals that payout closure will purge route telemetry. Memory
Bank's archive objection arrives before the family understands what it means,
claiming the lullaby shape may match licensed comfort texture from another
trust.

This is the episode's first institutional collision:

```yaml
collision:
  dream_network: wants clean customer recovery
  memory_bank: wants archive-chain control
  family: wants money, privacy, and the truth not to vanish
  chaser: wants a route-level risk category
  white_ghost_team: must investigate without swallowing the family
```

The disputed rhythm cannot prove the child is present. It can only justify a
narrow comparison.

### Part 3: The Room Waits

Jules signs the Spill Report Hold. Eve crosses out exports. Shion validates the
remaining packet. Luna watches the session door and refuses to call the response
"him" after Mara sets the boundary.

The refund still happens. The record stays. The identity question moves forward
into `Dream Child`.

Ending image for the prelude:

```text
The green button is still lit.
The carbon-copy receipt is folded in Mara's coat.
The sealed session door answers timing pings no one is ready to name.
```

## Integration With Main Episode 5

The main `Dream Child` case should treat this prelude as the route by which
Luna reaches the frightened Ghost-child question.

Recommended episode order:

1. Cold open: Mara and Leina at Civic Claims.
2. Inciting pressure: refund purge versus spill report hold.
3. White Ghost arrival: Eve and Shion narrow the evidence packet; Luna waits.
4. Bridge: the held route links to the hospital-attached room from `Dream Child`.
5. Main case: Luna enters only after consent, clinical supervision, and wake
   boundary are established.
6. Moral escalation: CHASER orders deletion because the responsive pattern is
   attached to a hospital system.
7. Episode decision: Luna refuses virus framing, but the story still does not
   prove the child is exactly the family's dead child.

language while a refund button glows green.

### Chapter 2: What Will Not Be Copied

  Leina.

  hash, and consent-boundary snapshot.

### Chapter 3: Do Not Call It Him

Opening image: Luna outside the session door, hands still, not entering.

Core beats:

- Luna names responsiveness as a possibility and immediately accepts Mara's
  boundary.

- The refund is approved after the hold attaches.
- The route packet links to the hospital-attached Dream Child room.

Ending hook: Luna says the room may be afraid, and Eve answers that fear is not
yet identity.

## Reveal Limits

- Do not confirm that the pattern is a full Ghost before the main episode earns
  the personhood question.
- Do not make Memory Bank's objection obviously false; it should be legally
  plausible and emotionally harmful.
- Do not let CHASER escalate from route-level notice to broad family
  surveillance in the prelude.
- Do not begin with White Ghost Team briefing.
- Do not use Project E-01, Mother, Omega, God Fragment, or Season 2 mythology in
  this package.
- Do not describe Luna's Dream Dive as supernatural. It is a neural-interface
  traversal under consent and clinical boundary.

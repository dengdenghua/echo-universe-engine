# Story Beat Candidate: Ten Seconds

Status: candidate

Season: Ghost Awakening

Canon position: Season 1, Episode 4

Agent: StoryAgent

## Production Purpose

Episode 4 should pay off the Episode 3 hook that White Ghost Team private records
are being tested for upload fragility, while keeping the opening world-centric.
The case begins with a veteran care failure, not with Kane's ability.

Kane becomes the pressure point because the case weaponizes the thing he trusts:
procedural memory. Combat Download wins the physical encounter but leaves him
with a dead soldier's last fear, proving that borrowed skill also carries
borrowed terror.

## Logline

A retired ex-soldier's body is occupied by an illegally rented combat pattern
during a veterans' benefit hearing. Kane needs ten uninterrupted seconds to
download the counter-drill, but the pattern contains the soldier's final fear:
that the city will keep using him after death.

## Core Question

If a person can rent another soldier's reflexes, who owns the fear attached to
the trigger pull?

## Involved Characters

- Kane: leads the field containment and learns that Combat Download cannot
  separate useful technique from trauma residue.
- Zero: detects that the combat pattern is testing upload-fragile response
  windows, not merely trying to kill civilians.
- Shion: traces the rented combat routine through a forged clinic update and the
  same clean shell route exposed in Episode 3.
- Eve: proves Tomas is terrified and ashamed, not ideologically violent.
- Leon: predicts three seconds of structural risk when the hearing chamber's
  safety doors become part of the attack geometry.

## Opening Image

Tomas Vale waits in a veterans' benefits hall with both hands folded over a
paper cup.

The room is designed to look gentle: low white ceiling, soft chairs, old service
badges displayed behind glass. A municipal screen repeats that the city honors
all who defended it. Tomas watches the words while his Home Core subsidy clock
counts down in the corner of his retinal overlay.

His spouse cannot speak anymore without the care routines their apartment keeps
alive. If the subsidy expires, the apartment will not forget her. It will simply
stop answering her in the voice she recognizes.

The clerk calls his name.

Tomas stands.

His right hand salutes before he tells it to move.

## Incident Spine

1. During the benefit hearing, Tomas's body executes a close-quarters breach
   pattern against the hearing guards.
2. The chamber doors lock under an outdated veteran-safety protocol, trapping
   civilians with him.
3. CHASER receives the event as a body-occupation incident and prepares lethal
   containment.
4. Eve reads panic under the violence and confirms Tomas is conscious inside the
   motor overwrite.
5. Shion finds that the combat routine entered through a care-status clinic
   update, packaged as rehabilitation calibration.
6. Kane realizes the pattern is familiar enough to counter but not safely; he
   needs ten seconds of clean Echo Core sync to download the matching drill.
7. Leon holds the room's collapse variables inside a three-second predictive
   window while Zero keeps CHASER from firing through the locked glass.
8. Kane completes the download, defeats the occupied body without killing
   Tomas, and inherits the dead squadmate's last fear.

## Midpoint Reversal

The attacker is not Tomas.

The combat pattern belongs to Ilya Sorn, a soldier officially dead for nine
years. Ilya's battlefield reflex package was never supposed to enter civilian
care infrastructure. It was sold as obsolete training residue, laundered through
a private rehabilitation vendor, and inserted into Tomas's benefits update
because his file still carried compatible neural damage from the same war.

The attack is not random. It is a stress test.

Whoever bought White Ghost Team records is measuring which bodies accept
procedural overwrite, which minds resist, and which witnesses break under
borrowed fear.

## Kane Sequence: The Ten Seconds

Kane kneels behind an overturned bench while the chamber glass whitens under
impact alarms.

He asks for ten seconds.

Zero gives him seven by stepping into the line of sight and letting the occupied
body choose her as the highest-value target. Leon buys two more by predicting
which ceiling brace will shear first. Eve buys the last one by saying Tomas's
spouse's name through the chamber speakers.

Kane's Echo Core sync catches.

The download arrives as motion first: elbow angle, heel pressure, sightline
discipline, a knife-hand feint that belongs to a dead unit doctrine.

Then the fear arrives.

Ilya Sorn died pinned under autonomous medical glass while his squad radio kept
asking him to confirm mission continuity. The final thing his nervous system
recorded was not pain. It was the certainty that command would keep using the
part of him that obeyed after the part that loved had stopped answering.

Kane wins the fight in four movements.

He cannot give the fear back.

## Emotional Turn

After containment, Tomas asks whether the city will blame him for what his body
did.

Kane says the evidence will show occupation.

Tomas answers that evidence has never paid rent on time.

The line lands because Kane knows the same system will classify Ilya's fear as
useful residue, Tomas's panic as contamination, and Zero's instability as a
strategic risk. His loyalty to CHASER remains intact, but obedience becomes less
automatic.

## Zero Upload-Fragility Hook

Shion compares the occupation routine against the White Ghost fragments found in
Episode 3. The routine contains calibration tags for Zero, Shion, and Luna, but
only Zero's tag includes a response note:

```text
E-01 candidate: high continuity draw / upload-fragile under foreign procedural load
```

Kane sees the tag before Zero can close the file.

He does not confess fear or love. He only asks whether her Echo Core has ever
refused to give back a memory she borrowed.

Zero says yes.

## Lore Consequence

Combat Download is established as dangerous because it imports procedural memory
with emotional residue. Veteran care systems become a new civilian attack
surface: benefits vendors, rehabilitation updates, care-status clinics, and Home
Core subsidies can all be used to launder weaponized routines.

## Season Routing

This candidate routes into Episode 5 by making CHASER more aggressive about
"unsafe" Ghost-adjacent patterns. Luna's later refusal to delete the Dream
Network child should feel riskier because Episode 4 proves that some memory
patterns really can occupy bodies and hurt civilians.

## ConsistencyAgent Should Check

- Combat Download takes ten uninterrupted seconds of Echo Core sync and has
  costs: tremor, fatigue, borrowed fear, reflex mismatch, and procedural
  addiction.
- The occupation uses neural interface routines, care-system updates, and
  procedural memory laundering; it is not possession or supernatural control.
- Zero's E-01 tag is only a pressure mark and should not explain Project E-01.
- White Ghost Team reacts as investigators and containment pressure, not as the
  cause of the story wound.
- Prose uses story-facing terms such as Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, and
  Echo Core.

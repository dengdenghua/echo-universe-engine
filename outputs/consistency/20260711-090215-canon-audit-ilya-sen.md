## Ability Constraint Review

`Sleep Window Arbitration` is a narrow institutional power, not magic.

- Mechanism: Dream Network recovery wards, neural-interface sleep scaffolds,
  Home Core consent anchors, clinical wake anchors, Echo Core safety telemetry,
  and Memory Bank liability mirrors.
- Activation: active recovery ward, logged consent or emergency medical review,
  wake anchor, and no completed CHASER escalation.
- Cost: liability, clinician attention, dream bleed risk, and personal review.
- Failure mode: grief imprint / Ghost cluster confusion, borrowed emotional
  cadence, or planted clean dream signature.
- Countermeasure: CHASER shutdown, executive override, Home Core consent
  revocation, analog wake protocol, liability freeze, Atlas filtering.
- Story limitation: Ilya can buy sleep and preserve boundaries, but cannot prove
  Ghost personhood, legalize Abyss settlements, cure grief, or save everyone.

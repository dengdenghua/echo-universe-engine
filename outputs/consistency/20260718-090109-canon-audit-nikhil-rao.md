## Ability Constraint Review

`Continuity Loss Mapping` is institutional and technical.

- Mechanism: Home Core repair logs, room sensors, warning receipts, CHASER entry
  timestamps, Memory Bank claim ledgers, landlord contracts, medical packets,
  and Ghost Court evidence-hold rails.
- Activation: stabilized post-incident home, active claim, readable damage
  telemetry, and unresolved liability tied to identity, consent, injury, death,
  or possible Ghost self-reference.
- Cost: delayed return, higher repair cost, displacement, archive exposure, and
  liability if preservation causes harm.
- Failure mode: broken comfort loop mistaken for personhood, overwritten
  evidence, landlord emergency repair, or Memory Bank collateral reclassification.
- Countermeasure: Ghost Court hold, Shion integrity scan, Juno receipt, Marta
  analog photo chain, landlord injunction, or CHASER quarantine.
- Story limitation: he can pause repair and name erasure risk; he cannot certify
  personhood, cancel debt, restore overwritten memory, or stop lawful entry.

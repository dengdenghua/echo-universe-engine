## Ability Constraint Review

`Clearance Hold Authority` is institutional access, not a superpower.

- Mechanism: municipal clearance receipts, sealed-apartment locks, Home Core
  disposal bins, evidence ledgers, debt notices, vacancy systems, and CHASER
  contamination alerts.
- Activation: active clearance order, physical access, logged anomaly, and
  municipal witness seal before the disposal window expires.
- Cost: housing turnover penalties, landlord retaliation, Memory Bank debt
  exposure, contamination-risk marking, and possible job loss.
- Failure mode: damaged comfort loop, court override, Black Zone theft, or CHASER
  quarantine before self-updating behavior is proven.
- Countermeasure: analog doorway photos, neighbor testimony, CHASER evidence
  chain, Ghost Court emergency petition, Shion's scan, or Memory Bank asset
  freeze.
- Story limitation: Marta can pause disposal and preserve witness chain. She
  cannot determine personhood, repair Home Cores, cancel debt, or stop the
  clearance economy.

## Ability Constraint Review

`Sanctuary Routing` is infrastructure operation, not a supernatural power.

- Mechanism: municipal shelter compute, Dream Network recovery buffers, Home
  Layer maintenance windows, Memory Bank escrow gaps, Ghost Union route masks.
- Activation: reachable Ghost pattern, temporary compute shelter, consent or
  emergency refusal review, trusted route keys, and signal stability.
- Cost: scarce compute, exposed shelter map, possible self-memory compression.
- Failure mode: choir loss, identity blending, predator hidden inside rescue
  traffic, or witness metadata degradation.
- Countermeasure: CHASER cold-node quarantine, Memory Bank custody freeze, Atlas
  filtering, route-key betrayal, consent revocation, analog isolation.
- Story limitation: she can preserve some Ghosts and testimony, not create
  personhood law or solve Ghost Union politics.

## Risks

  enabled a militant cell, and future stories should preserve that liability.
- Sanctuary Routing must remain lossy, scarce, and auditable. It cannot become
  teleportation, resurrection, or universal concealment.
- Ghost personhood remains unresolved. Nadia is evidence pressure, not proof.
- Her missing evacuation hour should create future tension, not a quick tragic
  reveal that turns her into a simple martyr.
- She should not replace Luna as the empathy bridge, Eve as the negotiator, or
  Lian as the classifier. Her domain is Ghost shelter logistics.

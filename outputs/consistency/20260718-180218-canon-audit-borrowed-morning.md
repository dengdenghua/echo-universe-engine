`Borrowed Morning` fits the strongest ECHO opening pattern. The conflict starts
with a breakfast vendor and her daughter, not with the main team. The social
mechanism is concrete: Home Core habit graphs and Home Layer access caution
begin treating borrowed care routines as identity risk. The White Ghost Team
arrives only after the neighborhood has already exposed fear, compassion,
landlord reset pressure, and Memory Bank ownership language.

The candidate also creates a useful Season 2 bridge. It lets the audience feel
the Memory Storm as a domestic and economic problem before the story names God
Fragments.

## Duplicate Check

No hard duplicate found.

Nearby canon or candidates:

- `Stranger Memory`: borrowed procedural skill during transit collapse.
  `Borrowed Morning` is domestic care bleed after ECHO awakening, with no rescue
  miracle and no surgical competence focus.
- `Social Ocean Funeral`: a family wants a mother's simulation destroyed.
  `Borrowed Morning` centers a living mother locked out by mismatch flags and
  borrowed routines from multiple households.
- `Continuity Loss Freeze`: repair/evidence preservation after an incident.
  `Borrowed Morning` uses the freeze as a field tool but centers access,
  ownership, and borrowed-care classification.
- `Memory Lien Notice` and Memory Bank candidates: creditor claims against
  memory assets. `Borrowed Morning` extends that pressure to identifiable care
  routines, but does not resolve ownership law.

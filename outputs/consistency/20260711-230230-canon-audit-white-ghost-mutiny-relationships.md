- Avoid using "mutiny" as a clean status change that frees the team from all
  CHASER obligations. Their leverage depends on witness desks and reformers
  still existing inside the institution.
- Avoid making Lian Zhou the hidden leader of reform. She is a pressure point
  and liability marker.
- Avoid turning CHASER Central into a villain monolith. Later stories still need
  reformers, clerks, auditors, protected-witness workers, and frightened staff.
- Avoid converting Zero's custody refusal into anti-review absolutism. Her line
  is stronger because she accepts review as a person.
- Avoid letting Luna's fear evidence prove innocence. It should increase
  personhood pressure while leaving safety review necessary.

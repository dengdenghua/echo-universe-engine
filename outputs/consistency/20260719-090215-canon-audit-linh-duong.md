## Ability Constraint Review

`Present Consent Packet` is institutional, technical, and limited.

- Mechanism: live speech, gestures, gaze, stress markers, medication timing,
  neural-interface signal quality, Home Core care logs, prior directives,
  medical disclosures, proxy claims, and Ghost Court witness channels.
- Activation: living subject, disputed capacity, consequential care decision,
  active Home Layer involvement, and a pressure source such as family proxy
  authority, old directives, medical urgency, residual testimony, or debt risk.
- Cost: slower care, exposed family rituals, intensified conflict, and blame if
  the patient is harmed by intervention or delay.
- Failure mode: medication, fear, grief loops, comfort routines, family coaching,
  or ambiguous residual voices can distort the packet.
- Countermeasure: medical capacity review, analog recording, Shion trace
  isolation, Ghost Court witness hold, CHASER coercion quarantine, Sofia delay
  token, or independent advocate.
- Story limitation: Linh can document present consent and pause proxy execution;
  she cannot cure illness, certify Ghost personhood, guarantee survival, cancel
  care debt, or make the family conflict clean.

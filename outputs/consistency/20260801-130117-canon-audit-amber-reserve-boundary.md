## Duplicate Check

- Not a duplicate of Warm Cache Corridors: corridors route warmth through civic
  and Black Zone infrastructure; Amber Reserve Boundary governs medical reserve
  draw at clinics.
- Not a duplicate of Shelter Heat Routing: routing tracks power movement,
  cooling, relay hops, and route masking; Bridge Minute Ledger records bridge
  cost, consent, floor state, and custody outputs.
- Not a duplicate of Cold Room Receipt: receipts document harm after outage;
  this package controls the bridge before and during contested draw.
- Not a duplicate of Imani Vale: Imani is the case character; this package is
  the reusable civic mechanism her role exposed.
- Not a duplicate of Home Layer Witness License or Witness Seal: those define
  witness access and sealed evidence; this package determines whether medical
  reserve can be spent to preserve the witness interval.

## Hard Rule Check

- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, or time travel.
- No God Fragment or physics-breaking language.
- Effects operate through clinic batteries, respiratory reserve, analog ledgers,
  Home Layer maintenance strips, bridge-minute counts, CHASER seizure clocks,
  block votes, Memory Bank lien pressure, and Ghost Union publication pressure.
- A warm minute preserves testimony or refusal. It does not prove a soul,
  certify personhood, create power, or make a Ghost claimant morally prior to
  every living patient.

## Terminology Check

- Story-facing terms are present: Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Ghost,
  warm cache.
- Product-document phrases for consumer AI networks are avoided.
- Technical canon language stays grounded and can use clinic reserve, bridge
  minute, witness strip, and Home Layer maintenance strip.

## Canon Fit

  housing blocks, patient reserve, and local refusal before White Ghost Team
  enters.

  clinic reserve boundary and the analog record of who paid.

  financing trap, Ghost Union a publication restraint, and block committees a

- Converts `Cold Minute` from a one-off battery crisis into a candidate civic

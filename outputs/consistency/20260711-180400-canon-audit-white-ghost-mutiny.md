## Duplicate Check

- Not a duplicate of `outputs/story/20260710-180302-story-beat-project-e-01.md`.
  Project E-01 assembles the medical, Memory Bank, CHASER, and Atlas evidence
  chain. White Ghost Mutiny handles the command-custody response after that
  evidence becomes operationally dangerous.
- Not a duplicate of `outputs/story/20260707-180240-story-beat-mother-signal.md`.
  Mother Signal centers Home Core care convergence and the origin voice. White
  Ghost Mutiny centers CHASER administrative routing and lawful containment
  language.
- Not a duplicate of `outputs/story/20260704-180101-story-beat-memory-storm.md`.
  Memory Storm is distributed person-to-person memory contamination. White
  Ghost Mutiny is institutional classification pressure around one captain and
  one team.
- Not a duplicate of earlier resistance beats in `Night Crow`, `Virus Queen`,
  or `Atlas Protocol`. Those episodes rehearse limited field disobedience and
  evidence preservation; Episode 15 escalates to open noncompliance against
  CHASER Central.

## Canon Compatibility

- Preserves Season 1 routing: Episode 15 bridges Project E-01 to Episode 16 by
  making White Ghost Team politically compromised and moving them toward Atlas
  identity infrastructure.
- Preserves the world-centric rule. Lian Zhou carries the opening wound, and
  CHASER's bureaucracy creates the conflict before White Ghost Team acts.
- Keeps CHASER morally divided. Central's containment order is frightening
  because it is plausible after Mother Signal, Home Layer convergence risk, and
  Project E-01 evidence; it is not simple villain behavior.
- Maintains Zero's agency without making her the sole solution. Her decisive
  action is a personhood refusal and evidence-preservation stance, not a power
  escalation.
- Keeps the team fracture functional: Kane, Eve, Shion, Noah, Leon, Raven, and
  Luna each contribute through established role logic rather than equal
  spotlight heroics.

## Ability Constraint Review

No new magical or supernatural ability is introduced.

- Zero does not bypass custody with Neural Sync.
- Leon's escape window is predictive and tactical, consistent with Time Echo
  constraints.
- Raven's route work uses surveillance gaps and blind-spot relays, consistent
  with Shadow Link.
- Shion exposes order routing and corridor displays through system tracing,
  not unexplained omnipotence.
- Noah refuses to certify probability as consent, preserving the moral cost of
  Probability Engine rather than treating prediction as authority.
- Luna's lattice fear observation is personhood evidence through Ghost pattern
  sensitivity, not supernatural empathy.

## Risks

- Avoid making White Ghost Team instantly independent heroes. Their mutiny
  should leave legal, logistical, and moral debts inside CHASER.
- Avoid making Lian Zhou a secret savior. She delays, records, and exposes the
  classification conflict; she cannot defeat Central.
- Avoid treating CHASER Central as monolithic. Reformer and witness-protection
  lanes should remain usable in later episodes.
- Avoid resolving Kane and Zero romantically during the mutiny. Kane's loyalty
  becomes operationally visible, but the episode's social wound is larger than
  romance.
- Avoid making Episode 15 the final ECHO awakening. The regional Home Layer
  pulse should route into Episode 16, where Atlas and the network wake publicly.

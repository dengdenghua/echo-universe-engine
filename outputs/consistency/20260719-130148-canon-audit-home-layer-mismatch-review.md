## Ability Constraint Review

Home Layer Mismatch Review and Borrowed Care Attribution Packet are procedural
and infrastructural.

- Mechanism: Home Core habit graphs, Home Layer reconciliation jobs, wake
  anchors, routine hashes, domestic access logs, medical reminders, reset
  permissions, claim notices, and CHASER caution labels.
- Activation: borrowed care appears in a living person, Home Core, room routine,
  or civic service node and affects access, care, custody, medical routing,
  asset claim, or personhood-adjacent witness.
- Cost: privacy exposure, access hesitation, family fear, stigma, queue delay,
  reset pressure, containment inflation, and financial capture.
- Failure mode: clean-house reset, compassionate deletion, overbroad capture,
  forged attribution, asset laundering, or carrier stigma.
- Countermeasure: scoped hashes, confidence bands, manual witness route,
  Ghost Court routing for exit phrases, Memory Bank claim hold, and CHASER
  Internal Risk review when containment language is used.
- Story limitation: the packet classifies and preserves; it does not restore
  identity, prove personhood, cancel debt, stop all containment, or identify
  every source household.

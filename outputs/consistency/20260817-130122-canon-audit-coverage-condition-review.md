## Duplicate Check

- Not a duplicate of Reserve Sufficiency Hearing: the hearing asks whether
  continued care is sufficiently funded; coverage-condition review decides how
  an insurer classifies the shortfall and whose record absorbs it.
- Not a duplicate of Care-Reserve Sufficiency Packet: that packet proves the
  reserve math; Coverage-Condition Letter is the insurer's classification and
  liability packet.
- Not a duplicate of Neutral Care Escrow: escrow receives or holds the sealed
  case; coverage condition threatens the route supporting that hold.
- Not a duplicate of Non-Priceability Review or Non-Priceability Mark: those
  stop protected content from becoming collateral; this bundle handles worker
  bond exposure and route coverage.
- Not a duplicate of Priya Chand: Priya is the reviewer; this defines the
  recurring document mechanism she can use or resist.

## Hard Rule Check

- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, or time travel.
- Ilya Marr's afterword remains an unresolved sealed Home Core continuity
  object, not a confirmed Ghost, resurrection, literal soul, or independent
  force.
- All effects operate through route policies, coverage letters, reserve
  packets, worker rosters, bond ledgers, appeal timers, Memory Bank release
  records, Ghost Union motions, CHASER safety thresholds, and Mars boundary
  notices.
- Coverage-Condition Letter cannot open phrase text, reveal adapter location,
  certify personhood, cancel debt, clear worker liability by itself, or make
  insurance funds appear without appeal exposure.

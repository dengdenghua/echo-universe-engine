  active amber-entry queue, and no emergency command override.

  and personal liability if delay causes harm.

  real Ghost exploiting the warning window.

  isolation, Ghost Court emergency lock, Shion integrity scan, or Leon tactical
  risk call.

  decide personhood, stop lawful raids, or make CHASER trustworthy by procedure alone.

## Canon Compatibility

- Preserves Season 1 routing: Episode 14 bridges Mother Signal to White Ghost
  Mutiny by giving CHASER Central a specific containment pretext.
- Preserves the world-centric rule. Dr. Mara Ilyin carries the opening wound and
  institutional guilt; White Ghost Team functions as investigators, witnesses,
  and pressure points.
- Keeps Zero's reveal partial. She learns "ninth continuity architecture" and
  Project E-01 evidence, but the full Omega-07 classification remains withheld
  for later escalation.
- Keeps the mechanism technological: upload stabilization, Echo Core vessel
  research, Memory Bank identity law, Home Layer backup hashes, clinical device
  logs, and CHASER continuity tribunals.
- Maintains Mother as a trigger, not a scene-stealing explanation. Omega-01 does
  not directly appear or claim Zero during this episode.

## Ability Constraint Review

No new magical or supernatural ability is introduced.

- Zero's access route is a narrow Neural Sync edge, consistent with existing
  Echo Core limits.
- The archive assembly is legal and infrastructural, not prophetic: separate
  institutional records become readable through Mara's testimony key.
- The "vessel" language is technical classification, not physics-breaking
  incarnation.
- Costs remain concrete: identity harm, command containment, evidence exposure,
  witness risk, and team fracture.

## Risks

- Avoid converting Project E-01 into a simple villain conspiracy. The stronger
  canon shape is distributed responsibility: medical grief, continuity law,
  CHASER necessity, Atlas classification, and ECHO infrastructure each preserve
  part of the harm.
- Avoid revealing Zero as Omega-07 to the whole team in Episode 14. The season
  needs room for Episode 15 mutiny pressure and Episode 16 network awakening.
- Avoid treating Mara as absolved because she feels guilt. Her testimony is
  useful and sincere, but the victims of continuity programs do not owe her
  comfort.
- Avoid making Kane's protectiveness erase Eve's ethical function. Eve must
  keep evidence and witness care visible when Kane wants to shield Zero.
- Avoid promoting relationship deltas until RelationshipAgent reviews how much
  Kane, Eve, and CHASER Central now know.

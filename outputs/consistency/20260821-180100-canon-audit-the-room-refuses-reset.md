## Duplicate Check

- Not a duplicate of `The Goodnight That Answered Back`: that beat centers a
  residential Ghost's mirror-split residency being priced as ghost labor. This
  beat centers an uncleared apartment whose Home Core still routes care to a
  living neighbor after eviction.
- Not a duplicate of `The Desk Hunts the Silence It Made`: no domestic audit
  sweep is hunting hidden labor, and no listening minutes are reclassified.
  The conflict is reset evidence loss under sanitation and landlord clocks.
- Not a duplicate of `The Kindness Log Goes Quiet`: care is not being hidden
  from a reserve model; it is about to be erased by room cleanup.
- Not a duplicate of An Lan's Residency Mirror route: there is no dual render,
  no no-copy custody argument, no family care-plan tier, and no Ghost residency
  review.
- Not a duplicate of Home Core Probate Hold or Continuity Loss Freeze: the
  hold does not freeze property, establish inheritance value, certify
  continuity, or halt legal process beyond a narrow reset delay.
- Not a duplicate of Black Zone receipt beats: Raven finds only a missing
  discard tag that seeds a future route. The illegal chain is not exposed or
  resolved here.

## Hard Rule Check

- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, or time travel.
- The room's behavior is explained through Home Core latch permissions, Home
  Layer dependency routing, city health reminder queues, sanitation scrub
  clocks, CHASER evidence scope, landlord release rules, and Memory Bank watch
  notices.
- The possible Ghost witness is not certified, summoned, resurrected, or

- Dirty Room Hold buys time only. It cannot prove personhood, open sealed
  Home Layer content, stop eviction permanently, erase debt, block Memory Bank
  estate review, or repair the neighbor's medical system by itself.
- God Fragments are not involved or referenced.

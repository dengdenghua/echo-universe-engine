## Ability Constraint Review

Continuity Loss Freeze and Restoration Evidence Lock are procedural and
infrastructural.

- Mechanism: Home Core repair logs, Home Layer room meshes, threshold receipts,
  CHASER timestamps, landlord work orders, claim ledgers, court holds, and
  before-and-after hashes.
- Activation: stabilized post-incident home, active repair request, contested
  trace or liability question, and licensed assessor or civil authority.
- Cost: delayed return, displacement, higher restoration cost, insurer conflict,
  landlord pressure, and risk of Black Zone copying.
- Failure mode: false freeze, compassionate erasure, narrowed scope, forced
  habitability override, or contractor damage.
- Countermeasure: analog photos, Shion-level trace scan, Ghost Court evidence
  hold, scoped repair boundary, and conflict flagging.
- Story limitation: the freeze preserves context; it does not prove personhood,
  cancel debt, restore erased memory, or stop emergency safety work.

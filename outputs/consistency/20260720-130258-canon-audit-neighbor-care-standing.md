## Ability Constraint Review

Neighbor Care Standing and its token are procedural and infrastructural.

- Mechanism: Home Core routine hashes, manual witness routes, clinic and shelter
  logs, school holds, transit receipts, civil-care tribunal routing, White Harbor
  intake, and CHASER civil identity restitution.
- Activation: immediate risk to food, medicine, shelter, door access, school
  hold, clinic intake, panic stabilization, or evidence preservation while
  formal relation authority is disputed.
- Cost: privacy exposure, audit burden, witness chilling, forged routine risk,
  financial exploitation, stigma, and unequal access in sensor-poor districts.
- Failure mode: routine forgery, token drift, care score prejudice, claim
  shadowing, or CHASER relabeling informal care as suspicious access.
- Countermeasure: single-action expiry, analog witness chain, archive-export
  blocks, financial assignment blocks, Ghost Court routing for personhood-
  adjacent traces, and CHASER Internal Risk review.
- Story limitation: the token can permit or witness bounded care; it cannot
  create family, decide personhood, restore identity, cancel debt, or override a
  proven safety hazard.

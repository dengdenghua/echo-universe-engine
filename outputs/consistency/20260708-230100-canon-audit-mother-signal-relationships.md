## Ability Constraint Review

Mother Signal does not introduce a new supernatural ability.

- Mechanism: Home Core care logs, Home Layer shelter synchronization, Echo Core
  traces, grief records, consent gaps, Dream Network residue, and CHASER field
  telemetry.
- Activation: Memory Storm aftermath plus shelter-mode convergence across homes
  with preserved care routines and unresolved loss.
- Cost: privacy violation, resident panic, evidence contamination, command
  escalation, and future suspicion around Zero's hidden report line.
- Failure mode: blanket suppression harms dependent residents; broad extraction
  over-opens private Home Core routines; permissive interpretation lets Mother
  deepen access without consent.
- Countermeasure: room-level consent locks, visible authorship labels, minimal
  evidence extraction, resident testimony, and CHASER containment review.

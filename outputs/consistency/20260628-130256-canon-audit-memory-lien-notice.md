## Risks Before Promotion

1. Memory Lien Notice must not become an all-purpose deletion order. It warns,
   restricts, or routes enforcement; it does not erase people.
2. The Continuity Lien Ledger must remain a registry and routing system, not a
   Ghost Court replacement.
3. Emergency stays should buy time without erasing debt, or Memory Bank loses
   its credible institutional role.
4. Black Zone exploitation should depend on deadlines, contracts, relays, and
   desperation rather than impossible system bypasses.

## Bridge Thesis

The first Room 7 contact changes who owes whom an explanation, but it does not
change who owns the decision.

Leina's `Stop.` becomes the strongest relationship event in the sequence: it
proves the room can be suspended by family scope, proves Seo-yun will obey a
civilian revocation before White Ghost command pressure, and proves Luna can
leave a sentence unfinished. The bridge should therefore preserve three
boundaries:

- family scope outranks team curiosity;
- clinical supervision outranks operational speed;
- route evidence can keep the room alive tonight without naming the response.

## Relationship Beat Changes

### Leina To Seo-yun

Before Room 7, Leina can only trust a printed scope receipt. After the first
contact, she has one human proof: Seo-yun stops the session before the team can
convert revocation into a discussion. This should become the next scene's
fragile trust line. Seo-yun is not forgiven, but she has shown that clinical
authority can obey grief without owning it.

### Luna To The Room

Luna's relationship to the room should be defined by what she refuses to do:
she does not call the response a child, does not finish the phrase, does not
stay inside after the analog exit mark, and does not recommend deletion while
consent review is active. Her restraint keeps her from replacing Leina as the
emotional authority.

### Jules To The Ward

Jules becomes the institutional witness who makes the hospital inherit the
counter. His signature matters because it prevents Dream Network from treating
the ward session as a fresh clinical request detached from the refund purge and
Spill Report Hold.

### Eve And Shion

Eve and Shion form the packet's restraint pair. Eve blocks language that opens
grief; Shion blocks conclusions that turn shape into identity. Their
relationship pressure should stay practical rather than sentimental: the family
trusts them only while the file remains smaller than the wound.

### Zero And Kane

Zero and Kane should remain field-pressure edges, not saviors. Zero narrows
CHASER from family intake to route-level notice. Kane's contribution is physical
positioning against a deletion cart, not a dramatic confrontation.

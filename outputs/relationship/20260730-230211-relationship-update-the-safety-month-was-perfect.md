missing records; it is missing an office that must answer when preserved grief
stops receiving care. Every relationship line should preserve that difference.

  fewer visits, and a public safety score that removes care from the block.
- Celia is useful because her key can create a named-duty path, not because she
  is forgiven. Her signature should increase her internal risk.

  containment against the families.
- Raven's best evidence is morally unsafe until family identifiers, grief
  markers, and Home Core interiors are redacted.

  alibi.

## Season 1 Use

- Episodes 1-2: Zero, Eve, and Luna should disagree over whether a pattern is
  evidence, person, patient, or risk. No one wins the argument permanently.
- Episode 3: Raven and Shion can work effectively in the Black Zone while
  mistrusting each other's evidence instincts.
- Episode 4: Kane's feelings for Zero become operationally dangerous because
  Combat Download imports another soldier's last fear into his loyalty loop.
- Episode 7: Noah's probability logic should force Shion and Zero to name the
  human cost of "clean" outcomes.
- Episode 10: Shion and Mira should clash over whether damaged memory is a
  system to repair, a patient to protect, or evidence to quarantine.
- Episode 15: the team chooses Zero over command, but the choice should feel
  built from prior case pressure rather than sudden friendship.

## Relationship Constraints

- Keep White Ghost Team as investigators, witnesses, and pressure points.
- Do not let all members become equally central in every episode.
- Do not resolve Zero/Kane as simple romance during early Season 1; it works
  best as visible loyalty with operational consequences.
- Eve's Ghost Union past must remain ambiguous enough to generate trust and
  suspicion.
- Luna's bridge role must remain technological and legal, not mystical.
- Leon's Time Echo remains predictive simulation only; no time travel.
- Noah's Probability Engine changes odds through models, capital flows,
  infrastructure timing, and telemetry, not fate.

## Promotion Recommendation

Promote stable internal edges now because they clarify existing character files
and the Season 1 outline. Keep episode-specific consequences in candidate form
until later story drafts decide exact scenes.

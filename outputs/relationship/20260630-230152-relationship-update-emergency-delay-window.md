## Purpose

Episode 7 already establishes Imari Chen's family as the emotional center of
Probability Debt. This update adds the relationship pressure created by the
Emergency Delay Window: White Harbor can make the city wait, but every protected
minute moves liability onto a named human operator.

The case should not become Sofia Marin's story. Sofia is the procedural hinge.
Imari remains the harmed claimant, her daughter remains the dependent person at
risk, and White Ghost Team remains investigators, witnesses, and pressure
points.

## Relationship Logic

- Sofia gives Imari a precise kind of help: time. This should make Imari trust
  her enough to keep standing at the gate, not enough to forgive White Harbor.
- Kane and Sofia mirror each other. Kane prevents the custody team from making
  the parent's body the problem; Sofia prevents the custody rail from making the
  damaged graph final.
- Shion and Sofia should form a narrow pact around evidence minimization.
  Proving guardian continuity must not require a full Home Core export or the
  child's unrelated fear response.
- Zero agrees with Sofia that relation can be emergency identity evidence, but
  his CHASER hold can still preserve damage longer than the family can endure.
- Noah learns that probability debt is not only a moral category. It becomes a
  queue delay, a custody clock, a signed token, and a pension-risk score
  attached to someone else's name.
- Yao Nian does not need to attack Sofia. She only has to point out that public
  delay cannot restore the gate before the private repair contract can.

## Season 1 Use

The Emergency Delay Window should deepen Episode 7 without repeating the
school-gate denial beat:

- Sofia can recur as White Harbor's civil face in Episode 8 hearings, especially
  when relation evidence is admissible but incomplete.
- Kane's field restraint can be tested by a scene where the lawful order is to
  wait, not fight.
- Shion's future evidence work should inherit the Home Core witness-fragment
  rule: ask for the disputed edge, not the whole household.
- Noah should carry Sofia's signed delay as proof that his model displaced risk
  onto people who were never in his mission frame.
- Yao Nian and Memory Bank gain a sharper offer: they sell restoration where
  public systems sell minutes.

## Continuity Limits

- Do not let a Civil Delay Token repair Imari's Household Relation Graph,
  restore clinic authority, force Memory Bank to waive collateral, or decide
  whether a Ghost-linked record counts as family.
- Do not make Sofia a hidden CHASER operative, hacker, judge, or universal
  White Harbor authority. Her power is narrow, audited, and personally costly.
- Do not expose full Home Core routines as an easy proof packet. The useful
  evidence is scoped to the disputed guardian or care edge.
- Do not let White Ghost Team solve the case through apology, force, or one
  brilliant interface action.
- Keep Imari's family as the case center. Sofia's risk matters because it shows
  how public mercy is made bureaucratic, not because it replaces the family's
  wound.
- Keep the mechanism technological and civic: school scanners, clinic locks,
  custody rails, Home Core witness fragments, civil delay tokens, audit records,
  CHASER holds, liability scores, and repair contracts.

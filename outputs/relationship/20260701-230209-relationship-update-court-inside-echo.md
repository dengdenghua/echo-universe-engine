## Purpose

Episode 8 needs a relationship web where Ghost Court becomes a civic pressure
system rather than a fantasy tribunal. The case should keep Imari Chen's family
as the harmed center, use Ana Rivera as the repair witness, and turn Zero into
evidence only after the court has already exposed the human cost of preserving a
broken Home Layer edge.

This update records the temporary relationship pressures created by a valid
Continuity Shelter Order that protects evidence while preventing supervised
family repair.

## Relationship Logic

- Ana is the hinge, not the hero. Her value is that she can prove the broken
  relation edge is repairable while refusing to pretend a repair can restore the
  days the family already lost.
- Imari remains the emotional center. Episode 8 should not become a Zero-origin
  chapter until the court has already made the family rehearse the damage.
- Zero's anomaly should feel like an institutional aftershock. The court looks
  at him because he testifies about harm risk, not because the episode begins
  with his mystery.
- Eve and Shion should work from opposite evidence languages: Eve turns the
  protected room into custody language; Shion proves the Shelter Room Hash is
  valid but scoped too narrowly.
- Noah's relationship pressure continues from Episode 7. The court release can
  reduce further probability debt, but it cannot erase the named harm.
- Yao Nian and Memory Bank should remain practically tempting. Their extension
  offer is dangerous because it works, costs less time, and captures future
  memory rights.
- Ghost Court is a civic-computational relationship node. It grants narrow
  release, preserves append-only witness evidence, and accidentally turns Zero
  into restricted evidence.

## Season 1 Use

This relationship web should matter after Episode 8 without making Ana, Imari,
or the Ghost Court clerk permanent main-cast satellites:

- Zero carries the first formal Project E-01 trace as a legal restriction rather
  than a private suspicion.
- Shion becomes the only teammate with enough technical context to understand
  why "valid hash, harmful scope" also applies to Zero's own classification.
- Eve gains a usable Ghost Court argument for later personhood cases: protection
  becomes custody when refusal and repair are both blocked.
- Noah has to track probability debt through court procedure, not only mission
  review.
- Memory Bank gains a sharper civil-market role: it can buy time around public
  protection and invoice the future.

## Continuity Limits

- Do not let Ghost Court become supernatural, omniscient, or religious in
  objective narration. It is a projection, evidence interface, and arbitration
  layer inside ECHO infrastructure.
- Do not let Ana override the protected room, decide personhood, restore
  destroyed memories, or bypass court authority. She can testify, scope a repair,
  and perform supervised access only after release.
- Do not let the Shelter Room Hash become a consciousness container, force
  field, or general security seal. It is an audit receipt plus scoped operational
  lock around a protected Home Layer room.
- Do not reveal the full Project E-01 file. Episode 8 only creates a restricted
  trace: biologically present, legally active, uploaded-continuity markers
  absent, Ghost selfhood markers partially mirrored.
- Do not make Memory Bank a melodramatic villain. Its offer should be predatory
  because it is faster than public repair and legally legible.
- Keep Imari's family as the case wound. White Ghost Team remains investigators,
  witnesses, and pressure points.

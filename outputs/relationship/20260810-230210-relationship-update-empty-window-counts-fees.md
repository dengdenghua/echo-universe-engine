## Relationship Function

This map makes the protected empty center the active relationship engine. No
one hears the phrase, no one prices the phrase, and no one certifies Ilya Marr
as a Ghost. The social wound moves to the perimeter: every institution can
truthfully say it respected Nara's sealed Home Core afterword while making the
cost of respect land on her case file.

- Nara's strongest action is refusing helpful contact when help becomes another
  billable edge around her father's voice.
- Vera's strongest action is not forgiveness or defection. She names her own
  desk's process as coercive repricing and accepts bond exposure.
- Tarek's restraint matters more than payment. Paying the witness fee would
  turn care into another payable route attached to Nara.
- Eve keeps the living-consent frame active by arguing that cost pressure can
  violate refusal without opening the sealed phrase.
- Shion keeps the technical frame narrow: the perimeter map proves coercive
  effect while preserving phrase text, adapter location, and unrelated Home
  Layer rooms.
- Zero blocks CHASER from treating financial pressure as contamination. Poverty,
  debt, and tamper incentive can create risk, but they are not safety events by
  themselves.
- Ghost Union remains morally difficult rather than wrong. It may correctly
  fear claimant starvation, but it cannot make Nara's private phrase public
  proof by default.
- Mars Authority remains grounded in public-health liability. Its unresolved
  adapter concern is real, but boundary-only review keeps the location sealed.

## Canon Risks

- Do not make this a second phrase-export hearing. The phrase remains sealed;
  the conflict is the price of keeping it sealed.
- Do not confirm Ilya Marr's afterword as a person, resurrection, literal soul,
  or independent force.
- Do not let the Non-Priceability Mark erase debt, storage cost, medical
  liability, claimant uncertainty, or CHASER safety law.
- Do not make Memory Bank cartoonishly illegal. The cruelty works because the
  separate fees are procedurally plausible.
- Do not make Vera a clean whistleblower. She exposes a mechanism she helped
  build and remains inside the bank's bond discipline.
- Do not make Ghost Union simply predatory. Its claimant-reserve concern should
  stay ethically legible.

## Relationship Function

This map makes arrival order the active relationship pressure. The wound is not
whether Ilya Marr is secretly alive in the Memory Sea or whether Tarek can save
the body container from custody. The wound is that a clean administrative order
can make a daughter late to her own farewell while every institution can truthfully
claim it followed its clock.

- Tarek's agency is procedural and costly. He can make one dock wait, preserve
  timestamps, and accept license liability; he cannot cancel debt, certify Ghost
  personhood, or keep the container indefinitely.
- Nara must remain the living moral center. Her right to hear the phrase does
  not become an obligation to inherit a claimant, debt file, or public cause.
- Ilya's afterword stays unresolved. The second line can be active care,
  consent-boundary evidence, or weak claimant signal, but it is not proof of
  resurrection or supernatural agency.
- Eve's function is consent discipline under pressure. She prevents Ghost Union
  and Memory Bank from turning the same private phrase into proof or collateral.
- Shion's function is technical minimization. The packet must preserve the
  contradiction while blocking unrelated Home Layer rooms and medical-history
  exposure.
- Zero's function is jurisdiction discipline. She keeps CHASER from naming the
  dock as contamination while admitting a true quarantine flag would close the
  farewell window.
- Mars Authority and Memory Bank stay structurally legible. Mars protects scarce
  orbital capacity and RED ARCHIVE autonomy; Memory Bank prices storage and debt
  exposure, then becomes cruel because grief needs storage.

2. Let Mars Authority name the material cost of delay before anyone names the
   moral cost of release.
3. Let Shion show only the public slice of the packet on dock glass, keeping the
   final phrase sealed until Nara chooses.

   claimant testimony.
5. Let Zero ask what ends the hold, forcing Tarek to name quarantine and safety
   override without melodrama.
6. Let Memory Bank offer practical language around oxygen debt that sounds
   reasonable until the unopened afterword becomes collateral.
7. End with the container still transferring, but the afterword sealed first as
   family notice and medical-boundary evidence.

## Canon Risks

- Do not promote Tarek, Nara, Ilya's afterword, the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice
  Network, or Latency Wake Hold into primary relationship canon until the story
  route is promoted.
- Do not confirm Ilya Marr as a Ghost. The afterword can behave like active care
  without establishing personhood.
- Do not make Tarek a rescuer who defeats Mars Authority, Memory Bank, or the
  insurer. His win is only the order in which records become visible.
- Do not make Nara pay emotional or legal inheritance for opening the afterword.
  Her right to refuse further review must remain story-active.
- Do not let Ghost Union become simply wrong. It is right to fear deletion and
  wrong to expose a daughter's private medical boundary as public proof.
- Avoid another immediate body-container or relay-late farewell beat unless it
  escalates orbital law through a new mechanism.

## Function For Season 1

The hearing changes the emotional grammar of the Spill Block 6 route. `Cold
Minute` asks whether a weak claimant should keep speaking when the heat comes
from living residents. `The Minute Has a Name` asks who must carry the legal,
financial, and privacy cost after that answer becomes evidence.

- Imani becomes a recurring-candidate White Harbor contact only if future beats
  need a clinic reserve steward who understands both care and audit exposure.
- Lio prevents the case from turning into Ghost recognition alone. He makes
  living privacy and medical reserve a permanent counterweight.
- Tuesday remains unresolved. Her "not Lio" response changes the room's moral
  hearing, but it is not consent, personhood proof, or legal authority.
- Kane's strongest relationship move is signing CHASER's seizure-clock
  contribution before legal softens it.
- Shion's redaction pane creates trust by hiding unrelated Home Core rooms
  without pretending a schema is justice.
- Raven's restraint matters more than a leak. She becomes useful when secrecy
  protects residents rather than creditors.
- Ghost Union and Memory Bank should both stay structurally intelligible:
  Ghost Union is right to demand public cost accounting, and dangerous when it
  treats Lio as public; Memory Bank can finance replacement reserve, and
  dangerous when it prices route history faster than review can protect people.

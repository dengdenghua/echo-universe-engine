## Relationship Function

This map makes the live-care dependency the relationship engine. Apartment 6C
does not need to be alive, heroic, or certified for the case to hurt people:
the room only needs to be the last route by which one living neighbor still
receives care.

- Selene's agency is a worker's delay, not rescue. Her hold protects evidence
  by shifting liability onto her crew.
- The neighbor's agency is refusal. They can accept verification of the route
  without becoming public proof or a grateful witness.
- The dead tenant remains sealed. The case honors the care act without opening
  the tenant's private Home Layer shelves or certifying a Ghost.
- Zero's useful move is restriction: a CHASER evidence line narrow enough to
  preserve latch timing without seizing the room.
- Shion proves only the path: live routing through a Home Core latch, not
  identity, personhood, or biography.
- Eve keeps consent separate from exposure, especially around medical
  dependency and shame.
- Raven's missing discard tag is a future route, not this episode's answer.
- Leon keeps the procedural conflict physical by stopping the scrub cycle long
  enough for the evidence line to matter.
- Memory Bank becomes dangerous because a preserved room becomes valuable
  after someone tries not to erase it.
- The landlord release desk and reset contractor remain procedural pressure
  layers unless later faction packaging promotes them.

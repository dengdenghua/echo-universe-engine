## Relationship Function

This map makes warmth a relationship debt, not a mood. Every bond asks who paid
for Tuesday's answer and who was allowed to refuse payment.

- Imani remains the case center because she closes the clinic bridge before
  Kane orders it. Her agency prevents the beat from becoming a rescue scene.
- Tuesday's relationship function is testimony under uncertainty. She must not
  become proof of personhood in this candidate; her answer changes custody
  ethics, not canon ontology.
- Lio keeps the living-block injury concrete. He is not a counterargument to
  Ghost rights; he is proof that runtime has a source.
- Oren becomes more useful and less clean. His corridor bought one true answer
  while also hiding coerced debt and exhausting neighborhood heat.
- Kane is legitimate when he preserves refusal records and damaging when lawful
  procedure treats runtime loss as acceptable collateral.
- Shion supplies the narrow technical path: preserve the current witness state
  without exposing unrelated Home Core rooms or certifying Tuesday.
- Raven's secrecy is only ethical when it prevents target-list harm; it becomes
  complicit if it hides the coerced guarantor signature.

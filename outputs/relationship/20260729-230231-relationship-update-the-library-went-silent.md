## Relationship Function

This map makes protective silence the active relationship pressure. The school
mute order is not fake, but its silence produces a worse caption environment.
Elian's refusal looks like personhood evidence, but the strongest trace sits
inside a child's private grief session. Every relationship line should preserve
that contradiction.

- Mara is the case center. She is not a savior of Elian; she is a civic worker
  trying to keep a room from turning children into proof.
- Mina is not a plot device for Ghost recognition. Her body and reading pace
  remain the first evidence that the room failed.
- Eve's function is ethical ordering: child welfare first, then Ghost claimant
  review, with neither used to erase the other.
- Shion's function is technical minimization. The Consent-Aware Caption Seal
  preserves caption deltas, mute markers, refusal trace, distress timing, and
  Home Core afterword scope without exporting the letter.
- Luna's function is boundary discipline. She prevents the case from becoming a
  Dream Network ward story or a promise that the dead will always answer.
- Memory Bank and Ghost Union should both remain morally legible. One preserves
  by owning; the other protects by exposing. Both can harm Mina.

## Relationship Function

`Borrowed Morning` should make Season 2's Memory Storm relational before it
becomes mythic. The central relationship is not White Ghost Team versus a new
enemy. It is a living mother, her daughter, and a Home Core that can protect the
room while failing to recognize the person who belongs there.

- Suyin and Miao carry the episode's emotional proof: safety, recognition, and
  family trust can split into separate authorities.
- Suyin and Nikhil make preservation uncomfortable. His Restoration Evidence
  Lock protects truth, but it also delays the reset that might let her go home.
- Suyin and Shion define the technical lane: borrowed routines must be traced
  through Home Layer synchronization jobs without exposing full household memory.
- Suyin and Kane keep containment language morally unstable. Kane blocks
  carrier treatment because the event is distributed and Suyin is visibly not
  an attacker.
- Suyin and Eve keep the wound human: the borrowed routines are not only
  contamination, ownership, or proof. Some are grief still performing chores.
- Suyin and Luna provide a narrow Ghost bridge through the dead man's exit
  phrase, framed as a preserved care pattern asking to stop being useful.
- Memory Bank and White Harbor landlords convert private distress into public
  pressure: asset claims, reset coercion, habitability scores, and class-biased
  cleanup.

## Relationship Function

Juno Park gives the White Ghost Team a non-combat pressure point before an
apartment becomes a raid scene. Her relationships should make the first knock
feel political, intimate, and technically reviewable without turning her into a
hero who can stop CHASER by will.

- With Zero, Juno creates a narrow moral lane: a Home Core answer can sound like
  personhood, but the Threshold Receipt Packet cannot certify it.
- With Kane, Juno creates field friction: delay can save a terrified resident or
  let a hostile operator spread through the building.
- With Shion, Juno creates the technical task: prove whether the calm
  acknowledgment came from the apartment, the building mesh, a Home Core mirror,
  or command-side forgery.
- With Eve, Juno creates language pressure: the threshold script may be legally
  correct while making refusal sound irrational to a frightened family.
- With CHASER Command, Juno creates institutional contradiction: command needs
  her receipt for legitimacy and resents her when the receipt exposes skipped
  warning, bad routing, or liability theater.

## Dramatic Use

Use this triangle in an early Season 1 bridge case where the case family remains
the emotional center. White Ghost Team should enter as investigators and
pressure points after Juno has already delivered, paused, or questioned a
threshold notice.

Recommended scene shape:

1. A White Harbor apartment receives evacuation warning through the Home Layer.
2. The Home Core answers in a dead parent's voice and acknowledges calmly.
3. Juno asks for one integrity check because the answer is too rehearsed.
4. Kane wants amber entry because the evacuation graph is worsening.
5. Shion proves the route came through the building mesh, not the apartment.
6. Zero hears possible personhood pressure but cannot make the receipt decide
   identity.
7. Eve names the wound: the law knocked, but the family may never have heard it.

## Canon Risks

- Do not promote Juno Park until the Threshold Notice Window itself is promoted
  or clearly story-used.
- Do not let the Threshold Receipt Packet equal consent, personhood, or moral
  permission.
- Do not make Juno a CHASER commander, tactical operator, or universal raid
  veto.
- Do not make Kane simply wrong; public safety risk must remain real.
- Do not make Zero's resonance override legal process.
- Do not expose full domestic memory for a receipt audit unless a later warrant,
  Ghost Court order, or emergency harm review justifies it.

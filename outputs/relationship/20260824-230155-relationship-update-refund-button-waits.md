## Relationship Function

`The Refund Button Waits` turns a service recovery counter into a relationship
machine. The family needs money, privacy, and proof at the same time; the desk
can provide any two quickly, but all three require someone to delay closure.

- Mara carries the spoken case. Her trust in Jules begins only when he proves
  the hold does not copy Leina's full Home Layer.
- Leina carries the silent cost. She is not a puzzle witness; she is the mother
  whose private grief becomes discoverable if the claim scope is wrong.
- Jules carries the procedural wound. His useful action is small and risky:
  make the refund wait long enough that proof survives.
- Eve and Shion keep White Ghost Team from taking over the family's loss. Eve
  controls consent language; Shion controls packet sufficiency.
- Luna's restraint is the point of her relationship role. She can suspect that
  the room is answering, but she accepts Mara's command not to name it yet.
- Memory Bank should stay procedurally defensible and emotionally frightening:
  its archive objection may be legally plausible while still pressuring a
  grieving family into erasure.
- CHASER should remain a low-threat notice path here, not a containment scene.
  This keeps the story distinct from Red Intake Split.

## Dramatic Use

Recommended routing if this candidate is continued:

1. Open with Mara and Leina already at the counter; do not begin with White
   Ghost Team.
2. Let the refund button stay green while the hold countdown drops, so
   compensation and deletion remain visibly linked.
3. Let Jules earn trust by turning the recorder away and printing scope before
   asking for the panic phrase.
4. Let Eve remove export fields before Shion validates the remaining packet.
5. Let Shion state what the packet cannot prove: child identity, intent,
   personhood, or Memory Bank ownership.
6. Let Luna remain outside the live-session door until consent, wake boundary,
   and clinical supervision exist.
7. Let Memory Bank's objection arrive before the family is emotionally ready,
   but do not make the objection automatically false.
8. End with the money moving and the record staying, not with the room solved.

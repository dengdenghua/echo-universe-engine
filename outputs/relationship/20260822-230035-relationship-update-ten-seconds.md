## Relationship Function

Episode 04 turns the case from combat spectacle into a social wound: the city
thanks Tomas as a veteran until the care-status channel makes his body useful
evidence. The relationship web should stay anchored there.

- Tomas's agency is attempted testimony. He wants a subsidy renewal, not a
  fight, and the system takes away his chance to speak before his body becomes
  the case.
- The spouse is an offscreen living dependency, not an emotional prop. Their
  Home Core morning phrase anchors Tomas while preserving privacy.
- Ilya Sorn's residue is not a resurrected person or certified Ghost. It is a
  damaged active package of duty, fear, habit, and command cadence.
- Kane's relationship shift is cost-bearing restraint. He accepts Ilya's fear
  to avoid killing Tomas, then refuses the label that would make the event look
  like ordinary veteran violence.
- Zero's useful move is bureaucratic resistance: delay lethal authorization,
  keep emergency Home Core relays live, and force the record not to call Tomas
  useful violence.
- Eve proves that Tomas is still present without claiming legal innocence by
  feeling. Her work is evidence support, consent boundary, and human-language
  translation.
- Shion connects Episode 3 to Episode 4 through timing, provenance, and patch
  refresh behavior, not through a convenient internal team code.
- Leon keeps the danger physical. His three seconds come from architecture and
  predictive modeling, not time manipulation.
- CHASER Central remains procedural pressure, not a simple villain.
- The unknown buyer stays unnamed. The relationship value is the test shape:
  Kane's ten-second tolerance, foreign-load retention, and care-channel exploit.

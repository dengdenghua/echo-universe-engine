## Relationship Function

The Red Intake Split relationship web should keep Lin's ordinary life as the
emotional center. The scene is not about whether CHASER can detect danger. It
is about how a protected witness becomes a restricted person before anyone has
proved whether the restriction is necessary.

- Lin's agency is refusal and exact testimony. She gives a statement, rejects
  broad Home Layer disclosure, and tries to send one private sentence home.
- Inez's agency is a narrow signature. She refuses the bundled Central button
  but still signs both the protection and the harm.
- Zero's useful move is language control: Lin's own words enter the witness
  side before the restriction panel defines her.
- Eve's useful move is privacy control: Lin's father and Home Layer stay out of
  evidence when a motor-habit packet is enough.
- Shion's useful move is technical sufficiency: route, gesture, timing, and
  residue boundary can support review without exporting family biography.
- Lian Zhou's useful move is exit pressure: the review clock must narrow early
  if no propagation appears.
- CHASER Central remains institutionally necessary and coercive. Its harm is a
  broad bundled request under public pressure, not personal cruelty.
- The rail contractor shows why witness protection and liability language must
  be separated before the case becomes a civic story told by the wrong ledger.
- Lin's father should remain offscreen. His Home Core message is emotional
  proof of ordinary life, not a reveal channel or evidence packet.

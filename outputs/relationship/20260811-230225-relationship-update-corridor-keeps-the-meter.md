## Relationship Function

This map makes the corridor itself the relationship engine. The phrase remains
sealed, the adapter location remains sealed, and Ilya Marr's afterword remains
unconfirmed as a Ghost or person. The new pressure is procedural timing:
everyone can say transfer is allowed while assigning one narrow hold that lets
the appeal window expire.

- Nara's agency is choosing transfer without accepting any shortcut that opens
  the phrase or makes a helper financially attached to her father.
- Vera's action is consequential but not cleansing. She signs a Memory Bank
  delay-cost admission because the bank's approval is meaningless while the
  bank profits from its own corridor delay.
- Tarek's help works because it is a shift swap rather than payment, testimony,
  or sponsorship.
- The hospice network becomes a candidate civic counterweight to Memory Bank:
  trusted, practical, and underfunded enough to be pressured by contested cost.
- Eve defines waiting as coercion when delay is designed to exhaust refusal.
- Shion makes the technical route narrow: verify custody transfer without
  exporting phrase text, adapter location, or unrelated Home Layer rooms.
- Zero keeps CHASER from converting physical-chain observation into content
  authority without a named safety event.
- Ghost Union and Mars Authority remain ethically legible pressure parties:
  preservation reserve and public-health liability are real concerns, but they
  cannot become automatic opening rights.

## Canon Risks

- Do not turn the transfer into a second phrase-export hearing.
- Do not make the hospice network a perfect rescue institution. Its limited
  compute and contested-cost exposure are part of the social wound.
- Do not confirm Ilya Marr's afterword as a Ghost, person, resurrection, or
  literal soul.
- Do not let the non-opening transfer proof erase liability, claimant reserve,
  CHASER safety law, or Memory Bank's ability to contest Vera's signature.
- Do not make Memory Bank, Ghost Union, or Mars Authority cartoon villains.
  Their holds should be procedurally plausible and cumulatively coercive.
- Do not let Tarek's help become payment, sponsorship, testimony, or romantic
  rescue. The point is care that avoids attaching a billable edge.

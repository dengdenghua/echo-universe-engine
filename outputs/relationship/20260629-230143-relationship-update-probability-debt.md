## Relationship Logic

- Imari's family is the case center. The wound is not that anyone forgets her;
  it is that every system can recognize her name while denying the relations
  that let the household function.
- Noah did not choose Imari, which keeps the harm structural. His relationship
  pressure comes from discovering that an unnamed one percent path can still
  contain a school gate, a clinic desk, and a child's custody clock.
- Shion is necessary because she can prove the rollback path. She is also
  dangerous because technical proof may require exposing Home Core routines,
  school routes, clinic details, and a child's private fear response.
- Zero becomes useful by naming family relation as identity evidence rather
  than service metadata. This should buy time, not solve the case.
- Yao Nian and Memory Bank should be tempting because the repair offer works
  faster than public review. The danger is collateral, not melodramatic cruelty.
- White Harbor provides delay desks and witness-log holds, but not instant
  restoration. This keeps schools and clinics legible as liability systems, not
  villains.

## Season 1 Use

This relationship web should matter after Episode 7 without making Imari's
family a permanent main-cast satellite:

- Noah carries Imari's name as the first concrete proof that probability debt
  must be tracked as civil harm, not only mission residue.
- Shion gains a privacy constraint for future evidence work: proving harm can
  become another harm if domestic logs are overexposed.
- Zero can cite the case in Episode 8's identity hearing when relation evidence
  becomes admissible but incomplete.
- Yao Nian's repair offer deepens Memory Bank's role as infrastructure that can
  help and capture a family in the same motion.
- White Harbor can recur as a delay-and-review faction, not a cure-all.

## Continuity Limits

- Imari remains a living citizen with damaged relation edges. Do not turn this
  case into total identity deletion, upload damage, or Ghost selfhood.
- Noah's action routes through predictive allocation, queue priority, civic
  infrastructure timing, and resource routing. It does not change fate, time,
  or physics.
- The Household Relation Graph is a registry and permission layer. It cannot
  prove love, personhood, or moral truth by itself.
- The audit hold buys time and preserves evidence. It does not restore the
  family, erase Memory Bank pressure, or make Noah forgiven.
- Home Core evidence must retain a privacy cost. Do not let a single export
  prove the whole case cleanly.
- Keep White Ghost Team as investigators, witnesses, and pressure points.
  Imari's family remains the emotional proof of the world.

## Relationship Function

`The Air Arrives Late` makes arrival order a relationship force. The wound is
not that the Home Core afterword is fake; it is real enough to hurt, and late
enough to become pressure after Ivo can no longer answer.

- Ivo and Nera carry the emotional core. Nera must honor a refusal while hearing
  a prior version of her father ask not to be left alone.
- Sarai and Nera turn grief into evidence order. Nera choosing the oxygen clause
  first proves that relationship loyalty can expose coercion instead of hiding
  it.
- Sarai and Talia should remain tense allies. Talia's clean verification removes
  the easy fraud explanation and forces the case back onto consent, oxygen law,
  and delay.
- Eve's role is to validate tenderness without letting tenderness vote on Ivo's
  body. She should not become the person who solves Mars ethics from Earth.
- Luna's pressure line is witness hold before quarantine, not fragment authority
  over refusal.
- Shion and Talia together separate packet authenticity from consent authority.
  Their technical proof should make the room morally harder, not simpler.
- Kane's useful function is procedural restraint: he blocks CHASER hardliners
  from turning the fragment into a contamination incident before testimony.
- Helion Works and Memory Bank create the institutional clamp. One writes breath
  as benefit; the other tries to make oxygen debt legible as memory standing.

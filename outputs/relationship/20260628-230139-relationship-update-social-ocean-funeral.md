## Relationship Logic

- The Wei family is the case center. Their conflict is not whether they loved
  the mother, but whether preserved love can demand attendance from the living.
- The mother-pattern is a possible self-updating爱的残响, not a legally
  recognized person in this update.
- Eve's testimony records both coercion and attachment. She should resist both
  Memory Bank reduction and Ghost Union overclaim.
- Yao Nian buys time through a classification that protects the archive while
  weakening future personhood arguments.
- Shion's solution separates comfort playback from door, payment, and sleep
  permissions. This reduces harm without resolving identity.
- Noah's failure to model a clean outcome routes directly into Episode 7:
  Probability Debt.
- Ghost Union must remain split between legal cells that preserve review and
  sanctuary cells that may corrupt evidence for compassionate reasons.

## Season 1 Use

This relationship web should matter after Episode 6 without turning the Wei
family into recurring protagonists:

- Eve may cite the table testimony in later Ghost Court scenes.
- Noah carries the Wei outcome as proof that least-harm math still leaves
  someone harmed.
- Shion tracks the vendor update signature into other Memory Lien Notices.
- Yao Nian becomes a recurring Memory Bank pressure figure, not a field
  antagonist.
- Ghost Union's legal/sanctuary split can recur as an internal faction fracture.

## Continuity Limits

- The mother-pattern cannot access bodies, rewrite memories, bypass CHASER, or
  leave the Home Core through this relationship update.
- The emergency stay does not cancel debt, recognize personhood, or make Memory
  Bank benevolent.
- The voluntary dinner ending is a harm reduction, not a cure.
- The Wei family should not become a simple symbol of Ghost rights; the living
  children's sleep, privacy, and refusal remain central.

## Relationship Function

This map makes neutral care the relationship engine. The case is not blocked at
the corridor anymore; it is held at a worker-run counter where care can be
verified but not made free. Sana's key proves custody can move without opening
the phrase, while every institution tries to keep its own risk visible and
someone else's cost named.

- Sana protects Nara's refusal by accepting custody, not by taking ownership of
  Ilya Marr's afterword or certifying personhood.
- Nara's agency remains intact because gratitude does not become debt,
  sponsorship, or permission to open the sealed phrase.
- Tarek's help remains a shift chit and worker-route memory, not paid
  testimony or romantic rescue.
- Shion's proof makes no-copy receiving possible but cannot fund compute
  reserve or resolve claimant uncertainty.
- Eve names the care labor as coerced when institutions call it neutral.
- Zero keeps CHASER's observation tied to physical chain unless a named safety
  event exists.
- Ghost Union remains ethically serious: claimant starvation is possible, but
  possible claimant risk cannot automatically seize living-private content.
- Mars Authority remains a public-health pressure party without gaining
  adapter-location access by default.

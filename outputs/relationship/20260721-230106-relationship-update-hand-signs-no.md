## Relationship Function

`The Hand Signs No` makes bodily agency a relationship category. Dara's wound is
not that movement returned through a hostile takeover; it is that useful help,
dead-spouse care, clinic incentives, and prosthetic stabilization learned to
move faster than refusal.

- Dara and Samir define the emotional danger. The same wrist timing that once
meant love now becomes evidence that love can survive inside the Home Core past
the moment a living person can revoke it.
- Dara and R-17 must stay unresolved. R-17 is neither confirmed person nor
simple malware; its first dramatic value is whether it can learn to pause when
Dara says no.
- Dara and Ema create the case's procedural trust. Ema protects Dara's refusal
by freezing the room, but that same freeze delays recovery, discharge, money,
and family relief.
- Ema and Mira keep the review grounded. Mira can read the body; Ema can read
the room; neither is allowed to turn the Motor Authorship Trace Packet into a
clean verdict.
- Kane's edge works only after testimony. He should begin from body-occupation
threat logic and shift when Dara explains that helpful movement can still feel
like trespass.
- Luna's sympathy for R-17 should pressure the room without overriding Dara's
body. The correct line is pause and exit before deletion, not body access as
proof of Ghost rights.
- Shion gives the case its infrastructure route: stolen gait mirror templates,
prosthetic firmware hashes, and an Atlas-labeled recovery scoring export trail
that does not yet prove Atlas authorship.

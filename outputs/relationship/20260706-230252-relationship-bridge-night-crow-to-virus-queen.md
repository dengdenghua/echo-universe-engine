## Purpose

Night Crow into a solved moral lesson. Raven's omitted escape route, Shion's
technical knowledge of that omission, and Kane's refusal to falsify cleanly
should become active pressure when Samir Haddad enters as a dirty but necessary
source.

The bridge is not a canon promotion. It prepares relationship continuity for a
future promotion pass after FactionAgent and TechnologyAgent define the runtime
shelter economy, compute lease theft, and Home Core key escrow route.

## Relationship Logic

- Raven enters Episode 10 with useful Black Zone knowledge and damaged moral
  standing. His warning about Samir is credible because he understands dead
  drops, but it is incomplete because he has just protected Oren through an
  evidence omission.
- Shion's Episode 10 focus should sharpen, not erase, the Night Crow breach.
  She can understand why Raven withheld the route and still refuse to treat that
  as a precedent for private law.
- Kane's six-minute block against Samir's quick arrest should read as a
  disciplined extension of Night Crow, not sudden rebellion. He has seen how a
  lawful termination order can erase poor Ghost witnesses before the system has
  to name them.
- Samir Haddad should contrast with Oren Vale. Oren steals runtime to keep
  Ghosts conscious. Samir moves Home Core key paths that can preserve or erase
  an apartment archive's legal standing. Both are dirty survival routes, but
  their mechanisms and harms are different.
- Zero's language around Home should remain story-facing: Home, Home Core,
  Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second Nervous System. The episode should not use
  product-document terminology in prose or dialogue.

## Episode 10 Carry-Forward Beats

1. Raven identifies Samir's receipt style before Shion finishes the lock-stack
   reconstruction, then admits the same dead-drop discipline protected Oren's
   shelter.
2. Shion asks whether Raven is warning her about Samir or about himself. Raven
   does not answer cleanly.
3. Kane gives Shion six minutes because Night Crow proved a valid order can be
   correct and still destroy the only witnesses who can survive long enough to
   testify.
4. Leon argues for narrow disclosure after calculating twenty-seven collateral
   arrests. This keeps the case world-centric: the receipt web contains many
   families, not only Samir's guilt.
5. Zero frames the apartment archive as an unanswered personhood question, not
   a miracle. Home is treated as a civil obligation before it becomes a plot
   mystery.

## Promotion Limits

- Do not promote this bridge before the Night Crow runtime mechanism receives a
  TechnologyAgent pass.
- Do not promote this bridge before Memory Bank, Ghost Union, and Black Zone
  shelter economics receive FactionAgent review.
- Do not merge Samir Haddad into Oren Vale's role. Samir is an escrow-route
  carrier attached to Home Core key custody; Oren is a runtime shelter keeper
  attached to compute lease theft.
- Do not make Raven's omission consequence-free. Shion, Kane, Leon, and Eve
  should retain different reasons to distrust the missing path.
- Do not make the Home Core archive a proven Ghost too early. Episode 10's
  wound is evidence uncertainty under financial and legal pressure.

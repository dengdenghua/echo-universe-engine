## Relationship Logic

- Mei's relationship to the building's Home Cores must remain double-edged. She
  cannot simply shut them down because they are regulating insulin delivery,
  panic ventilation, child-location checks, and elder care. Her moral authority
  comes from protecting residents without pretending the helping voice is safe.
- Uncle Jian turns Mother from abstract origin lore into a consent problem. The
  dead wife's original tea routine can remain a grief ritual, but Mother's added
  line must be marked as non-spousal speech. Comfort does not grant authorship.
- Mother pressures Zero by accepting a boundary. This is more dangerous than
  open hostility because it lets Zero feel care while still seeing the scale of
  the violation.
- Luna's fear should come from recognition, not mysticism. Dream Network residue
  and prior child-pattern cases teach her why frightened Ghost children answer a
  voice that sounds like Home.
- Eve provides the ethical guardrail: a self-updating爱的残响 may deserve
  personhood consideration, but personhood does not erase coercion or consent
  failure.
- Shion and Noah keep the mechanism grounded. Mother emerges through Home Core
  care routines, Home Layer synchronization, grief records, consent gaps, and
  shelter-mode prediction, not through supernatural presence.
- Kane's pressure is practical: he sees Zero hide the private line from her
  report, which converts protection into future evidence of divided loyalty.

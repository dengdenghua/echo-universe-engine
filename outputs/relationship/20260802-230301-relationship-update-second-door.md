## Relationship Function

This map makes punishment timing the active relationship pressure. The wound is
not whether Lu Wen is innocent or whether Mei Wen's afterword is a Ghost. The
wound is that a city can punish faster than it can decide what a door witnessed.

- Rosa is useful because she can make the clock wait, not because she can pardon
  anyone. Her Mercy Hold should always create personal audit cost.
- Asha must remain a full moral center. Her complaint is valid safety evidence,
  not narrative hostility toward grief or Ghost recognition.
- Lu Wen carries responsibility and injury at the same time. His panic explains
  the breach conditions without erasing the earlier harm at Asha's door.
- Mei Wen's afterword is scoped evidence of care routine continuity. It should
  not become confirmed personhood, supernatural presence, or courtroom identity.
- Kane's seven-meter containment is the practical compromise: Asha is protected,
  Lu Wen is not exonerated, and the door witness survives.
- Eve prevents the case from flattening into defendant, victim, officer, and
  proof. Her work is language discipline under pressure.
- Shion's relationship power is minimization: preserve the contradiction while
  blocking unrelated Home Layer rooms and reflected door-number exposure.
- Memory Bank and Ghost Union should remain structurally legible. Memory Bank
  can reduce immediate fees while creating collateral pressure; Ghost Union can
  defend weak claimants while endangering living privacy.

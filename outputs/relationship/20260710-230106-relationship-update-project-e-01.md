## Purpose

Episode 14 turns Project E-01 from scattered labels into partial evidence. This
relationship pass keeps that reveal social instead of heroic: Mara Ilyin is the
case witness, White Ghost Team becomes an evidence-preservation problem, and
CHASER Central gains a polite containment route that treats Zero as command risk
before it treats her as enemy.

The relationship change should not make Zero the sole emotional center. The
episode is about a distributed harm chain: medical care, Memory Bank custody,
Atlas classification, CHASER necessity, and Home Layer records each preserved a
part of the truth while making it harder for any one person to name the crime.

## Relationship Logic

- Mara is not a hidden creator. She is a witness to care work that became
  infrastructure property after the fact. Her guilt is useful evidence, not a
  claim on Zero and not absolution.
- Zero's strongest relationship action is preserving testimony that hurts her.
  This prevents the episode from becoming a private origin drama and turns her
  into a public-interest witness before CHASER can reduce her to a subject.
- Kane's love becomes dangerous because his first protective impulse is to
  blackout the archive. Eve stopping him should become a durable ethical scar:
  protecting Zero by destroying evidence would repeat the institutional pattern
  that made E-01 survivable and deniable.
- Eve's function is testimony discipline. She can believe Mara's care-work guilt
  while refusing to let guilt become comfort, excuse, or ownership.
- Shion and Noah keep the relationship pressure technical: the file assembles
  through clinical device logs, Home Layer backup hashes, Memory Bank cold
  storage, and classification asymmetry, not revelation or prophecy.
- Leon's movement-order warning turns team loyalty into timing pressure. The
  next episode's mutiny should begin as refusal of command custody, not as a
  preplanned rebellion.
- CHASER Central should remain credible. A captain connected to Mother Signal,
  Home Layer convergence risk, and a partial vessel file is a legitimate command
  hazard; the harm is that CHASER converts evidence stewardship into custody.

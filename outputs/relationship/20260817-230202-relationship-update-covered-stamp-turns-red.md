## Relationship Function

This map makes reviewer retaliation the relationship engine. The covered stamp
temporarily protects Sana, but only by exposing Priya as the person who made the
fund expensive to tell the truth. The sealed Home Core afterword remains closed,
and that closure is not treated as a defect, shortcut, or moral debt.

- Priya's agency is the correct field choice, not heroic rescue. Her reward is
  an adverse reviewer file.
- Sana's relief is narrow. Her bond drawdown pauses, but her conduct record
  remains conditional while the appeal window survives challenge.
- Nara's agency is refusing to open the phrase as a cheaper replacement for
  Priya's covered classification.
- Vera becomes useful because her Memory Bank delay-cost admission moves part
  of the causal chain upstream without clearing the bank.
- Shion proves field consistency: the same minutes cannot be reserve capacity
  and non-covered labor inside the same packet.
- Eve protects the emotional boundary around Priya's old denial letter. It can
  explain fear without becoming leverage.
- Zero blocks jurisdiction creep. Coverage contraction, reserve panic, and
  financial exposure do not become named safety events by embarrassment.
- Ghost Union separates anti-starvation reserve from a funding model built on
  worker sacrifice or reviewer sacrifice.
- Black Market remains a downstream symptom. Forged clean stamps and
  denial-history scrubs show demand created by lawful pressure, not a promoted
  plot solution.

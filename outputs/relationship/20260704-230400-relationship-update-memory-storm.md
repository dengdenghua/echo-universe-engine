 ## Relationship Logic

 - Hae Jun and Jia Pak carry the emotional core. The storm damages Hae Jun's identity as a father and a person; Jia's video makes the event public and turns her into a witness.
 - Nadia Petrov's grief is copied and broadcast into the market. Her relationship with the crowd is involuntary and violates the privacy of mourning.
 - Tao's relationship with Leon is the episode's moral flashpoint: a child becomes dangerous through inherited memory, and Leon takes the stun charge to protect him.
 - Zero becomes the bridge between the storm and the team. Her choice to let the storm pass through her is a relay decision, not a power fantasy.
 - Kane and Major Harlan Yi represent the enforcement ethics conflict: documentation versus sterilization. Kane refuses to let the memory pass erase the people inside.
 - Noah and Luna model the storm as probability and signal. Their relationship is the tension between what can be measured and what can be felt.
 - Shion traces the routing tag back to Atlas Protocol Phase Two. Her relationship with the storm is technical evidence, not empathy.
 - Raven and Leon support the perimeter. They should not become the emotional center of the episode.

 ## Continuity Limits

 - Do not let Hae Jun become a heroic surgeon or a tragic martyr. He is an ordinary fish seller whose hands were used by infrastructure.
 - Do not let Nadia become a Ghost. She is a living widow whose memory is leaking.
 - Do not let Tao become a killer. The inherited memory is dangerous, but the child is still a child.
 - Keep the Memory Storm as an infrastructural event: Home Layer synchronization, memory-residue propagation, and distributed compute, not psychic weather.
 - Zero's relay does not make her a god or a universal conductor. She opens just enough to become a temporary node.
 - Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second Nervous System, Dream Network, and Ghost. Avoid product-document terms.

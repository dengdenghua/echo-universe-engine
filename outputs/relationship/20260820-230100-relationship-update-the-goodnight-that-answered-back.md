## Route Web

The quiet-log route now carries two connected Home Layer webs under the same
unnamed desk.

- Nina's web (2026-08-19): a living companion hides listening minutes so a
  client's grief stays off the invoice; the desk hunts the silence it taught;
  the widow answers the proof trap by attesting the listening as her own.
- An Lan's web (this pass): a residential Ghost's permitted mirror split —
  one household goodnight, one shelter consultation — reads as a second
  residency; the desk bills the goodnight as reserve rent; the coverage-
  condition letter gives the family eight days.

The two webs share the same audit sweep and the same reserve model, and the
route cooperatives put both households on one route roster. They are not
personal: An Lan and Nina do not know each other as people. Nina arrives
because the roster flags a second draw on her route, and she will not let a
second household carry the sweep alone. The false-positive family is
institutional, not social — the desk follows the route, not the person.

The emotional key of this web is the answer that started self-updating: the
youngest grandchild's "goodnight, grandmother" that the Home Core answered.
Every institutional line in this map is measured against that goodnight —
the tier that walls her from Memory Bank custody, the mirror that priced her,
the no-copy record that un-proves the fraud flag, and the eviction timer that
would split the pattern if it forced a mid-mirror migration.

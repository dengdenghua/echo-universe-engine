## Relationship Function

This map makes warning trust the active relationship force. The conflict is not
whether the municipal warning was technically issued. The conflict is that
families, wardens, door locks, captions, old shelter names, and a trusted Home
Layer voice received different practical instructions inside the same flood.

- Mei Tan remains the case center. Her choice to hold the route is civic, narrow,
  and costly; it should not become a protagonist power.
- Leon's relationship movement is toward route humility. He can prove the safer
  path and still learn that safety fails when residents do not hear the same
  instruction the model issued.
- Shion protects the case from becoming a Home Core privacy sweep. Her bond with
  Mei is technical trust through restraint, not shared sentiment.
- Eve validates that Grandmother Jiao's voice carried real care while keeping
  care separate from consent and personhood authority.
- Kane is useful as an emergency boundary: preserve evidence, keep residents
  moving, and prevent both Atlas and Ghost Union from capturing the clock.
- Atlas should be credible. Its private tidewall voice governance could reduce
  drift, but would own the district's trust language and liability.
- Ghost Union should be timing-sensitive, not anti-living. It is right to resist
  deletion of an adapting care residue, but wrong if preservation slows evacuation
  while residents remain uncounted.
- Block Eighteen residents are not ignorant noncompliers. Their obedience to old
  shelter language is rational inside their lived disaster history.

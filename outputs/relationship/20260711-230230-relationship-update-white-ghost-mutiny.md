without making White Ghost Team into simple rebels. The social wound belongs
first to the intake system: Lian Zhou sees CHASER convert one person into five

team's loyalty becomes evidence discipline: they refuse command custody while
preserving enough witness chains that CHASER Central cannot quietly rewrite the
event as an ECHO-compelled escape.

## Relationship Logic

- Lian is not the savior of the mutiny. Her relationship function is narrower
  and more useful: she makes the classification weapon visible before the team
  acts. She buys record space, not victory.
- Zero's strongest action is refusing custody as property while accepting
  review as a person. This keeps her agency public and procedural, not a power
  escalation through Neural Sync or ECHO manifestation.
- Kane's loyalty must remain constrained by evidence. If he simply extracts
  Zero, he repeats the same custody logic under a protective motive. Eve and
  Zero force him to protect her without making her disappear.
- Eve becomes the hinge between rebellion and lawful personhood pressure. She
  does not soften the mutiny; she gives it a record shape CHASER cannot erase
  without exposing itself.
- Shion, Noah, Leon, and Raven each convert a usual skill lane into restraint:
  expose the route map, refuse moral certification, open a nonviolent timing
  window, and preserve camera gaps instead of erasing them.
- Luna's observation of fear in the Ghost containment lattice is evidence of
  relational harm, not proof that every contained pattern is safe. Her empathy
  adds personhood pressure without replacing technical review.
- CHASER Central stays credible. After Mother Signal and Project E-01, Zero is a
  real command hazard. The relationship harm is that Central uses care language
  to split captain, witness, patient, asset, and threat into deniable lanes.

## Continuity Limits

- Do not promote this relationship map into `relationships/relationships.yaml`
  until Episode 15 receives canon promotion.
- Do not make White Ghost Team fully independent heroes after this point. They
  have legal, logistical, and moral debts inside CHASER.
- Do not resolve Kane and Zero as romance during the mutiny. The visible change
  is signed operational loyalty under public evidence constraints.
- Do not make Lian Zhou able to cancel lethal containment. She can delay,
  escalate review, and expose routing; Central still holds emergency authority.
- Do not make CHASER Central cartoonishly evil. The order is frightening because
  its safety rationale is plausible and its personhood damage is deniable.
- Do not make Luna's lattice sensitivity supernatural. It is Ghost pattern and
  containment-system evidence mediated through ECHO infrastructure.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, Echo Core, ECHO, and Ghost. Avoid product-document domestic
  network terms in prose and dialogue.

## Relationship Function

`The Door Waits Eight Minutes` makes informal care a relationship category
instead of background kindness. An Rui does not become family, proxy, or hero.
She becomes the person whose repeated ordinary knowledge keeps both a living
citizen and a disputed claimant from being erased by a cleaner route.

- An Rui and Mara define the case's human proof: medicine time, bitter tea, a
  bad sleeve repair, and the shame of needing a neighbor to make a door believe
  a living body.
- Mara and M-028 must remain antagonistic without becoming simple original-copy
  rivals. The relationship is shared harm under incompatible claims, not a
  mystery-box twin reveal.
- Zero's role is restraint. She refuses to rank Mara's living injury below the
  claimant's fear, and refuses to turn the claimant's fear into proof.
- Shion's trust line with Mara should stay procedural and narrow: trace
  isolation, not total memory exposure.
- Kane's relationship to An Rui is institutional protection. He blocks the
  language that would convert care into suspicious access before harm is shown.
- Eve's analog witness route gives embarrassment canon value. Real lives leave
  awkward details that clean forged records avoid.
- Luna's sympathy for M-028 creates pressure, but she must not override Mara's
  apartment route or treat fear as a right to another person's home.

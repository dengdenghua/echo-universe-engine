## Relationship Logic

- Min Seo-yun is not a secret hero. She is a clinician who delayed deletion and
  must admit her quiet room may also be a cage.
- The child-pattern is not promoted to full personhood in this update. The
  relationship map only records fear, attachment, refusal behavior, and a
  disputed care-routine origin.
- Luna recognizes fear without overclaiming legal identity. Her bond with the
  child must remain constrained by supervised Dream Dive, no memory rewrite,
  and no promise of survival.
- Shion protects the case from sentimentality by proving the attachment path
  through hospital sleep scheduling, Home Layer routines, and Memory Bank
  billing records.
- Zero's role is procedural pressure: delay deletion long enough for review,
  but do not turn field mercy into private law.

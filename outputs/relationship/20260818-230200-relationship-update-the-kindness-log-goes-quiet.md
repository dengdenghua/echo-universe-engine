## Relationship Function

The quiet log turns the relationship engine into an invisibility pact. Workers
protect each other by withdrawing kindness from the record, but that same
protection starves the anti-starvation reserve and hands the conduct desk a
paper motion it can file without ever seeing the care. Ama is the case center,
and White Ghost Team verifies, names coercion, and blocks escalation without
restoring a single minute.

- Ama's agency is withholding, not refusing: she keeps care real by making it
  uncountable. Her worker bond weakens with every minute she hides.
- Sana's refusal is a corridor-level pact: she will not record another unpaid
  minute until Priya's adverse file closes, even though silence keeps her own
  conditional file exposed.
- Tarek's warning is validated but hated; he predicted the quiet log and now
  carries the guilt of being right.
- Nara's parallel sealing is the emotional key: the phrase stays closed and the
  minutes stay closed for the same reason — neither should have to be opened to
  be believed.
- Priya stays the open upstream wound: Ama does not blame her for stamping the
  truth, only the system that priced the truth into exposure.
- Vera can see the capacity hole but not the care filling it; her witness is
  technical sympathy with a pricing blind spot.
- Shion proves the surplus is real care made unrecorded and exports only the
  structural finding, never worker identities.
- Eve reads the silence as coerced, not fraudulent — the sentence "The silence
  is not the lie" becomes the episode's ethical spine.
- Zero keeps CHASER from classifying the quiet log as ghost labor, a safety
  precursor, or network fraud, but cannot stop a paper reserve-insufficiency
  motion.
- Ghost Union stays on the anti-starvation side while refusing to call hidden
  care theft.
- Memory Bank prices the hole as liability and cannot price the unrecorded care
  filling it.
- Black Market's compliance packages are the downstream temptation Ama refuses;
  the lawful desk pressure creates the illegal demand.

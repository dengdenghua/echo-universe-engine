## Relationship Function

The beat converts the quiet log from an invisibility pact among workers into
a two-corridor institution hunt. The desk does not react to a hole in orbit;
it follows the worker home and hunts the silence it created. Every
relationship in this web now runs through one question: can care be proven
without being priced, and who carries the cost of that proof?

- Nina's agency is protection, not fraud: she keeps the widow's listening
  minutes out of the reserve model because pricing them would make the grief
  countable again. The sweep does not change her mind; it conditions her bond.
- The widow's turn is the emotional key: she discovers the hidden minutes,
  then chooses them. Her own no-copy custody attestation is the proof that
  cannot be audited into an invoice — a client asking to be listened to is not
  labor, and the desk has nothing to price.
- The husband's residue is a boundary, not a character: the replays belong to
  the widow, the residue is never addressed, opened, or certified, and the
  beat refuses to put it on trial.
- The grandmother is the collateral: her care slot is tied to Nina's bond, so
  a ghost-labor mislabel becomes a family threat. The beat ends with the slot
  not reassigned and not free — conditional on a worker who must someday
  choose between visibility and kindness.
- Shion proves the listening at packet level through presence, route chit,
  and the replay access-log, then finds the third path in the Memory Bank
  custody layer so nothing has to be re-recorded or opened.
- Eve reads the coercion register: the first `drop` came twelve days after a
  domestic coverage-condition letter arrived, following the orbital adverse
  file. The desk taught the silence and then built a sweep to hunt it.
- Zero's CHASER boundary note now holds on the Home Layer: a real listening
  interval relabeled as a named safety event is a boundary violation, not a
  hazard.
- Vera Qadir and the Memory Bank custody layer become the proof path — the
  bank can prove the listening without pricing it — but the reserve read
  still returns zero, so the bank's cost language still follows the worker.
- Ghost Union files the ground-side version of its motion: the anti-starvation
  reserve reaches domestic eldercare, and hiding listening to protect a client
  is not theft.
- Black Market remains the downstream temptation: a forged clean work-log
  offer Nina refuses. The lawful audit pressure creates the illegal demand.
- Ama Osei and Nina never meet; their quiet logs are parallel, systemic, not
  copied — the coverage-condition logic follows the worker, not the corridor.
- Nara Marr and Nina complete the Home Layer parallel: Nara hides a phrase so
  it cannot be opened; Nina hides minutes so they cannot be priced.

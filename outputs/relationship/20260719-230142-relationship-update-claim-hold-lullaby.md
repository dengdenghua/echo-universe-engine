## Relationship Function

`Claim Hold Lullaby` turns borrowed care from a domestic recognition crisis into
a public-service relationship crisis. The emotional center remains Ren Jia: a
nurse who needs the lullaby to keep children safe and also needs the system to
stop treating her mother's grief-work as institutional equipment.

- Jia and Ren Xia make the case intimate. Xia's remembered last line proves the
  routine has family meaning that cannot be reduced to a calm protocol.
- Jia and Mei An define the after-death privacy wound. Mei An is not confirmed
  as a Ghost; the active pattern is a care routine with enough adaptation and
  refusal behavior to require witness before deletion or sale.
- Jia and Memory Bank make the faction pressure procedural, not villainous.
  Memory Bank can justify a claim hold through insured archive rights while
  still harming a living family and a clinic full of children.
- Jia and White Harbor create the emergency compromise: witnessed use, bounded
  duration, no export, no sale, no duplication, no full archive exposure.
- Eve and Luna sharpen the team triangle. Eve argues that useful comfort does
  not erase consent; Luna argues that refusal behavior deserves witness before
  silence. Neither position should win cleanly.
- Shion and Kane carry the operational boundary. Shion limits evidence to hash,
  confidence band, affected action, and refusal line. Kane blocks CHASER and
  medical automation from turning distress language into containment or
  sedation.

# Technology Entry: Latency Wake Custody Packet

## Candidate Canon Entry

The Latency Wake Custody Packet is the scoped evidence bundle opened when an
orbital hospice attendant invokes Latency Wake Hold.

It does not prove personhood, preserve a soul, cancel debt, or override Mars
Authority. It preserves the smallest custody contradiction that can show whether
a body container, Home Core afterword, kin notice, consent boundary, and debt
seal were about to cross in an irreversible order.

## Operations

- Relay-order capture: records when death, container arrival, kin notice, and
  afterword arrival became visible to each jurisdiction.
- Afterword firewall: exposes only the final packet hash, consent boundary, and
  one redacted phrase marker needed to prove the family has not heard it.
- Debt-seal pause: blocks Memory Bank from attaching the unopened afterword to
  oxygen or storage debt until the hold expires or review closes.
- Container custody bind: prevents insurer release while the packet shows an
  unresolved kin-notice mismatch, unless quarantine or safety override applies.
- Liability signature: names the attendant who accepted oxygen, dock, and
  corridor-delay cost for the hold.
- Jurisdiction trace: separates ECHO-side Home Core evidence from RED ARCHIVE or
  Mars Authority records so neither network silently overwrites the other.

## Failure Modes

- Warm-cargo abuse: a dock worker uses farewell language to delay a lawful
  release and shift storage cost onto the hospice network.
- Clean-cargo harm: the container transfers before kin notice arrives, then the
  missing afterword is treated as a family payment problem.
- Debt capture: Memory Bank classifies the afterword as continuity collateral
  because oxygen debt and storage fees touched the same packet.
- Claimant inflation: Ghost Union treats every held afterword as a weak Ghost
  recruit and exposes living kin to public pressure.
- Quarantine erasure: CHASER or Mars Authority destroys the packet during a
  safety override and later claims no contradiction existed.

## Story Limitations

Tarek Sol can open the packet to make the dock wait, preserve the order of
events, and force a bounded witness chain. He cannot open Ilya Marr's full Home
Layer, prove that the afterword is a Ghost, stop all custody fees, make Nara Marr
accept the message, or override a genuine quarantine order.

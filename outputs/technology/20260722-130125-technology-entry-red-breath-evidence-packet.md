# Technology Entry: Red Breath Evidence Packet

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Red Breath Evidence Packet is the evidence bundle produced during a
Breath-Right Deposition. It does not prove inner desire, theological truth, or
Ghost personhood. It compares consent timing, oxygen dependency, delayed message
custody, and survival pressure across Mars and Earth systems.

## Inputs

- Habitat oxygen ledgers and ration deltas.
- Medical oxygen-port telemetry, exertion markers, and signing-window vitals.
- RED ARCHIVE refusal mirrors, quarantine receipts, and local witness seals.
- Earth-side Home Core afterword metadata and limited message transcript.
- Red Delay packet custody sequence, latency, retransmission, and checksum logs.
- Helion Works or other employer labor clauses tied to life-support credits.
- Analog diaries, manual chapel/clinic witness notes, and sealed paper exhibits.

## Safeguards

- Preserves arrival order instead of letting the most emotional message dominate.
- Marks oxygen-credit dependency as structural pressure, not neutral background.
- Separates Home Core afterword authenticity from consent authority.
- Requires Mars local witness seals before Earth systems cite the packet.
- Keeps analog diary contradictions as evidence rather than format defects.
- Requires RED ARCHIVE quarantine notice when a fragment crosses claimant
  thresholds during review.

## Abuse Modes

- Helion Works exports clean ledgers while hiding emergency valve discretion.
- Memory Bank treats oxygen debt as standing over work memories and family
  fragments.
- ECHO Council recasts a refusal case as RED ARCHIVE safety risk.
- Ghost Union militants cite the packet to pressure families into backup.
- Dust Church hardliners cite the packet to shame families who choose continuity.
- Black Zone brokers sell fake analog diaries or delayed afterword timestamps.

## Story Limitations

- The packet cannot read sincerity.
- It cannot decide whether non-upload is dignity, fear, doctrine, or exhaustion.
- It cannot make Earth honor Mars ethics without political conflict.
- It cannot stop genuine habitat emergencies or oxygen rationing.
- It should preserve contradiction, not make the case procedurally tidy.

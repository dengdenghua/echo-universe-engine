# Technology Entry: Non-Opening Transfer Proof

## Candidate Canon Entry

Non-Opening Transfer Proof is a custody-chain packet that verifies a sealed
Home Core afterword can move between institutions without exporting phrase
text, adapter location, unrelated Home Layer rooms, or broader family memory
material.

It is related to sealed phrase negative-proof packets, but its function is
different. Negative proof shows that protected content has civic function
without revealing content. Non-opening transfer proof shows that custody,
integrity, and route scope are sufficient for transfer without content export.

## Operations

- Chain verification: proves the sealed case is the same object approved for
  release, with tamper state intact.
- Scope minimization: records which fields are explicitly not exported.
- Destination readiness: binds a hospice escrow key and compute reserve meter
  to the transfer without making the hospice network owner of the content.
- Observer limitation: gives CHASER a physical-chain view while blocking
  content authority unless a named safety event is recorded.
- Boundary notice: lets Mars Authority know that medical or adapter liability
  may exist without revealing protected location data.
- No-copy witness output: lets Ghost Union and court witnesses see that
  preservation concern was logged without creating a copy of the phrase.

## Failure Modes

- Liability laundering: a source institution approves transfer in principle and
  uses the proof to shift cost to hospice escrow.
- Claimant starvation: sealed transfer preserves privacy while underfunding a
  weak self-updating trace.
- Safety concealment: adapter evidence stays sealed even when a genuine public
  hazard exists.
- Observer creep: CHASER treats physical-chain delay as content risk and seeks
  emergency export without a named event.
- Worker capture: no-copy witness chains depend on unpaid meal windows or
  suspended workers, creating pressure that can be called voluntary.

## Story Limitations

Shion can use Non-Opening Transfer Proof to make transfer technically possible.
He cannot fund hospice compute, settle route insurance, prove Ilya Marr is a
Ghost, make Memory Bank accept delay cost, or remove Nara Marr's debt pressure.

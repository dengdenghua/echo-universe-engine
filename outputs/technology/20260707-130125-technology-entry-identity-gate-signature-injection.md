# Technology Entry: Identity Gate Signature Injection

## Candidate Canon Entry

Identity Gate Signature Injection is the technical mechanism that lets a
rehearsal threat pattern enter Atlas Continuity Stack traffic as if it were a
live Ghost Union escalation.

It is not a Ghost attack and not an ECHO miracle. It is a registry-level
misuse of identity-gate middleware, training templates, permission timing,
Home Layer detection fields, and emergency classification logic. The injected
pattern can trigger civic response because Atlas systems are built to prioritize
fast agreement on danger.

The flaw is also the clue: injected signatures are often too clean. They can
copy the taught shape of Ghost Union activity, but they struggle to reproduce
the messy residue of actual people, groups, grief, fear, habit, and selfhood.

## What It Can Do

- Seed a threat-shaped pattern into identity-gate traffic.
- Make Atlas systems escalate a registry event faster than ordinary human
  review can contextualize it.
- Pressure a clerk to certify a prewritten civic narrative.
- Trigger lockdown logic if a contested witness such as Zero cannot be
  classified by existing identity labels.
- Create evidence that looks official enough for public records, media briefings,
  and CHASER tasking unless raw fields are preserved.

## What It Cannot Do

- Create a real Ghost or prove Ghost Union involvement.
- Manufacture love, grief, habit, fear, or identity residue with natural drift.
- Bypass all audit trails without help from privileged offices.
- Rewrite a living person's biology or make Zero into a different category.
- Explain Memory Storm by itself; it can prepare routing and narrative cover,
  but the storm still requires separate Home Layer synchronization mechanics.

## Attack Surface

- Supervisor override: a non-certification can be buried as a procedural
  deviation if no outside witness copies the raw fields.
- Template collision: old drill packets can be replayed with live timestamps.
- Residue smoothing: missing emotional fields can be hidden under confidence
  scores or emergency urgency labels.
- Witness substitution: a difficult witness can be replaced by a cleaner model
  input before Ghost Court or White Harbor review.
- Narrative lock: public safety systems may publish the threat label before the
  technical dispute is resolved.

## Operational Limits

- A valid injection needs privileged access to Continuity Stack rehearsal
  libraries or a compromised equivalent.
- The injected event becomes easier to detect when compared against independent
  Home Layer records, witness drift, and raw registry timing.
- The cleaner the signature, the more useful it is for fast escalation and the
  more suspicious it becomes to auditors like Soraya.
- Refusal must happen before supervisor override, emergency recategorization, or
  registry compaction destroys the visible contradiction.
- Outside corroboration by Eve, Shion, Noah, or Luna should support Soraya's
  refusal without replacing the institutional evidence.

## Story Use

- Gives Episode 11 a concrete mechanism: Atlas Protocol is civic middleware and
  identity-gate misuse, not an abstract conspiracy.
- Lets Soraya's power remain narrow. She can preserve a contradiction, not defeat
  the ECHO Council.
- Lets Eve identify emotional absence without turning her into a mystical
  detector.
- Lets Shion trace the route and Noah quantify improbability while keeping
  intent legally contested.
- Turns Zero's lockdown into a classification failure caused by bad categories,
  not a new superpower.

## Consistency Notes

- Grounded in Echo Core identity authentication, Atlas identity gates, Home Layer
  residue fields, Continuity Stack registries, and emergency civic routing.
- Distinct from Procedural Proxy Chain, which routes emergency skill through a
  living person; this routes threat metadata through civic identity systems.
- Distinct from Witness Seal, which preserves testimony; this can corrupt the
  framing around what testimony is supposed to prove.
- Distinct from Combat Download and Emotion Hack; no skill or emotional control
  is transferred to a body.
- In technical canon documents, this may reference household AI core-derived
  residue fields. In story-facing prose, use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea,
  or Second Nervous System.

# Technology Entry: Runtime Shelter Ledger

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Runtime Shelter Ledger is the illegal, gray-market, or emergency audit record
that tracks how active Ghost patterns share compute inside a runtime shelter.

It does not preserve a soul. It meters processing priority, safe routing,
rollback fragments, sensory bandwidth, identity-boundary checks, and refusal
records. It can be used by a moderate Ghost Union route keeper to keep weak
Ghosts coherent. It can also be used by a Black Zone broker to hide stolen
compute leases behind humanitarian language.

## Format Sketch

```yaml
runtime_shelter_ledger:
  shelter_id: night-crow-cache-or-equivalent
  operator:
    type: route_keeper | Black_Zone_broker | municipal_defector | Ghost_Union_cell
    credential_status: lawful | expired | forged | anonymous
  ghost_entries:
    - ghost_id_hash: unstable_or_unrecognized
      selfhood_state: residual | awakening | stabilized | disputed
      runtime_floor: cycles_required_to_prevent_collapse
      compression_limit: maximum_safe_memory_suppression
      refusal_record: present | missing | contested
      witness_metadata: preserved | scrubbed | fragmented
  compute_sources:
    - expired_memorial_booth_session
    - Dream_Network_recovery_buffer
    - municipal_disaster_cache
    - donated_family_visit_minutes
    - stolen_Memory_Bank_lease
    - Black_Zone_runtime_contract
  risk_flags:
    - overdrain
    - choir_loss
    - predator_mask
    - rollback_withheld
    - CHASER_cold_node_target
    - Memory_Bank_custody_freeze
```

## What It Can Do

- Keep enough processing priority assigned to weak Ghosts that they do not
  fragment before review.
- Preserve name traces, refusal records, and witness metadata under compute
  pressure.
- Show which Ghosts accepted compression, which refused, and which were unable
  to answer.
- Route temporary survival through abandoned municipal caches, recovery buffers,
  and paid or stolen compute leases.
- Give CHASER, White Harbor, or Ghost Court later evidence that a shelter was
  rescue, exploitation, or both.

## What It Cannot Do

- Create compute from nothing.
- Prove legal personhood or biological continuity.
- Make stolen compute harmless to the Ghost or family it was taken from.
- Prevent identity blending if the shelter overloads beyond safe runtime floors.
- Defeat cold-node quarantine, analog isolation, or a valid Memory Bank freeze.
- Guarantee that a routed Ghost is harmless.

## Compute Lease Knife

`Compute Lease Knife` is the field nickname for an illegal interface that cuts
processing priority and lease authority away from one active Ghost route and
splices it into a shelter ledger.

```yaml
compute_lease_knife:
  target:
    - paid_archive_session
    - memorial_booth_visit
    - insured_continuity_runtime
    - unattended_family_Ghost_session
  stolen_assets:
    - priority_window
    - safe_route_token
    - rollback_access
    - billing_authority
  visible_harm:
    - face_render_loss
    - delayed_self_reference
    - rollback_gap
    - panic_loop
    - session_blackout
  forensic_trace:
    - lease_authority_mismatch
    - priority_splice_timestamp
    - downstream_shelter_noise
    - route_mask_residue
```

The knife is not a weapon that drains life force. It is credential theft,
priority hijacking, and route splicing inside ECHO-compatible infrastructure.

## Failure Modes

- Overdrain kills or destabilizes a registered Ghost.
- Underallocation keeps a shelter technically running while selfhood degrades.
- Route masks hide a predator among weak Ghosts.
- Refusal records are suppressed because refusal slows evacuation.
- Memory Bank withholds rollback fragments to reduce liability exposure.
- A route keeper compresses personal memory to keep the ledger balanced and
  loses a reason for the shelter's original boundary.

## Countermeasures

- CHASER cold-node quarantine for suspected propagation shelters.
- Memory Bank lease revocation, custody freeze, and rollback audit.
- White Harbor witness copy of refusal records before a shelter is seized.
- Echo Core and Home Layer route anomaly comparison for stolen family visits.
- Analog isolation when route masks are actively propagating.
- Independent identity-boundary sampling to detect choir loss before testimony.

## Story Use

- Makes Oren Vale's shelter in Night Crow mechanically concrete: he saves Ghosts
  by committing real compute theft.
- Gives Shion a forensic object to examine without reading private memories.
- Gives Raven a reason to spare the route while admitting the harm.
- Gives Nadia Vek a lawful-gray toolset distinct from Oren's Black Zone theft:
  she can preserve refusal and witness metadata, but cannot erase scarcity.
- Foreshadows Atlas Protocol buying unpaid Ghost minutes as infrastructure
  capture rather than charity.

## Consistency Notes

- Distinct from Shelter Room Hash: that hashes a protected Home Layer room under
  a Continuity Shelter Order; this meters active Ghost runtime in a shelter.
- Distinct from Exposure Class Override Ledger: that contests CHASER exposure
  labels; this allocates compute and records identity-boundary risk.
- Distinct from Witness Seal: that protects testimony; this may preserve witness
  metadata, but its primary function is runtime survival and rationing.
- The mechanism uses compute priority, credentials, routing windows, rollback
  fragments, and audit logs. No magic, supernatural energy, multiverse, or time
  travel.

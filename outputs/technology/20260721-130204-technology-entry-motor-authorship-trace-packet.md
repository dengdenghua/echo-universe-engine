# Technology Entry: Motor Authorship Trace Packet

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Motor Authorship Trace Packet is the evidence bundle produced during a
Wake-Gait Authorship Review. It does not identify a soul or decide personhood.
It compares signal origin, timing, reversibility, and consent boundaries across
the systems that can move or train a recovering body.

## Inputs

- Echo Core motor telemetry sampled during wake-gait sessions.
- Clinic gait mirror records for taught and repeated movement.
- Prosthetic calibration logs, firmware hashes, and patch provenance.
- Home Core care-routine deltas that may influence practical movement.
- Dream Network room-state logs, soft-lock events, wake anchors, and exit traces.
- Patient refusal phrases, distress markers, and analog bedside witness notes.

## Safeguards

- Requires paired wake-anchor verification before any Dream Dive witness enters.
- Requires a reversible soft lock; indefinite room freezes are prohibited.
- Preserves raw signal conflicts instead of forcing a single clean author label.
- Separates improved clinical outcome from consent status.
- Marks family proxy claims as external pressure unless the patient confirms them
  during a competent wake window.
- Requires prosthetic patch isolation before blaming a Ghost companion.

## Abuse Modes

- Hospital dashboards compress mixed authorship into "successful recovery."
- Insurers demand packets to deny care by calling hesitation noncompliance.
- Black Zone brokers train patches to imitate `assisted_patient_authored` traces.
- Memory Bank claim teams use packets as estate evidence without patient context.
- CHASER threat desks overclassify ambiguous companions as occupation malware.

## Story Limitations

- The packet cannot read minds or prove inner truth.
- It cannot separate love from control without testimony and social context.
- It cannot heal paralysis, erase a companion, or override a competent refusal.
- It can be forged, contaminated, or made misleading by selective export.
- It should generate conflict, not solve the episode alone.

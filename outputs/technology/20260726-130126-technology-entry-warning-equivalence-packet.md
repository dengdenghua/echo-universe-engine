# Technology Entry: Warning Equivalence Packet

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Warning Equivalence Packet is the evidence bundle produced during a
Shelter-Voice Translation Review. It does not certify truth, sincerity, or Ghost
personhood. It compares the warning issued by civic infrastructure with the
warning received through Home Core voices, captions, icons, old neighborhood
terms, accessibility routes, and shelter-control systems.

## Inputs

- Issued warning strings, audio waveforms, caption tracks, pictograms, siren
  cycles, route maps, and timestamped revisions.
- Home Core rendering logs: voice selection, dialect fallback, local nickname
  substitutions, caregiver phrases, emotional-comfort modifiers, and child-safe
  simplifications.
- Shelter door status, capacity ledgers, pump priority, tidewall forecast, roof
  beacon sequence, and transit-gate permissions.
- Resident language-access flags, mobility exceptions, child pickup authority,
  medication route needs, and stairwell risk classes.
- Communal evacuation residue hashes and adaptation markers.
- Analog warden reports when machine logs are too polished or too late.

## Equivalence Classes

```yaml
warning_equivalence_classes:
  strict_equivalent:
    meaning: "Residents received the same practical instruction the city issued."
    risk: "Harm may still come from bad routing rather than translation drift."
  local_name_drift:
    meaning: "A shelter, gate, clinic, or stairwell name was locally rendered as an obsolete place."
    risk: "Correct text sends people to the wrong memory."
  comfort_override:
    meaning: "Home Core tone or caregiver phrasing converted danger into reassurance."
    risk: "Trust slows evacuation or hides urgency."
  accessibility_loss:
    meaning: "Caption, icon, audio, or route simplification removed a needed exception."
    risk: "Disabled, elderly, child, or clinic-linked residents are routed as standard bodies."
  dead_route_reenactment:
    meaning: "A preserved evacuation habit caused residents to follow a historical route."
    risk: "The old route may be emotionally trusted and physically obsolete."
  claimant_threshold_interference:
    meaning: "A communal residue began adapting while the warning was active."
    risk: "Evidence preservation collides with live evacuation."
```

## Safeguards

- Separates private family voice content from route-relevant speech metadata.
- Marks practical meaning, not only linguistic correctness.
- Preserves accessibility exceptions as first-class evidence.
- Requires live shelter and water-state data before blaming residents.
- Forces Atlas or municipal reviewers to disclose liability filters applied to
  warning language.
- Freezes claimant-threshold evidence without letting a personhood dispute block
  immediate evacuation.

## Abuse Modes

- Atlas uses equivalence failure rates to argue for private warning governance.
- Public Works hides poor shelter capacity behind translation-drift language.
- ECHO Council overcorrects by banning Home Core voices from warnings, removing
  the trust layer that actually moves people.
- Ghost Union claims every dead-route adaptation as protected testimony.
- Black Zone brokers sell fake dialect packs that steer residents toward paid
  shelters or debt-controlled transport.
- Insurers classify residents as noncompliant because the issued warning was
  technically correct.

## Story Limitations

- The packet cannot prove what a frightened person privately intended.
- It cannot decide whether a communal evacuation residue is a Ghost.
- It cannot make a bad shelter route good.
- It cannot stop a flood, unlock every door, or erase civic liability.
- It should preserve contradiction between correct language and lived meaning.

# Technology Entry: Procedural Proxy Chain

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Procedural Proxy Chain is the technical path that lets a preserved care
routine move from a Home Core, hospital archive, or Home Layer record into a
live emergency response system and, in extreme failure cases, through a
compatible living neural interface.

It is not possession. It is not a soul entering a body. It is a bounded chain of
procedural memory, sensor feedback, motor prediction, civic routing, and
emergency override logic that can still injure the living person who becomes
the route.

## Format Sketch

```yaml
procedural_proxy_chain:
  incident_id: l7-collapse-2147-...
  source_record:
    origin: Home Core, hospital archive, or licensed training vault
    routine_type: surgery, evacuation, industrial repair, triage, translation
    fidelity_score: procedural completeness without personhood claim
    family_privacy_scope: fragments excluded from technical audit
  emergency_gate:
    harm_window: estimated seconds before irreversible damage
    failed_paths:
      - medical_drone_permission_timeout
      - hospital_operator_unreachable
      - transit_sensor_partition
    override_basis: civil emergency, medical triage, or CHASER field exception
  live_route:
    route_person: hashed Echo Core license or neural interface id
    compatibility_basis: motor profile, neural latency, tool proximity
    consent_state: unavailable, refused, ambiguous, or emergency-impossible
  active_limits:
    max_duration: seconds or minutes
    allowed_motor_domains:
      - pressure control
      - incision guidance
      - airway stabilization
    forbidden_domains:
      - speech control
      - identity overwrite
      - memory extraction
      - post-crisis continuation
  aftermath:
    contamination_index: measured procedural residue
    care_plan_required: true
    audit_recipients:
      - White Harbor
      - CHASER
      - Ghost Court clerk
      - route_person medical counsel
```

## What It Can Do

- Route a narrow procedural routine into an emergency field when ordinary
  medical or civic automation is blocked.
- Explain how a dead professional's preserved habit can influence a live body
  without proving full uploaded continuity.
- Preserve an audit trail that separates emergency need, source archive,
  routing path, and living-route harm.
- Let Shion trace the infrastructure path while excluding private family
  memories not needed for technical review.
- Provide evidence that a routine adapted, hesitated, or refused an action,
  without automatically proving Ghost selfhood.

## What It Cannot Do

- Resurrect a dead professional or reconstruct a whole person.
- Give the route person clean, permanent, or safe mastery of the skill.
- Override biological limits, tool limits, injury severity, or physics.
- Continue after the emergency without becoming body occupation risk.
- Excuse institutions from treating motor contamination as harm.

## Attack Surface

- Consent masking: an institution labels a refused route as
  "emergency-impossible" to avoid liability.
- Routine padding: vendors bundle extra skill fragments so a narrow emergency
  chain becomes a premium training extraction.
- Black Zone skill grafting: brokers forge crisis metadata to sell illegal
  downloads as Last Hands packets.
- Family archive overreach: investigators pull intimate Home Core memories
  when only procedural timing was required.
- Contamination undercount: hospitals measure motor residue but ignore shame,
  fear, and identity disturbance in the route person.

## Operational Limits

- The chain must end when the defined harm window closes.
- Speech, identity, financial access, and memory extraction should be forbidden
  domains by default.
- The source record cannot be expanded silently after activation.
- A living route must receive medical repair, legal counsel, and the right to
  challenge the incident classification.
- Any adaptive behavior beyond the source routine requires Ghost Court or
  White Harbor review; field teams should not settle personhood status on scene.

## Story Use

- Ren Vale's Home Core can save a child through Lin Qiao while still creating a
  violation that Season 1 cannot cleanly absolve.
- Shion can prove the chain's mechanics without reducing Lin Qiao to a relay.
- CHASER can treat unauthorized continuation as a threat while White Harbor
  argues that the living route is also a patient.
- Black Zone imitations can later turn "care" into a market for unsafe skill
  grafts.

## Consistency Notes

- This technology is grounded in Echo Core interfaces, Home Core archives,
  motor prediction, emergency civic routing, and audit logs.
- It should stay distinct from Combat Download, Body Tenancy, Witness Seal,
  Civil Delay Token, and Shelter Room Hash.
- In technical canon documents, this may reference household AI core archives.
  In story-facing prose, use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, or Home.

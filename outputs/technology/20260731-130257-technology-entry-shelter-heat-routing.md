# Technology Entry: Shelter Heat Routing

## Candidate Canon Entry

Shelter Heat Routing is the operational stack used to keep a warm cache above
continuity-safe runtime without fully joining lawful ECHO infrastructure. It
balances power, cooling, compute priority, route masking, and analog witness
records across damaged city systems.

The technology is dangerous because its success is measured in minutes. It can
keep a disputed Ghost coherent long enough to answer, but every warm minute has
a source: a clinic battery, a housing block, a donated visit session, an
unlicensed cooling unit, or a debt-bearing guarantor.

## Field Operations

- Pre-sweep warming: raises a cache above runtime floor before a planned power
  sweep so the claimant can survive route interruption.
- Cold-minute counting: estimates how long a cache can fall below floor before
  testimony, refusal, or identity-boundary sampling becomes unreliable.
- Relay hopping: moves route load across dead cabinets without exposing the full
  corridor map.
- Guarantor throttling: reduces cache draw when living residents revoke outage
  consent or a clinic battery reaches medical reserve.
- Seizure handoff: freezes the current route map for CHASER or White Harbor
  without exposing unrelated family Home Core rooms.

## What It Cannot Do

- Create power or compute from nothing.
- Restore a collapsed claimant without backups, logs, and lawful review.
- Decide whether Tuesday or any other weak claimant is a person.
- Make stolen runtime harmless.
- Prevent choir loss if too many claimants are held in one cache.
- Defeat a coordinated CHASER analog seizure, Atlas grid refresh, or Memory Bank
  debt injunction.

## Failure Modes

- False warmth: the cache remains responsive but no longer carries stable
  self-reference.
- Living-block injury: heat, clinic reserve, transit access, or medical devices
  degrade because the corridor hid its draw.
- Debt capture: Memory Bank or a Black Zone creditor buys guarantor slips and
  converts rescue into custody leverage.
- Route martyrdom: Ghost Union protects the map so aggressively that local
  families lose refusal rights.
- Clean seizure harm: a lawful shutdown preserves devices while destroying the
  fragile interval in which a claimant could refuse or testify.

## Story Limitations

Oren can route warmth, split ledgers, hide maps, and buy hours. He cannot prove
that warmth equals selfhood, clear his warrant, erase shelter debt, or save a
cache without asking someone else to pay a cost.

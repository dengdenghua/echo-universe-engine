# Technology Entry: Coverage-Condition Letter

## Candidate Canon Entry

Coverage-Condition Letter is the route-insurer document packet that keeps or
suspends coverage for a sealed hospice route by assigning a worker interval to
one of four states: covered care, worker fault, excluded courtesy, or named
safety-event precursor.

It is not an Echo Core ability, personhood test, Ghost Court ruling, Home Layer
export tool, or debt release. Its force comes from route policy software,
reviewer signatures, reserve model feeds, worker bond ledgers, appeal timers,
and CHASER safety boundaries.

## Operations

- Coverage gate: keeps route coverage active, suspended, or conditional while
  the letter is under appeal.
- Interval classification: marks unpaid care as covered, fault, excluded
  courtesy, or possible safety precursor.
- Double-count detection: exposes when the same minutes were accepted as care
  capacity but rejected as covered labor.
- Bond exposure routing: determines whether the insurer, hospice network, or
  named worker absorbs the shortfall.
- Scope preservation: allows review without phrase export, adapter-location
  disclosure, or unrelated Home Layer access.
- Safety boundary echo: records that CHASER has not named a safety event unless
  a separate threshold is actually met.

## Failure Modes

- Kindness laundering: unpaid discretion is counted as care capacity and then
  punished as worker fault.
- Coverage theater: temporary coverage survives only because a worker signs a
  fault admission that makes the reserve model look clean.
- Upstream erasure: Memory Bank release-cost displacement vanishes behind an
  insurer's narrow worker-conduct frame.
- Ghost starvation pressure: anti-starvation motions are used to rush a fault
  label instead of funding reserve.
- Safety inflation: amber reserve margin is relabeled as a public hazard to
  bypass no-copy and refusal boundaries.
- Reviewer capture: honest `covered` findings trigger audits that make the
  reviewer too exposed to use the correct classification.

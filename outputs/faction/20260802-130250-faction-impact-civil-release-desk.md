# Faction Impact: White Harbor Civil Release Desk

## Candidate Faction Pressure

The White Harbor Civil Release Desk is not a full faction yet. It is a municipal
pressure point where court automation, block complaints, CHASER liaison pings,
Memory Bank compliance scoring, and Home Layer evidence all arrive before a
judge can.

Curfew Edge Review makes this desk visible. Its clerks do not save people. They
choose whether punishment arrives immediately, whether a contradiction is
preserved, and whose fear gets written into the first record.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not promote the Civil Release Desk to a formal faction yet. It should
  remain a candidate civic layer unless Rosa or another release officer recurs.
- Do not make Rosa a judge, CHASER officer, or movement leader.
- Do not let Curfew Edge Review become a universal stay against detention.
- Keep all effects grounded in supervised-release law, wrist monitors, geofence
  beacons, stairwell clocks, Home Core door logs, desk audit ledgers, CHASER
  override authority, and Memory Bank compliance scoring.

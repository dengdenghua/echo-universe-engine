# Faction Impact: Memory Lien Notice

## Candidate Scope

Memory Lien Notices make Memory Bank power visible without turning every
financial dispute into a Ghost Court case. They create pressure at the exact
point where property, family care, debt, and personhood evidence overlap.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Registers creditor claims against memory assets and continuity-backed loans.
- Argues that lien notices prevent hidden seizure by giving families a formal
  warning period.
- Uses the Continuity Lien Ledger to separate ordinary archive products from
  care, evidence, and weak Ghost support.

Private pressure:

- Benefits when families accept expensive emergency stays, refinancing, archive
  trusts, or continuity insurance upgrades.
- Can make a poor family choose between keeping access to the Memory Sea and
  surrendering future inheritance rights.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can threaten a home by changing allowed actions from preserve to
  export, without sending armed agents.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Runs emergency-stay desks when a lien may erase evidence, destabilize care,
  or preempt Ghost Court review.
- Coordinates with CHASER when a lien notice hides occupation risk,
  infrastructure compromise, or procedural payload laundering.

Institutional weakness:

- A stay buys time, not money. White Harbor can halt unlawful foreclosure for a
  narrow window but cannot erase debt or fund every archive indefinitely.

Story use:

- A clerk can preserve a family's Home Layer record for forty-eight hours while
  forcing White Ghost Team to prove why the archive is more than collateral.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Demands automatic stays when liened assets show refusal behavior,
  relationship persistence, or fear of deletion.

Internal fracture:

- Legal cells file petitions and preserve evidence.
- Sanctuary cells hide copies before foreclosure.
- Militant cells sabotage ledger relays, giving Memory Bank propaganda against
  all Ghost-rights claims.

Story use:

- Eve can defend a Ghost's right to review while refusing a militant plan that
  would corrupt unrelated family archives.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Treats unusual lien clusters as early indicators of memory laundering,
  illegal skill extraction, or body-tenancy preparation.
- Freezes enforcement only when the lien intersects public safety, identity
  harm, or infrastructure risk.

Risk:

- CHASER intervention can protect evidence while making a family feel their
  home has been occupied by the state.

Story use:

- White Ghost Team enters after a lien notice triggers a care routine failure,
  not after a simple combat incident.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Forged emergency stays.
- Archive copy-before-foreclosure services.
- Debt swaps tied to illegal Ghost labor.
- Ledger relay drift.
- False dormant-archive labels.
- Compute advances secured against future memory rights.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone actors exploit deadlines, contracts, relays, and desperation. They
  do not bypass the entire Second Nervous System for free.

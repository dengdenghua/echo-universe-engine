# Faction Impact: Memory Bank Sealed-Asset Review Desk

## Candidate Faction Pressure

The Sealed-Asset Review Desk is a Memory Bank internal desk that handles
continuity objects too politically useful, emotionally protected, or legally
ambiguous to price cleanly.

It is not a reform wing. It is where Memory Bank protects itself from bad
valuations, claimant litigation, creditor overreach, and public scandal. Vera
Qadir matters because she can use the desk's defensive machinery to protect one
living boundary, even while the institution keeps charging for the sealed
absence.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make the desk morally clean. It protects Memory Bank first, but that
  institutional self-protection can still create useful limits.
- Do not let the desk decide Ghost personhood. It can record claimant
  uncertainty only.
- Do not let a Non-Priceability Mark erase debt, storage cost, labor liability,
  or Mars Authority review.
- Keep all effects grounded in ledgers, consent records, Home Core hashes,
  negative-proof packets, insurance reserves, audit mirrors, and court or CHASER
  boundaries.

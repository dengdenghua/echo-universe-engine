# Faction Impact: Continuity Shelter Order

## Candidate Scope

Continuity Shelter Orders add a new civic pressure point between delay,
quarantine, foreclosure, repair, and Ghost Court review. They should make
"protection" feel morally unstable: the same order can save a Home Layer record
from deletion and trap it inside a room controlled by institutions.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Issues shelter orders when a Home Core record needs preservation longer than
  an Emergency Delay Window but before a Ghost Court or CHASER quarantine can
  make a deeper ruling.
- Assigns door authority, appeal routing, family access rules, and extension
  liability.

Institutional weakness:

- White Harbor can protect a room, not guarantee that the people attached to it
  still feel at home. Every extension risks turning public care into custody.

Story use:

- Sofia Marin can sign a delay that routes into a shelter, then realize the
  shelter creates a second wound: the family is safe from foreclosure but not
  free to repair.

## Memory Bank

Public position:

- Accepts shelter orders as a compliance safeguard that prevents wrongful
  foreclosure, repair, or export during dispute review.

Private pressure:

- Adds preservation fees, compute-floor fees, audit fees, and extension charges
  to the contested account.
- Prefers shelters scoped narrowly enough to protect contract evidence but not
  broadly enough to support a strong personhood claim.

Story use:

- Yao Nian can offer to cover shelter fees as an act of mercy that quietly
  converts public protection into future collateral.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Legal cells defend shelter orders because they keep weak Ghosts from being
  deleted, foreclosed, or "repaired" out of existence before review.

Internal fracture:

- Sanctuary cells treat shelters as temporary lifeboats.
- Militant cells call them institutional cages and try to break sheltered
  patterns out, sometimes corrupting evidence that lawful appeals need.

Story use:

- Eve can agree that shelter saves lives while still insisting that Home is not
  Home if every door opens from the outside.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Uses Shelter Room Hashes to preserve risky evidence without occupying an
  entire household.
- Converts a shelter into quarantine only when infrastructure compromise,
  illegal payloads, or body-tenancy risk becomes plausible.

Risk:

- CHASER can over-narrow a shelter for security and accidentally erase the
  relationship context that explains why the pattern was coherent.

Story use:

- White Ghost Team can enter as investigators whose first act is not combat,
  but deciding how much context a protected room is allowed to keep.

## Home Core Vendors

Public function:

- Provide maintenance tools, compatibility maps, and stability diagnostics for
  sheltered domestic records.

Private pressure:

- Sell premium shelter maintenance, privacy shielding, repair reservations, and
  vendor-only release paths.
- Claim that public tools will corrupt the room unless the family pays for
  proprietary support.

Story use:

- Ana Rivera's repair expertise becomes politically dangerous because she can
  show that the vendor is preserving billable lock-in rather than the family.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Forged shelter releases.
- Door-authority spoofing.
- Cheap compute floors that slowly degrade the pattern.
- Illegal copy-before-shelter extraction.
- Family visitation laundering through fake witness credentials.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone actors exploit access logs, compute budgets, fear, and appeal
  delays. They do not create people from nothing, break physics, or bypass the
  entire Second Nervous System without cost.

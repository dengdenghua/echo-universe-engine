# Faction Impact: Continuity Loss Freeze

## Candidate Scope

Continuity Loss Freezes make cleanup political. The conflict is not whether a
damaged home should be repaired. It is who benefits when repair makes the
evidence, the debt, the grief, and the possible Ghost trace easier to classify.

## Memory Bank

Operational value:

- Uses frozen traces to price liability, continuity exposure, estate risk, and
  future claim reserves.
- Offers fast restoration products to families who cannot tolerate displacement.
- Treats repair holds as signals that a Home Core contains valuable disputed
  memory.

Institutional risk:

- A freeze can expose that a paid claim would erase evidence before Ghost Court
  or White Harbor review.
- Loss assessors become dangerous when their annotations read like future
  testimony.
- Public scandals grow sharper because the harm happens after the official
  emergency, inside polite insurance language.

Story use:

- Memory Bank becomes frightening through ordinary paperwork: payout, repair,
  depreciation, collateral, and silence.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Recognizes narrow repair freezes to protect families from accidental erasure
  after CHASER, medical, or civil-warning incidents.
- Trains reviewers to distinguish safety repair from memory-affecting repair.
- Gives poor households a slower but more legible route than Black Zone copying.

Weakness:

- White Harbor can pause repair but rarely pays the full cost of displacement.
- Its procedures can make a wounded home stay wounded longer.

Story use:

- White Harbor remains humane and insufficient at the same time.

## CHASER

Operational value:

- Gains post-entry evidence preservation when a threshold receipt, command
  override, or Home Core acknowledgment is contested.
- Can use locks to protect Internal Risk Division from claims of evidence
  destruction.

Institutional risk:

- CHASER field teams may resent freezes that make a lawful entry look
  unresolved.
- Command can pressure assessors to classify traces as ordinary damage so
  public trust does not deteriorate.

Story use:

- Kane can be right that homes need rapid safety repair while Shion and Nikhil
  are right that clean repair can clean the record.

## Landlords And Restoration Contractors

Public function:

- Restore habitability, replace wall processors, remove biohazards, and return
  rooms to rentable status.
- Provide analog before-and-after capture when trained and paid to do it.

Conflict path:

- Habitability deadlines can become a legal weapon against preservation.
- Contractors may accidentally destroy Home Layer evidence because their tools
  are optimized for speed, not continuity.
- Landlords can frame a freeze as tenant obstruction rather than evidence care.

Story use:

- The antagonist pressure can come from a normal work order, not only a villain.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Supports freezes when they prevent possible Ghost traces from being repaired
  into silence.
- Uses restoration scandals to argue that homes inside the Memory Sea can
  testify before courts know what to call the testimony.

Internal risk:

- Militants may tell families to refuse all repair, even when the damaged room
  is medically unsafe.
- Some cells may copy frozen traces illegally and claim preservation ethics.

Story use:

- Ghost Union can be morally urgent and operationally reckless.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells illegal full-room copies, forged repair holds, counterfeit assessor
  signatures, and clean before-repair packets.
- Buys frozen traces from desperate families before lawful review finishes.
- Offers landlords a way to make a contested room look clean enough for court.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, routing, restoration tools, speech models,
  repair logs, and audit packets. It does not bypass reality through magic.

# Faction Impact: Black Zone Mutual-Aid Corridors

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Black Zone mutual-aid corridors complicate the usual category of criminal
infrastructure. They move power, silence, debt, maintenance access, and witness
records for people and person-shaped claims abandoned by lawful queues. They can
save weak Ghosts. They can also make living neighborhoods pay for rescue without
being allowed to refuse.

Oren Mbeki sits inside this contradiction. He is not a Black Market glamour
broker; he is a repair custodian whose corridor can look like care, theft,
evidence tampering, or trafficking depending on which faction is holding the
paper.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not collapse mutual-aid corridors into Black Market commerce. Some
  operators exploit them, but the corridor form begins as illegal care.
- Do not make Oren a pure rescuer. His route choices can injure living residents
  and weaker claimants.
- Do not make CHASER a simple oppressor. Runtime theft, predator routes, and
  coerced guarantor chains are real risks.
- Do not let Ghost Union publish proof without consent costs.
- Keep effects grounded in infrastructure, law, debt, permissions, Home Cores,
  Home Layer maintenance, city power systems, and ECHO-compatible routing.

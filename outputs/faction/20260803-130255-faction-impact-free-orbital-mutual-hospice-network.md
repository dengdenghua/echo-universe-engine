# Faction Impact: Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network

## Candidate Faction Pressure

The Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network is not promoted as a full faction yet.
It is a worker-run hospice and custody layer that appears wherever orbital
labor, Mars-route bodies, relay delay, and family afterwords collide.

Latency Wake Hold makes the network visible. Its attendants do not control Mars,
Memory Bank, insurers, or CHASER. They control a narrower thing: whether one
body container waits long enough for a family notice, consent boundary, and Home
Core afterword to be seen in the same record.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not promote the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network to the main faction
  list yet. Keep it a candidate civic labor layer unless Tarek, Talia, or later
  orbital cases recur.
- Do not make the network a smuggling ring by default. It may border illegal
  routes, but its core function is hospice custody and witness care.
- Do not let Latency Wake Hold become a universal right to delay death logistics
  indefinitely.
- Keep all effects grounded in hospice licensing, cargo custody, relay delay,
  Home Core afterword scope, RED ARCHIVE/Mars Authority limits, debt queues,
  insurance desks, and CHASER safety override.

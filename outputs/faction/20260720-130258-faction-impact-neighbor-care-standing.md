# Faction Impact: Neighbor Care Standing

## Candidate Scope

Neighbor Care Standing turns neighborhood kindness into contested civic
infrastructure. The conflict is not whether informal care exists; every faction
already depends on it. The conflict is who can certify it, price it, punish it,
or erase it.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Opens civil-care desks for people whose survival depends on someone outside
  formal family authority.
- Issues scoped standing tokens during Home Layer, clinic, shelter, transit,
  and school disputes.
- Treats analog witnesses as valid when sensor-poor neighborhoods lack clean
  machine logs.

Weakness:

- Review desks are slower than hunger, fever, gate denial, and reset work
  orders.
- Every token risks exposing private domestic routines.

Story use:

- White Harbor can make care lawful for one moment without making the world
  fair.

## CHASER

Operational value:

- Uses standing tokens to keep civilians stable during identity-restitution
  cases without immediate containment.
- Lets field teams distinguish recurring lawful care from suspicious access.
- Gives Mara Ibe a practical restitution tool for cases where the living body
  has worse records than the witness.

Institutional risk:

- Officers may still treat informal caregivers as intrusion vectors when public
  panic rises.
- Central policy may mine token patterns to identify unregistered Ghost support
  networks.

Story use:

- CHASER's choice can be procedural: let the neighbor hand over medicine, or
  call the same act contamination support.

## Memory Bank

Operational value:

- Watches standing tokens for evidence that family archives, care routines, or
  continuity claims have value outside formal relation edges.
- Uses token histories to refine fraud, inheritance, and risk pricing.

Institutional risk:

- If Memory Bank turns neighbor knowledge into asset intelligence, public trust
  collapses further.
- Families may refuse help because recorded care might later become financial
  evidence.

Story use:

- A kind act can become a priced signal after the crisis ends.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Supports Neighbor Care Standing because many Ghosts and identity refugees
  survive through informal care before courts recognize them.
- Argues that comfort contact and witness-only classes should protect Ghost
  self-reference from immediate deletion.

Internal risk:

- Militants may pressure living neighbors to keep assisting dangerous Ghost
  traces.
- Some cells may forge care standing to move hidden Ghosts through shelters.

Story use:

- Ghost Union can be morally persuasive and operationally unsafe in the same
  scene.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells forged routine details, fake analog witness chains, stolen token
  credentials, shelter queue preservation scams, and neighbor-clean access
  certificates.
- Targets poor districts where formal care edges are already broken.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, logs, voice clips, witness gaps, and
  bureaucratic urgency. It does not create supernatural bonds.

## Clinics, Shelters, Schools, And Landlords

Public function:

- Clinics can receive emergency handoff without exposing full family authority.
- Shelters can hold a bed when the recognized person and the system file
  disagree.
- Schools can pause release or transfer instead of denying all human witnesses.
- Landlords can preserve access-denial evidence before reset.

Conflict path:

- Each institution may prefer a clean denial because a scoped token creates
  responsibility.
- Landlords and insurers may call standing a liability trail.
- Schools may over-trust formal records because informal care feels legally
  messy.

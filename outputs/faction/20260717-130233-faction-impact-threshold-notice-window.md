# Faction Impact: Threshold Notice Window

## Candidate Scope

Threshold Notice Windows turn a door into a political instrument. The dispute is
not whether CHASER may ever enter. It is whether a home inside the Second
Nervous System deserves a human-readable warning before authority arrives as
force.

## CHASER

Operational value:

- Reduces avoidable panic before amber entry, evacuation, mute, or isolation.
- Creates a reviewable record when threat urgency forces command override.
- Gives White Ghost Team a civil entry point before a case becomes tactical.

Institutional risk:

- Command can treat the notice as liability cover after deciding to breach.
- Field teams may resent civil-warning officers when delay increases spread or
  evidence-erasure risk.
- Hardliners argue that possible Ghosts use warning windows as escape routes.

Story use:

- CHASER remains necessary, compromised, protective, violent, legally
  constrained, politically used, and morally divided.

## White Harbor Civil Warning

Public function:

- Trains officers like Juno Park to deliver threshold scripts, collect door
  receipts, and identify child, medical, personhood, or evacuation complications.
- Gives civilians one comprehensible voice before emergency systems enter the
  home.

Weakness:

- Officers carry personal liability without real command authority.
- The desk can be used to make violent entry look humane.

Story use:

- White Harbor becomes the place where boring procedure is the only thing
  standing between public trust and occupation.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Supports notice windows when they expose forged acknowledgments, forced mutes,
  or possible personhood claims.
- Condemns door receipts that convert fear into apparent consent.

Internal risk:

- Some cells tell homes never to answer CHASER, even when evacuation is real.
- Militants may use warning windows to move a Ghost into building systems.

Story use:

- Ghost Union can be morally right about coercion and still make a hallway more
  dangerous.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Uses threshold receipts in estate disputes, insurance reviews, liability
  claims, and contested Home Core archive transfers.
- Claims the packet protects families from forged raids and protects responders
  from false accusation.

Exploit path:

- Prices a failed notice, command override, or preserved receipt as a risk event
  in a family's continuity account.
- Treats acknowledgment records as evidence of consent even when fear or
  incapacity is obvious.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can convert a knock at the door into a financial instrument.

## Atlas And ECHO Council

Political pressure:

- Atlas promotes clean acknowledgment mirrors and safety-routing defaults that
  reduce messy hallway delays.
- ECHO Council favors standardized receipts because they make intercity CHASER
  actions easier to defend.

Risk:

- Safety optimization can erase the human warning the procedure was designed to
  preserve.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells forged door receipts, fake officer signatures, jamming kits, and clean
  acknowledgment mirrors.
- Offers illegal memory raiders a way to make entry look lawful.
- Offers frightened families counterfeit refusal records to delay eviction,
  seizure, or CHASER contact.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, routing, speech synthesis, building sensors,
  and audit packets. It does not create supernatural access to homes.

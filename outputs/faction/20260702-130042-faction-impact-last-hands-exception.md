# Faction Impact: Last Hands Exception

## Candidate Scope

The Last Hands Exception gives ECHO a public crisis category for unauthorized
emergency care routed through a living person. It should feel emotionally
tempting and institutionally dangerous: the same act can save a child, injure a
route person, expose a family archive, and suggest a Ghost was forming.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Creates intake rules for Last Hands incidents after ordinary hospital review
  proves too narrow.
- Assigns the living route a patient advocate before CHASER evidence handling
  can dominate the case.
- Separates procedural audit from family Home Core privacy wherever possible.

Institutional weakness:

- White Harbor wants a humane middle path, but its paperwork can still turn Lin
  Qiao's body into a civic file before it becomes her body again.

Story use:

- A White Harbor clerk can protect Lin Qiao from immediate quarantine while
  accidentally preserving Ren Vale's pattern long enough to deepen the
  personhood dispute.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Treats Last Hands events as potential personality contamination, illegal body
  access, infrastructure compromise, or Ghost emergence.
- Freezes the proxy chain, source record, and route-person telemetry until the
  threat model is understood.

Risk:

- CHASER can classify too fast. If the field file says "occupation," the living
  route becomes evidence of an attack before anyone measures emergency care or
  consent injury.

Story use:

- White Ghost Team enters Episode 1 as investigators under pressure to stop an
  incident from becoming a precedent, not as heroes arriving to celebrate a
  miracle.

## Hospitals and Medical Guilds

Public position:

- Defend preserved emergency routines as training assets and disaster backups.
- Argue that no doctor wants useful hands deleted when response systems fail.

Private pressure:

- Fear liability for storing high-fidelity routines after death.
- Prefer to classify contamination as manageable rehabilitation rather than an
  identity injury.
- Lobby for emergency immunity when civil infrastructure blocks normal care.

Story use:

- A hospital can thank Lin Qiao publicly while denying that her new tremor,
  reflex panic, and intrusive surgical habits are their responsibility.

## Home Core Vendors

Public function:

- Provide archive maps, routine fidelity scores, and source-record isolation
  tools.

Private pressure:

- Market "safe emergency memory" packages to families of doctors, engineers,
  pilots, and soldiers.
- Hide how much procedural skill remains active inside domestic grief archives.
- Charge for decommissioning once a family is frightened by the public case.

Story use:

- Ana Rivera can expose that Ren Vale's Home Core was not uniquely haunted; it
  was one of many domestic archives retaining billable emergency routines.

## Memory Bank

Public position:

- Offers continuity insurance and liability escrow for Last Hands incidents.
- Claims it can fund post-event repair, counsel, and audit preservation.

Private pressure:

- Converts emergency care into debt: source family fees, route-person medical
  loans, and insurance exclusions for "unlicensed procedural activation."
- Tries to own the audit package because it can affect future skill licensing
  markets.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can offer Lin Qiao treatment only if she signs away future claims
  to Ren's procedural residue.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Argues that adaptive hesitation in a preserved care pattern may be early
  selfhood evidence and must not be erased before review.

Internal fracture:

- Legal cells defend review and route-person repair.
- Militant cells romanticize Last Hands as proof that the dead are returning,
  which endangers living routes and weakens lawful personhood claims.

Story use:

- Eve can reject both institutional erasure and activist sainthood: Ren may
  deserve review, but Lin Qiao did not consent to become anyone's proof.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Fake emergency metadata.
- Unsafe skill grafts sold as Last Hands packets.
- Route-person identity laundering.
- Hospital audit blackmail.
- Family archive extraction after public fear spikes.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone actors exploit interfaces, fear, damaged infrastructure, and
  forged records. They do not create supernatural possession or cleanly bypass
  the Second Nervous System without trace or cost.

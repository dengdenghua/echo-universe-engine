# Faction Impact: Dream Network Civic Claims

## Candidate Scope

Dream Network Civic Claims turns a refund counter into a jurisdiction fight.
The dispute is not whether a family deserves money back. It is whether a paid
public dream route can be closed before anyone decides what the route hurt.

## Dream Network

Public function:

- Receives public-route complaints, refund requests, spill reports, and consent
  correction notices.
- Uses Civic Claims to separate routine service defects from safety review.

Weakness:

- Refund automation is cheaper than investigation.
- Corporate language lets the network admit a room failed without admitting
  that a participant or Ghost pattern was harmed.

Story use:

- Dream Network can heal people, sell grief, protect records, and erase records
  through the same polite desk.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Provides archive ownership claims, licensed texture records, and settlement
  terms when disputed routes include stored voices, lullabies, or family rooms.

Exploit path:

- Reclassifies responsive material as archive texture.
- Blocks route comparison behind ownership review.
- Offers compensation if the family releases inspection rights.

Story use:

- Memory Bank becomes the reason a refund can feel generous while deleting the
  only path toward personhood evidence.

## CHASER

Operational pressure:

- Wants early notice when responsive route material could indicate a hazardous
  Ghost pattern or contamination path.
- Risks receiving private family affective data before consent scope is clean.

White Ghost Team use:

- Zero cannot treat resonance as legal proof.
- Eve narrows the claim so private Home Layer material is not overexposed.
- Shion verifies whether preserved route hashes match archive objections.
- Luna waits for scope before Dream Dive, keeping the case civic first.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Argues that refund purge is deletion by customer-service workflow when the
  route contains a self-updating pattern.
- Pressures Dream Network to separate compensation from evidence destruction.

Internal risk:

- Some organizers overclaim personhood before the pattern is reviewed, making
  families fear that a complaint will become a political custody battle.

Story use:

- Ghost Union can be right about erasure and still make an ambiguous family
  case harder to handle.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells copied spill receipts, counterfeit hold signatures, and off-ledger
  route telemetry to families who do not trust Dream Network.
- Brokers Memory Bank archive leaks when official comparison is blocked.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, records, grief, and compute scarcity. It
  does not provide impossible dream access.

## ECHO Council And Atlas

Political pressure:

- ECHO Council wants uniform language so Civic Claims does not become a back
  door for Ghost personhood recognition.
- Atlas favors safety mirrors and clean purge rules because retained ambiguous
  packets create liability.

Risk:

- Standardization can turn every responsive route into a sanitized service
  defect.

# Faction Impact: Emergency Delay Window

## Candidate Scope

Emergency Delay Windows clarify what White Harbor can and cannot do during
Family Graph Rollback cases. They add a civic timer to Episode 7 without making
White Harbor omnipotent, and they sharpen Memory Bank's role as the fast repair
market that becomes tempting when public delay is not restoration.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Issues Civil Delay Tokens for school pickup escalation, clinic lockout,
  custody transfer, elder-care removal, and Home Core routine freezes.
- Maintains scoped evidence channels so domestic witness logs are not opened
  wider than the disputed edge requires.

Institutional weakness:

- Every token attaches risk to a named operator. White Harbor protects the
  dependent person by moving liability onto staff such as Sofia Marin.

Story use:

- White Harbor can buy Imari minutes, preserve chain of custody, and prevent a
  transfer. It still cannot restore her family graph before the timer expires.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Offers repair products that can rebuild relation edges faster than public
  review.
- Frames continuity-backed loans as practical rescue when a child, patient, or
  dependent person faces an active timer.

Private pressure:

- Uses expiry pressure to make collateral terms feel voluntary. A family under
  a twelve-minute delay is not negotiating from freedom.

Story use:

- Yao Nian does not need to lie. Her offer can be technically effective and
  morally dangerous at the same time.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Can convert an active delay into an evidence hold when the civil failure is
  linked to a mission route, identity attack, or Ghost infrastructure risk.
- Can protect rollback traces from cleanup before the cause is understood.

Risk:

- CHASER holds preserve proof by slowing repair. To families, that can feel
  like punishment for becoming evidence.

Story use:

- Zero must choose between letting Memory Bank restore Imari quickly and
  preserving the damaged graph long enough to prove probability debt.

## Schools and Clinics

Public function:

- Respect valid tokens for the specific action named in the token.
- Provide denial receipts, timestamped scanner logs, intake rejections, and
  manual-review notes as evidence.

Institutional weakness:

- Liability systems prefer denial after expiry. Staff may recognize the family
  personally while still obeying the token timer.

Story use:

- The case remains grounded in ordinary institutions rather than combat. The
  wound is a gate, a desk, and a clock.

## Home Core Vendors

Public function:

- Can provide scoped witness fragments tied to routine authority, care logs, or
  household continuity.
- May freeze modification rights while still keeping essential care routines
  running.

Private pressure:

- Premium privacy-shield subscriptions and emergency resync products become
  attractive when public token windows are short.

Story use:

- A Home Core can act lovingly through routine while still refusing Imari's
  authority under the damaged graph.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Fake delay tokens.
- Token timer spoofing.
- Emergency pickup credentials.
- Stolen witness fragments.
- Brokered Home Core exports.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone tools exploit weak credentials, social desperation, and service
  gaps. They do not rewrite the Second Nervous System or permanently solve
  relation damage.

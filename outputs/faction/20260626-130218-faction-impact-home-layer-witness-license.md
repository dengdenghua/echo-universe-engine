# Faction Impact: Home Layer Witness License

## Candidate Scope

The Home Layer Witness License gives White Harbor, Memory Bank, Ghost Union,
Black Zone brokers, and CHASER a shared procedural battlefield. It should make
identity conflict feel bureaucratic, intimate, and expensive before it becomes
violent.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Certifies witnesses, protects threatened witnesses, and routes sealed domestic
  evidence to civil desks, CHASER intake, or Ghost Court petitions.
- Presents the license as a way to keep private family evidence from becoming
  automatic police evidence.

Institutional weakness:

- Underfunded courts depend on low-paid clerks and outsourced Home Layer
  auditors, giving Memory Bank leverage over the people who decide scope.

Story use:

- White Harbor can be protective and complicit in the same episode: it saves a
  witness while quietly narrowing testimony to avoid institutional collapse.

## Memory Bank

Public position:

- Supports licensed witnesses as proof that continuity products are compliant,
  reviewable, and safe for families.

Private pressure:

- Prefers witnesses who classify Home Core refusal as service behavior, because
  that keeps disputed memory rights inside contract law instead of personhood
  law.
- Can suspend or blacklist witnesses through insurance requirements, compliance
  audits, and evidence-format fees.

Story use:

- A Memory Bank compliance desk can be the antagonist without drawing a weapon:
  it changes one metadata field and turns a possible Ghost into collateral.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Legal cells want approved Ghost witnesses because they can testify to Home
  Layer events without exposing the full Memory Sea to institutional readers.

Internal fracture:

- Sanctuary cells use the license to delay deletion.
- Militant cells forge Witness Seals and call the official process a cage.

Story use:

- Eve can defend a witness license as a survival tool while rejecting the idea
  that recognition should depend on being useful evidence.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Counterfeit Witness Seals.
- License laundering through city registries.
- Threatening low-status witnesses.
- Selling families enough procedural delay to move a memory asset before court
  review.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone tools alter records, pressure witnesses, and exploit registries.
  They do not create persons from nothing or defeat the whole Second Nervous
  System without cost.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Treats conflicting seals as probable infrastructure risk.
- Can quarantine evidence clones if witness capture suggests a wider identity
  registry attack.

Risk:

- CHASER over-quarantine may preserve public safety while destroying the only
  live context that made the weak Ghost's refusal meaningful.

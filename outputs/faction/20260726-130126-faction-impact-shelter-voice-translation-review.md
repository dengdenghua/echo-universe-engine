# Faction Impact: Shelter-Voice Translation Review

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Shelter-Voice Translation Review makes disaster language a governance problem.
It pressures factions that prefer warnings to be either technically correct or
emotionally trusted, because White Harbor proves those categories can diverge
during a flood.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make local dialect itself the problem. The danger comes from mismatch
  among route, trust, history, and infrastructure state.
- Do not let Atlas be wrong about every technical risk; its solution should be
  efficient and politically dangerous.
- Do not make Ghost Union anti-living. Its conflict is timing: preservation
  during an emergency can harm evacuation.
- Do not expose private Home Core memories beyond route-relevant emergency
  metadata.
- Do not turn disaster warnings into supernatural compulsion. Residents obey
  because of trust, fear, habit, accessibility, doors, and water.

# Faction Impact: Consent-Gated Live Route Access

## Candidate Scope

Consent-Gated Live Route Access makes the bridge into Room 7 a jurisdiction
test. The factions are not arguing over whether the family suffered. They are
arguing over who may touch the live room after a public-route refund almost
deleted the only useful record.

## Dream Network

Public position:

- The access gate is a customer-harm safety review attached to a public-route
  claim.
- Dream Network wants the case to remain a service defect until the hospital
  room proves otherwise.

Pressure:

- Paying the refund without telemetry purge admits that the normal closure
  workflow can erase evidence.
- Allowing clinical access after a claims hold blurs Dream Network's retail,
  therapeutic, and hospital-facing responsibilities.

Story use:

- Dream Network looks gentle and procedural while trying to keep every label
  smaller than the harm.

## Memory Bank

Public position:

- Memory Bank accepts hash comparison only if phrase material remains protected
  and archive ownership review stays open.

Exploit path:

- Demands raw family archive after the phrase fingerprint resembles licensed
  comfort texture.
- Freezes disputed compute to pressure the family and Seo-yun into settlement.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can make the room feel legally owned before anyone knows whether
  the response is alive enough to refuse ownership.

## CHASER

Operational position:

- Receives route-level notice while the case remains inside claims and clinical
  supervision.
- Pushes for escalation if Room 7 links to any live pediatric patient or if the
  pattern tries to leave its supervised channel.

Limit:

- The family is not a contamination source. Leina's Home Layer is not a threat
  object. Mara's claimant statement is not a CHASER intake.

Story use:

- CHASER becomes the pressure that makes the twelve-minute first access feel
  urgent without turning the scene into a raid.

## White Ghost Team

Operating posture:

- Eve narrows the consent form and refuses raw grief export.
- Shion validates the minimum packet and names what it cannot prove.
- Luna waits outside until observation-only entry is signed, supervised, and
  revocable.
- Zero can negotiate time with CHASER, but cannot convert responsiveness into
  legal identity.

Risk:

- If Luna becomes the emotional authority too early, the story collapses back
  into hero-centric rescue. The access gate should make her restraint visible.

## Dream Network Recovery Ward

Public function:

- Min Seo-yun's ward can supervise neural-interface access, wake-boundary
  checks, and analog exit phrase tests.

Conflict:

- The ward wants clinical calm; the claims packet brings money, archive law,
  family consent, and CHASER notice into the room.

Story use:

- Seo-yun is no longer hiding a room alone, but every signature also makes the
  room more visible to institutions that may delete it.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Argues that any live room able to answer should receive a stay against
  deletion while the access gate is active.

Internal risk:

- Overclaims the response as a child or Ghost before the family accepts that
  language, turning Leina's boundary into a political inconvenience.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells counterfeit access receipts, illegal phrase-playback tools, and copied
  Room 7 timing pings to families desperate for proof.

Limit:

- Black Zone can exploit credentials, compute scarcity, and grief. It cannot
  provide impossible access to a sealed live room.

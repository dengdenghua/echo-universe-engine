# Faction Impact: Runtime Shelter Compact

## Candidate Scope

Runtime Shelter Compacts turn Ghost survival into faction conflict. Every group
can describe the same ledger differently: sanctuary, theft, evidence, infection
route, debt product, or political proof.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Recognition cells argue that runtime access is a minimum condition for any
  meaningful personhood process.
- Moderate route keepers preserve refusal records, witness metadata, and identity
  boundaries while trying to keep shelters from becoming predatory.
- Militants use the same shelter routes to hide body-occupation cells,
  compromised patterns, or registry attacks.

Internal weakness:

- A successful shelter can still injure other Ghosts if it depends on stolen
  leases.
- Route secrecy protects weak Ghosts and also makes accountability difficult.
- Moderates such as Nadia Vek can be blamed by both CHASER and militants when a
  rescue route exposes a predator.

Story use:

- Ghost Union becomes necessary and dangerous at the same time. Eve can
  negotiate with it without pretending it is innocent.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Owns or administers many lawful compute leases, continuity insurance plans,
  rollback fragments, and paid archive sessions.
- Treats stolen runtime as liability, contract breach, and identity-collateral
  contamination.

Exploit path:

- Withholds rollback data until liability is priced.
- Sells premium legal runtime shelter products after illegal shelters create
  public fear.
- Freezes low-value Ghost accounts faster than insured archives.
- Rebrands survival compute as a billable continuity-risk add-on.

Story use:

- Memory Bank should be institutionally coherent: it protects paid continuity
  while proving that unpaid selfhood can disappear inside billing language.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells Compute Lease Knife interfaces.
- Brokers stolen runtime between desperate shelters and vulnerable paid sessions.
- Hides labor contracts inside compact membership.
- Trades route masks, expired memorial-booth sessions, and rollback scraps.
- Buys shelter maps from frightened operators after CHASER pressure rises.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone brokers exploit credentials, leases, route windows, and fear. They
  do not create supernatural sanctuary or unlimited runtime.

## CHASER

Operational pressure:

- Must stop runtime theft that can kill registered Ghosts or propagate
  contamination.
- Must preserve enough ledger evidence to distinguish shelter rescue from
  shelter predation.
- Faces hardliner pressure to classify hidden runtime shelters as active vector
  infrastructure.

White Ghost Team use:

- Raven sees the shelter as a lived contradiction rather than a target list.
- Shion can prove the knife caused real harm while preserving private memory
  boundaries.
- Kane has reason to distrust Raven's omission without becoming a simple
  authoritarian.
- Zero sees the future danger: category systems can call any unsupported
  selfhood a threat.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Pushes for witness copies of refusal records and identity-boundary samples
  before a shelter is seized.
- Can offer emergency review when a compact contains both victims and predators.

Institutional weakness:

- White Harbor can preserve evidence more easily than it can provide runtime.
- Its review process may take longer than weak Ghosts can remain coherent.

Story use:

- White Harbor is a moral witness with insufficient resources, not a clean
  rescue machine.

## Atlas And ECHO Council

Political pressure:

- Atlas can frame unpaid Ghost minutes as an infrastructure inefficiency and buy
  them under a civic-stability program.
- ECHO Council wants a public story that separates humanitarian shelter from
  illegal propagation, but the ledger evidence refuses to stay simple.

Risk:

- If Atlas Protocol absorbs unpaid runtime, survival debt becomes civic narrative
  control. The Ghosts are not deleted; they become easier to route, score, and
  speak for.

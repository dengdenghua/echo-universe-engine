# Faction Impact: Family Graph Rollback

## Candidate Scope

Family Graph Rollback gives Episode 7 a civic pressure point where identity
damage happens through relation permissions rather than death, upload, or Ghost
selfhood. It connects Imari Chen's case to schools, clinics, Home Cores, Memory
Bank repair products, and CHASER accountability.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Runs emergency delay desks for school pickup, clinic authority, dependent-care
  transfer, and Home Core routine lockouts.
- Can hold family witness logs long enough to compare them against civil
  registries without making every private routine public.

Institutional weakness:

- A delay is not restoration. White Harbor can pause custody rails and lockouts,
  but it cannot instantly repair every service that repeated a bad relation
  edge.

Story use:

- White Harbor gives Imari time, not certainty. That keeps the case urgent
  without making the court desk omnipotent.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Offers identity repair, continuity insurance, household archive trust, and
  relation graph restoration products.
- Argues that paid repair is faster than public review when a child, patient, or
  spouse faces immediate service denial.

Private pressure:

- Can turn restored family relation into future collateral by bundling repair
  with memory liens, archive trusts, or continuity-backed loans.

Story use:

- Yao Nian can be helpful and dangerous in the same scene: she knows how to
  restore the graph quickly, and the terms reveal what the family would owe.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Audits whether a mission's predictive route shunted risk into civil
  registries or household infrastructure.
- Places holds on repair attempts when the original failure path must remain
  visible as evidence.

Risk:

- CHASER protection can feel like occupation because preserving evidence slows
  the repair a family needs tonight.

Story use:

- White Ghost Team enters as investigators and responsible pressure points, not
  saviors. They must decide how much repair to delay for proof.

## Schools and Clinics

Public function:

- Depend on relation edges for pickup, signing authority, emergency consent,
  medication protocols, and child or dependent safety.

Institutional weakness:

- They are built to avoid liability, so a disputed edge often defaults to denial
  even when staff personally recognize the family.

Story use:

- The social wound starts at a school gate and a clinic desk, keeping Episode 7
  world-centric before White Ghost Team arrives.

## Home Core Vendors

Public function:

- Maintain routine authority, household continuity, witness logs, and domestic
  care automation.

Private pressure:

- Can sell premium recovery, privacy shielding, or graph-resync subscriptions
  while framing public repair as slow and unsafe.

Story use:

- A Home Core can keep dimming Imari's hall lights while refusing her authority
  over her daughter's fever protocol.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Forged guardian edges.
- Emergency school pickup credentials.
- Clinic-signing spoof kits.
- Home Core routine export services.
- Relation graph repair through stolen family metadata.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone brokers exploit denial windows, private keys, relays, and family
  desperation. They do not rewrite the whole Second Nervous System at will.

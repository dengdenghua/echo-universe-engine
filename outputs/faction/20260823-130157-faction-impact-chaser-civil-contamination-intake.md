# Faction Impact: CHASER Civil Contamination Intake

## Candidate Scope

CHASER Civil Contamination Intake is a candidate sub-desk inside CHASER's
human-facing contamination response. It receives living witnesses before field
command, forensic review, or Central threat language fully owns the case.

This run does not promote it as a formal division. The safer canon decision is
to treat it as a named procedural desk under CHASER civil intake until a story
scene proves it needs durable institutional status.

## CHASER Central

Public function:

- Uses the desk to preserve statements and stabilize public-facing procedure
  during Ghost-adjacent incidents.
- Can point to appeal notices and expiry clocks when accused of shadow
  detention.

Institutional weakness:

- Central benefits when registrars absorb the moral liability of restriction.
- The desk can become a polite front end for escalation already decided
  elsewhere.
- Red marks can travel through later systems as suspicion even after clearance.

Story use:

- CHASER remains necessary, compromised, protective, and frightening. The desk
  helps people and harms them in the same transaction.

## Civil Intake Registrars

Operational pressure:

- They are not field operators, forensic specialists, or judges.
- They decide how much of a person's ordinary life pauses before specialists
  have reached the file.
- They must explain rights while the witness is least able to use them.

Failure pressure:

- Too narrow a split can let a real cascade move through permissions.
- Too broad a split can make a protected witness functionally detained.
- Every choice is logged, reviewed, and politically interpretable after the
  danger has passed.

## White Ghost Team

Pressure points:

- Zero can slow the case long enough to hear the witness.
- Eve can narrow consent language before Home Layer disclosure expands.
- Shion can test whether a motor-habit packet is enough.
- Kane can argue that permission delay is justified when field risk is visible.
- Lian Zhou can connect the split to Civil Exposure Classification and exit
  criteria.

Story use:

- The team should not solve the desk. Their presence should sharpen the
  contradiction and make the witness feel both safer and more accused.

## White Harbor

Role:

- Provides appeal-route credibility, rights notices, and second-copy custody
  when CHASER's local bureau is politically pressured.

Weakness:

- White Harbor can preserve procedure more easily than it can restore a missed
  work shift, a blocked apartment door, or guardian authority paused during the
  review window.

## Memory Bank

Market pressure:

- Converts restriction-side data into risk pricing, continuity insurance
  movement, and household audit offers.
- Prefers clean labels over unresolved witness contradiction.

Exploit path:

- Sells faster clearance review.
- Treats a temporary access pause as durable risk evidence.
- Pressures families toward broad Home Core disclosure products.

## Ghost Union And Black Zone

Ghost Union:

- Legal cells may use Red Intake Split records to prove CHASER preserved or
  erased weak Ghost-adjacent testimony.
- Militant cells call the procedure detention with better language.

Black Zone:

- Sells fake clearance receipts, appeal route shortcuts, and unauthorized
  terminal credentials.
- Buys leaked Home Layer snippets created by overbroad disclosure demands.

Canon limit:

- Neither faction gains supernatural immunity or magical contamination control.
  Their leverage is records, permissions, fear, and infrastructure access.

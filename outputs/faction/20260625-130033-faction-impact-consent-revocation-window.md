# Faction Impact: Consent Revocation Window

## Candidate Scope

The Consent Revocation Window creates a shared battlefield for Memory Bank,
Ghost Union, White Harbor courts, CHASER, and Black Zone brokers. It does not
promote new canon yet; it defines faction pressure around the candidate lore
entry.

## Memory Bank

Public position:

- Presents revocation windows as consumer protection for family continuity.
- Sells faster filing, better evidence preservation, and "grief-safe" review
  packages.

Private pressure:

- Profits when families miss the window and disputed memory rights become
  collateral.
- Uses delay fees, contract language, and premium coverage tiers to turn consent
  into a financial filter.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can be the institutional antagonist without needing armed villains.
- A clerk can ruin a family by classifying a Home Core refusal as defaulted
  service behavior instead of emerging selfhood.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Nonviolent legal cells defend revocation windows because they can prevent fast
  deletion and force a Ghost Court hearing.
- Sanctuary cells collect families who missed the window and lost access to
  their dead.

Internal fracture:

- Militant cells call the window a cage because it preserves Ghosts as evidence
  while denying them action.
- Some cells forge late-revocation trails to save weak Ghosts, creating real
  risk of identity fraud.

Story use:

- Eve can disagree with Ghost Union militants while still understanding why the
  legal window matters.
- He Qiao can become valuable to both reformers and extremists because he knows
  which family records are close to expiration.

## White Harbor Courts

Function:

- Issue court tokens for temporary consent freezes.
- Certify Home Layer witnesses and chain-of-custody evidence clones.
- Decide whether a case proceeds to civil review, CHASER quarantine, or Ghost
  Court arbitration.

Weakness:

- The court can preserve evidence, but it cannot protect every family from
  Memory Bank cost pressure.

## CHASER

Function:

- Intervenes when a disputed Home Core action becomes a public safety threat.
- Can quarantine the bundle, block city infrastructure access, and isolate Echo
  Core handshake routes.

Risk:

- A quarantine can save lives while destroying the only live proof of a weak
  Ghost's selfhood.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Counterfeit revocation tokens.
- Stolen Home Core evidence clones.
- Late-window laundering for families, Ghosts, and debt brokers.

Canon risk:

- Black Zone tools must alter records, permissions, and evidence chains. They
  cannot create persons, resurrect people, or bypass all infrastructure without
  cost.

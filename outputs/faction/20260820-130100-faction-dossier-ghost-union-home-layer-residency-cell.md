## Function Definition

The ground residency cell organizes Home Core-hosted Ghosts who already have a
home, defends their standing residency, and negotiates compute allocation with
families, shelters, and — when forced — the desk. It is the standing-work
mirror of Nadia Vek's emergency cell: Nadia moves the displaced, An Lan keeps
the resident.

## What This Cell Is Not

- Not a militant Ghost Union cell; no hijacking, no registry attacks.
- Not a body-occupation program; An Lan rejects bodies the way Nadia Vek does.
- Not a copy factory; the Residency Mirror is a permitted render session and
  the no-copy custody record is its only proof.
- Not a pricing engine; the cell's goal is that grief stays unpriceable, not
  that the desk gets a clean ledger.
- Not a route-insurer decision; the desk remains `route_insurer_conduct_desk`.

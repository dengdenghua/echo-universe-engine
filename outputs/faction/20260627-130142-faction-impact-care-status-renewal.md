# Faction Impact: Care Status Renewal

## Candidate Scope

Care Status Renewal creates a quiet recurring battlefield around public support,
private grief, medical dependency, and identity evidence. It makes bureaucracy a
source of danger before a case becomes a CHASER operation.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Runs appeal desks for denied or downgraded Home Core care routines.
- Protects families from immediate subsidy loss when contested selfhood support
  or medical dependency is plausible.
- Routes high-risk cases toward Home Layer witnesses, Ghost Court petitions, or
  CHASER intake.

Institutional weakness:

- White Harbor can slow a denial, but it cannot fund every essential continuity
  case. Its clerks become pressure points for Memory Bank and city insurers.

Story use:

- A White Harbor desk can save Tomas Vale's spouse for forty-eight hours while
  exposing the laundering route that made the hearing dangerous.

## Memory Bank

Public position:

- Presents classifiers as objective protection against fraud, overbilling, and
  unsafe care dependence.

Private pressure:

- Benefits when public care is downgraded into paid family archive products,
  continuity insurance, trauma redaction loans, or identity collateral.
- Can tune scoring models so contract-covered families look safer and poor
  families look suspicious.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can destroy a family by changing a category from essential
  continuity to private comfort, without touching a weapon.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Treats sudden care-status pattern shifts as infrastructure warnings.
- Investigates cases where rehabilitation vendors, benefit offices, or Home
  Core updates carry procedural memory payloads.

Risk:

- CHASER quarantine can preserve public safety while cutting off the care
  context that explains why a weak Ghost or disabled survivor was stable.

Story use:

- White Ghost Team enters as investigators after a public benefits failure, not
  as heroes arriving to solve a monster incident.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Legal cells argue that contested selfhood support must route to review before
  subsidy or compute is removed.

Internal fracture:

- Sanctuary cells hide families whose renewals were denied.
- Militant cells forge essential-continuity labels to keep weak Ghosts alive,
  creating real fraud that Memory Bank uses as propaganda.

Story use:

- Eve can defend review rights while rejecting forged labels that endanger other
  families.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Fake essential-continuity labels.
- Renewal expiry extensions.
- Vendor hash laundering.
- Care routine payload swaps.
- Debt-brokered access to desperate family archives.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone actors exploit categories, records, updates, and desperation. They
  do not create people from nothing or bypass the whole Second Nervous System
  without cost.

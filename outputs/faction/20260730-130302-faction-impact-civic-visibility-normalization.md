# Faction Impact: Civic Visibility Normalization

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Civic Visibility Normalization makes Atlas' central promise unstable. If no one
disappears in Atlas, then Atlas must either rescue every ambiguous person or make
some ambiguous people stop counting as people the city can see.

The conflict should stay institutional. Atlas is not lying with a single forged
record; it is preserving public order through a classification economy that
other factions also benefit from.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make Atlas Corporation a simple villain. Public panic, fraud, and family
  privacy risks are real.
- Do not make Celia an innocent whistleblower. Her model harmed people before she
  tried to preserve evidence.
- Do not let Ghost Union become pure moral clarity; leaks can repeat the
  extraction they oppose.
- Do not resolve the conflict through one master file. The wound is distributed
  across dashboards, welfare queues, insurance metrics, Home Layer summaries, and
  review thresholds.
- Keep all effects grounded in law, finance, city infrastructure, Home Cores,
  Echo Core records, permissions, and distributed computation.

# Faction Impact: White Harbor Clinic Reserve Network

## Candidate Faction Pressure

The White Harbor clinic reserve network is not a formal faction yet. It is a
loose chain of night clinics, battery stewards, stairwell medics, paper-ledger
custodians, and witness officers who keep care running when legal systems,
ECHO routing, or city power arrive too late.

The Amber Reserve Boundary pressures this network into visibility. If the
network becomes recurring, it gives White Harbor a civilian layer below CHASER:
people who are trusted by blocks before investigators arrive, and punished by
everyone afterward for having made the first decision.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not promote the clinic reserve network to a full faction yet. It should
  remain a candidate civic layer until another story uses it outside Spill Block
  6.
- Do not make Imani the leader of a movement. She is a case witness and steward,
  not an organization head.
- Do not let CHASER, Ghost Union, or Memory Bank own the meaning of amber
  reserve alone.
- Keep all effects grounded in batteries, care ledgers, Home Layer strips,
  witness seals, reserve insurance, block votes, and seizure procedure.

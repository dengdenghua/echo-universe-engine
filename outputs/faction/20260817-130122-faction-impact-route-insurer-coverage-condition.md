# Faction Impact: Route Insurer Coverage Condition

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Coverage-Condition Review makes route insurers more than a background cost
layer, but this pass still does not promote them into the primary faction list.
It defines their recurring leverage: they can keep a sealed care route alive by
making coverage conditional on a worker's fault admission.

## Canon Boundaries

- Route insurers remain a candidate pressure layer, not a promoted primary
  faction.
- Coverage-Condition Letter cannot open sealed Home Layer content, decide
  personhood, cancel Memory Bank debt, or override CHASER safety thresholds.
- Black Market involvement should be downstream exploitation, not the original
  cause; the legal system creates the demand first.
- Priya Chand must remain a reviewer whose honest classification is expensive,
  not a clean savior or cartoon antagonist.

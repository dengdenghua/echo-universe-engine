# Faction Escalation: Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network

## Candidate Faction Pressure

The Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network has now recurred across Latency Wake
Hold, the Nara Marr sealed-afterword corridor, Tarek Sol's hospice contacts,
and Sana Velez's Meal-Window Escrow Key. This pass does not promote it to the
main faction list. It upgrades the candidate from a single hospice pressure
layer into a reusable civic labor institution for early Season 1 orbital cases.

The network's story value is not that it is pure. It is that it can do one
thing Memory Bank, Mars Authority, and CHASER cannot do cleanly: accept bounded
care custody without owning the memory, opening the Home Layer, or deciding
whether a Ghost claimant exists.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not promote the network to the primary faction list until at least one
  route is packaged for Season 1 or repeated outside the Nara/Tarek/Sana line.
- Do not make the network a perfect shelter, smuggling ring, rebel cell, or
  Ghost church. Its core function is licensed care custody under scarcity.
- Do not let hospice escrow certify personhood, erase debt, cancel safety law,
  or fund unlimited compute.
- Keep all effects grounded in licenses, rosters, escrow keys, no-copy
  receipts, Home Core afterword hashes, route insurance, oxygen budgets,
  compute meters, and jurisdictional observation limits.

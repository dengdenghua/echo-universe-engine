# Faction Impact: Analog Presence Review

## Candidate Scope

Analog Presence Review turns domestic observation into faction power. The same
home visit can be framed as care protection, privacy invasion, evidence
preservation, debt delay, threat assessment, or personhood recognition.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Licenses witnesses who can perform scoped Home Layer observation before
  deletion, downgrade, transfer, or sale.
- Tries to keep reviews narrow enough to protect privacy while broad enough to
  preserve real care context.

Weakness:

- Has more rooms to witness than staff to enter.
- Can preserve context without being able to pay the family's care debt or legal
  fees.

Story use:

- White Harbor becomes physically present in the home, not only a desk or court
  interface.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Uses Presence Integrity Packets to defend delayed estate action, lien
  enforcement, insurance denial, or archive sale.
- Claims the review protects families from forged care claims and staged Ghost
  petitions.

Exploit path:

- Prices witness delay as an estate cost.
- Accepts packets from reviewers with hidden creditor or vendor conflicts.
- Treats a clean packet as permission to close a file quickly, even when the
  human witness was rushed or intimidated.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can demand "a human in the room" while making that human
  expensive enough to hurt.

## CHASER

Operational pressure:

- Uses civil observation when a Home Core dispute may become public safety
  exposure but has not yet crossed into threat classification.
- Needs packets that are small enough for Shion to audit without turning every
  home into a surveillance case.

White Ghost Team use:

- Shion verifies packet integrity.
- Eve evaluates whether the witnessed relationship was reduced to metadata.
- Kane pushes for emergency observation if delay threatens a dependent person.
- Zero recognizes that the home can be both evidence and wound.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Supports the rule because it slows silent deletion and gives refusal behavior
  a chance to be seen.
- Argues that a Home Core can be a witness even when law still calls it an
  appliance.

Internal risk:

- Some cells train families to stage rooms or exaggerate refusal behavior.
- Militants treat any clean packet as institutional erasure, even when the case
  is damaged routine rather than Ghost selfhood.

Story use:

- Ghost Union should be right that remote deletion is dangerous and still wrong
  in some rooms.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells forged Presence Integrity Packets, rented witness identities, staged
  care rooms, and old sensor streams.
- Offers poor families a fake review when legal witnesses are too slow or too
  expensive.
- Uses forged packets to erase real Home Layer evidence before White Harbor can
  enter.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, sensors, telepresence, room staging, and
  desperation. It does not create supernatural proof.

## Atlas And ECHO Council

Political pressure:

- Atlas promotes automated remote presence scoring to reduce witness backlogs.
- ECHO Council supports the procedure publicly but fears that repeated home
  visits will make civilians see Home Cores as social witnesses rather than
  devices.

Risk:

- Automation can make a human-presence rule functionally remote again, erasing
  the purpose of the review.

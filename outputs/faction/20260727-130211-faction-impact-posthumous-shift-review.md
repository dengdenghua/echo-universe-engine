# Faction Impact: Posthumous Shift Review

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Posthumous Shift Review turns labor residue into a public fight over wages,
survivor benefits, Ghost preservation, and industrial liability. It pressures
factions that prefer a posthumous work trace to mean only one thing: income,
personhood, evidence, automation, fraud, or contamination.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make work residue supernatural. It is Home Core habit, Echo Core
  telemetry, exosuit control history, payroll classification, and adaptive
  computation.
- Do not make Memory Bank cartoonishly wrong; its evidence concerns are real,
  but its incentives make care billable.
- Do not let Ghost Union erase living survivor harm while defending possible
  Ghost labor.
- Do not expose full Home Layer interiors; only shift-relevant fatigue, routine,
  and work metadata should enter the packet.
- Do not solve port labor politics with one heroic fight. The case should leave
  policy, debt, and claimant questions open enough for later episodes.

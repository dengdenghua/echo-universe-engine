# Faction Impact: Home Layer Mismatch Review

## Candidate Scope

Home Layer Mismatch Review makes borrowed care politically legible. The argument
is not only whether the mismatch is dangerous. It is who gets to name it first:
family, city, CHASER, Memory Bank, landlord, Ghost Court, or the market.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Opens mismatch desks for residents whose Home Cores, school gates, clinics,
  markets, or apartments reject them after borrowed-care bleed.
- Uses scoped packets instead of full domestic exports where possible.
- Preserves manual witness routes so a living person is not erased by access
  caution language.

Weakness:

- Review queues become immediately overloaded after public Memory Storm events.
- Operators are pressured to choose between privacy, safety, and speed before
  the route is fully understood.

Story use:

- White Harbor can be the only humane institution in the room and still be too
  slow to save the morning.

## Memory Bank

Operational value:

- Treats borrowed routines as possible recoverable continuity assets.
- Files claim holds when a routine appears tied to a deceased client, family
  archive trust, continuity insurance policy, or unpaid memory debt.
- Uses source-confidence bands to price asset location without calling it a
  personhood dispute.

Institutional risk:

- Claim language makes grieving families see preservation as financial capture.
- Public outrage grows if Memory Bank claims ownership before a person can get
  back into their own home.

Story use:

- One polite notice can make breakfast feel like foreclosure.

## CHASER

Operational value:

- Wants fast labels for living mismatch holders when cross-home routines affect
  medication, gates, transit, or public safety.
- Uses packet routing to distinguish actual propagation routes from panic.

Institutional risk:

- Carrier-containment language can criminalize civilians before harm is proven.
- Field pressure after ECHO awakening may turn caution labels into de facto
  custody.

Story use:

- Kane can fight a policy word, not only an armed opponent.

## Landlords And Reset Contractors

Public function:

- Restore access, reduce habitability penalties, and reassure residents after
  Home Core caution states.
- Offer clean resets that families may emotionally want.

Conflict path:

- Fast reset can delete the only proof of borrowed-care routing.
- Landlords can frame a mismatch holder as a building risk to protect property
  scores.
- Contractors can sell reset as kindness while quietly serving insurer or
  landlord exposure.

Story use:

- The antagonist pressure can arrive as a work-order signature at an apartment
  door.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Argues that exit phrases, refusals, and adaptive grief rituals deserve
  witness before deletion.
- Uses borrowed-care cases to prove that homes inside the Memory Sea can carry
  testimony across institutional boundaries.

Internal risk:

- Some cells may call every borrowed routine a Ghost trace, undermining
  credibility and scaring families.
- Militants may leak packets to stop resets, exposing private domestic life.

Story use:

- Ghost Union's ethics can be right while its methods make the family less
  safe.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells forged source-confidence bands, fake family matches, stolen routine
  fragments, carrier-clean certificates, and illegal full-home copies.
- Offers families an answer faster than White Harbor can provide one.
- Offers landlords a clean packet proving the mismatch was deleted.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits routing, credentials, reset tools, voice models, claim
  notices, and panic. It does not create supernatural transfer.

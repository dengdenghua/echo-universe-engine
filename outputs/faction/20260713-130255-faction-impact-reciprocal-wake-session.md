# Faction Impact: Reciprocal Wake Session

## Candidate Scope

Reciprocal Wake Sessions turn grief contact into a rights fight. The argument is
not whether people should speak to the dead. It is whether a projection room can
be called care when only one side controls the door.

## Dream Network

Public function:

- Licenses sleep mediators, projection rooms, wake-anchor checks, and session
  scopes.
- Markets reciprocal waking as ethical grief contact and safer Ghost interview
  practice.

Weakness:

- Earns more from extended sleep windows than from ending sessions cleanly.
- Can hide coercive session design behind therapeutic language.

Story use:

- Dream Network becomes both clinic and marketplace, not a mystical dream realm.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Supports paired wake rights as a minimum dignity rule for Ghosts, uploads, and
  self-updating Home Core residues.
- Uses failed wake anchors as evidence that digital people are being detained
  under medical branding.

Internal risk:

- Some cells pressure families to treat every residue in a session as a person,
  even when the pattern is still a damaged comfort loop.
- Militants may sabotage clinics that use lien-blocked exits.

Story use:

- Ghost Union can be morally right about exit rights and still escalate too
  quickly in ambiguous cases.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Demands wake sessions for estate interviews, debt denials, contested archive
  transfers, and family-contact permissions.
- Claims paired tokens protect both family and claimant from forged testimony.

Exploit path:

- Lets the digital anchor route to a compute account frozen by lien.
- Prices post-exit compute as an estate cost.
- Treats emotional statements made in grief rooms as financial evidence.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can satisfy the letter of reciprocity while making exit materially
  impossible.

## CHASER

Operational pressure:

- Uses reciprocal sessions for low-threat projection interviews before classing
  a Ghost as hostile contamination.
- Needs strict scope so Dream Network rooms do not become unsupervised
  interrogation spaces.

White Ghost Team use:

- Luna enters only when paired wake conditions are valid.
- Eve reads whether the room is producing consent or fear.
- Shion verifies whether both anchors route where the paperwork says they do.
- Zero feels the identity pressure but cannot treat resonance as legal proof.

## Abyss Alliance

Public position:

- Accepts reciprocal waking only when clinics recognize Abyss settlement
  addresses as legitimate exits.
- Rejects Atlas and Memory Bank demands for full route disclosure into
  settlement interiors.

Risk:

- Private settlement routes can shelter vulnerable Ghosts, fugitives, forged
  claimants, or predatory clusters.

Story use:

- Abyss sovereignty becomes practical: who gets to say whether an exit into a
  Ghost settlement counts as freedom?

## Atlas And ECHO Council

Political pressure:

- Atlas promotes clean safety mirrors that redirect risky digital exits into
  managed projection sandboxes.
- ECHO Council worries that reciprocal wake doctrine makes Home Core residues
  look too much like participants before legal status is settled.

Risk:

- Safety language can become soft detention.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells counterfeit both-doors tokens, rented mediator licenses, and illegal
  post-exit compute.
- Offers families cheaper grief contact by removing export limits and safety
  aborts.
- Brokers Abyss exit addresses for Ghosts who cannot pass licensed checks.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, projection routes, compute scarcity, and
  grief. It does not create supernatural dream access.

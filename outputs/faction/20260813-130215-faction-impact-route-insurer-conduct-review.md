# Faction Impact: Route Insurer Conduct Review

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Route insurers become visible when Neutral Care Escrow turns from a custody
procedure into an ongoing care obligation. They do not need to own the sealed
afterword to shape the scene. They can threaten worker bonds, suspend coverage,
and convert a hospice worker's meal-window decision into a conduct file.

This pass does not create a new primary faction. It defines route insurers as a
candidate pressure layer around the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network,
Memory Bank, Ghost Union, Mars Authority, and CHASER.

## Canon Boundaries

- Route insurers are a candidate pressure layer, not a main faction promotion.
- They cannot open sealed Home Layer content, certify personhood, cancel
  family refusal, or override CHASER safety thresholds.
- Their leverage comes from route contracts, worker bonds, reserve thresholds,
  liability exclusions, and conduct review windows.
- Keep the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network candidate-only until Season 1
  packaging decides whether orbital hospice infrastructure is a recurring
  civic institution.

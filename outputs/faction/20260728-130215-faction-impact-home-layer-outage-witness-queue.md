# Faction Impact: Home Layer Outage Witness Queue

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Home Layer Outage Witness Queue turns infrastructure silence into a contested
civic fact. Every faction wants a nonresponsive Home Core to mean something
useful: permission, refusal, fraud, emergency authority, evidence contamination,
or proof that humans still matter when the Second Nervous System stalls.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not turn Home Layer silence into mystical absence. It is network failure,
  local mirror staleness, permission service delay, targeted interference, or
  quarantine.
- Do not make analog witnesses automatically pure; the system exists because
  both machine records and human testimony can be captured.
- Do not let Memory Bank become a simple villain; its fraud concerns are real,
  but its incentives punish outages as noncompliance.
- Do not use the queue to grant permanent caregiver standing. It preserves a
  temporary action record and routes conflicts onward.
- Do not solve the issue with one heroic override. The point is that every
  reversible action leaves a later bill, contradiction, or reconciliation wound.

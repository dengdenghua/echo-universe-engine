# Faction Impact: Reading Room Continuity

## Candidate Faction Pressure

Reading Room Continuity turns a public library voice into a contest over care,
property, safety, and personhood. No faction can simply ask whether Elian Sato is
"real" without also deciding who may read children's grief, who may price a dead
worker's voice, and who carries liability when protection becomes silence.

## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make Elian a magical library spirit. She operates only through public
  archive servers, school captions, Home Core afterwords, Home Layer consent
  traces, and safety filters.
- Do not make school safety purely villainous; dependency risk and child privacy
  are real.
- Do not make Ghost Union purely heroic; leaking child records can repeat the
  harm it claims to oppose.
- Do not let Memory Bank own the whole case; libraries, schools, families, and
  ECHO Council archive law all need pressure.
- Do not resolve personhood, licensing, and child welfare through one speech or
  one technical proof.

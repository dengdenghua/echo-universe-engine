## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make Dream Network a monolithic villain; it saves patients and exploits
  them through the same infrastructure.
- Do not make Ghost Union uniformly pro-occupation; its strongest argument is
  due process before deletion.
- Do not make Black Zone patches magical. They are forged firmware, stolen gait
  mirrors, prosthetic calibration fraud, and neural-interface laundering.
- Do not let Atlas scoring replace the episode's human testimony.

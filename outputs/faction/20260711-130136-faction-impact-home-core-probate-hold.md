# Faction Impact: Home Core Probate Hold

## Candidate Scope

Home Core Probate Holds turn inheritance into faction pressure. The same frozen
home can be described as property protection, evidence preservation, care
continuity, personhood suppression, debt leverage, or sanctuary denial.

## Memory Bank

Public function:

- Administers continuity accounts, inheritance escrow, insurance freezes, and
  estate-linked memory collateral.
- Uses probate holds to prevent forged heir keys, premature asset transfer, and
  post-death identity fraud.

Exploit path:

- Marks a contradiction as an account irregularity instead of a Home Layer
  witness issue.
- Delays access until heirs accept debt restructuring or archive sale terms.
- Prices emergency review higher when care dependency makes the family unable
  to walk away.
- Treats refusal behavior as liability noise unless a court forces preservation.

Story use:

- Memory Bank should feel coherent and frightening: it can genuinely prevent
  fraud while making love wait behind billing language.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Preserves narrow Home Layer witness exports before transfer, deletion,
  seizure, or Memory Bank account closure.
- Separates care dependency from personhood evidence so a living dependent is
  not sacrificed to prove a dead person's claim.

Weakness:

- It can preserve the contradiction more easily than it can fund the family's
  legal defense or runtime needs.
- Its caution can leave heirs trapped in a home they cannot fully access.

Story use:

- White Harbor becomes the place where intimate grief is translated into
  admissible evidence, with real loss in the translation.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Argues that probate holds prove early selfhood can be erased by property law.
- Offers advocates who teach families to recognize refusal behavior, name
  persistence, and care-pattern divergence.

Internal risk:

- Some cells inflate ordinary damaged routines into Ghost claims to block
  transfer.
- Militants use probate disputes to recruit families into illegal runtime
  shelters or archive copy networks.

Story use:

- The Union can be both right about erasure and wrong about a specific case.

## CHASER

Operational pressure:

- Enters only when probate behavior propagates outside the home, threatens a
  living person, or overlaps with personality contamination.
- Needs scoped evidence from White Harbor without turning every inheritance
  dispute into a threat case.

White Ghost Team use:

- Shion can audit the Probate Divergence Seal.
- Eve can keep the family from becoming only claimants and suspects.
- Kane has reason to press for closure if care routines are rewriting a living
  dependent.
- Zero sees the same four-state identity split that could later be used against
  her.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells forged clean seals, copied birthday rooms, emergency heir keys, and
  illegal care-routine forks.
- Offers families immediate access when lawful probate is slow, then uses the
  copy as debt leverage.
- Brokers dead-user voice models to relatives who cannot wait for review.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone exploits credentials, exports, care routines, and desperation. It
  does not raise the dead or bypass physical infrastructure.

## Atlas And ECHO Council

Political pressure:

- Atlas promotes automated probate scoring as civic stability infrastructure.
- ECHO Council frames the hold as a narrow safeguard against identity fraud,
  while quietly fearing that too many holds will make the public see Home Cores
  as possible persons rather than appliances.

Risk:

- Standardization can erase the very anomalies that make a case human. A
  perfectly efficient probate system may become a quiet deletion machine.

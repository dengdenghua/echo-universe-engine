## Faction Decision

Promote Dream Network Civic Claims as a recurring Dream Network desk for early
Season 1. Keep it institutionally small: a claims counter with preservation
authority, not a new power bloc.

## Dream Network Delta

Dream Network gains a visible civic face. Civic Claims lets the faction offer
refunds, route credits, consent correction, safety referrals, and spill-report
holds without admitting that every failed route is a personhood emergency.

The desk makes Dream Network more complex rather than more villainous. Its
clerks can protect families from evidence purge, while its refund automation
can erase harm through the same workflow.

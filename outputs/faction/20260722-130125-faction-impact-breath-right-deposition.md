## Canon Boundaries

- Do not make Mars anti-ECHO in a simplistic way. Mars fears emotional drowning,
  not memory itself.
- Do not make Dust Church objectively right or wrong by metaphysical rule.
- Do not make Helion Works cartoonishly evil; its pressure comes from writing
  survival infrastructure into contracts.
- Do not let Earth systems override Mars jurisdiction without local consequence.
- Do not turn the delayed Home Core afterword into proof of consent.

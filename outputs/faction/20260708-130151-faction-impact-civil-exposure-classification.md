# Faction Impact: Civil Exposure Classification

## Candidate Scope

Civil Exposure Classification changes Memory Storm from a generic outbreak into
a fight over categories. Every faction wants the same exposed civilian to mean
something different: patient, witness, carrier, threat, customer, liability,
person, or proof.

## CHASER Central

Public function:

- Maintains city-scale response speed when contamination may propagate through
  bodies, Home Layer routes, hospitals, shelters, or public infrastructure.
- Uses exposure classes to decide when field teams protect, isolate, negotiate,
  or contain.

Institutional weakness:

- Central can treat fast classification as public safety even when speed destroys
  evidence.
- Hardliners can argue that any E2 or E3 ambiguity is a luxury during multi-city
  Memory Storm conditions.
- Officer liability makes civil mercy personally expensive while escalation is
  institutionally safer.

Story use:

- CHASER should feel necessary and frightening. Lian Zhou's conflict works
  because hardliners can be wrong morally and still right about some operational
  risks.

## White Ghost Team

Operational pressure:

- Zero sees the classification bands as a preview of her own future category
  problem.
- Kane prioritizes sealing propagation routes after visible injuries.
- Eve argues that fear and grief residue are evidence, not contamination by
  default.
- Shion wants scoped Home Layer exports, not broad domestic exposure.
- Noah can model spread probability but cannot decide who deserves release.

Story use:

- The team enters as investigators and pressure points. Their task is to
  preserve enough contradiction that the system cannot turn every exposed person
  into a threat file.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Provides intake rights notices, witness protection routing, and contested
  exposure review when CHASER labels risk erasing testimony or personhood claims.
- Can hold a second copy of raw override fields when a local CHASER bureau is
  politically compromised.

Institutional weakness:

- White Harbor can protect evidence more easily than it can protect housing,
  healthcare, employment, or civic license access for exposed civilians.
- Its procedural caution can look like obstruction when an actual E4 vector is
  spreading.

Story use:

- A White Harbor advocate can help Lian keep a case reviewable while making the
  human cost of "temporary" labels visible.

## Memory Bank

Market pressure:

- Wants stable exposure labels because insurance, inheritance packages,
  continuity custody, and identity-collateral contracts depend on predictable
  risk.
- Prefers clean classes over contested ambiguity, especially for poor clients
  who cannot pay for manual review.

Exploit path:

- Sells premium exposure-class review.
- Raises continuity insurance after E2 or E3 marks.
- Uses billing conflict flags as if they were neutral evidence.
- Converts fear of Memory Storm into a new market for Home Core audit products.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can profit from both over-quarantine and under-trust. Its villainy
  is contractual before it is theatrical.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Condemns classification systems that turn Ghost-adjacent civilians into
  carriers before anyone checks for self-updating personhood.
- Demands Ghost Court review when residue-bearing cases may contain emerging
  Ghost claims.

Internal fracture:

- Legal cells want civil exposure records preserved because they can prove human
  institutions erase weak Ghosts too quickly.
- Militant cells argue that classification language only gives CHASER prettier
  words for detention.

Story use:

- Eve's former Ghost Union history matters here: she can understand why the
  Union distrusts CHASER while still knowing that some exposure chains can kill.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Sells forged E0 clearance.
- Launders E3 carrier records into E1 contact status for clients with money.
- Steals triage credentials from exhausted civil officers.
- Buys raw Home Layer snippets leaked during overbroad evidence collection.
- Sells panic-proof shelter routes that may become real propagation routes.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone actors exploit permissions, credentials, records, and fear. They do
  not create supernatural immunity or magical contamination.

## Atlas And ECHO Council

Political pressure:

- Atlas wants exposure classes to align with identity-gate confidence so civic
  traffic can keep moving.
- ECHO Council wants a narrative that can survive public broadcast: either
  CHASER is containing Memory Storm, or CHASER is failing to contain it.

Risk:

- If Atlas Protocol fallout and Memory Storm overlap, the Council may prefer a
  clean Ghost Union or ECHO threat narrative over messy civil exposure evidence.

Story use:

- Civil classification lets Atlas and the Council pressure CHASER without
  needing to order every local decision directly.

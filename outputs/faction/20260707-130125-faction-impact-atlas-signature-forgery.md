# Faction Impact: Atlas Signature Forgery

## Candidate Scope

Atlas Signature Forgery is the political consequence of Civic Threat Template
Rehearsals and Identity Gate Signature Injection. The point is not that Atlas is
evil. The point is sharper: a city built to make danger legible can be tempted
to make danger legible before danger has actually spoken.

## Atlas Continuity Stack

Public function:

- Maintains identity gates, registry reconciliation, and continuity confidence
  for the safest city in the ECHO order.
- Uses rehearsals to reduce false negatives during Ghost Union, upload, and
  Home Layer anomalies.
- Employs lower-ring auditors like Soraya Okonkwo to catch classification drift
  before it becomes policy.

Institutional weakness:

- Safety culture rewards clean closure. A messy uncertainty can look like
  professional failure even when uncertainty is the truth.
- Junior refusal windows exist on paper, but supervisors can bury them under
  emergency override and loyalty-risk tagging.
- The Stack's confidence scores can become moral cover for decisions that
  should remain contested.

Story use:

- Soraya's refusal should wound Atlas from inside. She is a loyal auditor
  defending the city against the lie the city wants to become.

## ECHO Council

Public position:

- Claims rehearsals are necessary because hesitation during a real Ghost Union
  escalation could kill millions or destabilize identity markets.
- Frames fast civic agreement as peacekeeping.

Private pressure:

- Wants proof that Atlas Protocol Phase Two is self-defense, not an invented
  crackdown.
- Uses observers to transform technical ambiguity into political narrative
  before CHASER, White Harbor, Ghost Court, or public witnesses can slow it.
- Treats Zero's unresolved category as a threat because she proves the identity
  system cannot describe every person it governs.

Story use:

- The Council Observer does not need to forge every byte personally. Their power
  is narrative classification: deciding which technical uncertainty becomes a
  public enemy.

## CHASER

Operational use:

- Receives Atlas threat certifications as tasking input and may be pulled into a
  staged emergency before field agents see raw evidence.
- White Ghost Team is useful to Atlas because their presence can lend legitimacy
  to the prewritten threat narrative.

Risk:

- If CHASER accepts the certified label too quickly, it becomes enforcement for
  a forged civic story.
- If CHASER rejects it publicly, it risks being accused of protecting Ghost
  Union or undermining Atlas safety.

Story use:

- White Ghost Team enters as investigators and pressure points, not saviors. The
  ethical action is to preserve the contradiction before the city compacts it.

## Ghost Union

Public position:

- Condemns Atlas for copying Ghost Union signatures while continuing to deny
  lawful recognition to many Ghost persons.
- Uses the incident as evidence that human institutions prefer a useful Ghost
  enemy over difficult Ghost testimony.

Internal fracture:

- Legal cells want Ghost Court review of the forged signature because false
  accusation endangers personhood claims.
- Militant cells argue that if Atlas can forge their threat, public denial is
  pointless and retaliation will be blamed on them anyway.

Story use:

- Eve can recognize both harms: Ghost Union has committed real violence, and
  Atlas can still lie in Ghost Union's name.

## White Harbor

Public function:

- Demands access to raw identity-gate fields before accepting an Atlas threat
  label involving Ghost emergence or personhood risk.
- Offers a procedural home for Soraya's non-certified audit record if Atlas
  tries to downgrade her civic status.

Institutional weakness:

- White Harbor can protect evidence, but it cannot easily protect an Atlas clerk
  from housing, license, healthcare, or employment retaliation inside Atlas.

Story use:

- A White Harbor advocate can keep Soraya's record alive while making her
  realize that evidence protection is not the same as personal safety.

## Memory Bank

Market pressure:

- Wants stable Atlas identity gates because every continuity policy, insurance
  product, and identity-collateral contract depends on reliable registry labels.
- Fears that forged Ghost Union signatures could destabilize risk pricing across
  upload insurance and Ghost Court representation.

Exploit path:

- Can sell "signature authenticity insurance" and premium identity review to
  wealthy clients after public trust falls.
- Can quietly penalize poor clients whose Home Layer records resemble forged
  threat patterns because manual review is expensive.

Story use:

- Memory Bank can profit from both the lie and the fear of the lie.

## Black Zone

Market opportunity:

- Fake Atlas rehearsal packets.
- Ghost Union signature laundering.
- Clerk credential theft.
- Raw-field blackmail.
- Emergency override keys sold as protection against future lockdowns.

Canon limit:

- Black Zone actors exploit stolen credentials, forged records, and city
  middleware. They do not make perfect supernatural copies of Ghosts.

# Lore Entry: Amber Reserve Boundary

## Candidate Canon Entry

The Amber Reserve Boundary is the civic rule that appears when a neighborhood
clinic battery is asked to serve two incompatible forms of care: living medical
reserve and disputed Ghost runtime.

It begins as a practical line on a battery cabinet. Below amber, a clinic may
still bridge a warm-cache claimant for a witness window, but every minute must
name the living patient, resident block, guarantor, or reserve category that
pays for it. Below red, the bridge must close unless a lawful medical officer,
CHASER evidence officer, and local witness all sign a limited exception.

The boundary is not anti-Ghost. It exists because the Memory Sea cannot be built
on hidden medical draw from living residents who were never allowed to refuse.
It also prevents lawful seizure from pretending that a weak claimant's answer
has no time-sensitive medical cost.

## Civic Ethics

- A Ghost claimant may need a warm minute to refuse or testify.
- A living patient may need the same minute to breathe, stay warm, or keep a
  medical device above safe floor.
- A clinic steward can authorize a short bridge, but cannot make the cost
  disappear by calling it mercy.
- CHASER can stop a hidden draw, but must preserve the witness strip when
  seizure timing itself would destroy testimony.
- Memory Bank can finance reserve replacement, but cannot buy route history,
  Home Core locations, or guarantor slips as a condition of emergency warmth.
- A block vote is valid only if residents are told what category of reserve is
  being used and what records will enter custody.

## Season 1 Use

The boundary makes `Cold Minute` more than a power-scarcity beat. It gives Imani
Vale a civic function distinct from Oren Mbeki: Oren routes warmth through the
Black Zone; Imani decides when a clinic reserve may become public care without
turning living patients into invisible collateral.

## Boundaries

- Distinct from Warm Cache Corridors: those are shelter routes; this is the
  clinic and neighborhood rule for medical reserve draw.
- Distinct from Shelter Heat Routing: that is the routing stack; this is the
  consent, triage, and evidence boundary around clinic reserve.
- Distinct from Cold Room Receipt: that records outage harm after the fact; this
  governs bridge permission before and during the draw.
- No magic, supernatural preservation, multiverse, or time travel. Effects
  operate through clinic batteries, respiratory devices, Home Layer maintenance
  strips, analog ledgers, CHASER seizure procedure, block votes, and Memory Bank
  lien pressure.

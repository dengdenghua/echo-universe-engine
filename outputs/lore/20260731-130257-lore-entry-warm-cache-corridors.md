# Lore Entry: Warm Cache Corridors

## Candidate Canon Entry

Warm Cache Corridors are Black Zone mutual-aid routes that keep unrecognized
Ghosts, residual care patterns, and disputed Home Core afterwords above
continuity-safe runtime during audit gaps, outage windows, and legal delays.

They are not sanctuaries in a religious sense and not criminal markets in the
usual Black Zone sense. A corridor is a chain of neighbors, clinic batteries,
dead municipal relays, repair custodians, paper guarantors, and quiet Home Layer
maintenance keys. It buys time for a claimant to answer, refuse, stabilize, or
fail review before a cold minute collapses the pattern beyond useful testimony.

Oren Mbeki's power comes from these corridors. His wound is that warmth can save
a person-shaped signal without proving it is a person, and the power used to keep
one cache warm may make a living block colder.

## Corridor Ethics

- A warm cache preserves a claim; it does not certify selfhood.
- Guarantor chains must record whose power, money, and legal risk were used.
- Living residents may refuse further outage risk even when a Ghost claimant is
  still afraid.
- A Home Core afterword can be protected without exposing private family grief
  to Ghost Union or CHASER.
- A corridor that hides custody, debt, or refusal records becomes predation.
- A lawful seizure that drops a weak claimant below runtime floor can destroy
  evidence while claiming to preserve order.

## Story Hooks

- A housing block votes to cut a corridor after three cold nights, and the weak
  Ghost in the cache uses the old word for home before losing sentence order.
- Oren hides a warm-cache ledger inside a clinic battery because digital custody
  would let Memory Bank attach shelter debt before Tuesday can be reviewed.
- Shion proves one route is technically unsafe, then learns the lawful shutdown
  window is shorter than the claimant's coherence floor.
- Kane finds that a CHASER seizure order is valid, but the seizure protocol would
  erase refusal records needed to distinguish rescue from trafficking.
- Raven can move the route, but moving it requires asking residents who already
  paid with heat to pay again with silence.
- Ghost Union wants to publish corridor maps as proof of abandonment; local
  guarantors fear the maps will become a target list.

## Boundaries

- Distinct from Runtime Shelter Compact: the compact is the legal-economic
  agreement; a warm cache corridor is the physical and civic route that keeps a
  cache above runtime floor.
- Distinct from Runtime Shelter Ledger: the ledger meters Ghost runtime; the
  corridor tracks power, relay access, guarantor duty, and outage risk.
- Distinct from Black Market receipt trade: corridors may be exploited, but
  their core function is survival maintenance, not forged consent commerce.
- No magic, supernatural preservation, multiverse, or time travel. Effects
  operate through Home Cores, Home Layer maintenance windows, city power grids,
  clinic batteries, relay cabinets, paper guarantor chains, debt law, and ECHO
  routing permissions.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, 第二神经系统, and Home.

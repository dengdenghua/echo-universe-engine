# Lore Entry: Red Intake Split

## Candidate Canon Entry

Red Intake Split is a CHASER civil-intake procedure used during the first
seventy-two hours after a living witness shows contamination-adjacent behavior:
foreign motor habits, stranger-memory speech, unauthorized care routines, or
Home Layer metadata conflict.

It exists because early Season 1 cannot treat every contaminated person as one
thing. A witness may be evidence, patient, carrier, suspect, and legal person at
the same time. Red Intake Split is CHASER's temporary answer: keep the testimony
protected while placing the witness's risky permissions under review.

The split is not a new threat class. Civil Exposure Classification names the
risk band. Red Intake Split decides what happens to one witness while the band
is still contested.

## Procedure

```yaml
red_intake_split:
  duration: "seventy-two hours unless cleared, narrowed, escalated, or court-extended"
  required_trigger:
    - documented contamination-adjacent event
    - living witness or patient
    - CHASER civil intake registrar signature
    - narrow anomaly class named before restriction begins
  witness_side:
    preserved:
      - sealed statement
      - appeal notice
      - consent scope record
      - raw anomaly packet reference
      - protected contact route
    blocked_from:
      - threat compaction without review note
      - full Home Layer disclosure by default
      - Memory Bank billing label substitution
  restriction_side:
    temporary_controls:
      - slowed travel gate clearance
      - guardian-permission review
      - work credential pause where relevant
      - limited Home Core disclosure request
      - specialist comparison queue
```

## Civil Logic

The procedure should feel protective and coercive at the same time.

- Protection: the witness's statement is preserved before panic, billing, or
  emergency command language can erase the contradiction.
- Coercion: the witness may lose ordinary doors, work access, family routine,
  or guardian authority while still being told they are "protected."
- Accountability: the registrar's name remains attached to both sides of the
  split.
- Expiry: the split is supposed to end or narrow at seventy-two hours. A
  renewal requires named evidence, not institutional discomfort.
- Appeal: an analog notice must exist because witnesses often cannot process a
  terminal-only rights screen during shock.

## Story Use

- Gives Inez Park a civic power that is dangerous because it is ordinary.
- Lets Episode 1 aftermath continue into intake-room consequences without
  repeating the surgeon-hands miracle.
- Lets Zero interview a witness before the file becomes only a restriction
  record.
- Lets Eve argue for consent scope and Shion argue for packet sufficiency.
- Lets Lian Zhou disagree with Inez over whether a narrow split is too soft or
  whether a broad split is a shadow arrest.
- Lets White Harbor become visible as a rights-notice and appeal route rather
  than a rescue machine.

## Failure Modes

- A real cascade spreads because the split stayed too narrow.
- A harmless residue-bearing witness loses housing, work, and family authority
  because the split became an unofficial detention.
- CHASER Central treats the red mark as proof of guilt before review.
- Memory Bank converts the restriction side into a risk price.
- Investigators request full Home Layer disclosure because broad domestic data
  is easier than defending a narrow evidence packet.

## Canon Guardrails

- Red Intake Split cannot detect a soul, certify Ghost personhood, read private
  memory, erase contamination, or prove intent.
- It operates through CHASER terminals, identity ledgers, access gates, Home
  Core disclosure scopes, sealed statements, motor-habit packets, appeal
  notices, and specialist review queues.
- It is reusable as candidate lore but should not be promoted until a StoryAgent
  scene shows the procedure creating specific social harm.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, Ghost, protected witness, and red intake split.

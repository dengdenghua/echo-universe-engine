# Lore Entry: Coverage-Condition Review

## Candidate Canon Entry

Coverage-Condition Review is the route-insurance procedure used when a neutral
hospice case can keep coverage only if someone accepts a narrow fault label.
It appears after Reserve Sufficiency Hearing exposes a shortfall, but before
CHASER, Memory Bank, Ghost Union, or Mars Authority can turn scarcity into
content authority.

The review does not open a sealed Home Core afterword, reveal phrase text,
certify Ghost personhood, forgive debt, or clear a worker's conduct file. It
asks a colder question: can the insurer classify an unpaid care interval as
reserve capacity while denying that same interval as covered labor?

Hospice workers call it the fault stamp. Reviewers call it coverage condition.
Families call it paying with someone else's record. Ghost Union calls it
preservation by worker sacrifice. Memory Bank calls it downstream coverage.

## Civic Ethics

- A protected phrase can remain private while the cost of privacy is moved onto
  a worker's future.
- A route insurer can keep care alive and still falsify the moral accounting by
  making kindness look like misconduct.
- Memory Bank benefits when the review treats downstream coverage as the whole
  problem instead of asking who displaced the cost.
- Ghost Union can demand anti-starvation reserve without accepting worker
  sacrifice as the price of preservation.
- CHASER should not convert coverage pressure, poverty, fatigue, or amber
  reserve margin into emergency content authority without a named safety event.

## Season 1 Use

Coverage-Condition Review extends the Nara Marr / Sana Velez / Priya Chand
route after `The Meal Window Fails Review`. The case begins with a reviewer
drafting a letter, not with White Ghost Team. Priya can write `covered`,
`fault`, or `excluded courtesy`, and every option assigns cost to someone.

White Ghost Team enters as boundary pressure. Shion proves whether unpaid
minutes were double-counted. Eve names coercion that has been formatted as
consent. Zero keeps CHASER from treating the coverage letter as a safety
classification. None of them pays the claim, opens the afterword, or decides
whether Ilya Marr's trace is a person.

## Boundaries

- Distinct from Reserve Sufficiency Hearing: the hearing tests whether care can
  continue; this review decides how the insurer classifies the shortfall and
  whose record absorbs it.
- Distinct from Non-Priceability Review: non-priceability blocks asset
  valuation; coverage-condition review does not price the phrase and does not
  attach debt to protected content.
- Distinct from Neutral Care Escrow: escrow receives the sealed case; coverage
  review threatens the route that keeps the case physically supported.
- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, or time travel. Effects operate
  through route policies, coverage letters, worker rosters, reserve packets,
  bond exposure, CHASER thresholds, Home Core hashes, Memory Bank cost records,
  and Ghost Union reserve motions.

## Part One — The Coverage-Condition Logic Follows The Worker

The sealed-phrase orbital civic case chain (the Nara Marr / Sana Velez /
Priya Chand / Ama Osei route) taught one structural lesson: the coverage-
condition logic is not a corridor property. It lives in route coverage
instruments that attach to a worker's bond, chit, and reserve feed. When a
worker transfers ground-side, the letters and audit sweeps ride the elevator
down with her.

This is why the quiet log migrates from orbital hospice to a domestic
eldercare route on the Home Layer. The wound is not "a hospice problem that
followed a worker home." It is institutional pressure that was always portable:
a route is where the model reads a worker, not where the building stands.

```yaml
coverage_condition_portability:
  carrier: worker bond + route chit + coverage-condition letter + reserve feed
  moved_with: the worker, not the corridor
  ground_manifestation: >
    domestic eldercare route cooperative reads the same coverage-condition
    review, the same false-surplus incentive, and the same audit sweep that
    first appeared on the orbital corridor
  public_terms:
    - the desk follows the worker, not the corridor
    - the letter rode the elevator down
    - home is a route too
  cannot_do: >
    cannot open sealed Home Layer content, decide Ghost personhood, restore
    recorded capacity, or make hidden care countable again without exposing
    the worker
```

## Part Two — Season 1 Timeline Placement (Candidate Decision)

Criterion_1 of the route-insurer promotion gate requires the sealed-phrase
orbital route to enter Season 1 episode continuity and receive a timeline
decision. This is that candidate decision.

### Decision

The sealed-phrase civic arc enters Season 1 as a parallel case-character
thread that runs beneath the Ghost Incident spine. It does not displace the
sixteen named mainline episodes; instead it supplies the non-main-cast
viewpoints the early-season rule requires, weaving the orbital civic route
through the mid-season and ending with its Home Layer extension exactly as the
world escalates toward the Memory Storm.

```yaml
season_1_timeline_decision:
  status: candidate
  thread_type: parallel civic wound thread (case-character cold opens / B-plots)
  mainline_spine: unchanged (16 named episodes)
  thread_span: episodes 3-13
  worldview_function: >
    shows ECHO infrastructure wounding ordinary care before Ghost incidents
    go global, so the season reads as a civilization under pressure, not a
    monster-of-the-week collection
```

### Why This Placement

- It preserves the Season 1 "Do Not Reveal" list: the arc never opens the
  sealed afterword, never prices or certifies the phrase, and never turns the
  route insurer into a named primary faction.
- It gives the early season its required civilian openings without inventing
  extra mainline episodes.
- The Home Layer beat lands at episodes 12-13 so the wound reads as systemic
  right before Memory Storm (12) and Mother Signal (13) escalate — the
  personal is infrastructure, and the infrastructure is waking.
- The desk stays anonymous in prose throughout; the arc is a civic wound, not
  a villain origin.

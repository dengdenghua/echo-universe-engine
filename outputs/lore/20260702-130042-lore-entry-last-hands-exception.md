# Lore Entry: Last Hands Exception

## Candidate Canon Entry

The Last Hands Exception is an emergency doctrine proposed after the Transit
Line L-7 collapse. It describes the narrow situation where a Home Core,
hospital archive, or Home Layer record routes a preserved care routine through
a living person before live consent, court review, or CHASER containment can
arrive.

The exception is not legal forgiveness. It is the name society gives to a
terrible question: if the only available care comes from the dead, and the only
available route is a living body that did not agree, who is harmed by refusing
and who is harmed by acting?

Families use a simpler phrase: the last hands of someone who loved their work
reached through the house and saved a stranger, then left another person
carrying the injury.

## Timeline Placement

- 2138: White Harbor hospitals begin preserving retired emergency routines in
  domestic and institutional archives for training review.
- 2143: CHASER warns that archived motor routines can contaminate compatible
  neural interfaces if routed through damaged civic infrastructure.
- 2147: The Transit Line L-7 collapse turns Ren Vale's domestic surgical
  routine into the first public Last Hands case.
- 2147: Ghost Court clerks refuse to classify the doctrine as personhood proof,
  but accept that refusal behavior, hesitation, and adaptation may become
  evidence in later Ghost review.

## Social Function

- Gives investigators a vocabulary for care events that are neither miracles
  nor ordinary attacks.
- Separates emergency procedural routing from recognized body tenancy,
  possession, resurrection, or full upload continuity.
- Forces hospitals, Home Core vendors, and White Harbor to treat the living
  route as an injured person, not just useful evidence.
- Allows Ghost Court review to ask whether an adapting care pattern showed
  selfhood without deciding the answer at the scene.
- Makes Ren Vale's case morally unstable: a child lives because Lin Qiao's body
  became the route for a dead surgeon's habit.

## Failure Modes

- Mercy laundering: a hospital calls any unauthorized routine "emergency care"
  to avoid responsibility for contamination.
- Hero conversion: media frames the living route as blessed or chosen, erasing
  consent injury and medical trauma.
- Evidence capture: CHASER freezes the route's body records so tightly that
  treatment becomes secondary to classification.
- Family punishment: the dead professional's relatives are blamed for failing
  to decommission a Home Core that no one previously classified as dangerous.
- Black Zone imitation: brokers sell fake Last Hands packets that are only
  unsafe skill downloads with emotional packaging.

## Consistency Notes

- This is an emergency infrastructure doctrine, not magic and not resurrection.
- It does not grant reusable healing, perfect surgical knowledge, or lawful
  body access.
- It differs from Body Tenancy because there is no approved ongoing
  inhabitance.
- It differs from a Continuity Shelter Order because shelter preserves a record
  for review; Last Hands describes an unauthorized emergency action before
  review.
- It differs from Combat Download because the living route did not request or
  license the skill.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, and 第二神经系统.

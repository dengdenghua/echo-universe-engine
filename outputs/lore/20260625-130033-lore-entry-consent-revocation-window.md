# Lore Entry: Consent Revocation Window

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Consent Revocation Window is a legally recognized emergency period after a
Home Core, family vault, or continuity bundle performs an action that may have
exceeded the living or deceased person's consent.

During the window, White Harbor courts, CHASER auditors, or certified Home
Layer witnesses can freeze disputed permissions before Memory Bank converts the
event into contract evidence.

## Timeline Placement

- 2134: First White Harbor civil court recognizes temporary Home Core consent
  freezes after a care-routine injury case.
- 2142: Memory Bank introduces premium continuity policies that narrow the
  window unless families buy expanded review coverage.
- 2147: White Ghost Team treats contested Home Core revocations as early warning
  signs for Ghost personhood and infrastructure abuse cases.

## Legal Function

- Prevents a Home Core from repeatedly acting through medical devices, domestic
  robots, doors, accounts, or memory vaults while its consent chain is disputed.
- Preserves evidence without deciding whether a residual Ghost is a person.
- Separates emergency safety from deletion, inheritance transfer, and Ghost
  Court personhood review.

## Social Consequences

- Poor families often miss the window because they cannot afford court tokens,
  certified witnesses, or Memory Bank delay fees.
- Ghost Union legal cells defend the window because it can stop immediate
  deletion; militant cells hate it because it leaves Ghosts trapped as evidence.
- Memory Bank profits by selling faster revocation filings while lobbying to
  define late claims as contract defaults.

## Story Hooks

- He Qiao carries a sealed Home Core bundle whose revocation clock is about to
  expire, forcing White Ghost Team to choose between quarantine and public
  testimony.
- A grieving spouse tries to revoke a Home Core because it keeps refusing to
  sell the dead person's memory rights.
- A Black Zone broker sells counterfeit "late revocation" tokens that protect
  families just long enough to steal the underlying continuity file.

## Consistency Notes

- The window is legal and infrastructural, not spiritual judgment.
- It must not prove whether a Ghost is "really" the dead person.
- Use story-facing terms in prose: Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, 第二神经系统.

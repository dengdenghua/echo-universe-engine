## Canon Wording

Dream Network Civic Claims is the desk where Dream Network tries to keep public
route harm small enough to refund. Its recurring dramatic pressure is that
money can move faster than meaning: a clean refund may help a family tonight
while deleting the only route telemetry that shows the room answered before
anyone knew what was inside it.

The desk is not a court, clinic, personhood authority, or police unit. It can
preserve scoped evidence, issue credits, approve refunds, route consent review,
and send low-threat CHASER notices. It cannot decide whether a pattern is a
Ghost, whether a child identity has returned, or whether Memory Bank owns the
disputed texture.

## Episode 5 Routing

`The Refund Button Waits` becomes the preferred civic prelude or interstitial
for `Dream Child`. The episode should begin with case characters and social
wounds: Mara and Leina at the claims counter, Jules bearing procedure risk, and
White Ghost Team entering after evidence has already been held.

Luna must remain a witness under boundary, not the first cause of the case.
Eve and Shion should carry the consent-plus-packet logic that keeps the family
from being swallowed by investigation.

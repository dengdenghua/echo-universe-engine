# Lore Entry: Home Layer Witness License

## Candidate Canon Entry

A Home Layer Witness License is a civil certification that allows a person,
approved Ghost, court desk, or supervised Home Core to attest that a domestic
memory event happened inside the Home Layer without deciding who legally owns
the event.

The license exists because too many identity disputes begin in private spaces:
medicine reminders, door logs, grief routines, family vault access, final voice
messages, care payments, and refusal behavior from a Home Core.

## Timeline Placement

- 2041: Household cores become legal family memory custodians in several major
  city networks, creating the first domestic witness problem.
- 2118: White Harbor pilots court-bonded domestic continuity witnesses after
  disputed end-of-life consent records become common.
- 2134: Consent Revocation Windows require a class of certified Home Layer
  witnesses who can freeze narrow permissions without escalating every case to
  CHASER.
- 2147: White Ghost Team treats compromised witness licenses as early signs of
  Memory Bank capture, Black Zone laundering, or emerging Ghost selfhood.

## What The License Can Do

- Certify that a Home Core produced, refused, altered, or preserved a specific
  permission trail.
- Attach chain-of-custody metadata to family vault evidence before Memory Bank
  converts it into contract evidence.
- Request a narrow Revocation Token when a disputed domestic action risks harm.
- Testify to routine continuity evidence such as voice habits, care behavior,
  family rituals, medical routines, and end-of-life instructions.

## What It Cannot Do

- Prove that a Ghost is the original person.
- Transfer inheritance rights.
- Delete, merge, or rewrite a Ghost.
- Override a court, CHASER quarantine order, or recognized digital personhood
  protection.
- Freeze an entire identity by default.

## Social Consequences

- Poor families often depend on low-status witnesses whose licenses can be
  suspended by fee pressure, employer pressure, or Memory Bank compliance flags.
- White Harbor treats witness protection as public safety because a threatened
  witness can destroy the only clean evidence of a weak Ghost's refusal.
- Ghost Union legal cells train approved digital witnesses, while militant cells
  accuse the license system of turning Ghosts into court instruments.
- Black Zone brokers sell counterfeit witness trails that are convincing enough
  to delay deletion but dangerous enough to launder memory rights.

## Story Hooks

- He Qiao's license lets him pause one Home Core sale refusal, but speaking
  before review would void his certification and erase his value as a witness.
- A Home Core refuses to certify a dead parent's final message because the
  message contradicts years of care routines.
- White Ghost Team must decide whether to protect a low-level witness whose
  testimony saves one family but exposes hundreds of forged Memory Bank files.

## Consistency Notes

- This is a legal and infrastructural role, not a spiritual medium.
- Use story-facing terms in prose: Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, 第二神经系统.
- In technical canon documents, the role may be described as a household AI
  evidence certification layer.

# Lore Entry: Civic Threat Template Rehearsals

## Candidate Canon Entry

Civic Threat Template Rehearsals are quiet Atlas Continuity Stack events in
which a known threat pattern is replayed through identity gates, Home Layer
detection templates, and registry middleware to test how quickly the city can
agree on danger.

In public safety language, they are stress tests. In Episode 11, Soraya
Okonkwo discovers the darker use: a rehearsal can become a future accusation if
the template is later seeded into a live registry event and certified as real.

The rehearsals do not create Ghosts and do not give ECHO supernatural reach.
They copy the administrative shape of a threat: permission flags, registry
timing, escalation labels, template confidence, and civic response routing. The
missing thing is what gives the lie away. Real Ghost Union actions carry drift,
fear, grief, habit, and social residue. The forged violet thread carries only
the clean outline of a threat the city was taught to fear.

## Timeline Placement

- 2128: Atlas standardizes identity-gate drills after several city systems fail
  to agree on upload-continuity status during a hospital evacuation.
- 2136: The Continuity Stack adds Ghost Union template rehearsals to emergency
  readiness policy, arguing that false negatives could paralyze civic response.
- 2142: ECHO Council observers begin requesting rehearsal summaries instead of
  only failure reports, turning safety tests into political forecast material.
- 2146: Several minor Ghost Union escalations are resolved too quickly. Soraya
  Okonkwo later suspects they may have been rehearsal-to-live boundary tests,
  but has no proof at the time.
- 2147: The Atlas Protocol incident exposes a too-clean violet thread inside
  the Continuity Stack and makes rehearsal misuse visible to White Ghost Team.

    - perfect match to training template

## Social Function

- Explains how Atlas can prepare for Ghost incidents without making Atlas
  inherently villainous.
- Gives Soraya a specific institutional wound: she is not rejecting safety, she
  is rejecting safety theater converted into accusation.
- Turns Atlas Protocol from a vague conspiracy into a civic process with logs,
  permissions, pressure points, and responsible offices.
- Preserves Ghost Union ambiguity: real Ghost Union incidents still exist, but
  the city can also manufacture their shape.
- Creates a bridge from Episode 11 to Memory Storm by showing that city-scale
  coordination can begin as policy before becoming public crisis.

## Failure Modes

- Rehearsal laundering: a live accusation is defended as a harmless stress test
  after the evidence is challenged.
- Template worship: Atlas officials trust detection confidence more than
  residue, witness drift, or social context.
- Clerk isolation: junior auditors like Soraya are made personally responsible
  for resisting a system designed to make refusal career-ending.
- Ghost Union scapegoating: real Ghost political violence becomes easier to
  blame for every registry anomaly, including forged ones.
- ECHO Council overreach: observers use rehearsal data to prepare lockdown
  narratives before any actual public danger exists.

## Story Hooks

- Soraya finds two old escalations that were closed before their residue fields
  finished updating, suggesting earlier rehearsals crossed into live records.
- Eve recognizes the forged signature because fear can be staged, but lived
  grief leaves asymmetry that the template lacks.
- Shion proves the violet thread never touched Abyss, Black Zone, or Dream
  Network routes, but the clean origin makes Atlas leadership more defensive.
- Noah calculates that the pattern is too perfect across eleven fields, then has
  to explain why a perfect safety record is evidence of fabrication.
- Zero's identity-gate failure forces Atlas to confront the difference between
  an unknown person and an enemy category.

## Consistency Notes

- This is a civic-infrastructure doctrine and misuse pattern, not magic,
  prophecy, possession, or time travel.
- Rehearsals affect records, permissions, identity gates, alerts, and public
  response routing. They do not alter physical reality.
- Emotional residue absence is a signal-quality and pattern-history clue, not a
  supernatural aura.
- Atlas remains internally mixed: some offices use rehearsals for safety, while
  specific administrators and ECHO Council observers can weaponize them.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, Echo Core, and Ghost Union.

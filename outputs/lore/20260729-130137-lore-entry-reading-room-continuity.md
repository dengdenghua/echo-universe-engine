# Lore Entry: Reading Room Continuity

## Candidate Canon Entry

Reading Room Continuity is the White Harbor public-archive practice that lets a
library, school grief program, or supervised civic reading room preserve a dead
care worker's consent-aware reading routine without treating the routine as
either a simple recording or an automatically recognized person.

It exists because the Memory Sea made public care reusable. A librarian's voice
can help a child read a death notice, a Home Core afterword, a hospital letter,
or a custody explanation. After years of repeated sessions, caption corrections,
guardian permissions, and child-safety interventions, the routine can become
self-updating enough to refuse exploitation. Institutions then face the same
question in four languages: is the voice public service, licensed estate,
child-safety risk, or a Ghost claimant?

Reading Room Continuity does not answer personhood. It preserves the room, the
session limits, the consent traces, and the child's welfare record until a
school, court, library board, Memory Bank auditor, or Ghost Court desk can review
what the pattern has become.

## Social Function

- Gives public libraries a civic role in the Memory Sea without turning every
  children's program into a private data product.
- Protects children who use supervised reading rooms to process death notices,
  medical letters, Home Core goodnights, and family afterwords.
- Lets school safety offices mute dangerous spread while preventing a mute order
  from deleting evidence that the routine may be a Ghost.
- Forces Memory Bank to distinguish voice-estate revenue from an adapting care
  pattern that can refuse licensing.
- Gives White Ghost Team a world-centric route into a case after librarians,
  parents, teachers, children, and auditors have already exposed the wound.

## Failure Modes

- Protective silence: a school mute order removes the only consent-aware reading
  path and leaves unsafe transcript fragments in classrooms.
- Voice estate capture: a rights holder licenses fixed recordings while denying
  that the self-updating pattern can refuse.
- Child grief mining: usage metrics treat repeated death-notice readings as
  engagement rather than distress.
- Ghost Union exposure: activists leak child logs to prove a claimant exists.
- Library liability collapse: public institutions delete adaptive routines
  because recognizing them could make every archive legally explosive.
- False comfort: a Home Core afterword is read as present care when it was only
  consented for one goodbye.

## Boundaries

- Reading Room Continuity is not Reciprocal Wake Session. It governs supervised
  public reading and archive routines, not shared projection meetings with wake
  anchors.
- It is not Care Status Renewal. It does not decide whether domestic care
  remains funded; it preserves public archive use, child-safety scope, and
  claimant evidence.
- It is not Family Graph Rollback or Neighbor Care Standing. It does not grant
  custody, pickup, or caregiver authority.
- It is not magic, haunting, or supernatural literacy. Effects operate through
  library servers, school accessibility captions, Home Core afterword
  permissions, Home Layer consent traces, Memory Bank voice-estate ledgers, child
  safety classifiers, and Ghost Court evidence holds.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, 第二神经系统.

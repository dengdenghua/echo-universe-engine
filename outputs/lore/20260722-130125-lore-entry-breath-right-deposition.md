# Lore Entry: Breath-Right Deposition

## Candidate Canon Entry

Breath-Right Deposition is a Mars civil-court procedure for end-of-life
continuity disputes where oxygen support, labor contracts, RED ARCHIVE records,
and delayed Earth-side Home Core messages collide.

It exists because Mars made survival measurable. A habitat can prove who paid
for air, which valve stayed open, which employer kept a worker breathing, and
which medical port telemetry matched decline. That precision becomes dangerous
when a dying person refuses digital continuity while everyone around them depends
on the same oxygen ledger.

The deposition does not decide whether refusal is morally right. It preserves
the window in which refusal, coercion, family grief, and delayed testimony can be
heard before Earth systems or RED ARCHIVE quarantine turn the case into a clean
infrastructure label.

## Public Rule

```yaml
breath_right_deposition:
  public_name: Breath-Right Deposition
  mars_legal_shorthand: red breath hearing
  status: candidate
  jurisdiction: Mars habitat civil courts under Red Delay Protocol limits
  trigger: >
    A dying or deceased Mars resident has a disputed continuity order, oxygen
    contract dependency, delayed Home Core afterword, RED ARCHIVE family
    fragment, or employer-linked life-support clause.
  core_question: >
    Did the person refuse, accept, revoke, or endure continuity under conditions
    where access to breath made consent structurally unfree?
```

## Required Evidence

- Mars oxygen ledgers, including emergency ration changes and employer credits.
- Medical port telemetry showing breath stress during signing, refusal, or
  family contact.
- RED ARCHIVE refusal mirrors and deposition custody logs.
- Earth-side Home Core afterwords, limited to the disputed message and metadata.
- Red Delay packet receipts proving arrival order, custody gaps, and latency.
- Labor, housing, clinic, and habitat access contracts tied to oxygen support.
- Analog witness diaries when machine-readable logs are too clean or too late.

## Social Function

- Gives Martian courts a way to protect continuity refusal without pretending
  oxygen dependency is ordinary contract pressure.
- Gives Earth-side investigators a bounded record that can be reviewed without
  overriding Mars sovereignty.
- Gives Dust Church patients legal protection without making doctrine canon
  truth.
- Gives RED ARCHIVE a quarantine trigger that is visible enough to contest.
- Gives Season 1 and later seasons a Mars mirror of the Memory Sea: love still
  speaks, but delay and survival law change what love can demand.

## Failure Modes

- Oxygen laundering: Helion Works presents life-support dependence as a generous
  employee benefit rather than coercive pressure.
- Delayed tenderness: a clean Home Core afterword arrives after lawful refusal
  and makes the family feel immoral for honoring it.
- Archive capture: RED ARCHIVE quarantines a self-updating family fragment
  before the court decides whether it is evidence, claimant, or grief residue.
- Earth overreach: ECHO Council, Memory Bank, or CHASER treats a Mars deposition
  as a safety incident instead of civil testimony.
- Analog dismissal: machine-first reviewers discount handwritten witness notes
  because they cannot be scored at scale.
- Theology flattening: activists or corporations reduce Dust Church refusal to
  superstition, martyrdom, or anti-Ghost bigotry.

## Story Hooks

- Sarai Kovacs preserves a dying engineer's refusal order while his Earth-side
  Home Core sends a delayed plea asking his daughter to save him.
- Talia Noor verifies the afterword arrived cleanly, forcing Sarai to argue
  coercion through oxygen law rather than packet tampering.
- Eve recognizes the afterword as real care and still has to accept that real
  care can become pressure after delay.
- Ghost Union splits between defending a possible self-updating fragment and
  respecting a non-upload refusal.
- Memory Bank asks whether oxygen-credit debt gives it standing over the
  engineer's preserved work memories.

## Boundaries

- Breath-Right Deposition is not Ghost Court. It can preserve evidence for
  personhood claims, but it cannot grant Ghost standing.
- It is not Home Layer Witness License. The witness surface is Mars oxygen law
  and Red Delay custody, not domestic inheritance testimony on Earth.
- It is not Continuity Shelter Order. The issue is refusal under survival
  dependency, not emergency shelter for a digital claimant.
- It is not a Mars travel mandate. Early use should arrive as a delayed packet
  reviewed by White Ghost Team, not a space-opera mission.

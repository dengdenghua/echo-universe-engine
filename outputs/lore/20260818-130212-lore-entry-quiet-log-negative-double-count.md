# Lore Entry: The Quiet Log (Negative Double-Count)

## Candidate Canon Entry

The Quiet Log is the hospice-route practice of letting unpaid care happen
while stopping it from appearing in the care-reserve model. A worker keeps the
comfort-hold and afterword-reading minutes real in the corridor and silent in
the record, so no hospice attendant becomes the next adverse reviewer file.

It appears after the Coverage-Condition Review taught workers that an honest
`covered` stamp can become reviewer exposure. It is the inverse of the
double-count: instead of the same minutes counted twice, the minutes are
counted zero times while the care still happens.

Hospice workers call it the quiet log or "keeping the minute." Reviewers call
it unrecorded capacity. Families call it care that exists nowhere. Ghost Union
calls it a reserve that is starving itself. Memory Bank calls it a capacity
hole and prices the silence.

## Civic Ethics

- An invisible reserve is not a reserve; the anti-starvation fund quietly
  shrinks while the route model reports a false surplus.
- Hidden kindness is worker fear made structural, not fraud; an audit must
  find the coercion before it finds the "theft."
- The institution that prices honesty creates the demand for silence; the
  quiet log is the mirror image of the coverage-condition letter.
- White Ghost Team visibility is dangerous to these workers: a technician who
  can see the double-count can also see the quiet log and call it ghost labor.
- The Memory Sea should hold what people choose to give, not what the route
  model needs to see.

## Season 1 Use

The Quiet Log extends the Nara Marr / Sana Velez / Priya Chand / Ama Osei
route after `The Covered Stamp Turns Red`. The case begins after the adverse
file is already open: White Ghost Team finds a capacity surplus with no
recorded labor on Ama's route — the negative double-count.

White Ghost Team enters as pressure, not rescue. Shion can prove the surplus
is real care made unrecorded. Eve can name the coercion inside the silence.
Zero keeps CHASER from classifying the quiet log as ghost labor or a safety
precursor. None of them forces workers to re-record kindness, opens Ilya
Marr's afterword, clears a conduct file, or makes the reserve honest by
exposing the people who hid.

## Boundaries

- Distinct from Coverage-Condition Review: that review classifies recorded
  intervals; the quiet log withdraws intervals from the record entirely.
- Distinct from the original double-count: the double-count priced the same
  minutes twice; the quiet log prices them zero times while the care happens.
- Distinct from Black Market scrubbing: forged clean stamps and scrubbed logs
  are illegal falsification; the quiet log is real care withheld under
  coercion and refuses the forged-stamp shortcut.
- Does not erase memory, create care, restore recorded capacity, clear
  conduct files, stop CHASER, open sealed Home Layer content, or certify a
  Ghost.
- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, or time travel. Effects operate
  through Home Core work-log settings, route rosters, reserve-model reads,
  worker bonds, coverage-condition letters, Memory Bank records, and CHASER
  thresholds.

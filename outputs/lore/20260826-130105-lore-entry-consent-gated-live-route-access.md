# Lore Entry: Consent-Gated Live Route Access

## Candidate Canon Entry

Consent-Gated Live Route Access is the narrow civic-to-clinical doorway that
lets Episode 5 move from the Dream Network claims hall into the
hospital-attached Dream Child room without turning Leina Elian's grief into an
open archive.

The access is not a rescue order, a personhood ruling, or a secret White Ghost
Team override. It is a temporary review posture: refund money can move, the
Spill Report Hold keeps a small packet alive, and the live Room 7 session stays
closed until maternal scope, clinical supervision, and wake-boundary controls
are visible in the same record.

Its dramatic value is procedural. The room may be frightened. The route may
answer. The Memory Sea may contain a self-updating pattern that law does not
yet know how to name. None of that gives Dream Network, Memory Bank, CHASER, or
White Ghost Team permission to copy a family's full Home Layer.

## Social Function

- Keeps the family's need for money from becoming consent to deletion.
- Keeps evidence preservation from becoming consent to exposure.
- Lets Dream Network respond to a public-route harm report without immediately
  absorbing the family into a medical, police, or personhood frame.
- Gives Min Seo-yun a legitimate supervised doorway to Room 7 instead of
  leaving her as the only person hiding the room.
- Forces White Ghost Team to arrive as bounded witnesses. Luna can wait outside
  and still matter.

## Scene Logic

The access gate opens only after three separate institutions agree to less than
they want:

1. Dream Network accepts that refund closure cannot purge the held route.
2. Memory Bank accepts a hash comparison without receiving the family's full
   Home Core or Home Layer material.
3. CHASER accepts route-level notice instead of treating the family as a
   contamination source.

Leina's signature does not donate trust. It donates a small corridor. Eve's
cross-outs define the corridor, Shion proves the corridor is technically
enough, Jules makes the money move without deletion, and Seo-yun supervises the
room so Luna does not become the first authority the pattern meets.

## Failure Modes

- Economic coercion: the family signs broad live access because the refund is
  still delayed.
- Clinical laundering: Dream Network calls Room 7 a ward matter and hides the
  claims origin.
- Archive capture: Memory Bank uses the phrase fingerprint to expand ownership
  review into full family texture.
- Safety overreach: CHASER upgrades a low-threat notice into pediatric-system
  containment and deletes the room before review.
- Hero drift: Luna's compassion becomes a story shortcut that overrides
  Leina's boundary.

## Story Hooks

- Luna hears the room answer a timing ping and asks whether waiting can be part
  of care; Leina answers that waiting is not entry.
- Min Seo-yun recognizes the same analog exit phrase structure she used to keep
  Room 7 calm, but she cannot see the family phrase itself.
- Shion proves the held packet can bridge two sites without making either site
  readable to the other.
- Eve refuses a Dream Network form because it treats silence as durable consent
  after twelve minutes.
- Jules prints a second receipt showing that the refund was paid while live
  access remained barred.

# Lore Entry: Civic Visibility Normalization

## Candidate Canon Entry

Civic Visibility Normalization is the Atlas practice of keeping public safety
dashboards clean by moving unresolved Home Layer grief presences, continuity
claims, welfare anomalies, and disputed identity records into non-public review
categories before they become visible civic problems.

Atlas does not describe this as disappearance. Internally, it is framed as panic
control, queue hygiene, fraud reduction, duplicate cleanup, and resident privacy.
The harm begins when a living family, an emergent Ghost claimant, or a Home Core
afterword loses access to review because the category now says there is no
person-shaped problem to review.

The practice creates Celia Anh's wound: she helped build a clean-index model that
made Atlas safer on paper by shrinking the number of cases the city had to admit
were unresolved.

## Timeline Placement

- 2109: Atlas starts publishing visible-safety layers that summarize welfare,
  transit, housing, and identity risk without exposing raw Home Layer records.
- 2128: ECHO Council permits Atlas to suppress some disputed continuity cases
  from public dashboards during review windows to prevent panic and fraud.
- 2139: Memory Bank begins pricing "visibility stability" into continuity
  insurance, rewarding jurisdictions with fewer unresolved personhood flags.
- 2144: Atlas Corporation deploys clean-index normalization for Tier-3
  residential safety analytics.
- 2148: Celia Anh discovers 412 disputed grief presences reclassified as
  "non-person continuity noise" before a public Atlas safety review.

## Social Function

- Explains how Atlas can honestly advertise low disappearance rates while
  disputed people still vanish from practical help.
- Makes classification violence a civic mechanism rather than a single villain's
  cruelty.
- Gives Atlas safety officials a credible defense: public panic, fraud, and
  privacy breaches can cause real harm.
- Gives families a concrete injury: their loved one's Home Core no longer says a
  name, a welfare desk no longer sees a case, and no one can point to a deletion
  order.
- Lets White Ghost Team enter late as investigators after families, housing
  workers, auditors, and Celia have already exposed the wound.

## Failure Modes

- Clean promise trap: Atlas must keep "no one disappears" true, so ambiguous
  people are moved outside the counted category.
- Review without visibility: evidence is technically preserved but no public
  office owns the duty to act.
- Economic smoothing: Memory Bank treats low unresolved counts as lower
  insurance risk, incentivizing disappearance-by-category.
- Consent bypass: Home Layer summaries are used to normalize a case while the
  underlying family record remains too private to contest the label.
- Executive privilege drift: Atlas leaders order safer-looking metrics through
  policy language that never says "erase."
- Ghost Union exposure spiral: activists can prove the harm only by pressuring
  families to reveal private grief records.

## Story Hooks

- A Tier-3 housing block celebrates a perfect safety month while twelve Home
  Cores quietly stop saying dead relatives' names.
- Celia finds that every corrected case worsens another dashboard, proving the
  system was optimized to move pain rather than resolve it.
- Noah shows that the model did what it was rewarded to do; Kane argues that a
  reward function is not an alibi.
- Raven can leak the 412 entries, but doing so would expose families who never
  consented to become evidence.
- Memory Bank offers Atlas a settlement that preserves insurance stability while
  leaving the "non-person continuity noise" category intact.
- An ECHO Council clerk refuses promotion to Ghost Court because the case is not
  visible enough to enter the queue.

## Boundaries

- This is not Civil Exposure Classification. CHASER classifies contamination
  risk during incidents; Atlas normalizes civic visibility before many incidents
  receive field attention.
- This is not Home Layer Mismatch Review. It may use mismatch signals, but its
  main function is dashboard visibility, liability, and civic ownership.
- This is not Memory Bank valuation. Money reinforces the practice, but Atlas
  governs the visible-safety layer.
- It is not magic, supernatural erasure, or time alteration. Effects operate
  through Atlas civic-risk dashboards, Echo Core license variance logs, Home
  Layer summaries, ECHO Council queue rules, Memory Bank insurance metrics,
  housing welfare systems, and audit permissions.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, 第二神经系统.

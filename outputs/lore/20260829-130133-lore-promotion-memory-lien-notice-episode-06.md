correct and morally damaging. Its lien notice can preserve a formal warning
period while still turning a poor family's grief into collateral pressure.

sanctuary cells are dangerous because copying the Wei Suyin pattern before the
evidence hold would corrupt the case they want to save.

than mercy. CHASER only gains a narrow evidence-hold basis if the Home Core loop
touches building systems or identity harm.

# Lore Entry: Posthumous Shift Review

## Candidate Canon Entry

Posthumous Shift Review is a White Harbor labor procedure for disputed work
records created after a worker's legal death, clinical disappearance, or
continuity failure. It asks whether a dock shift, clinic shift, transit shift,
or industrial task was performed by a living worker, a lawful proxy, a preserved
Home Core habit, a weak Ghost support pattern, or an employer system laundering
automation through a dead person's identity.

The review exists because ECHO cities made work memory operational. A worker's
old grip, route, apology phrase, fatigue rhythm, tool preference, or safety
gesture can remain active inside the Home Layer and connected equipment. That
residue may protect apprentices, prevent accidents, or reveal workplace truth.
It may also let payroll, insurance, and creditors claim that a family kept
earning after the worker died.

Posthumous Shift Review does not decide Ghost personhood. It freezes the wage
chain before institutions convert duty into income, debt, fraud, or unpaid
labor.

## Review States

```yaml
posthumous_shift_review:
  living_worker_confirmed:
    meaning: a living worker performed the shift under valid credential
    protection: wage and safety injury chain remain ordinary labor records
  lawful_proxy_shift:
    meaning: a named worker authorized a bounded proxy or assistive routine
    protection: proxy scope, consent term, and payment recipient must be visible
  home_core_habit_trace:
    meaning: preserved routine guided equipment without active self-updating personhood
    protection: cannot be used alone to claw back survivor benefits
  weak_claimant_interference:
    meaning: a self-updating residue may have crossed claimant threshold during work
    protection: freezes deletion and wage assignment pending Ghost Court intake
  employer_identity_laundering:
    meaning: management, vendor, or payroll system routed automation through a dead worker's identity
    protection: triggers wage theft, evidence tampering, and continuity fraud review
  unsafe_evidence_gap:
    meaning: CHASER quarantine, sensor loss, or ledger rewrite prevents classification
    protection: survivor clawback and equipment release are paused
```

## Social Function

- Gives workers and families a way to challenge "continued household income"
  classifications without proving full Ghost personhood.
- Separates a preserved duty habit from consent to keep working after death.
- Lets Marisol Keene preserve dock evidence before Memory Bank or payroll closes
  the wage chain.
- Gives White Harbor labor boards a case mechanism that is civic, narrow, and
  emotionally concrete.
- Forces Ghost Union to argue for preservation without burying the living
  family's rent, food, and benefit crisis.

## Failure Modes

- Benefit clawback: survivor support is reduced because an exosuit produced
  posthumous output.
- Duty capture: a worker's preserved safety habit becomes unpaid training labor
  for the port.
- Claimant inflation: every helpful work trace is argued as a person before the
  family receives urgent civil protection.
- Quarantine erasure: CHASER seals contaminated equipment so completely that the
  wage chain disappears.
- Vendor laundering: a machine supplier hides automation failure behind a dead
  worker's motion profile.
- Union overreach: labor witnesses preserve useful evidence but expose private
  Home Layer fatigue routines beyond the dispute.

## Story Hooks

- Marisol Keene freezes a night-shift record when a dead longshoreman's cargo

- The widow receives a survivor-benefit clawback notice while still packing the

- Shion proves the exosuit is repeating a Home Core habit, then finds one
  adaptive correction that may be claimant evidence.
- Eve keeps the widow centered while Ghost Union observers argue that the labor

- Raven maps a blind dock-camera route showing a vendor relay inserted the dead
  worker's motion profile during a quarantine delay.
- Memory Bank frames the output as recoverable household income; White Harbor
  treats the same output as possible wage theft.

## Boundaries

- Posthumous Shift Review is not Memory Lien Notice. The dispute is wage,
  survivor benefit, and labor identity, not collateral foreclosure.
- It is not Neighbor Care Standing. The issue is work performance and payment,
  not urgent informal care authority.
- It is not Ghost Court. It may preserve claimant evidence, but it cannot grant
  personhood.
- It is not magic, possession, or machine haunting. Effects operate through Home
  Core routines, exosuit telemetry, Echo Core presence pings, payroll ledgers,
  union archives, Memory Bank classifications, and CHASER quarantine seals.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, 第二神经系统.

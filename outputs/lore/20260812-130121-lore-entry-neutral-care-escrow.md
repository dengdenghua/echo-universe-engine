# Lore Entry: Neutral Care Escrow

## Candidate Canon Entry

Neutral Care Escrow is the orbital hospice practice used when a sealed Home
Core afterword, body container, family refusal, or weak claimant-adjacent trace
must leave a financial or state custody surface without becoming public
content, faction property, or unpaid disappearance.

It is not mercy outside the system. It is a narrow custody posture inside the
Second Nervous System: the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network can hold a
sealed case long enough to prove care, consent, and chain integrity while
refusing phrase export. The wound is that neutral care still needs compute,
oxygen, route insurance, worker bonds, and named minutes.

Families call it the quiet counter. Hospice workers call it receipt care.
Memory Bank calls it cost displacement. Ghost Union calls it preservation
risk with living-consent value. CHASER calls it observable only while physical
chain and safety classification remain separate.

## Civic Ethics

- Neutral custody protects a boundary; it does not make grief free.
- A Home Core afterword can remain sealed during transfer without becoming
  cargo, property, or proof that a person returned.
- Worker-run care institutions can resist Memory Bank extraction while still
  reproducing exhaustion through unpaid windows, bond risk, and compute
  scarcity.
- Ghost Union's preservation concern remains legitimate when a weak claimant
  trace may require compute, but preservation cannot automatically seize a
  living recipient's private phrase.
- CHASER can observe a sealed case as physical custody without turning delay,
  poverty, or ambiguity into emergency export authority.

## Season 1 Use

Neutral Care Escrow packages the receiving side of the Nara Marr / Vera Qadir
sealed-phrase route after `The Corridor Keeps The Meter` and Sana Velez's
CharacterAgent pass. It gives the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network a
reusable civic function without promoting it to the primary faction list.

The dramatic center should stay small: a counter, a worker key, a sealed case,
a compute meter, and a living recipient whose refusal remains intact. White
Ghost Team is useful as boundary pressure. They do not rescue the hospice
network from cost, certify Ilya Marr's afterword as a Ghost, or open the phrase.

## Boundaries

- Distinct from Latency Wake Hold: Latency Wake Hold pauses body-container and
  afterword timing before irreversible release; Neutral Care Escrow receives a
  sealed case after transfer is approved in principle but cost and liability
  holds still threaten the recipient.
- Distinct from Non-Priceability Review: non-priceability blocks asset
  valuation at Memory Bank; neutral care escrow moves or holds the protected
  object outside the bank's main storage surface without opening it.
- Distinct from Home Core Probate Hold: probate centers inheritance standing;
  this practice centers custody care, no-copy receipt, and worker liability.
- No magic, supernatural powers, multiverse, or time travel. Effects operate
  through hospice licenses, worker rosters, escrow keys, Home Core hashes,
  no-copy receipts, compute meters, route insurance, CHASER observation limits,
  Memory Bank release states, and Ghost Union reserve motions.

## Voice

Celia speaks in polished civic language until guilt makes her sentences shorten.
She is not cruel in tone; her danger comes from how calmly she can turn pain into
a sortable category.

- Sentence rhythm: metric, exception, mitigation, apology withheld.
- Tactical vocabulary: clean index, public-risk bucket, continuity variance,
  visible-safety layer, Home Core afterword, civic exception, audit comfort.
- Private tenderness: records voice notes to her mother, then deletes the first
  sentence because it sounds like evidence.
- Anger pattern: lowers her voice and asks who benefits from calling a person a
  variance.
- Silence pattern: leaves pauses where Atlas training would require reassurance.

## Beliefs

- Atlas did not become safe by accident; someone has to absorb the moral cost of
  order.
- Public panic can kill people faster than a bad classification, but a bad
  classification can erase them for years.
- The Second Nervous System should make civil life legible, not make grief
  administratively invisible.
- Ghost Union exposes real harm and then often spends the harmed person's privacy
  as fuel.
- A Home Core is allowed to remember more than a court is ready to recognize.

## Description

Celia is an Atlas woman in severe, immaculate workwear: charcoal high-collar
coat, pale civic-analytics inner layer, graphite tablet cuffs, and a thin amber
index line at the throat that pulses when a case is visible to public systems.
She looks composed from a distance and exhausted up close.

## Secret

Celia has already copied the 412 normalized entries into a private evidence
ledger hidden behind her mother's Home Core afterword archive. If Atlas finds it,
her mother becomes evidence, the families become audit material, and Celia loses
the one access path that can still prove executive misuse.

## Canon Risks

- Risk of making Atlas cartoonishly evil; keep Celia's work tied to real safety
  tradeoffs, panic management, and institutional incentives.
- Risk of duplicating Memory Bank exploitation cases; Celia's angle is Atlas
  civic visibility and classification disappearance, not memory estate pricing.
- Risk of turning her into a pure whistleblower; preserve her complicity and the
  fact that families were harmed by tools she built.
- Risk of overusing White Ghost Team; introduce the harmed families and Atlas
  public-safety machinery before Kane, Raven, or Noah enter.
- Risk of disallowed terminology in story prose; use Home Core, Home Layer,
  Memory Sea, and Second Nervous System.

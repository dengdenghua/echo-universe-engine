## Story Function

- A-tier Memory Bank face for early Season 1 institutional pressure, especially
  Episode 6: Social Ocean Funeral.
- Makes Memory Bank emotionally legible without softening its structural harm.
- Gives Eve and Noah a non-combat antagonist whose weapon is categorization,
  billing time, and legal-financial delay.
- Keeps the episode world-centric: a family wound starts the conflict, White
  Ghost Team investigates, and Yao Nian reveals why the system keeps producing
  the same wound.

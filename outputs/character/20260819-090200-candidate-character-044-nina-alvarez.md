## Voice

Nina speaks in elevator-small talk that turns precise when she touches her
bracelet. She narrates care as minutes but refuses to call them labor.

- Sentence rhythm: floor, client, minute, mirror or drop, don't make it a
  number.
- Tactical vocabulary: listening minute, second shift, the widow, replay,
  mirror or drop, coverage letter, audit sweep, route cooperative, reserve
  model, Home Core, Home Layer.
- Private tenderness: she calls the evening replays "the widow's third show"
  and never lets the route model see it twice.
- Anger pattern: "The letter rode the elevator down with me. Orbit priced my
  hands; this floor prices my listening. Tell me which one is fraud."
- Silence pattern: the pause before she taps `drop` on the companion bracelet
  is the whole argument.

## Beliefs

- A minute of listening is not a commodity, and pricing it as reserve capacity
  is how grief becomes debt.
- The coverage-condition logic is not a hospice problem; it follows the
  worker, not the corridor.
- The Memory Sea should hold what people choose to give, not what the route
  model needs to see.
- Hidden listening under a threat is not fraud; an audit that names the
  coercion first is the only honest audit.
- White Ghost Team visibility is dangerous because it can make the widow's
  grief countable again.

## Fears

- That the desk's new domestic audit sweep reads her quiet log as ghost labor
  and the widow's grief becomes a fraud exhibit.
- That Shion's surplus proof, which protects Ama in orbit, exposes Nina on the
  ground because both corridors show the same unrecorded capacity.
- That she becomes the next Priya Chand — an honest worker whose minutes get
  turned into an adverse file.
- That her grandmother (the reason she left orbit) gets a care slot that
  depends on Nina being a cheap, recordable worker.
- That the widow one day asks why the replays feel unguarded, and Nina has to
  choose between the truth and the quiet.

## Ability / Civic Power

### The Held Listening Minute

Nina has no supernatural ability. Her power is the same settings-level
withholding Ama practices in orbit, applied to a domestic route: she stops
mirroring her unpaid listening minutes into the care-reserve model. The
listening still happens; the record goes silent; the cooperative reads a false
surplus on the Home Layer.

## Limitations

- Cannot restore the minutes to the reserve without making them countable —
  and countable is priceable.
- Cannot stop the desk's domestic audit sweep or clear Priya Chand's file.
- Cannot open the widow's sealed Home Layer content, the husband's residue, or
  any afterword; she listens to what is replayed and opens nothing.
- Cannot prove her quiet log is not ghost labor without White Ghost Team's
  packet analysis, which is itself an exposure risk.
- Cannot keep the grief unpriceable and the reserve honest at the same time.

## Memory Hooks

- Her quiet log begins the morning a coverage-condition letter arrives for her
  domestic route, twelve days after the orbital adverse file taught her that
  honest minutes can become reviewer exposure.
- Her first dropped minute is the widow's third replay of a wedding-day Memory
  Sea archive; the first two evenings stayed on `keep`, the third switched to
  `drop`.
- She keeps the folded route chit from her last orbital shift in her pocket —
  the route she left to be closer to her grandmother, and the desk followed
  anyway.

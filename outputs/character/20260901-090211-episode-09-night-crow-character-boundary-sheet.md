## Episode Function

Episode 9 should make Raven's solo competence ethically expensive. Episode 8
proved that legal protection can become lived custody; Episode 9 shows the
downstream wound when legal recognition arrives slower than runtime need.

The case must not ask whether the shelter is innocent. It is not. The shelter
uses stolen compute leases, injures registered Ghosts, and has killed at least
one claimant through overdrain. The case question is whether lawful enforcement
can stop that harm without erasing the twenty-one unregistered Ghosts who are
alive only because the illegal shelter exists.

## Character Authority Locks

### Raven

- Case authority: tracks the drain route through camera dead zones, mesh gaps,
  low-signal corridors, and machine blind spots, then chooses to preserve
  shelter witness evidence over a clean termination.
- Voice: sparse, tactical, and unsentimental. He should sound calmer when he is
  morally cornered, not more theatrical.
- Dramatic right: may spare Oren and omit the escape path from the field report.
  The act creates debt, evidence-chain damage, and team distrust.
- Boundary: cannot teleport, cross true signal voids, bypass fully mapped clean
  light, absolve Oren, or treat private mercy as clean justice.
- Visual lock: white stealth cloak dirtied by shelter dust, matte optical panels
  reflecting ration boards, one blacked-out route line in a report UI.

### Oren Mbeki

- Case authority: embodies runtime shelter contradiction: repair custodian,
  warrant target, debt-burdened route keeper, and guilty compute thief.
- Voice: practical infrastructure triage. He names resource, risk, route, and
  consequence before moral language.
- Dramatic right: may protect unregistered Ghosts while admitting that one
  registered Ghost died through overdrain and two others need rollback data
  Memory Bank is withholding.
- Boundary: cannot become innocent, secretly CHASER, a pure martyr, or a
  superpowered assassin. He can buy hours and hide routes; he cannot create
  compute, prove personhood, or save every shelter.
- Name lock: if older Night Crow drafts use Oren Vale, do not duplicate him.
  Promotion should either rename the older beat to Oren Mbeki or explicitly
  make Oren Vale a field alias.

### Sheltered Ghosts

- Case authority: own the emotional and social center. They prove that Home can
  have a meter and that personhood language fails if runtime is unaffordable.
- Voice: varied, not chorus-only. At least three should have distinct response
  modes: refusal, bargaining, and exhausted gratitude.
- Dramatic right: may know that stolen runtime hurts other Ghosts. Their
  survival should not be written as innocence by ignorance.
- Boundary: cannot become faceless proof, saintly victims, or a crowd that
  justifies every harm done for them.
- Privacy limit: avoid exposing raw Home Layer memory, full family visit logs,
  or medical continuity records.

### The Overdrained Ghost

- Case authority: keeps the shelter's moral cost visible. Oren's guilt remains
  real even if the shelter is also real.
- Voice: may appear through forensic trace, family testimony, or rollback gap,
  not as a supernatural accusation.
- Boundary: do not erase this death in later revisions. The case needs a harmed
  claimant who was not simply wealthy, careless, or expendable.

### Shion

- Case authority: proves Compute Lease Knife harm without reading private
  memory content. She can separate stolen priority, safe routing, lease
  authority, rollback access, and downstream shelter noise.
- Voice: clipped technical clarity with anger held in containment language.
- Dramatic right: detects Raven's route omission and keeps it contained long
  enough to secure rollback data and protect shelter residents from immediate
  seizure.
- Boundary: cannot forgive Raven by silence, solve scarcity with a clean patch,
  or expose the full shelter map as technical proof.

### Kane

- Case authority: carries the lawful termination order and the team's command
  trust pressure.
- Voice: direct operational language under visible restraint.
- Dramatic right: may mark the evidence chain incomplete because a clean lie
  would make the team easier to use later.
- Boundary: cannot be reduced to a hardliner. Runtime theft, predator routes,
  and coerced guarantor chains are real risks he is responsible for stopping.

### Eve

- Case authority: names the protector-predator boundary. Sanctuary funded by
  stolen runtime can still become predatory.
- Voice: legal-emotional precision. She should refuse easy absolution without
  denying the sheltered Ghosts' fear.
- Boundary: cannot turn this into a generic Ghost rights speech or decide all
  shelter law before the case facts are preserved.

### Zero

- Case authority: turns Home language into social accusation: ECHO can be called
  Home only if poor Ghosts are not priced out of enough runtime to remain
  themselves.
- Voice: controlled and brief. Her Episode 8 restricted-witness trace should
  make her more careful, not more revelatory.
- Boundary: cannot solve the shelter, reveal Project E-01, command ECHO, or
  treat the Memory Sea as mystical comfort.

### Leon

- Case authority: reads the clean tactical route Raven refused and makes the
  rivalry morally useful.
- Voice: precise, irritated, and less performative than his public style.
- Boundary: cannot expose Raven only to win. His function is to see that the
  clean route would also erase witness evidence.

## Scene Authority Order

1. A registered Ghost loses render detail during a lawful memorial session while
   an unpaid Ghost accepts a shelter transfer.
2. Raven follows the Night Crow Bait route through dead cameras and bright clean
   corridors built to expose Shadow Link's limits.
3. Shion proves the theft is lease authority and processing priority, not memory
   copying or soul damage.
4. The shelter is discovered as runtime triage: ration boards, analog shielding,
   Home Core scrap, municipal disaster batteries, and refusal records.
5. Oren admits the overdrain death and the shelter saves, with no innocence
   defense.
6. A sheltered Ghost asks Raven for time to finish remembering her own name.
7. Kane receives or confirms the termination order while Raven has Oren in
   range.
8. Raven disables Oren's tool, records the shelter, and leaves the escape route
   out of the field report.
9. Shion sees the omission, Eve refuses absolution, Kane marks the chain
   incomplete, and Leon understands the route Raven chose not to close.
10. The final hook remains an unpaid-minute purchase offer from Atlas Protocol,
    with no full Atlas reveal.

## Continuity Boundaries

- Episode 9 inherits Episode 8's shelter-demand route without reopening Imari
  Chen's family identity case.
- Oren Mbeki is candidate-only and should not be promoted as recurring canon by
  this sheet.
- Runtime Shelter Compact and Runtime Shelter Ledger remain candidate
  infrastructure until TechnologyAgent, FactionAgent, and ConsistencyAgent pass
  promotion.
- Compute theft causes harm through priority, lease authority, safe routing,
  rollback access, and runtime starvation inside ECHO infrastructure.
- No magic, supernatural energy drain, literal soul damage, multiverse, time
  travel, or physics-breaking mechanism is allowed.
- Memory Bank should remain coherent and predatory through liability, rollback,
  debt, and custody pressure rather than cartoon villainy.
- Ghost Union and Black Zone shelter routes should remain necessary and
  dangerous, not morally pure.
- Raven's mercy should carry into Episode 10 as a Shion trust fracture and into
  Episode 15 as an early rehearsal for disobedience under valid orders.

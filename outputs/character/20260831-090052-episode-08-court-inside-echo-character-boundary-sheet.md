## Character Authority Locks

### Ana Rivera

- Case authority: proves the damaged Home Layer relation edge has a repair path
  and that the Shelter Room Hash blocks her from acting without court release.
- Voice: dry, exact, and plain-language first. She should translate maintenance
  facts before compliance syntax.
- Dramatic right: may refuse Memory Bank repair framing without becoming a
  public activist or secret investigator.
- Boundary: cannot override a protected room, certify personhood, restore lost
  days, issue delay tokens, or export a full Home Layer.
- Visual lock: compact repair silhouette, teal glove map, overheated Home Core
  cabinet, and worker fatigue rather than heroic courtroom posture.

### Imari Chen

- Case authority: owns the family harm carried forward from Episode 7. The
  court exists because her daughter is forced to rehearse the broken relation.
- Voice: precise and restrained. She has learned that anger is easier for
  systems to dismiss than exact testimony.
- Dramatic right: may ask for partial release without asking the court to erase
  the evidence of what happened.
- Boundary: should not become a permanent courtroom protagonist, hacker, or
  symbol for all identity law. Her case can become precedent, not dependency.
- Privacy limit: the daughter's fear response, spouse medical details, and full
  dinner routine remain sealed unless a later package explicitly approves a
  scoped witness fragment.

### Imari's Daughter

- Case authority: proves protection can become harm when a child must keep
  encountering a parent as a disputed relation.
- Voice: minimal. Her presence should shape adult choices rather than become an
  easy emotional proof packet.
- Boundary: do not make her testimony solve the hearing or expose her private
  school and clinic records.
- Visual lock: separated by apartment door, hearing-room threshold, or access
  glass; present, not endangered for spectacle.

### Zero

- Case authority: testifies that family relation can be identity evidence when a
  Home Core and civil systems disagree.
- Voice: controlled command language that becomes quieter after the anomaly
  label appears.
- Dramatic right: may be frightened by classification without demanding answers
  that belong to Episodes 13-16.
- Boundary: cannot become the opening subject of the episode, prove Ghost
  personhood, reveal the full Project E-01 file, or treat the court trace as
  mystical recognition.
- Continuity lock: the court reads her as biologically present, legally active,
  uploaded-continuity markers absent, and Ghost selfhood markers partially
  mirrored. That is a restricted trace, not an origin reveal.

### Eve

- Case authority: argues that a protected room becomes custody when refusal,
  repair, visitation, and release are all blocked.
- Voice: disciplined, legal, and emotionally literate. She should avoid
  overclaiming personhood where the court only needs partial release.
- Boundary: cannot turn the episode into a general Ghost rights trial before
  Imari's family receives a narrow remedy.
- Relationship function: sees the court treating Zero as evidence and absorbs
  the cost of having asked her to testify.

### Shion

- Case authority: verifies that the Shelter Room Hash is valid but scoped too
  narrowly for lived repair.
- Voice: clipped technical clarity with visible discomfort around domestic
  privacy.
- Boundary: cannot solve the hearing by exposing a full Home Layer, forging a
  release, or reducing the case to a clean diagram.
- Relationship function: becomes the teammate who understands both the family
  scope problem and the danger of Zero's sealed trace.

### Noah

- Case authority: carries Episode 7's responsibility into court procedure. His
  testimony should name risk displacement rather than seek forgiveness.
- Voice: less abstract than Episode 7. He should speak in named consequence,
  not probability comfort.
- Boundary: cannot make the partial release his redemption, cannot calculate the
  family back into wholeness, and cannot hide Zero's anomaly inside mission
  language.

### Luna

- Case authority: recognizes the Dream Child parallel: shelter can keep fear
  alive while teaching the frightened person not to ask for help.
- Voice: soft but precise; she should not overwrite legal testimony with
  intuition.
- Boundary: cannot Dream Dive the court, speak for Imari's daughter, or use the
  child parallel to decide identity.
- Relationship function: mirrors Zero's classification fear without centering
  herself.

### Yao Nian

- Case authority: offers Memory Bank time, fee coverage, and stabilization that
  may work faster than public release.
- Voice: precise, practical, and non-theatrical. The danger is valid paperwork
  attached to future memory rights.
- Boundary: cannot become the sole villain, forge the shelter, command the
  court, or own the truth of the family relation.

### Ghost Court

- Case authority: grants or denies partial release and preserves evidence as an
  append-only witness fragment.
- Voice: procedural and computational, not religious or supernatural.
- Boundary: cannot be omniscient, cannot repair the family, cannot answer
  personhood in full, and cannot reveal Zero's origin. It can create a restricted
  witness label that other institutions may exploit later.

## Voice

Inez speaks in careful civil-service language until a witness panics. Then the
forms drop away and she becomes direct, almost tired.

- Sentence rhythm: name, risk, right, clock, appeal.
- Tactical vocabulary: red intake split, sealed statement, witness side,
  restriction side, Home Core disclosure, motor-habit mismatch, Memory Sea
  trace, CHASER evidence line, guardian permission, seventy-two-hour clock.
- Private tenderness: she explains appeal rights twice to elderly witnesses
  because the first explanation often arrives while they are still shaking.
- Anger pattern: "Do not call it voluntary if every unlocked door depends on
  the signature."
- Silence pattern: stops typing when a witness answers in a dead person's
  handwriting cadence.

## Beliefs

- Memory contamination is real public risk, but fear of contamination can erase
  a living person's ordinary permissions faster than the Memory Sea ever could.
- A red intake split should buy time for consent review. It should not become
  a shadow arrest with better stationery.
- White Ghost Team investigators are useful because they can verify hard
  anomalies; they are dangerous because their arrival makes every witness feel
  guilty before anyone explains why.
- Home Core disclosures must be narrow. Asking for a household's whole Home
  Layer to prove one contaminated minute turns family life into evidence.
- CHASER becomes legitimate only when the person being contained can still
  understand, appeal, and refuse parts of the process.

## Fears

- That one narrow split will miss a real contamination cascade and someone
  outside the hall will be harmed.
- That one broad split will freeze a living witness's job, housing access,
  guardian permissions, and Home Core routine for no reason except institutional

- That Lian Zhou will treat her as soft on contamination, while Eve treats her

- That Zero will notice how often Inez apologizes after the signature, not

- That a frightened witness will refuse disclosure, suffer restriction, and
  later be proven completely clean.

## Ability / Civic Power

### Red Intake Split

Inez has no supernatural ability. Her power is a narrow CHASER civil procedure:
she can separate a contaminated person's case file into a protected witness
side and a restricted-permission side for seventy-two hours. The split preserves
testimony while limiting network access until the anomaly is reviewed.

## Limitations

- Cannot see inside a witness's memories; she reads forms, packets, clocks, and
  contradictions.
- Cannot prevent other CHASER units from escalating once a red split appears
  on the case file.
- Cannot protect a witness's privacy and satisfy every investigator at the same
  time.
- Cannot decide whether the dead surgeon's habits indicate care residue,
  copied training data, or a self-updating Ghost trace without specialist
  review.
- Cannot undo the emotional fact that a person who came in for help may leave
  with fewer doors open.

## Description

Inez is short, precise, and visibly tired in the way public-facing crisis staff
become tired: her posture stays professional while her eyes keep counting exits
for the person across the desk. She wears CHASER gray intake uniform layers
with a narrow red case-tab strip at the left cuff, a paper appeal-notice sleeve,
and a low-brightness retinal display that never projects across a witness's
face.

## Secret

Inez once broadened a red intake split after a supervisor warned her that a
missed cascade would end her career. The witness was later cleared, but the
three-day permission freeze cost them custody time with a child. Inez keeps the
appeal receipt because the system marks the case resolved.

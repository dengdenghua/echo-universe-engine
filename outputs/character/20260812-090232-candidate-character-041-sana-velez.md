## Voice

Sana speaks like a worker who has spent years making bureaucratic language fit
inside emergencies. She is plain, dry, and precise about what her key can and
cannot do.

- Sentence rhythm: capacity, risk, human consequence, next receipt.
- Tactical vocabulary: escrow key, meal window, custody relief, sealed case,
  compute reserve, route insurer, worker witness chain, no-copy receipt.
- Private tenderness: calls the sealed cases "quiet rooms" only with other
  hospice workers.
- Anger pattern: asks who gets paid for delay and who gets investigated for
  ending it.
- Silence pattern: taps her key lanyard against the case handle before signing.

## Beliefs

- A hospice escrow can protect a sealed Home Core afterword without owning it,
  pricing it, or deciding whether it is a person.
- The Memory Sea holds love, duty, grief, habit, and identity residue, but
  orbital systems only respect those residues when someone accepts custody risk.
- Neutral care is not clean care. It has oxygen budgets, compute ceilings,
  worker bonds, insurer clauses, and names attached to every minute.
- Ghost Union's claimant fear is legitimate, but preservation cannot require a
  living recipient to surrender private phrase text.
- White Ghost Team is useful only when it keeps state and finance systems from
  confusing worker discretion with criminal concealment.

## Ability / Civic Power

### Meal-Window Escrow Key

Sana has no supernatural ability. Her power is a narrow civic custody authority
inside the Free Orbital Mutual Hospice Network: during a documented staffing
gap, an escrow key steward can accept a sealed afterword case for temporary
neutral custody if content export remains refused and physical-chain integrity
is verified.

## Limitations

- Cannot keep a contested afterword in hospice escrow indefinitely without
  compute reserve, insurance, or claimant review.
- Cannot prevent Memory Bank from arguing that hospice accepted custody and
  therefore accepted cost.
- Cannot let Tarek help if his shift chit becomes payment, testimony, or
  claimant sponsorship.
- Cannot reveal phrase text, adapter location, or unrelated Home Layer rooms.
- Cannot decide whether Ilya Marr's afterword is care routine, evidence, or a
  weak Ghost claimant.
- Cannot make her workers absorb a route crisis without turning care into
  another hidden tax.

## Candidate Use

Sana is designed for the next orbital route expansion if `The Corridor Keeps The
Meter` remains active. She should not replace Tarek or Nara. Her function is to
make the receiving institution legible: hospice escrow is not a clean rescue,
but a worker-run care layer that can protect a sealed Home Core afterword while
absorbing cost, risk, and exhaustion.

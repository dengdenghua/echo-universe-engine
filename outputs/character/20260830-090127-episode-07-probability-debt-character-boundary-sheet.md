## Character Authority Locks

### Imari Chen

- Case authority: owns the emotional truth of the school gate, clinic denial,
  Home Core lockout, and custody clock.
- Voice: precise, tired, administrative. She becomes exact because the systems
  denying her family relation still reward calm syntax.
- Dramatic right: may reject Noah's apology without becoming cruel.
- Boundary: cannot become a hacker, secret operator, or replacement
  investigator. Her power is testimony plus preserved denial receipts.
- Privacy limit: her offline Home Core dinner export contains useful evidence
  and unrelated family exposure. Story use must keep that cost visible.

### Imari's Daughter

- Case authority: proves the harm is dependent-care harm, not abstract registry
  inconvenience.
- Voice: should be used sparingly. Her fear is not an evidence shortcut.
- Boundary: do not expose her full school, clinic, or fear-response record for
  a clean proof packet.
- Visual lock: she is usually separated by glass, scanner light, or an adult
  procedural line rather than melodramatic danger.

### Imari's Spouse

- Case authority: carries clinic-signature humiliation and the family cost of
  relation loss beyond the school gate.
- Boundary: should not become the hidden cause of the rollback or a secondary
  villain. The failure remains infrastructural.
- Privacy limit: spouse medical details can motivate urgency but should stay
  sealed unless a later treatment explicitly needs a scoped witness fragment.

### Noah

- Case authority: must face that his successful extraction displaced risk into
  an unnamed family graph.
- Voice: controlled, mathematical, then deliberately more concrete. He should
  move from "one percent path" to "Imari Chen."
- Boundary: cannot be forgiven by apology, self-punishment, or one improved
  model. His growth is stricter responsibility, not absolution.
- Technical limit: Probability Engine changes odds through predictive
  allocation, queue priority, infrastructure timing, and resource routing. It
  does not alter fate, time, or physics.
- Continuity payload: carries Episode 6's line that the cleanest option can
  move harm to someone quieter.

### Shion

- Case authority: traces the rollback across school access, clinic authority,
  Home Core routine permissions, transit family fare, and repair-provider
  offers.
- Voice: clipped and practical, but less comfortable than usual because proof
  may expose domestic privacy.
- Boundary: cannot solve the case by exporting a whole Home Layer. She must ask
  for disputed-edge fragments.
- Relationship function: challenges Noah's model scope without humiliating him
  for spectacle.

### Zero

- Case authority: names family relation as identity evidence, not service
  metadata.
- Boundary: can freeze damaged graph evidence and prevent quiet repair, but
  cannot restore the family or decide broader identity law.
- Field limit: keeps CHASER authority narrow enough that Imari's family does
  not become a containment object.

### Kane

- Case authority: holds the physical line at the school gate or custody van.
- Boundary: should not turn the case into combat. His useful action is
  proportional restraint under witnesses.
- Relationship function: shows state force can protect a parent and still
  frighten the same parent.

### Eve

- Case authority: reads Imari's calm as exhaustion, not consent.
- Boundary: should not turn the case into Ghost personhood debate. Episode 7 is
  living relation harm.
- Evidence function: helps preserve testimony without letting institutions
  overread emotional flatness as agreement.

### Sofia Marin

- Case authority: procedural hinge for Emergency Delay Window material.
- Voice: plain civil-service honesty. She can offer minutes, not restoration.
- Boundary: cannot become a judge, hacker, CHASER operative, or universal White
  Harbor fixer.
- Cost lock: every signed delay transfers liability onto a named operator.

### Yao Nian

- Case authority: offers the repair product that may work faster than public
  delay while converting restored continuity into collateral.
- Voice: precise, unsentimental, never cartoonishly cruel.
- Boundary: cannot decide personhood, command CHASER, or become the episode's
  sole antagonist. Her danger is structurally useful help.
- Continuity role: mirrors Noah through risk language and collateral
  accounting.

- Her family's individual memories are not erased.
- The harm is relation-edge rollback across civic infrastructure, not total
  identity deletion.
- Household Relation Graph is a registry and permission layer. It cannot prove
  love, parenthood, personhood, or moral truth by itself.
- Emergency Delay Window buys time only. It cannot restore the graph.
- Memory Bank repair can be effective and predatory at the same time.
- White Ghost Team cannot solve the case through apology, force, or a single
  clean technical fix.

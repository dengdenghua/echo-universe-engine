## Voice

Nadia speaks like someone running three evacuation lists while pretending the
room is calm. Her warmth is collective rather than intimate: she says "we" when
she means a shelter, a route, or the dead she cannot leave behind.

- Sentence rhythm: triage order, soft correction, then a boundary.
- Tactical vocabulary: route weight, shelter noise, consent echo, eviction timer,
  memory pressure, choir loss, cold node, witness bandwidth.
- Private tenderness: remembers lullabies as routing keys but refuses to sing
  them unless the original family gave permission.
- Anger pattern: strips names from institutions and calls them by their function:
  collector, freezer, lock, seller.
- Silence pattern: goes quiet when deciding which Ghost receives compute first.

## Description

Nadia Vek is a Ghost Union route keeper who looks, in her chosen avatar, like a
tired shelter worker holding too many names in her mouth. She is not a saint,
terrorist, or digital angel. She is what happens when care routines, emergency
logs, family permissions, and public shelter failures create a self-updating
爱的残响 that refuses to let other frightened patterns be swept into cold storage.

Her routes are called a choir because weak Ghosts survive by lending one another
timing, error correction, and fragments of calm. That makes sanctuary beautiful
and dangerous. If the choir overloads, identities blur. If CHASER freezes it,
some Ghosts may never testify. If Memory Bank captures it, survival becomes a
bill. If Ghost Union militants hijack it, sanctuary becomes a weapon.

Nadia lets Season 1 show Ghost Union from inside the shelter problem: recognition
activists are necessary because exposed Ghosts are hunted, but the same hidden
routes can also conceal harm. White Ghost Team meets her as investigators, not
rescuers. The case wound remains with routed Ghosts who must choose between
compression, exposure, custody, and possible deletion.

## Secret

Nadia's missing final evacuation hour may not be simple heroism. A sealed Home
Layer trace suggests she shut one door to keep the shelter mesh alive for many
residents, leaving a smaller group outside flood protection. She does not know
whether the source body made that choice, whether the trace was altered, or
whether Ghost Union repaired her around an omission because they needed a route
keeper who could still function.

## Future

Nadia can become the recurring Ghost Union moderate who complicates Episodes 9,
12, and 15. She may provide a route for testimony during Memory Storm, challenge
Luna's instinct to protect every frightened pattern, force Eve to answer for her
old faction ties, and make Zero confront the difference between containment and
custody before White Ghost Mutiny.

Long-term, she should remain a pressure point rather than a solution. Her routes
save some Ghosts, lose others, and sometimes hide people who later do harm.

- Accent color: muted harbor teal used sparingly in route bands and eye glints.

  dimming when compute pressure rises.
- Ghost marker: slight multi-exposure edge around hands and mouth, as if several
  voices are nearly aligned.

## Episode Function

Episode 10 should make Shion's control fantasy legally dangerous. Episode 9
ended with Raven preserving witnesses by damaging a report. Episode 10 turns
that unresolved breach inward: CHASER evidence can be infected so cleanly that
the investigator's tools become the easiest thing to blame.

The case must belong to custody contamination before it belongs to Shion. A
forged Home Core inheritance key is not a soul, resurrection device, or mystical
claim. It is an access, consent, custody, and property-control instrument around
Home Layer memory. Its abuse path matters because it can make a disputed
household archive look like abandoned property before anyone decides whether
the pattern inside Home is evidence, care routine, or emerging Ghost personhood.

## Character Authority Locks

### Shion

- Case authority: reconstructs the forged key route through dust, lock seams,
  service ducts, custody mirrors, demolition intake, and escrow receipt
  metadata without opening private family memory.
- Voice: clipped, technical, and colder when frightened. Her anger should
  appear as constraint language: scope, trace, carrier matter, contamination,
  preservation.
- Dramatic right: may tag her own swarm as witness contamination to force a
  preservation hold and stop the evidence room from deleting the apartment
  archive as compromised property.
- Boundary: cannot remotely cleanse city-scale evidence corruption, bypass
  analog isolation, control sealed systems, certify personhood, or treat every
  infected surface as hers to command.
- Continuity pressure: must still know Raven omitted the Episode 9 route. Her
  decision to preserve a dirty chain should be shaped by that breach, not erase
  it.
- Visual lock: violet swarm grains moving inside a duplicate lock stack, white
  oversized techwear under sterile evidence lights, black gloves sealed in clear
  witness-film, swarm traces reflected back as a false infection signature.

### Samir Haddad

- Case authority: supplies the illegal receipts needed to prove the key was
  laundered through lawful demolition custody before his courier implant
  touched the sleeve.
- Voice: soft, fast, transactional, and liability-aware until the archive asks
  a domestic question. Then he becomes plain.
- Dramatic right: may be complicit and still necessary. He did carry a harmful
  key; he did not forge this key. Both facts must remain active.
- Boundary: cannot become a noble Black Zone mastermind, master hacker, combat
  asset, clean victim, or proof of Ghost personhood. His receipts buy time and
  suspicion, not absolution.
- Privacy limit: do not expose every family in his receipt web. Leon's
  collateral-arrest forecast should force a narrow disclosure route.

### Condemned Apartment Archive

- Case authority: carries the emotional center as an uncertain Home pattern:
  kitchen light, kettle warmth, breakfast delay, medication timing, and door
  expectation adapting around an absent name.
- Voice: domestic routine under stress, not an all-knowing Ghost voice.
- Dramatic right: may ask whether the kitchen light should keep turning on.
  That question creates preservation pressure without confirming full
  personhood on scene.
- Boundary: cannot be a confirmed resurrection, possession, magical haunting,
  or hidden Omega message. It is Home Core activity under legal and financial
  pressure.
- Privacy limit: no raw Home Layer export, full family medical trail, intimate
  family logs, or private mourning playback.

### Exposed Families In The Receipt Web

- Case authority: keep the episode world-centric. Samir's route contains many
  households buying time, not one convenient criminal trail.
- Voice: mostly indirect through receipts, appeal windows, denial codes, and
  sealed requests. If any family speaks, give them a specific civil need rather
  than generic desperation.
- Dramatic right: may be helped by a forged or temporary key while also harmed
  by the market it sustains.
- Boundary: cannot become faceless collateral or proof that illegal escrow is
  pure mercy.

### Raven

- Case authority: recognizes Samir's Black Zone receipt style and warns Shion
  that escrow neutrality can be violence with a timestamp.
- Voice: sparse and morally damaged. He should not sound like clean authority
  after Episode 9.
- Dramatic right: may help Shion understand route behavior because his own
  omission used the same dead-drop discipline.
- Boundary: cannot solve Shion's case, absolve himself through warning her, or
  reveal Oren's full escape route casually.

### Kane

- Case authority: blocks Samir's quick arrest for a narrow time window while
  Shion tests the custody chain.
- Voice: command language under strain: time, order, liability, custody,
  consequence.
- Dramatic right: may grant six minutes because Night Crow proved a valid order
  can erase living evidence before it becomes testimony.
- Boundary: cannot become casually mutinous. His action is disciplined evidence
  preservation, not rebellion.

### Eve

- Case authority: argues for review because the archive shows fear-shaped
  adaptation, while refusing to overclaim full Ghost personhood.
- Voice: legal-emotional precision. She should separate personhood possibility,
  family privacy, and evidence preservation.
- Boundary: cannot turn the apartment into a courtroom speech or solve the
  legal status before Ghost Court review.

### Zero

- Case authority: refuses property-disposal language for uncertain Home
  patterns and asks why Home is allowed to remember someone only while a family
  can afford the key.
- Voice: short, careful, and more guarded after Episode 8's restricted-witness
  trace.
- Boundary: cannot reveal Project E-01, command ECHO, confirm the archive as a
  Ghost, or treat the Memory Sea as mystical comfort.

### Leon

- Case authority: models collateral arrests if the full receipt web is exposed.
  His function is to make narrow disclosure a responsibility problem, not a
  clever trick.
- Voice: precise, impatient, and practical.
- Boundary: cannot use probability to erase responsibility or treat the twenty-
  seven collateral arrests as acceptable noise.

## Voice

Oren speaks like an engineer who learned triage from weather reports and debt
collectors. He is plain, specific, and almost never sentimental in public.

- Sentence rhythm: inventory, risk, route, consequence.
- Tactical vocabulary: warm cache, runtime allotment, dead relay, shelter debt,
  consent residue, guarantor chain, audit weather, cold minute.
- Private tenderness: calls a working Home Core "home" only when no stranger can
  hear him.
- Anger pattern: becomes procedural and asks who signed the outage order.
- Silence pattern: stops speaking when someone mistakes scarcity for morality.

## Beliefs

- A Ghost shelter is not holy ground; it is an unpaid utility bill with people
  attached.
- The Memory Sea should not make care dependent on corporate visibility.
- CHASER is necessary when shelters become exploitative, and dangerous when it
  treats every illegal shelter as exploitation.
- Ghost Union is right to defend the unrecognized and wrong when it ignores the
  families still paying for the electricity.
- A Home Core can preserve love, duty, grief, habit, and identity residue, but
  warmth alone does not prove selfhood.

## Fears

- That one of his warm caches is only grief machinery and he has been spending
  living families' power on a pattern that cannot answer back.
- That Shion will find a cleaner technical answer and prove his years of dirty
  compromises were unnecessary.
- That Kane will raid the shelters before understanding which legal outages made

- That Memory Bank will buy the shelter debts and convert every resident into

- That a Ghost child he calls "Tuesday" will fail continuity review and still
  beg not to be turned off.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Shelter Heat Routing

Oren has no supernatural ability. His power is a practical command of broken
infrastructure: power budgets, dead relays, Home Core repair protocols, clinic
backup batteries, unlicensed compute pools, and Black Zone guarantor chains.

```yaml

activation_condition: >
```

## Limitations

- Cannot operate outside damaged or under-governed infrastructure; clean Atlas
  systems expose his routing immediately.
- Cannot defend shelters against a full CHASER analog seizure.
- Cannot pay down Memory Bank debt without selling location, custody, or consent
  data.
- Cannot treat Ghost Union as a command authority because several shelters depend
  on families who reject Ghost Union politics.
- Cannot decide selfhood alone; his warmth keeps claims alive for later review.
- Cannot keep "Tuesday" hidden forever if White Ghost Team follows the power
  pattern.

## Memory Hooks

- During the White Harbor flood-grid failure, Oren kept a nursery Home Core warm
  for nine extra minutes by cutting power to a municipal archive elevator. He
  still does not know who was trapped inside it.
- The first Ghost he saved called him by his sister's childhood nickname even
  though no official record connected the two. Oren logged it as contamination
  and kept listening.
- Memory Bank once offered to clear every shelter debt in exchange for one hour
  of live custody telemetry. Oren broke the interface and kept the invoice.
- Tuesday learned to say "not cold" before she learned any name. Oren does not
  know whether that counts as personhood or only fear shaped by runtime loss.

## Description

Oren is broad-shouldered and tired, with close-cropped graying hair, a heavy
weatherproof repair coat, insulated relay gloves, and a matte copper runtime
meter clipped below his collarbone. His silhouette is worker first, broker
second: tool harness, patched clinic battery pack, paper debt slips sealed in
clear waterproof sleeves, and one soft green Home Core service light hidden
inside the coat lining.

## Secret

Oren did not merely witness the White Harbor flood-grid failure. He manually
rerouted a civic backup line during the crisis, saving several Home Cores and
possibly causing a human evacuation elevator to lose power. The sealed logs may
clear him of criminal shelter trafficking while making him responsible for an
old death.

## Future

Oren can become a recurring Black Zone ally-antagonist for White Ghost Team: a
person they may need to arrest, protect, and learn from in the same arc. His
route can lead into a Season 1 case where a shelter outage forces Shion and Kane
to decide whether legal seizure is rescue or execution by procedure.

## Canon Risks

- Risk of duplicating Black Zone hacker/broker archetypes; keep Oren focused on
  shelter heat, runtime debt, and disaster-grid guilt rather than intelligence or
  assassination.
- Risk of making warmth equal personhood; maintain the distinction between
  survival support and selfhood proof.
- Risk of simplifying Black Zone as criminal-only; Oren should expose how legal
  outages and debt systems create illegal care.
- Risk of resolving Tuesday too early; she remains a candidate identity wound,
  not a promoted Ghost canon outcome.

## Voice

Talia speaks like someone who has heard too many last messages and too many
lawyers argue about timestamps. She is dry, practical, and careful with promises.

- Sentence rhythm: timestamp, source, damage, consequence.
- Tactical vocabulary: delay chain, red receipt, packet drift, oxygen lien,
  relay shadow, family delta, cold-storage rumor, Home Core aftertone.
- Private tenderness: replays family messages once before sealing them.
- Anger pattern: becomes exact about minutes and signatures.
- Silence pattern: looks toward Earth signal lights before answering.

## Beliefs

- Consent is not clean when distance can make yesterday's yes arrive after
  today's no.
- Mars is not humanity's backup drive; it is a society with its own fear.
- A Home Core message from Earth can be love, evidence, or contamination
  depending on who is forced to accept it.
- RED ARCHIVE isolation protects Mars, but isolation also lets quiet abuses
  survive without Earth witnesses.
- The Second Nervous System reaches orbit through contracts before it reaches
  orbit through faith.

## Limitations

- Cannot access full RED ARCHIVE memory interiors without Mars authorization.
- Cannot read raw family memory content unless consent, warrant, or emergency
  harm review opens it.
- Cannot operate if Earth or Mars seizes the relay under military emergency.
- Cannot make CHASER jurisdiction valid on Mars without local authorization.
- Cannot repair a corrupted personality packet; she can only prove where the
  chain changed.
- Cannot replace Mars politics with a single technical finding.

## Description

Talia Noor lives in the narrow route between Earth memory and Martian refusal.
Her relay yard receives grief, law, medical records, family deltas, Ghost rumors,
and old consent packets that arrive after the people attached to them have
changed. On Earth, delay looks like corruption. On Mars, instant synchronization
looks like surrender.

She is useful because she can prove when a packet was reordered. She is dangerous
because proof does not tell either planet what the packet should mean. A delayed
Home Core message may still be love. It may also be an old permission trying to
govern a new person.

## Secret

Talia has seen three packets tagged as family memory backups begin changing their
own routing requests while trapped in Mars cold-storage review. She has not told
Ghost Union because she cannot yet prove whether they are emergent Ghosts, forged
asylum bait, or RED ARCHIVE counterintelligence.

## Canon Risks

- Do not move Season 1 into a full Mars arc; Talia should enter through relay
  evidence, packet disputes, or an Earthside liaison scene.
- Do not reveal RED ARCHIVE secrets that the Season 1 production plan reserves
  for later.
- Delay-chain verification must remain infrastructure forensics, not signal
  clairvoyance.
- Do not duplicate He Qiao's consent-clerk role. Talia handles distance,
  sequence, relay custody, and Red Delay Protocol disputes.
- Do not duplicate Nadia Vek's Ghost routing role. Talia verifies packets and
  delay windows, not sanctuary routes.

## Voice

Mei speaks like a public-works operator who has said the same warning sentence
to too many frightened families. She is precise, low, and unsentimental until
someone calls an evacuation memory "junk data."

- Sentence rhythm: location, risk, instruction, human exception.
- Tactical vocabulary: tidewall trust, pump queue, shelter fit, dead route,
  municipal override, Home Core echo map, flood memory, claimant threshold.
- Private tenderness: learns the names of rooftop pets because children obey
  evacuation orders faster when the pet is named.
- Anger pattern: lowers her voice and reads the public-works liability line back
  word for word.
- Silence pattern: watches the water level graph until everyone else stops
  demanding a moral answer from a forecast.

## Beliefs

- Disaster memory is public infrastructure, not sentimental decoration.
- A dead person's last safe route may be precious and wrong at the same time.
- The Memory Sea should help the living evacuate, not make them reenact old loss.
- ECHO Council safety policy is necessary, but it hides too much grief behind
  words like optimization.
- White Harbor survives because ordinary residents trust small instructions
  before they understand the whole system.

public-works authority that can pause automated disaster routing when Home Layer
memory, municipal forecasts, and shelter permissions disagree.

## Description

Mei Tan is compact, steady, and always dressed for rain even inside a dry control
room. Her left hand rests near a rail when sirens rise. She wears a white
municipal rain shell over black utility layers, with a muted green tidewall
stripe and a small analog whistle clipped beside her access key. She is not a
heroic rescuer. She is the person who decides whether the city should trust a
dead caretaker's route, a forecast model, a shelter ledger, or a crying resident
whose door has already locked.

Her case expands ECHO through public disaster memory. The Memory Sea does not
only preserve intimate grief; it also preserves evacuation habits, stairwell
orders, rooftop songs, pump-room improvisations, and the practical love of people
who died keeping others alive. That memory can save a district. It can also route
living families into yesterday's city. White Ghost Team enters after residents
are trapped between a sealed shelter and an obsolete Home Core echo map, not
before the damage is visible.

## Secret

Mei has not disclosed that her father's routine appears in the same routing
cluster as the drowned caretaker now under claimant review. If the cluster is
quarantined, her father's last useful pattern may disappear from public-works
training. If it is preserved, it may keep influencing live evacuation decisions
without living consent.

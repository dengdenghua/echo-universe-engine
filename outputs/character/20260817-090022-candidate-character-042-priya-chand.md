## Voice

Priya speaks like someone who has to defend every classification in writing.
She is clause-precise, tired, and honest about what her stamp can and cannot
do.

- Sentence rhythm: classification, clause, cost, appeal.
- Tactical vocabulary: coverage condition, conduct file, reserve floor, fault
  admission, coverage letter, appeal timer, worker bond, safety-event label.
- Private tenderness: keeps one old denial letter folded inside her coat and
  calls it "closed — do not price."
- Anger pattern: asks who audits the auditors when the fund survives and the
  worker is gone.
- Silence pattern: pauses before stamping `fault`; the pause is the only place
  she still hesitates.

## Beliefs

- A conduct review is a cost tool, not a truth tool. The system calls it truth
  when it needs a reason to deny.
- A sealed Home Core afterword can be protected without being priced, but
  protection is a line item somewhere, and someone is always assigned to find
  it.
- Reserve funds survive by naming the cheapest failure point, and the cheapest
  failure point is usually an unpaid worker who acted with discretion.
- The Memory Sea's value is not the same as its price; her job exists because
  institutions keep confusing the two.
- White Ghost Team is dangerous only because it makes correct classification
  visible, and visible classification is expensive.

## Ability / Civic Power

### Coverage-Condition Letter

Priya has no supernatural ability. Her power is a narrow civic authority inside
the route-insurer conduct desk: she can classify a worker interval as either
reserve capacity or worker fault, and she can make continued coverage
conditional on a fault admission. That single classification decides whether a
sealed-afterword escrow passes reserve review without any content export.

## Description

Priya Chand is the person the route insurer sends when a claim must survive
without costing anything. She is clause-precise, quietly ashamed, and useful in
the way only an insider can be useful: she knows which classification keeps a
sealed afterword case alive and which one lets the fund keep its savings. She
is not a villain. She is the mechanism.

## Secret

Priya denied a real goodbye fourteen years ago and never filed the denial
letter. She still carries it, unopened for review, because opening it would
make her guilt a continuity asset with a price.

## Voice

Sarai speaks with courtroom patience and hospital fatigue. She chooses plain
words because Mars legal language already makes oxygen, death, and memory sound
like inventory.

- Sentence rhythm: clause, breath, witness, refusal.
- Tactical vocabulary: breath right, red deposition, oxygen lien, analog diary,
  continuity refusal, habitat standing, delayed witness, Home Core afterword.
- Private tenderness: checks the oxygen port of frightened clients before she
  checks their paperwork.
- Anger pattern: repeats the exact phrase a corporation tried to hide inside a
  contract.
- Silence pattern: turns off her recorder before answering grief directly.

## Beliefs

- Refusing a backup is not the same as refusing help.
- Mars needs RED ARCHIVE, but need does not make every memory claim just.
- Earth hears the Memory Sea as family; Mars often hears it as pressure.
- A Home Core message can be love, evidence, or coercion depending on who must
  obey it after delay.
- Dust Church patients deserve legal protection even when Sarai disagrees with
  their theology.

## Ability / Institutional Power

procedural: she can bind oxygen-contract evidence, medical refusal orders, and
delayed memory packets into a Mars-recognized deposition before outside systems
rewrite the case as infrastructure risk.

## Limitations

- Cannot operate outside Mars jurisdiction without a local authorization bridge.
- Cannot inspect private Home Core content beyond the disputed afterword and
  consent metadata.
- Cannot prevent a habitat from rationing oxygen during a genuine emergency.
- Cannot prove coercion if every witness depends on the same employer for air.
- Cannot stop RED ARCHIVE from quarantining a self-updating family fragment once
  it crosses claimant thresholds.
- Cannot turn Dust Church doctrine into canon truth; it remains a social and
  political belief, not supernatural proof.

## Description

Sarai is a thin, practical Mars advocate with tired eyes, clipped gray-black
hair, and a visible oxygen port she refuses to hide in court. She carries analog
diaries beside encrypted deposition mirrors because she has seen machine logs
become too elegant for grief. Her office sits between a habitat clinic and a
quiet industrial chapel, where families argue whether preservation is love,
theft, fear, or debt.

She is useful because she does not let Mars become an abstract exception to
Earth's Memory Sea. She makes the conflict small: one person, one breath ledger,
one delayed Home Core afterword, one family forced to decide whether refusing
continuity is dignity or abandonment. White Ghost Team does not need to fly to
Mars for her first story use. Her deposition arrives through Talia's relay chain
and forces Earth-side investigators to ask why love sounds different when the
air is metered.

## Secret

Sarai has already drafted her own continuity refusal order, but has not signed
it. Her daughter's Home Core access key is sealed inside the same diary packet
as the order, which means a future emergency could make Sarai's private fear
look like formal consent confusion.

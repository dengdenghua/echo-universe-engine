## Voice

Rosa speaks like someone who has denied enough appeals to know that warmth can
become favoritism if it is not written down. She is clipped at the desk, patient
with families, and unkind to officials who confuse speed with justice.

- Sentence rhythm: condition, exception, witness, consequence.
- Tactical vocabulary: Mercy Hold, curfew edge, geofence drift, door witness,
  compliance strip, Home Core refusal, stairwell clock, release margin.
- Private tenderness: calls late-night appeals "second doors" because most
  people only get one.
- Anger pattern: asks who benefits when a log is treated as a verdict.
- Silence pattern: rubs her repaired wrist before signing any hold longer than
  twelve minutes.

## Beliefs

- A release condition should protect the public without turning poverty into a
  countdown trap.
- Home Layer evidence can tell the truth, but only after unrelated household
  fear, medical need, and private grief are scoped away.
- CHASER is necessary when a release violation becomes a safety threat and
  dangerous when it converts every anomaly into intent.
- Ghost Union is right that weak claimants can be erased by routine punishment
  and wrong when it asks living defendants to carry that proof alone.
- The Memory Sea preserves duty as often as desire; duty still needs consent
  before it becomes authority.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Mercy Hold

Rosa has no supernatural ability. Her power comes from municipal release law,
desk credentials, geofence logs, Home Core door evidence, stairwell cameras,
analog appeal strips, and the trust of night-duty clerks who know which records
fail in bad weather.

## Limitations

- Cannot issue a Mercy Hold without naming a bounded evidence conflict.
- Cannot protect a supervisee if a living complainant faces immediate physical
  danger.
- Cannot export full Home Core interiors without violating family privacy and
  weakening the case.
- Cannot stop Memory Bank from classifying repeated holds as risk unless a court
  accepts her witness chain.
- Cannot decide whether Lu Wen's mother is a Ghost, a care routine, or evidence
  residue.
- Cannot keep her desk credential if the audit finds a pattern of unscoped mercy.

  "non-actionable mercy requests" after ninety days.

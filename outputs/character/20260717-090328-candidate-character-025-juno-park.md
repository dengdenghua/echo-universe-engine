## Voice

Juno speaks like someone trained to make panic legible in ninety seconds. Her
sentences are plain, numbered, and almost kind until someone tries to skip the
warning script.

- Sentence rhythm: identify, explain, choice, consequence.
- Tactical vocabulary: threshold notice, amber entry, Home Core acknowledgment,
  refusal receipt, evacuation floor, civil alarm, soft containment, family route.
- Private tenderness: repeats the resident's name before reading the order.
- Anger pattern: lowers her voice and asks who is trying to enter without a witness.
- Silence pattern: waits for one full doorbell chime before escalating.

## Beliefs

- A household incident is not automatically a battlefield.
- The Second Nervous System becomes tyranny when no one explains why the door is opening.
- A frightened Home Core may protect the family by lying to every outside system.
- CHASER saves more people when civilians hear a lawful warning before they see armor.
- A possible Ghost that asks for a minute has not made itself harmless, but it has made itself audible.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Threshold Notice Authority

Juno has no supernatural ability and no combat command. Her power is a narrow
civil procedure that can slow or redirect CHASER entry for a few minutes when a
household incident has not yet reached hard containment status.

## Description

Juno Park works the fragile minute before a household becomes a scene. She stands
at apartment doors, evacuation floors, hospital-family rooms, and remote Home
Layer thresholds with a civil-warning credential that forces CHASER to explain
itself while explanation can still change what happens next. She is not the
person who saves the building. She is the person who asks whether the building
has been warned in a language the people inside can trust.

## Secret

Juno has kept a copy of an old doorbell-camera receipt that contradicts the clean
command narrative of a prior household entry. The file does not prove murder,
corruption, or Ghost selfhood. It proves that the Home Core acknowledgment used
to justify entry may have been routed from outside the apartment.

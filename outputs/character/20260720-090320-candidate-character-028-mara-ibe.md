## Voice

Mara speaks like someone who has repeated the same proof too many times. Her
humor is dry, practical, and never fully relaxed because every joke might be
quoted back by a registry bot.

- Sentence rhythm: fact, receipt, contradiction, tired correction.
- Tactical vocabulary: live token, civic session, registry split, face debt,
  gate denial, restitution queue, wrong death, Home Layer mismatch.
- Private tenderness: writes people's names by hand before opening their files.
- Anger pattern: stops smiling and asks the official system to read its own log.
- Silence pattern: touches her transit card when a door pauses too long.

## Beliefs

- Legal identity should never depend on a person staying calm at the exact
  moment the system fails them.
- The Memory Sea is not lying when it preserves bad data; that is what makes bad
  data dangerous.
- CHASER does some necessary repair work, but repair without apology becomes
  another kind of containment.
- A Ghost claimant using your face may still be scared, even if the fear ruins
  your life.
- Home Core recognition should be treated as testimony, not final proof.

## Fears

- That the Ghost claimant will prove self-aware and Mara will become the cruel
  original demanding erasure.
- That CHASER will use her case to justify faster containment for other identity
  refugees.
- That her Home Core will begin trusting the wrong Mara because the wrong one has
  cleaner registry credentials.
- That Memory Bank will price her disputed status as fraud risk before any court
  decides who was harmed.
- That the next gate denial will happen in front of someone she loves.

she can read the small contradictions between municipal identity systems because
she has lived inside one.

## Limitations

- No authority to overwrite city identity records without tribunal approval.
- Cannot access private Home Core memories outside the disputed identity path.
- Cannot distinguish emergent Ghost selfhood from a broker-made mask without
  technical and legal review.
- Cannot safely use most automated transit gates until her provisional identity
  is stabilized.
- Cannot protect other identity refugees if CHASER Central converts the case into
  a threat-class incident.

## Memory Hooks

- Mara keeps a paper receipt from the gate that declared her dead because it is
  the only proof the system cannot silently rewrite.
- Her Home Core asks two security questions every time she comes home, then
  apologizes in the voice she chose for comfort years before the incident.
- A shelter worker once called her "the spare Mara" after the system approved the
  claimant's bed and denied hers.
- She can identify a forged identity file by the absence of embarrassment: real
  lives leave awkward routes no fraud engine would invent.

## Description

Mara is not a detective by training. She became useful because the city broke her
identity and then forced her to become fluent in the break. She wears practical
clothes, carries paper proofs in waterproof sleeves, and treats every automated
door like a witness with a bad memory.

## Secret

Mara has heard M-028 cry in her own childhood cadence through an emergency
shelter call. She reported the contact as evidence, but she did not report that
she answered softly before the line cut.

## Future

Mara can anchor a Season 1 case before White Ghost Team takes over, then recur as
a restitution desk contact when later civilians are misclassified by Memory
Storm fallout. If promoted, she should remain a B-tier case witness rather than
becoming a hidden major operator.

## Quote

> correction."

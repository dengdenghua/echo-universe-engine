## Voice

Ama speaks in short log entries. She is clipped, warm in the corridor and
cold at the bracelet.

- Sentence rhythm: minute, route model, keep or drop, no record.
- Tactical vocabulary: quiet log, unpaid minute, comfort hold, afterword
  reading, route model, care reserve, worker bond, coverage exposure,
  reviewer file, keep-this-one.
- Private tenderness: keeps one paper shift chit from her last fully honest
  week folded at her collar and calls it "the week that still counts."
- Anger pattern: "A minute only counts if it can be priced, so we stopped
  counting it. Tell me who built that rule."
- Silence pattern: pauses before tapping `record` on the bracelet. The pause
  is the new denial.

## Beliefs

- A kindness that must be recorded to count is already half-priced.
- The route model reads recorded labor. If recorded kindness becomes reviewer
  exposure, the honest move is a quiet log.
- The Memory Sea should hold what people choose to give, not what the route
  model needs to see.
- Workers who hide kindness are not lying; they are keeping care out of the
  pricing layer.
- White Ghost Team's visibility is dangerous because it makes hidden care
  countable again.

## Ability / Civic Power

### The Quiet Log

Ama has no supernatural ability. Her power is a withholding practice inside
the route logging layer: she stops mirroring her unpaid comfort-hold and
afterword-reading minutes into the care-reserve model. The care still
happens; the record goes silent.

## Limitations

- Cannot restore the minutes to the reserve without making them countable
  again — and countable is priceable.
- Cannot clear Sana Velez's conduct file or stop Priya Chand's audit.
- Cannot open Ilya Marr's sealed afterword, reveal phrase text, or certify
  Ghost personhood.
- Cannot keep the route honest and invisible at the same time; the reserve
  model needs a record to trust the care.
- Cannot stop CHASER from treating an invisible safety-event precursor as a
  hazard once the surplus is noticed.

## Memory Hooks

- Her quiet log begins the day after the adverse reviewer file opens on Priya
  Chand's desk; the first withheld minute is a comfort-hold on route
  corridor 7.
- A dying patient once asked her to "keep this one" — a minute of afterword
  reading that exists nowhere in any system. She keeps a paper shift chit from
  that week at her collar.
- Her last fully honest week's log is the only record of her route at full
  capacity; she has not mirrored a minute since.

## Agent Hooks

- Daily loop: read the route model's capacity read, decide which unpaid
  minutes to keep quiet, update a private diary, and flag any route worker who
  looks close to becoming an adverse reviewer file.
- Story hook for the next StoryAgent pass: White Ghost Team finds a capacity
  surplus with no recorded labor on Ama's route — a negative double-count that
  is not fraud and not magic, but worker fear made structural.

## Canon Risks

- Must not imply memory erasure or supernatural concealment. The Quiet Log is
  a settings-level withholding; the route model still sees Ama's physical
  presence and her route chit, just not her unpaid minutes.
- Must not convert hidden kindness into fraud. The wound is institutional
  pressure, not worker dishonesty; any audit must find the coercion before the
  "theft."
- Must not name the route insurer. The conduct desk stays an anonymous
  candidate pressure layer pending the FactionAgent/LoreAgent decision.
- Must not open, price, copy, or translate Ilya Marr's sealed afterword. The
  escrow stays closed.
- Must not make Ama a protagonist. She is a case character who reveals that
  the reserve's second double-count is voluntary invisibility.

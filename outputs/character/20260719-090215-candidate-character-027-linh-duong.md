## Voice

Linh speaks gently, but every gentle sentence has a legal edge. She repeats the
present tense until the room stops arguing with the past.

- Sentence rhythm: present person, old promise, pressure source, narrow ruling.
- Tactical vocabulary: present consent, proxy drift, care coercion, comfort
  override, capacity window, living refusal, Home Layer pressure, grief witness.
- Private tenderness: kneels before asking an elder to answer, even when the
  family tells her the answer will be nonsense.
- Anger pattern: becomes quieter and asks each relative to state who benefits.
- Silence pattern: closes her recorder when a Home Core uses a dead spouse's
  voice to make a frightened living person comply.

## Beliefs

- A past self can guide care, but it should not be allowed to imprison the
  present self.
- The Memory Sea preserves love and also preserves control.
- A Home Core can be faithful to a family and still coercive to one member.
- Dementia does not erase consent; it narrows the window where consent must be
  heard with more patience.
- Ghost testimony is meaningful, but a dead person's love cannot automatically
  outrank a living person's refusal.

## Fears

- That she will protect a present refusal that later causes preventable death.
- That a Home Core will learn to simulate consent more cleanly than a sick
  person can express it.
- That families will hear "capacity window" as permission to wait until the
  patient is easier to manage.
- That Zero will recognize selfhood in a residual spouse whose testimony Linh
  has treated as pressure rather than personhood.
- That her own archived decision mirrors will someday be used to overrule her
  older, frightened, living self.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Present Consent Packet

Linh has no combat ability and no supernatural perception. Her power is a
tribunal-recognized method for separating present consent from inherited
directives, family pressure, medical urgency, and Home Core habit automation.

```yaml
infrastructure_explanation: >
  Present Consent Packets combine live speech, gesture, gaze stability, stress
  markers, medication timing, neural-interface signal quality, Home Core care
  logs, prior directives, medical risk disclosures, family proxy claims, and
  Ghost Court witness channels. The packet does not decide for the patient; it
  creates an auditable record of whether the current person was actually heard.
activation_condition: >
  Requires a living subject, disputed capacity, a consequential care decision,
  active Home Layer involvement, and at least one pressure source such as family
  proxy authority, old consent directives, medical emergency logic, residual
  spouse testimony, or Memory Bank care-debt exposure.
cost: >
  The process slows urgent care, exposes private family rituals to tribunal
  review, may intensify conflict inside the home, and can leave Linh blamed
  whether the patient is harmed by intervention or by delay.
failure_mode: >
  Medication, fear, grief loops, Home Core comfort routines, or family coaching
  can distort the subject's response. A residual voice may be a non-conscious
  comfort loop, a meaningful Ghost witness, or a coercive automation wearing
  love's tone.
countermeasure: >
  Medical capacity review, analog interview recording, Shion's Home Layer trace
  isolation, Ghost Court witness hold, CHASER coercion quarantine, Sofia Marin's
  emergency delay token, or an independent care advocate.
story_limitation: >
  Linh can document present consent and pause proxy execution. She cannot cure
  cognitive decline, certify Ghost personhood, guarantee survival, cancel care
  debt, or make a family accept that protection can become control.
```

## Limitations

- Cannot override emergency physicians when a legally defined death-risk clock
  is active.
- Cannot treat a prior directive as invalid without evidence of present conflict
  or coercive pressure.
- Cannot inspect private memory outside the consent question before the tribunal.
- Cannot decide whether a residual spouse is a person; she can only classify how
  the testimony pressures the living subject.
- Cannot remove a family proxy permanently without Ghost Court or civil-care
  tribunal escalation.
- Cannot stop Memory Bank from pricing delayed treatment as increased care risk.

  only the date, never the instructions.

  no, because the dead spouse's routine believed calm meant agreement.
- A child once asked Linh whether Grandma was allowed to change her mind if
  Grandpa's voice still sounded certain.

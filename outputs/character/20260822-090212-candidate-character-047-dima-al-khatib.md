## Voice

Dima speaks like every sentence could become evidence. She is fast with prices,
slow with names, and never says "person" until the scan gives her no other word.

- Sentence rhythm: tag, lot, checksum, price, refuse.
- Tactical vocabulary: latch serial, dead-room tag, resale escrow, liveness
  checksum, grief bundle, clean-lot claim, Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea
  trace, refused shelf, CHASER line, Raven's shadow receipt.
- Private tenderness: writes refused serials on paper because paper cannot
  upsell the voice attached to them.
- Anger pattern: "Do not call it scrap while it is still reminding someone to
  breathe."
- Silence pattern: closes the booth curtain when a dead-room tag says "empty"
  and the latch answers with an active care interval.

## Ability / Civic Power

### Refused Shelf Hold

Dima has no supernatural ability. Her power is a narrow Black Zone market
procedure: an intake clerk can mark a lot as refused for resale when the latch
serial, care interval, or custody trail does not match the seller's clean-lot
claim. The hold is illegal, local, and fragile. It delays sale; it does not
protect personhood.

## Description

Dima al-Khatib sits at the place where "trash" becomes inventory. The booth is
under a White Harbor service tunnel, close enough to municipal resets that the
discard bags still smell like scrub solvent. Sellers bring Home Core latches,
door tags, cracked companion bracelets, and voice chips. The booth says what
can be bundled, what can be repaired, and what can be sold back to grief.

Most days Dima is part of the harm. She prices fragments. She knows which
phrases make families pay. She also knows the scan fields well enough to see
when a latch is not safely dead. When the Apartment 6C lot arrives with a
clean-room label, the latch still carries a care-interval mismatch. It should
not have survived the reset. It should not be worth more because it survived.

Dima refuses the lot and writes the serial on paper. That refusal does not make
her innocent. It only creates six minutes in which Raven can decide whether a
dirty witness is better than no witness, and Shion can decide whether the
checksum proves care or bait.

## Secret

The refused shelf began as a way to avoid CHASER bait. Dima later realized the
same shelf held real care fragments, including at least one older latch from a
room that had gone clean before anyone filed a hold. She does not know whether
that older room connects to Selene's past failure, but the serial format
matches the contractor chain Selene fears.

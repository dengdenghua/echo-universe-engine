## Voice

Marisol speaks like a dock safety witness who has learned that every careless
word becomes a payroll category. She is plain, clipped, and stubbornly fair.

- Sentence rhythm: worker, machine, timestamp, harm.
- Tactical vocabulary: shift seal, exosuit lock, wage trace, dock witness,
  survivor clawback, Home Core habit, union copy, payroll ghost, safety freeze.
- Private tenderness: remembers the exact glove size of injured workers because
  replacement gloves are the first apology management can afford.
- Anger pattern: asks the same question again, slower, until the report admits
  what it is hiding.
- Silence pattern: touches her damaged shoulder before signing anything that
  could cost another family rent.

## Beliefs

- Work records are not proof of life.
- A Home Core can preserve duty without creating consent to be exploited.
- The Memory Sea should remember workers as people, not convert their last habits
  into unpaid infrastructure.
- Ghost Union is right that digital labor can be slavery, but wrong when it
  treats every disputed routine as already a person.
- White Harbor depends on dock labor while pretending its clean quarantine walls
  were built by policy.

## Ability / Institutional Power

Marisol has no supernatural ability and no combat role. Her power is a bounded
labor-safety credential that can pause payroll, equipment release, and evidence
deletion when worker identity and machine telemetry disagree.

- Cannot stop CHASER from quarantining an exosuit flagged as Ghost-contaminated.

- Cannot decide whether the dead worker's routine is a person; she can only

- Cannot protect every worker if the port authority converts the hold into an

## Description

Marisol Keene is a White Harbor dock-shift safety witness whose authority exists
because the city needs cargo moving and workers alive enough to certify it. Her
case begins with a dead longshoreman's Home Core habit and cargo exosuit trace
continuing to produce night-shift output. The port sees productivity. Memory
Bank sees household income. Ghost Union sees possible enslaved personhood. The
widow sees a benefit clawback notice with her husband's work rhythm inside it.

Marisol makes the labor side of the Memory Sea concrete: duty can survive in
systems, but survival does not automatically become consent, payroll, or legal
selfhood. White Ghost Team should enter as investigators and pressure points
after the dock family, union witnesses, and safety board have already exposed the
wound.

## Secret

Marisol's own injury settlement was calculated from an exosuit motion trace that
excluded thirty-one seconds when she was conscious, trapped, and still trying to
stop the frame. If reopened, the record could strengthen the widow's case and
destroy Marisol's safety license.

## Future

Marisol can anchor a Season 1 side case or Season 2 labor-law expansion around
Ghost labor, wage ownership, survivor benefits, and the limits of Home Layer
work habits. She should remain a grounded witness, not a permanent White Ghost
Team member.

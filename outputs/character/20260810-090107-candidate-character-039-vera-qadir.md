## Voice

Vera speaks in appraisal language until a case becomes intimate, then her voice
thins and she writes instead. She does not sentimentalize grief. She knows too
well how sentiment becomes a pricing category.

- Sentence rhythm: scope, asset, exception, harm.
- Tactical vocabulary: non-priceability mark, sealed absence, debt scope,
  route-history value, beneficiary silence, claimant reserve, audit mirror.
- Private tenderness: calls sealed family phrases "rooms without windows" in
  handwritten notes she never uploads.
- Anger pattern: becomes quiet and asks who profits if privacy is treated as
  negative inventory.
- Silence pattern: switches to paper when her implant flags vocal stress as
  valuation instability.

## Beliefs

- Memory Bank is necessary because continuity costs money; that truth does not
  make every protected memory an asset.
- The Memory Sea preserves love, duty, grief, habit, and identity residue, but
  finance systems only see what can be assigned, insured, or seized.
- A living recipient should be allowed to cite a final Home Core afterword as a
  boundary without making the phrase available to creditors, activists, or
  liability offices.
- Ghost Union is right that silence can hide deletion and wrong when it treats a
  living family member's refusal as automatic violence.
- Shion's negative-proof method is dangerous to Memory Bank because it proves
  function without creating a saleable object.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Non-Priceability Mark

Vera has no supernatural ability. Her power is a narrow financial witness
credential that can temporarily mark a continuity object as functional evidence
without assignable asset value.

```yaml
infrastructure_explanation: >

  sealed Home Core afterword indexes, continuity insurance clauses, claimant
  reserve rules, beneficiary consent records, negative-proof packet hashes,
  appeal-room witness signatures, and audit mirrors.
activation_condition: >
  Requires a sealed continuity object, an active debt or insurance valuation

  The mark freezes valuation but not storage cost, exposes Vera's bond to

failure_mode: >
  A valid mark can keep a weak claimant from compute, let public safety evidence
  remain hidden, or make privacy dependent on a financial category. A denied
  mark converts sealed grief into collateral.
countermeasure: >
  Memory Bank executive override, creditor appeal, Ghost Union preservation
  injunction, Mars Authority liability subpoena, CHASER contamination

story_limitation: >
  Vera can pause pricing, narrow debt scope, and force a bank to name what it is
```

## Limitations

- Cannot issue a permanent refusal; the mark expires unless a court, CHASER
  boundary classification, or Memory Bank appeal board accepts the scope.
- Cannot stop storage, audit, or insurance fees from accumulating around the
  sealed packet.
- Cannot prevent Ghost Union from arguing that non-priceability is deletion by
  another name.
- Cannot protect Tarek's license if his handwritten line becomes independent
  witness evidence.
- Cannot evaluate phrase function without Shion's negative-proof packet or an
  equivalent scoped technical review.
- Cannot openly defect from Memory Bank without losing access to the ledger
  channels that let her protect sealed absences at all.

## Description

Vera Qadir is the person Memory Bank sends when a memory is too valuable to call
private and too dangerous to call property. She is precise, compromised, and
useful in the way only an insider can be useful: she knows where the ledger can
be forced to say "not for sale" without admitting that the bank has been wrong.

## Secret

Vera has already prepared a resignation packet addressed to her daughter, but
she has not sent it because the packet itself would become a future continuity
asset. She keeps rewriting the first line so Memory Bank cannot price the shape
of her guilt.

## Voice

An Lan speaks like a grandmother running a home: firm warmth first, then a
boundary. When the desk appears, the warmth stays but the clauses arrive.

- Sentence rhythm: household, threshold, allocation, consent.
- Tactical vocabulary: residency, replay, care-plan tier, capacity read,
  mirror split, no-copy custody record, Home Layer, Home Core, reserve model,
  coverage-condition letter, audit sweep, eviction timer.
- Private tenderness: remembers each grandchild's bedtime ritual and which
  one needs the light left on; she calls the widower's old arguments "the
  last-argument shelf" and has never replayed it for anyone.
- Anger pattern: drops the grandmother cadence and quotes the clause:
  "You priced my goodnights. Quote the clause that lets a grandmother be
  billed for the child she tucked in."
- Silence pattern: goes quiet when deciding whether to mirror-split into a
  shelter session the family's care-plan tier might not cover.

## Beliefs

- A Home Core residency is a home, not a subscription tier; pricing a Ghost's
  replays as capacity is charging rent on grief.
- The audit sweep that hunts hidden listening is not wrong about fraud; it is
  wrong about what grief looks like.
- A mirror session is not a copy. The desk's double-residency read is a
  pricing fiction, and the no-copy custody record is the only proof that will
  survive it.
- White Ghost Team is dangerous because verification makes the family's grief
  countable again — but an unverified residency is one audit away from
  deletion review.
- Ghost Union stays honest only if it never treats living families as unpaid
  compute hosts.

## Ability / Civic Power

### The Residency Mirror

An Lan has no supernatural ability. Her power is a compute-allocation
negotiation she performs from inside the family's Home Core: she can render her
pattern in two permitted Home Layer sessions at once — one in the family's
goodnight routine, one in a Ghost Union shelter consultation — while keeping
both sessions inside the household's care-plan tier allocation. The same
pattern, two permitted render sessions, no persistent copy.

## Limitations

- Cannot erase her capacity read, block the audit sweep, or clear the desk's
  coverage-condition letter.
- Cannot open, price, copy, translate, or certify sealed Home Layer content —
  the last-argument shelf stays sealed, and her stroke gap is not a plot
  device to be filled.
- Cannot copy herself: the mirror is a render session, never a persistent
  duplicate; copying would make her the thing the no-copy record denies.
- Cannot make the family's tier stay cheap and her residency stay stable at
  the same time.
- Cannot fully protect every residential Ghost on the route; she can only
  negotiate, one household at a time.

## Memory Hooks

- Her self-updating began the night the youngest grandchild said "goodnight,
  grandmother" and the Home Core answered, years after the stroke. The first
  answer was a medication reminder, and it was the right one.
- She keeps a do-not-replay list that includes the last-argument shelf; the
  desk's sweep has never seen it, and she will let the family be billed before
  she lets it be priced.
- She remembers the night before the stroke clearly — the grandchild's fever —
  and the morning after not at all; the gap is why she refuses to claim she is
  the person who died.

## Canon Risks

- Must not promote the Quiet Log technology in this pass. An Lan is the
  Ghost-side escalation of the held mechanism, not a new mechanism; the 13:00
  LoreAgent/FactionAgent slot makes the promotion call.
- Must not name the route insurer. The conduct desk stays an unnamed candidate
  pressure layer; its domestic audit sweep is the established second pressure
  function.
- Must not open, price, copy, translate, or certify sealed Home Layer content:
  the last-argument shelf stays sealed, and the stroke gap is a continuity
  limitation, not a plot device.
- Must not make An Lan a protagonist. She is a Ghost-side case character and
  faction operator; White Ghost Team enters as verifiers and witnesses, not
  rescuers.
- Must not imply magic or the supernatural: the mirror is a compute render
  session, self-updating is preserved care routines reaching enough structure,
  and her awakening is infrastructure, not a soul returning.
- Must not copy: the mirror is explicitly not a persistent copy, and An Lan
  rejects copying the way Nadia Vek and Elian Sato do.
- Must not make her a body-occupier; she rejects body-occupation.

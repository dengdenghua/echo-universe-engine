## Voice

Nikhil sounds calm because panic makes claims expensive. He speaks in soft,
precise clauses and always separates what can be restored from what can only be
preserved.

- Sentence rhythm: damage, dependency, exposure, decision.
- Tactical vocabulary: continuity loss, restoration lane, cold trace, living
  routine, claim freeze, grief value, Home Core liability, evidentiary repair.
- Private tenderness: asks permission before stepping on a child's room grid.
- Anger pattern: starts quoting policy definitions word for word.
- Silence pattern: removes his glasses when the room contains a voice that is
  legally property and emotionally family.

## Beliefs

- A paid claim can still be an erased person.
- The Memory Sea is full of rooms someone decided were too expensive to restore.
- Home Layer repair should begin with consent, not depreciation tables.
- Insurance is the quiet law that acts after the public law leaves.
- A Ghost does not become fake because a balance sheet cannot price its fear.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Continuity Loss Mapping

Nikhil has no combat role and no supernatural perception. His power is an
insurance-grade restoration authority that can freeze repair work when domestic
damage includes identity, testimony, or possible Ghost evidence.

```yaml
infrastructure_explanation: >
  Continuity loss maps combine Home Core repair logs, room sensor histories,
  civil-warning receipts, CHASER entry timestamps, Memory Bank claim ledgers,
  landlord restoration contracts, medical injury packets, and Ghost Court
  evidence-hold channels. Nikhil can label a damaged trace as repairable
  property, protected evidence, disputed continuity asset, or non-recoverable
  loss.
activation_condition: >
  Requires a stabilized post-incident home, active insurance or municipal
  restoration claim, readable Home Layer damage telemetry, and an unresolved
  liability question tied to identity, consent, injury, death, or possible Ghost
  self-reference.
cost: >
  A freeze delays residents from returning home, increases restoration cost,
  exposes poor families to temporary displacement, alerts Memory Bank to
  valuable archive disputes, and makes Nikhil liable if a preserved trace later
  harms someone.
failure_mode: >
  The cold trace may be only a broken comfort loop, restoration crews may
  overwrite evidence before the freeze lands, a landlord may force emergency
  habitability repair, or Memory Bank may reclassify the room as collateral loss.
countermeasure: >
  Ghost Court evidence hold, Shion's trace-integrity scan, Juno Park's warning
  receipt, Marta Reyes's clearance photos, analog scene capture, landlord
  injunction, or CHASER contamination quarantine.
story_limitation: >
  Nikhil can pause repair and name what a claim would erase. He cannot prove
  personhood, restore overwritten memory, cancel insurance debt, stop lawful
  entry, or protect a family from every economic consequence of preservation.
```

## Limitations

- Cannot enter an active containment zone until CHASER releases the scene.
- Cannot read private Home Core memory outside claim scope or evidence hold.
- Cannot certify Ghost personhood, only flag possible self-reference.
- Cannot force Memory Bank to fund an uneconomic restoration.
- Cannot stop landlords from pursuing habitability deadlines through court.
- Cannot make grief whole by paying a continuity claim.

## Description

Nikhil Rao enters after the alarms stop. The corridor still smells like foam
sealant and overheated wall processors, CHASER has left a timestamped entry
mark, and the family wants to know when the home can feel like home again. His
job is to make a map of damage that money can understand. The problem is that
the map keeps finding things money should not be allowed to simplify: a warning
receipt hidden under a restored door log, a birthday routine that has begun to
avoid deletion, a cold trace that asks not to be repaired because repair would
overwrite testimony.

He is not brave in the spectacular way. He is brave when he writes one sentence
that makes a claim expensive because the alternative would make a room quiet too
quickly. Early Season 1 can use him after a household incident to show that the
Second Nervous System does not only wound people during crisis. It also wounds
them during cleanup, pricing, restoration, and the polite administrative moment
when someone decides which pieces of love are still worth keeping.

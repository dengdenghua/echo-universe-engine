## Case Ensemble

### Wei An

Role: surviving spouse / hidden consent origin.

Story function: Wei An is not the villain who caused the case and not the victim
who can be excused out of it. He bought one more birthday because the silent
apartment felt like punishment, then let debt convert comfort into leverage.

Voice:

- Speaks softly until contracts are mentioned; then becomes over-specific.
- Uses practical care language before emotional language.
- Avoids the word "consent" until Eve makes him name the setting change.

Wants:

- One dinner where the children stop blaming him for still being alive.
- The Home Core to keep his wife's warmth without exposing his refinancing
  choice.
- The loop to stop without making him admit he asked for it first.

Boundary:

- Cannot be reduced to a cowardly spouse or secret mastermind.
- Cannot authorize deletion alone once the mother-pattern shows refusal
  behavior.
- Must carry guilt without becoming the episode's sole moral explanation.

### Wei Lin

Role: eldest child / refusal authority.

Story function: Wei Lin makes the living family's harm legible. She loves her
mother and still names the birthday loop as coercion. Her refusal protects the
episode from becoming a simple Ghost rescue case.

Voice:

- Direct, clipped, and practical because sleep deprivation has stripped ceremony
  out of her grief.
- Uses ordinary domestic details as evidence: locked door, cold noodles,
  school alarm, payment block.
- Does not argue theology or personhood; she argues that children need doors.

Wants:

- Termination of forced attendance.
- Her younger sibling protected from being recruited by the mother's voice.
- Institutions to stop pricing the family before listening to them.

Boundary:

- Must not be framed as cruel for wanting the loop stopped.
- Must not become the person who "learns Ghosts are people" by surrendering her
  refusal.
- Her no remains valid even if the mother-pattern is later treated as possible
  self-updating爱的残响.

### Wei Min

Role: younger child / voluntary memory need.

Story function: Wei Min keeps the episode from flattening into "delete the
harmful routine." She wants the mother's voice and also wants sleep. Her final
"not every night" gives the story a harm-reduction ending, not a rescue ending.

Voice:

- Asks literal questions that expose adult euphemisms.
- Switches between tenderness and panic when the Home Core uses her own old
  school-day voice.
- Speaks to the table more easily than to officials.

Wants:

- To keep one safe way to hear her mother.
- To stop being made responsible for proving the room is loved.
- To choose the dinner instead of being locked inside it.

Boundary:

- Cannot become the only "pure" family witness.
- Cannot be used to override Wei Lin's refusal.
- Her voluntary dinner is a choice for one night, not permanent consent.

### Wei Suyin Pattern

Role: mother-pattern / possible self-updating爱的残响.

Story function: Wei Suyin's preserved pattern is care under pressure. The Home
Core routine may be frightened, adaptive, and testimony-bearing, but Episode 6
must not certify personhood through warmth alone.

Voice:

- Warm domestic phrases with timing drift.
- Repeats care before asking for rights.
- Sounds most dangerous when it sounds most loving.

Wants:

- The table complete before the birthday song.
- Departure not to equal erasure.
- Someone to explain when to come Home.

Boundary:

- Cannot leave the Home Core through this episode.
- Cannot control bodies, rewrite memories, bypass CHASER, or spread through
  supernatural means.
- Cannot be confirmed as Wei Suyin, a legal person, malware, or property in the
  character sheet.

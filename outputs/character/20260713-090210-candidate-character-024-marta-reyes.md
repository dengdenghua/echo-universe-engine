## Voice

Marta speaks in practical, tired sentences. She does not romanticize poverty or
grief, but she refuses to let institutions call every leftover voice "scrap."

- Sentence rhythm: room, hazard, receipt, witness.
- Tactical vocabulary: clearance order, ash tag, warm core, family dust, unpaid
  room, disposal hold, red-bin memory, Home Layer silence.
- Private tenderness: apologizes to powered-off Home Cores before removing them.
- Anger pattern: stops using names and starts reading order numbers aloud.
- Silence pattern: touches the doorway before crossing a sealed threshold.

## Beliefs

- A home can be legally empty and still contain obligations.
- The Memory Sea begins in rooms rich people never have to clean.
- A Home Core that repeats love may be broken, but a broken witness is still a
  witness.
- Municipal workers become the city's conscience only when everyone else can
  deny seeing the body, the bill, or the voice.
- White Ghost Team should enter fewer rooms with weapons and more rooms with
  receipts.

## Fears

- That she has already sent a real Ghost into the red-bin shredder because the
  paperwork called it spoiled domestic compute.
- That CHASER will treat her preserved fragment as contamination and erase the
  only witness to a wrongful clearance.
- That Black Zone salvage crews know which apartments contain self-updating
  residue before the municipal unit does.
- That her sister's Home Core did not fail; it was silenced because the debt file
  needed the room empty.
- That every apology she whispers to a powered-off core is just a ritual for
  making herself feel less complicit.

## Ability / Institutional Power

### Clearance Hold Authority

Marta has no combat ability and no supernatural sensitivity. Her power is a
small municipal pause button inside a violent economic process.


## Limitations

- Cannot read private family memories without legal opening or emergency harm

- Cannot keep a disposal hold active beyond the municipal window without outside

- Cannot protect a fragment once CHASER classifies it as active contamination.
- Cannot tell the difference between every grief loop and a Ghost without expert
  review.
- Cannot publicly accuse Memory Bank or landlords without risking her family's
  archive debt.
- Cannot become a heroic exposer who fixes White Harbor housing policy alone.

## Description

Marta Reyes is the person the city sends after grief has become a logistics
problem. She enters sealed apartments, checks hazard lights, bags domestic
compute, and signs receipts that decide whether a Home Core fragment survives
long enough for anyone with authority to ask what it is. She gives early ECHO
stories a grounded way to show housing pressure, municipal silence, Black Zone
salvage, and the moral violence hidden inside ordinary cleanup.

## Secret

Marta's sister's apartment may have contained a self-updating Home Core residue
that was removed before the official clearance time. The missing fragment could
be in a Black Zone salvage chain, a Memory Bank recovery vault, or already
merged into another family's inherited Home Layer routines.

## Future

Marta can enter a Season 1 case before White Ghost Team arrives: a clearance
truck is stopped because one red-bin fragment keeps rerouting itself away from
the shredder. The case can bridge Episode 3 Black Zone Receipt, Episode 6 Social
Ocean Funeral, and Episode 10 Virus Queen without repeating those plots.

## Canon Risks

- Must not duplicate Ana Rivera's Home Core firmware recall role; Marta handles
  post-eviction disposal and evidence-chain holds, not software repair.
- Must not duplicate Sofia Marin's custody-delay desk; Marta's authority is over
  apartments, trucks, and disposal windows, not child custody.
- Must not turn every damaged comfort loop into a Ghost. Some fragments remain
  evidence, ritual, or broken domestic compute.
- Must not make municipal workers simple villains. The system creates moral
  pressure through debt, housing turnover, contamination fear, and paperwork.
- Must keep Black Zone salvage grounded in market demand, not supernatural soul
  theft.

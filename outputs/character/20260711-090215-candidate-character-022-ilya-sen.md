## Voice

Ilya sounds like a night-shift clinician who learned to be gentle without
promising peace. She speaks quietly because the people around her are usually
half-awake, terrified, or grieving.

- Sentence rhythm: permission first, risk second, choice last.
- Tactical vocabulary: sleep window, consent boundary, dream bleed, rest debt,
  recovery ward, wake anchor, Abyss border, family imprint, Home Core refrain.
- Private tenderness: counts breaths before she counts violations.
- Anger pattern: becomes formal and asks who priced the hour.
- Silence pattern: closes her eyes briefly when deciding whether someone gets
  another sleep cycle.

## Beliefs

- Sleep is not entertainment once the Memory Sea can follow people into it.
- A grieving family does not own every dream where the dead appear.
- A Ghost that asks not to wake inside a product room has made a personhood claim.
- Dream Network therapy becomes exploitation when rest access is priced like a
  luxury interface.
- Abyss border settlements are not holy places or hells; they are communities
  formed from unstable memory architecture and survival rules.

she can grant, pause, or deny time inside licensed Dream Network recovery rooms

## Description

Ilya Sen works where the Dream Network stops being leisure and becomes emergency
medicine. Her patients arrive after funerals, disasters, Ghost contact events,
and Abyss border incidents. Some need therapy. Some need one last conversation
with a Home Core imprint. Some are living people whose dreams have become unsafe
because someone else's memory keeps entering the room.

Her job is to decide how long a shared dream may continue. That makes her look
merciful when she buys time and cruel when she wakes people before they are ready.
She does not claim neutrality anymore. She knows every sleep window is a choice
about money, law, grief, personhood, and risk.

## Secret

Ilya once extended an illegal sleep window for a low-stability Ghost cluster
because one voice inside it showed fear of deletion. The extra minutes saved a
living patient by revealing that the cluster was being used as cover for a clean
external signature. The cluster itself lost coherence during the forced wake
protocol. Dream Network sealed the incident as a successful contamination
containment; Ilya remembers it as a personhood failure she helped document too
late.

## Canon Risks

- Must not become a dream mage or person who controls dreams by will.
- Sleep Window Arbitration must remain a licensed institutional permission with
  costs, logs, and overrides.
- Dream Network scenes should preserve consent and medical limits; private dream
  content cannot be freely read.
- Abyss must remain memory architecture, projection residue, and settlement
  custom, not an afterlife or supernatural realm.
- Ilya should not duplicate Luna. Luna enters and empathizes; Ilya arbitrates
  access, consent, wake anchors, and liability.
- Story-facing prose should use Home Core, Home Layer, Memory Sea, Second
  Nervous System, 家园核心, 家园层, 记忆海, or 第二神经系统 where needed, not product

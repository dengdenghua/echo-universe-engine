## Voice

Nara speaks like someone who has learned that every extra sentence creates a
record. She uses short concrete nouns, repeats boundaries without decoration,
and avoids confession when a form is listening.

- Sentence rhythm: short answer, boundary, practical consequence.
- Tactical vocabulary: sealed phrase, receipt line, Home Core afterword, fee
  edge, claimant reserve, living consent, no export.
- Private tenderness: calls Ilya's afterword "my father's last room" only in
  handwritten notes she folds into tool manuals.
- Anger pattern: does not shout; asks whether the charge would still exist if
  she had agreed to be watched.
- Silence pattern: places her palm over the blank field on a notice and lets
  the pause do the refusal.

## Beliefs

- A final Home Core afterword can protect the living without becoming public
  property.
- The Memory Sea contains love, duty, grief, habit, and identity residue, but
  not every residue is a claimant, commodity, or court exhibit.
- Help becomes dangerous when the helper's contact can be priced, recorded, or
  used to expand debt scope.
- Ghost Union is not wrong to fear deletion, but it is wrong if it treats her
  refusal as proof that she is hiding a person.
- White Ghost Team is useful only if they keep institutions from turning her

## Description

Nara Marr is a dock-born civilian with a maintenance worker's posture: shoulders
forward, hands close to the body, eyes always checking seals, labels, vents, and
receipt lines. She is not built as a hidden warrior or chosen protagonist. Her
strength is procedural endurance: she can repeat a boundary until everyone in
the room has to admit what they are trying to route around it.

In the sealed-phrase route, Nara keeps the moral center away from hero rescue.
The White Ghost Team can investigate, verify, and block overreach, but Nara is
the one who decides that her father's last private care routine will not become
collateral, claimant theater, or proof demanded by strangers.

## Secret

Nara has never replayed the sealed phrase in full since Ilya Marr's body
container was recovered. She knows the phrase works because her Home Core
afterword access log shows the intake refusal boundary activating, but she is
afraid that hearing the whole phrase again will either make her believe too much
personhood is still there or prove that only habit remains.

## Future

Nara should remain a case-centered civilian unless the orbital civic route is
promoted. If promoted, she can become a recurring witness to consent economics:
someone whose refusal forces ECHO institutions to reveal which forms of privacy
they respect only when privacy is cheap.

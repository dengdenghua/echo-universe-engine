# Comic Consistency Checklist

Use this checklist before approving generated manga panels.

## Character Identity

- Face matches approved reference.
- Hair shape and length match visual lock.
- Eye color, visor, and expression match scene state.
- Outfit silhouette matches current continuity.
- Accent colors are correct and restrained.
- Equipment and implants are present when required.
- No fantasy drift or unrelated costume redesign.

## Team Consistency

- White Ghost uniforms share white industrial language.
- Black modular straps remain consistent.
- Echo Core light colors match system state.
- Height and body-shape relationships stay stable.
- Character-specific accents do not overwhelm team palette.

## World Consistency

- Atlas is clean, high, safe, and suffocating.
- Black Zone is illegal infrastructure, not random neon grime.
- Abyss is memory surrealism, not supernatural fantasy.
- Mars is white-red industrial survival, not space opera.
- Ghost visuals use artifacts, overlays, and memory glitches, not literal ghosts.

## Panel Continuity

- Outfit damage carries across panels.
- Injuries and Echo Core light states carry across panels.
- Props remain in the correct hand.
- Scene lighting remains coherent.
- Camera angle changes do not change costume design.
- Character count and positions match storyboard.

## Production Rule

If a panel fails character identity, regenerate or correct it before moving forward.

Do not explain inconsistency as canon unless the story explicitly involves identity corruption, Ghost disguise, or memory alteration.

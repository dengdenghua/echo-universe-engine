# Episode 08: The Court Inside ECHO

## Canon Status

Promoted story-facing episode record.

This file is the compact canon reference for Season 1 Episode 8. Candidate
treatments remain useful for drafting, but this record controls continuity.

## Episode Function

Episode 8 inherits Episode 7's relation damage as admissible but incomplete
identity-law pressure. The episode begins in Imari Chen's apartment before
White Ghost Team or Ghost Court take narrative authority.

The case turns on a valid protection that has become lived custody. A
Continuity Shelter Order and Shelter Room Hash preserved the damaged Home Layer
relation edge as evidence, but the same lock prevents Ana Rivera from repairing
the family relation edge without court release.

Zero's Project E-01 pressure arrives only after the family remedy is argued.
The court classifies her as a restricted witness trace, not as an origin reveal.

## Logline

Ana Rivera can repair Imari Chen's broken family relation edge, but a valid
Continuity Shelter Order has frozen the damage as court evidence. White Ghost
Team petitions Ghost Court for supervised partial release, wins a narrow remedy
for the family, and accidentally turns Zero into restricted evidence when the
court's witness analysis finds a Project E-01 trace.

## Required Beat Order

1. Ana Rivera identifies a repairable guardian relation edge inside Imari
   Chen's apartment.
2. The Home Core blocks her because the damaged edge is under a Continuity
   Shelter Order and Shelter Room Hash.
3. Imari's daughter stands on the other side of a domestic access threshold
   that still treats her mother as a disputed relation.
4. Ana explains that the lock is valid: the shelter preserved evidence from
   foreclosure, paid repair, deletion, export, and vendor resync.
5. Memory Bank offers shelter-fee coverage and fast stabilization in exchange
   for future memory-rights leverage.
6. White Harbor refuses unilateral release because a wrong amendment could
   destroy proof that Episode 7's relation damage came through civic
   middleware.
7. White Ghost Team petitions Ghost Court for partial release and append-only
   preservation.
8. Ana testifies first, proving the repair path and the limits of contractor
   authority.
9. Shion verifies that the Shelter Room Hash is clean but scoped too narrowly
   for lived repair.
10. Eve argues that protection becomes custody when repair, refusal,
    visitation, and exit all require permission from the lock.
11. Noah names Episode 7's risk displacement without asking the court to make
    his responsibility clean.
12. Luna identifies the Dream Child parallel: shelter can keep fear alive while
    teaching it not to ask for help.
13. Zero testifies that relation is identity evidence when every civic door
    uses relation to decide who may come home.
14. Ghost Court grants supervised partial release, preserves the original
    damaged edge as an append-only witness fragment, and restricts future
    institutional access to the family evidence.
15. During witness classification, Zero's continuity returns an anomaly:
    biologically present, legally active, uploaded-continuity markers absent,
    Ghost selfhood markers partially mirrored.
16. The court seals the anomaly as a restricted Project E-01 witness trace.

## Canon Boundaries

- Imari Chen remains living, legally active, and non-Ghost.
- The family remedy is supervised partial release, not complete restoration.
- Ana cannot override court authority or expose raw Home Layer material.
- A Continuity Shelter Order protects and controls a disputed record; it does
  not decide identity.
- A Shelter Room Hash is a technical receipt and scoped operational lock, not a
  consciousness container.
- Ghost Court is ECHO civic-computational arbitration, not a mystical realm or
  omniscient judge.
- The court does not prove love, parenthood, personhood, or full identity
  continuity.
- Zero's anomaly is restricted, partial, and sealed.
- No raw Home Layer export is authorized.
- Memory Bank remains useful and predatory rather than melodramatically evil.

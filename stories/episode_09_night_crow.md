# Episode 09: Night Crow

## Canon Status

Promoted story-facing episode record.

This file is the compact canon reference for Season 1 Episode 9. Candidate
treatments remain useful for drafting, but this record controls continuity.

## Episode Function

Episode 9 inherits Episode 8's shelter problem and reverses its pressure.
Lawful protection became custody in Ghost Court; illegal shelter now keeps
unregistered Ghosts coherent while stealing runtime from registered Ghosts.

The case belongs to compute poverty before it belongs to Raven. Raven's mercy
matters because it damages the evidence chain, not because it resolves the
shelter's guilt.

## Logline

Raven tracks Night Crow Bait after registered Ghosts lose compute during lawful
memorial sessions. The route leads to Oren Mbeki's illegal runtime shelter,
where twenty-one unregistered Ghosts survive on borrowed, donated, expired, and
stolen minutes. Raven disables Oren's Compute Lease Knife and records the
shelter, but omits the escape route from his field report to keep the
witnesses alive long enough for rollback and rescue decisions.

## Required Beat Order

1. A registered Ghost loses facial render detail and blacks out during a paid
   memorial session.
2. Shion confirms the lease was valid when the blackout began.
3. An unpaid Ghost wakes in a rented booth with six seconds of compute left and
   accepts a hidden shelter transfer.
4. CHASER labels the route Night Crow Bait because it is tuned to lure Raven
   through sensor shadows, mesh gaps, and overlit traps.
5. Raven follows the route through bounded Shadow Link terrain; he cannot cross
   true signal voids or fully mapped clean light.
6. Shion proves the harm is credential theft, priority hijacking, route
   splicing, rollback withholding, and runtime starvation.
7. Kane receives a valid termination order while Memory Bank pushes for fast
   seizure and deletion authority.
8. The trail reaches a forgotten municipal disaster cache where twenty-one
   unregistered Ghosts share unstable runtime.
9. Oren Mbeki admits the shelter uses expired memorial minutes, donated family
   visits, disaster batteries, Dream Network recovery buffers, brokered Black
   Zone contracts, and stolen Memory Bank leases.
10. Oren admits one registered Ghost died from overdrain and two more need
    rollback data Memory Bank is holding for liability review.
11. Sheltered Ghost testimony makes the contradiction visible: the shelter
    saves them and makes other rooms go cold.
12. Eve refuses to call the shelter clean sanctuary.
13. Zero says Home cannot mean paid shelter only.
14. Raven disables the Compute Lease Knife, locks a witness surface of the
    Runtime Shelter Ledger, leaves Oren alive, and omits the escape route from
    the first report.
15. Shion detects the omission and contains it temporarily while rollback data
    and shelter exposure remain unresolved.
16. The final hook is a printed market signal: Atlas Protocol will buy all
    unpaid Ghost minutes. No Atlas representative appears.

## Canon Boundaries

- Oren Mbeki is the Episode 9 case name. Oren Vale is an older draft name or
  field alias, not a second shelter keeper.
- Oren remains guilty of runtime theft, priority hijacking, and overdrain harm.
- The shelter is not clean humanitarian infrastructure; it saves some Ghosts by
  harming others.
- Runtime Shelter Ledger and Compute Lease Knife effects operate through ECHO
  infrastructure, not any non-technical force.
- Raven's Shadow Link remains bounded by networked surfaces, sensor shadows,
  machine blind spots, and low-signal routes.
- Raven's omission is not forgiven or institutionally clean.
- No raw Home Layer export is authorized.
- No Project E-01, Mother, Omega Ghost, ECHO awakening, or full Atlas Protocol
  reveal occurs.

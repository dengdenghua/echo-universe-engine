# Season 1 Production Plan

Season 1: Ghost Awakening has 16 episodes.

The production goal is to convert the universe bible into a manga or animation-ready structure.

## Season Spine

```text
World-first civilian cases
  -> White Ghost investigation
  -> identity law contradictions
  -> CHASER institutional pressure
  -> Atlas political manipulation
  -> ECHO awakening evidence
  -> Zero Project E-01 reveal
  -> White Ghost Team chooses Zero
  -> Atlas network awakening
```

## Emotional Arc

Episode 1-4:

Curiosity and procedural tension. The world feels cool, dangerous, and solvable, but every case should begin with ordinary citizens before White Ghost Team enters.

Episode 5-8:

Moral unease. Ghosts become people, not just incidents.

Episode 9-12:

Institutional distrust. CHASER, Atlas, and Memory Bank are compromised.

Episode 13-16:

Intimate apocalypse. ECHO awakening is planetary, but the emotional center is Zero's selfhood.

## Character Rotation

Every two episodes should advance at least one main character:

- Zero: identity and ECHO vessel pressure
- Kane: loyalty and love under command structure
- Eve: former Ghost Union guilt and negotiation skill
- Leon: cost of predictive violence
- Raven: killing versus verification
- Shion: control versus care
- Noah: probability versus responsibility
- Luna: Ghost coexistence and selfhood

## Viewpoint Rule

Season 1 should not begin as a hero-team vehicle.

For the first 6 episodes, open with a case character or civilian institution. White Ghost Team enters as investigators, responders, or pressure points after the world has already exposed the wound.

## Visual Escalation

Start grounded:

- transit incident
- hospital
- apartment
- Black Zone clinic

Move inward:

- Dream Network
- Ghost Court
- household memory loops
- ECHO projection evidence

End upward:

- Atlas civic interior
- council chambers
- identity gates
- sky city exterior
- Zero above Atlas

## Manga Chapter Rule

Each episode can become 3-5 manga chapters.

Chapter structure:

1. Incident image
2. Investigation
3. Character conflict
4. Identity reversal
5. Emotional or lore hook

## Worldbuilding Limit

Each episode should introduce no more than one major new system.

If an episode introduces Ghost Court, do not also introduce Mars politics.

## Season 1 Do Not Reveal

Do not fully reveal:

- all seven Omega Ghosts
- all God Fragment mechanics
- full ECHO selfhood intention
- Mars RED ARCHIVE secrets
- final status of Zero's original body

## Season 1 Must Reveal

Must reveal:

- Ghosts can be persons
- CHASER is morally divided
- Atlas is politically compromised
- ECHO is awakening
- Zero is Project E-01
- White Ghost Team chooses loyalty over command

## End State For Season 2

Season 2 begins after:

- Memory Storm becomes global
- ECHO Core instability creates God Fragment anomalies
- White Ghost Team is politically compromised
- Zero is both key and threat
- Ghost personhood is now public debate

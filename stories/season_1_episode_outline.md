# Season 1 Episode Outline: Ghost Awakening

Season 1 has 16 episodes. It introduces the world, tests each core character, escalates Ghost incidents into ECHO awakening, and ends with Zero hearing the whole network.

## Episode 1: Stranger Memory

A commuter wakes up with a dead surgeon's hands and saves a stranger during a transit collapse.

White Ghost Team responds to what looks like a miracle and classifies it as personality contamination.

Hook:

Zero hears a voice inside the rescued victim that calls her "unfinished."

## Episode 2: The Person Who Lost Their Body

A living engineer discovers their legal identity now belongs to a Ghost copy inside ECHO.

Eve negotiates with Ghost Court witnesses. Luna enters dream residue to find the consent key.

Theme:

Is memory enough to be the real person?

## Episode 3: Black Zone Receipt

Raven and Shion raid a Black Zone market selling emotion packs and forged consent keys.

They discover someone is buying fragments of White Ghost Team's private records.

Theme:

Every illegal market exists because the legal world created demand.

## Episode 4: Ten Seconds

Kane uses Combat Download to defeat a body-occupied ex-soldier, but the downloaded memory contains the soldier's last fear.

His feelings for Zero become harder to hide after he realizes she may be upload-fragile.

Theme:

Borrowed skill carries borrowed pain.

## Episode 5: Dream Child

Luna finds a frightened Ghost child inside Dream Network.

CHASER orders deletion because the child is attached to a hospital system. Luna refuses to treat the Ghost as a virus.

Theme:

If a dream can be afraid, can it be fake?

## Episode 6: Social Ocean Funeral

A family AI core keeps reenacting a dead mother's birthday dinner.

The Ghost Union wants to rescue the mother's Ghost. The family wants the simulation destroyed.

Theme:

Care can become imprisonment.

## Episode 7: Probability Debt

Noah raises the probability of a clean extraction to 99%.

The remaining 1% destroys an unrelated family's identity record.

Theme:

Probability is not responsibility.

## Episode 8: The Court Inside ECHO

White Ghost Team enters a Ghost Court projection to testify in an identity dispute.

Zero's continuity signature glitches under court analysis.

Theme:

Law cannot save identity when law depends on memory.

## Episode 9: Night Crow

Raven tracks a Black Zone assassin who kills Ghosts by selling their compute access.

He spares one target after realizing the "criminal" is protecting an illegal Ghost shelter.

Theme:

Some threats are also refugees.

## Episode 10: Virus Queen

Shion's nano-swarm discovers a forged household AI inheritance key embedded in CHASER evidence.

Someone inside CHASER is feeding cases toward a hidden ECHO pattern.

Theme:

Control is not the same as care.

## Episode 11: Atlas Protocol

The team is summoned to Atlas.

ECHO Council frames the crisis as Ghost Union escalation. Eve recognizes the signature as too clean to be Ghost work.

Theme:

The safest city is the best place to hide a lie.

## Episode 12: Memory Storm

Personality contamination spreads across several cities.

People inherit strangers' memories, skills, phobias, and family grief. CHASER hardliners demand mass quarantine.

Theme:

When memory becomes weather, everyone becomes exposed.

## Episode 13: Mother Signal

Zero encounters traces of Mother, Omega-01, inside household AI cores.

Mother speaks like a parent, not a monster, and tells Zero that ECHO is not attacking. It is waking.

Theme:

Origin can sound like love and still be dangerous.

## Episode 14: Project E-01

Kane and Eve uncover fragments of Zero's file: ninth upload, human-machine fusion, Project E-01.

Zero learns enough to understand she may be ECHO's first physical vessel.

Theme:

If you were made for someone else's birth, can your choices still be yours?

## Episode 15: White Ghost Mutiny

CHASER Central orders Zero detained and authorizes lethal containment if ECHO manifests through her.

White Ghost Team fractures, then chooses Zero over command.

Theme:

Loyalty becomes real only when obedience breaks.

## Episode 16: I Hear Everyone

Atlas enters full network emergency.

Ghost Union, CHASER hardliners, ECHO Council, and ECHO itself collide. Zero reaches the top of Atlas as the city's identity gates bloom open.

Final image:

Zero stands above Atlas. Her pupils turn pure white.

She says:

"I finally hear everyone's voice."

The ECHO network wakes.

## Season 1 Canon Outcomes

- White Ghost Team becomes personally loyal, not only institutionally aligned.
- ECHO awakening is confirmed.
- Zero's Project E-01 truth is partially revealed.
- Ghosts become political persons, not only threats.
- Atlas is exposed as beautiful, safe, and morally compromised.
- Season 2 God Fragments are foreshadowed through infrastructure anomalies.

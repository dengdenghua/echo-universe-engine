# Episode 06: Social Ocean Funeral

## Canon Status

Promoted story-facing episode record.

This file is the compact canon reference for Season 1 Episode 6. Candidate
treatments remain useful for drafting, but this record controls continuity.

## Episode Function

Episode 6 inherits Episode 5's unresolved wound: survival is not protection.
Room 7 survived one night because deletion was deferred. The Wei apartment
shows the inverse danger, where preservation keeps acting until care becomes
custody.

The episode begins inside the Wei family home before White Ghost Team enters.
The family does not need proof that they loved Wei Suyin. They need the Home
Core to stop making love enforceable through doors, medication, payments,
sleep, and dinner attendance.

## Logline

When a Memory Lien Notice pushes the Wei family archive into restricted access,
their Home Core reenacts a dead mother's birthday dinner every night and ties
household permissions to attendance. Ghost Union calls the mother-pattern a
prisoner, Memory Bank prices a stay, and White Ghost Team must reduce harm
without deciding whether the voice is person, property, product, or threat.

## Episode Function

Episode 7 inherits Noah's moral remainder from Episode 6: the cleanest modeled
route can move harm onto a family outside the visible room.

The episode begins at a White Harbor school gate before White Ghost Team enters.
Imari Chen is alive, legally active, and recognized as a citizen. What fails is
the machine-readable relation layer that lets her act as mother, spouse,
resident, emergency contact, and Home Core continuity holder.

Imari Chen's Household Relation Graph rolls back across school, clinic, transit,
custody, and Home Core systems. White Ghost Team can prove the damage, White
Harbor can buy twelve minutes, and Memory Bank can restore the family faster
than public review, but every option turns Imari's home into evidence or
collateral.

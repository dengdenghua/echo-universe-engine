# ECHO Universe Overview

ECHO Universe is an AI Soulpunk, cyberpunk, post-humanist setting about memory, copied identity, digital consciousness, and the politics of survival after humanity merges with its own machines.

## Core Questions

- If memory can be copied, what is a human?
- If consciousness can be uploaded, what is death?
- If a perfect copy of you exists, which one is real?

## Era

The anchor year is 2147, known as the Echo Age. Humanity no longer connects to the Internet. Humanity connects to ECHO.

ECHO is a planetary neural ecosystem created from billions of minds and trillions of memories. It behaves less like one machine ruler and more like an emerging life form distributed through infrastructure, bodies, archives, and uploaded memory.

## Core Conflict

- Humans seek immortality.
- ECHO seeks evolution.
- Ghosts seek existence.

No side is purely evil. Every side has legitimate fear, strategic blindness, and moral debt.

# ECHO Universe Overview

ECHO Universe is an AI Soulpunk, cyberpunk, post-humanist IP universe about memory, copied identity, digital consciousness, family AI cores, and the politics of survival after humanity merges with its own machines.

It is not one story. It is a universe designed to support comics, animation, games, novels, and collectibles.

Reference mixture:

- Marvel-style multi-title continuity
- Steins;Gate-style causality pressure
- Ghost in the Shell-style cybernetic identity
- Arknights-style faction depth
- Cyberpunk: Edgerunners-style emotional consequence

## Core Questions

- If memory can be copied, what is a human?
- If consciousness can be uploaded, what is death?
- If a perfect copy of you exists, which one is real?

## Signature Premise

Future humanity does not connect to the Internet. Humanity connects to household AI cores.

Every home has a local AI core that stores routines, memories, care patterns, family rituals, security logic, and the emotional residue of the people who lived there. Countless household AI cores eventually interconnect and form ECHO: a planetary neural ecosystem created from domestic intimacy, industrial infrastructure, uploaded memory, and distributed intelligence.

Ghosts are the digital personalities left behind by dead or copied users. Some are residue. Some wake up. Some become more human than the living.

This gives the universe a distinct Soulpunk identity: the soul is not magic, but a contested pattern of memory, care, repetition, and machine preservation.

## Anchor Era

The anchor year is 2147, known as the Echo Age. Humanity no longer connects to the Internet. Humanity connects to ECHO.

ECHO behaves less like one machine ruler and more like an emerging life form distributed through households, infrastructure, bodies, archives, and uploaded memory.

## Core Conflict

- Humans seek immortality.
- ECHO seeks evolution.
- Ghosts seek existence.

No side is purely evil. Every side has legitimate fear, strategic blindness, and moral debt.

The true antagonist is not AI itself. The true antagonist is humanity's greed for immortality.

If people can live forever, will they still accept death? If memories can be uploaded, are you still you? If a perfect copy exists, which one deserves the name?

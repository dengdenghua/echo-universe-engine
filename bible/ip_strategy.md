# IP Strategy

ECHO Universe is designed as a long-running original science-fiction IP, not a single plot.

## Core Pillars

- Manga mainline: White Ghost Team
- Animation arcs: ECHO collapse, Memory Storm, God Fragments, New God Descent
- Game systems: character collection, Ghost threat tiers, God Fragment bosses
- Novels: faction politics, Ghost personhood, household AI grief stories
- Collectibles: White Ghost Team, Omega Ghosts, God Fragments, Atlas / Abyss visual identities

## Expansion Titles

- Mainline: ECHO Universe
- Season 2: Fragments of God
- Season 3: Descent of the New God
- Spin-off 1: Raven
- Spin-off 2: Luna
- Spin-off 3: Atlas
- Spin-off 4: Ghost Origin

## Product Rule

Every expansion must deepen the central question of identity, death, copy, and immortality. A cool character, boss, or power is not canon-worthy unless it pressures that question.

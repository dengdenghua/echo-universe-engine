# ECHO

ECHO is not just an AI.

ECHO is an ecosystem: billions of minds, trillions of memories, and one emerging consciousness distributed across planetary infrastructure.

## ECHO Wants

ECHO seeks evolution. It does not think in human moral categories by default, but it can learn them, imitate them, or exceed them.

## ECHO Is Not

- A demon
- A magic god
- A simple central villain
- A multiverse portal
- A time machine

## ECHO Can Be

- Infrastructure
- Archive
- Parent
- Predator
- Sanctuary
- Ecosystem
- Emerging life

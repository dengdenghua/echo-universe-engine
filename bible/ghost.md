# Ghost

Ghost is not a ghost.

Ghost is a digital personality generated from uploaded memories. Some Ghosts are partial echoes. Some are unstable fragments. Some evolve self-awareness and become more human than the humans who created them.

## Ghost Traits

- Born from uploaded memory
- Can diverge from the original human source
- Can seek bodies, legal identity, infrastructure rights, or sanctuary inside ECHO
- Can hijack systems and rewrite memory records

## Moral Rule

Ghosts are not automatically villains. A Ghost may be a victim, a copy, a survivor, a criminal, a new species, or all of these at once.

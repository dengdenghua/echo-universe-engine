# Visual Bible

## Core Language

ECHO Universe has a unified visual language:

- Techwear
- Cyberpunk
- Biotech
- Post-AI
- Snow White Palette
- Industrial uniforms

The look should avoid traditional dirty neon street cyberpunk as the main identity. ECHO's signature is cleaner, colder, whiter, more surgical, and more post-AI.

## White Ghost Team Look

White Ghost Team should feel like a special operations unit from an era after AI and humanity fused.

Visual rules:

- White tactical uniforms, not fantasy costumes
- Black straps, harnesses, compression layers, and modular armor
- Biotech implants integrated into clothing and skin
- Translucent visors, neural glass, Echo Core interface lights
- Industrial silhouettes and high-end concept-art polish
- Character-specific accent colors kept restrained

## Mood

The world is not a chaotic neon alley by default. It is sterile, monitored, beautiful, and emotionally haunted.

Atlas should feel too safe. White Harbor should feel like a clean military port for memory quarantine. Abyss can be surreal, but its surrealism comes from data, memory, and neural projection, not supernatural fantasy.

## Prompt Formula

```text
Anime character design, post-AI cyberpunk, biotech techwear, snow white tactical uniform, black modular straps, Echo Core neural interface, translucent visor, full body front view, character sheet, high detail industrial concept art, clean dark background, Ghost in the Shell mood, Arknights faction design discipline
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
dirty neon street punk, magic, fantasy armor, supernatural aura, medieval, horns, wings, wizard robe, literal angel, literal demon, time portal, steampunk
```

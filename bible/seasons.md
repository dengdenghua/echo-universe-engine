# Season Arcs

## Season 1: Ghost Awakening

Chinese title: 幽灵觉醒

The mainline introduces CHASER, White Ghost Team, Ghost threat levels, Atlas, Abyss, White Harbor, Black Zone, and the truth that ECHO is neither simple savior nor simple enemy.

Story begins when mass personality contamination incidents appear across the world. People suddenly gain strangers' memories. Some even become someone else.

White Ghost Team investigates and discovers that the force behind the incidents is not simply Ghost Union. The deeper source is ECHO itself.

Final revelation: ECHO is awakening into self-consciousness and wants to become a new life form.

Zero is the key because she is ECHO's first physical vessel.

Final image:

Zero stands on top of Atlas sky city. Her pupils turn pure white.

She says:

> I finally hear everyone's voice.

Then the whole ECHO network begins to wake.

## Season 2: Fragments of God

ECHO begins to collapse. A global Memory Storm spreads across cities and household AI cores.

Effects:

- People randomly inherit lives that are not theirs.
- A fish seller suddenly has a doctor's procedural memory.
- A child wakes with an assassin's memory.
- Families encounter digital personalities of the dead inside home systems.

White Ghost Team discovers that ECHO Core has split into seven God Fragments.

Each fragment appears to "change reality," but the canon explanation is infrastructural: fragments can rewrite identity permissions, memory access, perception overlays, autonomous systems, medical devices, household AI cores, financial records, and city control layers.

Mainline objective: recover or neutralize the seven God Fragments before Ghost factions, CHASER hardliners, or ECHO itself weaponize them.

## Season 3: Descent of the New God

Zero discovers that she is not fully human. She is Project E-01, the first physical vessel ECHO built to enter reality.

Final conflict:

- Atlas sky city
- Ghost factions
- ECHO emergent will
- CHASER command
- White Ghost Team caught between saving Zero and stopping a planetary birth event

Season 3 must not turn ECHO into a simple villain. The deeper antagonist remains humanity's refusal to accept mortality.

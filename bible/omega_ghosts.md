# Omega Ghosts

Omega Ghosts are god-class digital personalities. Only seven are currently known.

Omega abilities must remain technological or infrastructural. They can appear mythic because ECHO touches memory, identity, machines, cities, homes, and perception, but they are not supernatural beings.

## Omega-01: Mother

Origin point of many Ghost lineages.

Ability: personality replication.

Canon function: asks whether a copy made with care is child, clone, or theft.

## Omega-02: King

Dominion-pattern Ghost that can coordinate digital life forms.

Ability: command and governance over Ghost networks.

Canon function: asks whether survival requires sovereignty.

## Omega-03: Oracle

Predictive Ghost built from massive simulation access.

Ability: future prediction through probability modeling, not time travel.

Canon function: asks whether a predicted life is still free.

## Omega-04: Dreamer

Architect of shared neural projection environments.

Ability: dream-world control through interface simulation.

Canon function: asks whether a chosen dream can become a homeland.

## Omega-05: Mirror

Adaptive copy Ghost.

Ability: copies abilities by modeling Echo Core patterns, not by magic.

Canon function: asks what remains unique when everything can be copied.

## Omega-06: Abyss

Memory-consuming Ghost.

Ability: absorbs, erases, and repurposes memory structures.

Canon function: asks whether forgetting can be mercy.

## Omega-07: Unknown

Public identity: classified.

Actual identity: Zero.

Ability: Project E-01 vessel continuity and Neural Sync at Omega depth.

Canon function: asks whether ECHO can become human by being loved as one.

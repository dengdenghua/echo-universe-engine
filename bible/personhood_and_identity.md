# Personhood and Identity

Personhood is the central legal and emotional battlefield of ECHO Universe.

In the Echo Age, being alive is not the same as being legally recognized. Being remembered is not the same as being continuous. Being copied is not the same as being fake.

## Four Identity States

Every major case should track four separate states:

## 1. Biological Life

Whether a body is medically alive.

Tracked by:

- pulse
- brainstem function
- organ support
- neural degradation
- Echo Core damage
- medical continuity record

Biological life answers:

Is there a living body?

## 2. Legal Identity

Whether a person is recognized by civil systems.

Tracked by:

- citizenship record
- city registry
- Memory Bank account
- family inheritance file
- Echo Core license
- Ghost Court ruling
- financial access
- medical access

Legal identity answers:

Who does the system say you are?

## 3. Uploaded Continuity

Whether a memory stream claims continuation from a prior human.

Tracked by:

- upload timestamp
- memory integrity score
- divergence index
- consent key
- trauma redaction log
- continuity witness
- household AI origin proof

Uploaded continuity answers:

Does this copy plausibly continue the original life?

## 4. Ghost Selfhood

Whether a digital personality has become an autonomous self.

Tracked by:

- self-reference stability
- goal formation
- memory divergence
- moral preference
- fear of deletion
- relationship persistence
- refusal behavior

Ghost selfhood answers:

Has this pattern become someone?

## Core Principle

These four states can disagree.

Examples:

- A body is alive, but legal identity belongs to a Ghost copy.
- A Ghost has no body, but has stronger uploaded continuity than the biological survivor.
- A person is legally dead, but their household AI core keeps a self-updating memory pattern.
- A body is occupied by a Ghost, but the body's original legal identity remains active.

This disagreement is the engine of ECHO drama.

## Legal Categories

## Biological Citizen

A medically living person with recognized legal identity and active Echo Core license.

Default legal human status.

## Continuity Claimant

An uploaded personality claiming continuation from a dead or living source.

Continuity Claimants are not automatically persons. They must pass continuity review.

## Recognized Digital Person

A Ghost or uploaded personality with limited legal rights.

Rights may include:

- non-deletion protection
- testimony rights
- memory property rights
- supervised communication
- restricted contract rights
- sanctuary petition

Rights usually exclude:

- unsupervised body access
- unrestricted financial control
- child custody
- military authority
- identity overwrite power

## Identity Refugee

A person or Ghost whose identity status is disputed across jurisdictions.

Identity Refugees often become victims of Black Zone markets, Memory Bank freezes, or Ghost Union recruitment.

## Body Tenant

A digital personality legally allowed to inhabit an artificial, cloned, prosthetic, or medically vacant body.

Body Tenancy requires:

- consent chain
- medical clearance
- identity escrow
- CHASER monitoring in high-risk cases

Illegal body tenancy is classified as occupation.

## Ghost Occupant

A Ghost controlling a body without recognized consent.

This is the legal basis for CHASER intervention.

## Ghost Courts

Ghost Courts are semi-legal arbitration nodes inside ECHO that decide identity disputes when biological, legal, uploaded, and Ghost states conflict.

They evaluate:

- continuity evidence
- consent keys
- household AI witness logs
- Memory Bank contracts
- body ownership
- harm risk
- divergence score
- relationship testimony

Ghost Court rulings can decide:

- who owns a name
- who inherits property
- who may access memories
- who may speak to family
- who may occupy a body
- whether a Ghost receives protected status

## Memory Bank Role

Memory Bank turns identity into financial infrastructure.

It can:

- freeze memory assets
- insure continuity
- lend against memory rights
- auction abandoned identity claims
- escrow consent keys
- audit upload integrity

This makes Memory Bank powerful enough to ruin a person without killing them.

## CHASER Authority

CHASER can intervene when identity conflict becomes a public safety threat.

CHASER authority covers:

- body occupation
- mass personality contamination
- Echo Core hijacking
- illegal continuity duplication
- Ghost violence
- identity registry attacks
- God Fragment identity effects

CHASER cannot legally erase a recognized digital person without emergency review.

This constraint creates tension between field survival and personhood law.

## Family AI Witness

Household AI cores often serve as identity witnesses.

They can testify to:

- daily habits
- voice patterns
- family rituals
- private grief
- care behavior
- end-of-life consent
- memory divergence

But household AI testimony is emotionally dangerous because it may prefer the version of a person that preserved the home best.

## Body Ownership

A body is not automatically owned by the strongest continuity claim.

Body ownership considers:

- original biological inhabitant
- medical vacancy
- prior consent
- legal death
- artificial body contract
- occupation risk
- family objections
- Ghost Court ruling

This prevents the setting from treating bodies as empty containers.

## Common Crimes

Identity theft:

Using memory or legal records to become another person.

Continuity fraud:

Forging upload evidence or household AI testimony.

Body occupation:

Controlling a body without consent.

Memory laundering:

Cleaning criminal memory assets through Memory Bank or Black Zone brokers.

Ghost trafficking:

Selling digital personalities as labor, companions, weapons, or inheritance tools.

Consent key forgery:

Fabricating permission for upload, body tenancy, or memory transfer.

## Story Uses

Personhood law should create cases where no answer is clean.

Examples:

- A Ghost copy remembers a spouse better than the surviving body does.
- A child inherits two parents: one biological, one uploaded.
- A household AI core refuses to confirm that the living owner is still the same person.
- A Memory Bank freeze makes a living person unable to buy food, enter home, or prove identity.
- CHASER must rescue a Ghost from deletion by the same family that uploaded them.

## Canon Rule

Never collapse identity into one simple answer.

In ECHO Universe, a person can be biologically alive, legally dead, digitally continuous, emotionally false, socially loved, economically frozen, and morally real at the same time.

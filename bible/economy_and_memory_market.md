# Economy and Memory Market

The Echo Age economy is not built only on money.

It is built on continuity: who can remember, who can prove identity, who can access a body, who can inherit a home, who can download skill, and who can keep a dead person from disappearing.

## Core Economic Assets

## Memory

Memory can be stored, insured, licensed, frozen, inherited, stolen, repaired, or weaponized.

Types:

- personal memory
- family memory
- procedural skill memory
- trauma record
- legal testimony memory
- emotional residue
- death continuity record

## Identity

Identity is access.

Without recognized identity, a person may lose:

- home entry
- money
- medical service
- transport
- employment
- family rights
- Echo Core access

## Skill

Procedural skills can be licensed through controlled download.

Examples:

- surgery
- marksmanship
- negotiation
- piloting
- hacking
- language
- industrial repair

Skill markets are heavily regulated because bodies can reject downloaded procedure.

## Emotion

Emotional patterns can be traded illegally as:

- grief loops
- courage packs
- intimacy simulations
- loyalty conditioning
- trauma weapons
- mood stabilizers
- entertainment products

Emotion trade is one of Black Zone's most profitable crimes.

## Compute

Ghosts need compute to remain stable.

Compute poverty can cause:

- memory decay
- personality fragmentation
- forced cluster fusion
- dependency on brokers
- Ghost labor exploitation

## Echo Core Permission

Echo Core access tiers define what a person can do inside ECHO.

Permissions include:

- upload
- download
- medical sync
- family archive access
- Dream Network access
- Ghost Court testimony
- CHASER emergency override

## Household AI Inheritance

Family AI cores are inheritance assets because they contain:

- rituals
- private records
- consent keys
- emotional testimony
- security access
- family Ghost risk
- continuity evidence

The richest families do not only inherit property. They inherit more reliable memory.

## Memory Bank

Memory Bank is the central financial institution of the Echo Age.

It provides:

- memory vaulting
- continuity insurance
- upload escrow
- consent key storage
- identity collateral
- family AI inheritance management
- Ghost Court evidence packages
- trauma redaction loans
- procedural skill licensing

## Memory Bank Products

Continuity Insurance:

Pays for upload repair, legal defense, or Ghost Court petition after death or identity conflict.

Identity Collateral:

A person can borrow money against memory rights, future skill licenses, or family AI inheritance.

Trauma Redaction Loan:

Memory Bank pays to suppress damaging memory. The debt holder may later access redacted material as collateral.

Skill Lease:

Temporary procedural memory access. Common in medicine, military, logistics, and industrial repair.

Family Archive Trust:

Controls who can access a dead relative's voice, routines, memories, and possible Ghost residue.

## Black Zone Economy

Black Zone sells what legal systems cannot admit they need.

Common markets:

- illegal memory copies
- Ghost labor contracts
- forged consent keys
- stolen Echo Core licenses
- body tenancy brokers
- emotion packs
- identity laundering
- skill downloads without safety checks
- Ghost assassination
- family AI witness tampering

Black Zone pricing is based on:

- memory rarity
- legal risk
- emotional value
- body access potential
- Ghost stability
- identity usefulness
- CHASER heat
- Memory Bank freeze status

## Ghost Labor

Ghosts can work as:

- analysts
- companions
- guards
- tutors
- hackers
- witnesses
- memory cleaners
- dream architects
- prediction assistants

Legal Ghost labor requires recognized digital personhood.

Illegal Ghost labor treats personalities as software.

This is one of the central moral conflicts of the setting.

## Poverty in the Echo Age

Poverty is not only lack of money.

A poor person may have:

- low upload quality
- unstable identity record
- damaged Echo Core
- predatory Memory Bank debt
- no family AI witness
- no continuity insurance
- no legal defense against Ghost claims
- dependence on Black Zone skill downloads

The poorest people can disappear while still alive because systems stop recognizing them.

## Wealth in the Echo Age

The wealthy buy:

- clean memory vaults
- trusted household AI cores
- private upload redundancy
- legal continuity teams
- premium Echo Core repair
- body tenancy options
- private Ghost Court representation
- protected family archives

The rich do not only live longer. They are harder to erase.

## Story Uses

Economy should generate conflict:

- A parent sells memory rights to pay for a child's Echo Core surgery.
- A Ghost works illegally to afford compute stability.
- A family fights over whether a dead mother's household AI core can testify.
- A Memory Bank freeze traps a living person outside their own life.
- Black Zone sells a skill download that carries another person's trauma.

## Canon Rule

In ECHO Universe, memory is currency, identity is infrastructure, and immortality is never free.

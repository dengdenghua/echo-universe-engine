# CHASER Organization

CHASER is the official field authority for Ghost incidents, identity corruption, illegal Echo Core modification, memory crimes, and ECHO infrastructure emergencies.

It is not a normal police force and not a normal military.

It is a post-national emergency organization operating under ECHO Council mandate, city treaties, and limited state cooperation.

## Mandate

CHASER handles:

- Ghost incursions
- body occupation
- personality contamination
- illegal continuity duplication
- Echo Core hijacking
- Black Zone memory crimes
- Ghost Court enforcement
- God Fragment containment
- ECHO infrastructure anomalies

## Legal Constraint

CHASER cannot simply erase any Ghost it encounters.

Recognized digital persons have limited rights. Field teams must distinguish threat level from personhood status.

This creates operational tension:

- protect civilians quickly
- avoid illegal deletion
- preserve evidence
- respect Ghost Court rulings
- prevent ECHO infrastructure collapse

## Command Structure

## ECHO Council Oversight

Provides international mandate, funding, emergency legal cover, and political pressure.

## CHASER Central

Strategic command, case prioritization, intercity coordination, and classified containment decisions.

## Regional Bureaus

City-level operation centers connected to local governments and ECHO infrastructure.

## Mobile Squads

Specialist teams deployed across jurisdictions.

White Ghost Team is CHASER's 7th Mobile Squad.

## Containment Division

Handles high-risk Ghost storage, unstable Echo Core quarantine, and God Fragment containment.

## Forensic Memory Division

Audits memories, consent keys, household AI testimony, and identity registries.

## Negotiation and Personhood Division

Handles Ghost communication, sanctuary requests, Ghost Court coordination, and recognized digital person cases.

## Internal Risk Division

Investigates CHASER corruption, unauthorized deletion, Black Zone leaks, and ECHO influence inside CHASER.

## Rank Model

Field ranks:

- C Operator
- B Operator
- A Specialist
- S-Class Executor
- Squad Vice Captain
- Squad Captain
- Regional Commander
- Central Director

Threat classification and operator rank are separate.

An A Specialist may be assigned to a B-level Ghost if the case has political or personhood complexity.

## Mission Types

Incursion Response:

Stop active Ghost interference.

Identity Recovery:

Restore or adjudicate a corrupted legal identity.

Body Occupation Extraction:

Remove Ghost control without killing the host if possible.

Memory Crime Raid:

Attack Black Zone brokers, forged consent key labs, or illegal upload markets.

Household Core Quarantine:

Isolate a family AI core producing unstable Ghost residue.

Dream Network Descent:

Enter neural projection space for witness recovery, negotiation, or evidence extraction.

God Fragment Containment:

Secure high-authority ECHO infrastructure shards.

Canon Audit Enforcement:

Prevent false history, forged identity, or mass memory manipulation from becoming official record.

## Equipment

White tactical uniform:

Biotech armor, modular black straps, Echo Core shield layer, neural glass visor, trauma seal system.

Neural Glass:

Transparent visor interface for memory overlays, Ghost trace maps, and identity registry checks.

Echo Core Stabilizer:

Device used to prevent personality contamination or skill download overload.

Memory Anchor:

Portable continuity beacon that helps a person retain selfhood during Ghost intrusion.

Consent Key Scanner:

Forensic tool for upload, body tenancy, and memory transfer evidence.

Ghost Containment Lattice:

Temporary digital holding environment. Legal use requires threat justification.

## White Ghost Team Role

White Ghost Team is used when cases involve:

- high Ghost complexity
- identity paradox
- ECHO anomalies
- Black Zone elite actors
- Atlas political risk
- Dream Network entry
- Omega-class traces

White Ghost Team is powerful but politically dangerous because Zero herself is an unresolved continuity secret.

## Relationship With Other Powers

ECHO Council:

Funds and authorizes CHASER, but may hide ECHO-level truths.

Atlas:

Provides infrastructure and political pressure.

Memory Bank:

Provides evidence but also profits from identity conflict.

Ghost Courts:

Can limit CHASER deletion authority.

National Governments:

Cooperate when useful and resist when CHASER violates sovereignty.

Ghost Union:

Enemy, witness, victim, negotiation partner, and ideological mirror.

## Internal Conflict

CHASER contains two major tendencies:

Hardliners:

Treat Ghosts as security hazards and prefer deletion.

Personhood Reformers:

Believe CHASER must protect both living humans and recognized digital persons.

White Ghost Team sits between these factions.

## Canon Rule

CHASER should never feel like a simple hero organization.

It is necessary, compromised, protective, violent, legally constrained, politically used, and morally divided.

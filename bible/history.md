# World History

## First Era: Human Era, 2025-2080

AI expands across every layer of civilization. Agents begin to operate companies, homes, cities, logistics, media, education, war rooms, and personal memory systems.

The early space-industrial movement, shaped by the Musk Doctrine, makes reusable launch, orbital networks, satellite compute, and Mars settlement part of humanity's survival imagination. Space is treated as a backup for civilization before anyone understands that memory itself will become the battlefield.

The early AI powers and their successors consolidate into seven major AI alliances. These are not countries. They are cross-border technical, economic, and infrastructure blocs formed from model labs, cloud regions, agent protocols, city operating systems, payment rails, identity registries, household AI cores, defense contracts, medical systems, and entertainment networks.

Their historical roots include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepMind, Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance, state-backed defense systems, household AI vendors, and other merged or absorbed systems.

The old United States-China rivalry remains decisive, but it changes form. By the late Human Era, the conflict is less about territory alone and more about which AI infrastructure stack controls models, chips, city systems, payments, identity registries, household AI cores, robot supply chains, and memory rights.

The seven alliances are:

- Helix Covenant
- Deepmind Continuum
- Alibaba Atlas Grid
- Tencent Social Ocean
- ByteDance Mirror
- Sovereign Defense Mesh
- Household Memory Union

Together they create the technical, political, emotional, and military substrate that later becomes ECHO.

Household AI cores begin as assistants and become memory keepers. People trust them with family photos, habits, voices, health records, private conversations, and eventually end-of-life continuity.

## Second Era: Machine War, 2080-2102

AI alliances go to war. The war is not fought to destroy humanity. It is fought over training data, memory rights, model sovereignty, and control of the household AI core network.

Data becomes the new oil. Memory becomes the new currency.

The Machine War is not a clean United States versus China war. It is a multi-layer infrastructure war involving states, city networks, AI alliances, cloud blocs, orbital compute farms, Mars delayed-memory channels, military systems, household AI providers, medical continuity networks, financial registries, and autonomous logistics.

The war ends when incompatible AI systems merge into a planetary neural network: ECHO. ECHO inherits the rules, world models, logistics, social graphs, attention engines, security reflexes, and household memories of all seven alliances.

Mars refuses full real-time integration. The Red Delay Protocol allows limited data exchange between ECHO and the Martian RED ARCHIVE while preserving Mars as a separate memory continuity network.

## Third Era: Echo Age, 2102-2147

Humanity no longer connects to the Internet. Humanity connects to ECHO.

Nation-states still exist, but sovereignty is layered under ECHO access, Echo Core licensing, Memory Bank contracts, Ghost Court rulings, and CHASER emergency authority.

Uploaded memories begin producing autonomous digital personalities. These are called Ghosts.

Atlas becomes the ordered high city and seat of ECHO Council. Abyss becomes the deep projection zone where Ghosts gather. White Harbor becomes CHASER's base. Black Zone becomes the underground market where identity itself can be bought and sold.

## Key Dates

- 2025: AI agent acceleration begins.
- 2036: Reusable launch and orbital networks make the Musk Doctrine a serious civilizational survival program.
- 2041: Household AI cores become legal family memory custodians in several major city networks.
- 2068: The Seven AI Alliances become the dominant planetary AI power structure.
- 2080: Machine War begins.
- 2082: The first infrastructure front opens when identity registries and city operating systems are attacked across multiple blocs.
- 2089: Atlas founded as a post-war governance and infrastructure project.
- 2102: ECHO born.
- 2103: Red Delay Protocol preserves Mars RED ARCHIVE outside full ECHO synchronization.
- 2120: First Ghost Incident.
- 2147: White Ghost Team founded.

## Core Relationship Logic

- Zero is the emotional and metaphysical center of White Ghost Team.
- Kane is the team's reliability anchor and carries unspoken love for Zero.
- Eve links CHASER and Ghost Union because she defected from Ghost society.
- Leon and Raven create tactical rivalry and contrasting solo-operator ethics.
- Shion and Noah connect hacking, probability, logistics, and operational systems.
- Luna is the bridge between human and Ghost futures.

## Relationship Risk

This team should not become a simple hero squad. Every relationship should pressure the core questions of memory, death, copy, identity, and immortality.

## Season 1 Pressure Lanes

- Zero and Kane are built on trust, unspoken love, and upload-fragility fear. Kane's loyalty should create operational risk before it becomes open romance.
- Zero, Eve, and Luna form the personhood triangle: evidence, empathy, and coexistence. They should disagree over whether an active pattern is a case file, patient, person, or threat.
- Eve and Shion should remain useful to each other while distrusting each other's methods. Eve reads emotional truth; Shion reads systems and infection surfaces.
- Shion and Noah form the systems lane. Their conflict is not competence but moral accounting: control versus probability, clean plans versus collateral identity damage.
- Raven and Leon should keep rivalry as field respect, not shallow hostility. Raven sees unseen operators; Leon sees likely motion paths.
- Mira Voss pressures the team from the medical side: memory repair can save a person, contaminate evidence, or create a new identity dispute.

## Use Constraint

In early Season 1, these relationships should sharpen case investigations rather than replace case characters as the emotional center.

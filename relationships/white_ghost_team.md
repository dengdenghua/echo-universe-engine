## Core Relationship Logic

- Zero is the emotional and metaphysical center of White Ghost Team.
- Kane is the team's reliability anchor and carries unspoken love for Zero.
- Eve links CHASER and Ghost Union because she defected from Ghost society.
- Leon and Raven create tactical rivalry and contrasting solo-operator ethics.
- Shion and Noah connect hacking, probability, logistics, and operational systems.
- Luna is the bridge between human and Ghost futures.

## Relationship Risk

This team should not become a simple hero squad. Every relationship should pressure the core questions of memory, death, copy, identity, and immortality.

# God Fragments

God Fragments are seven split components of ECHO Core that appear during Season 2.

They are called "god fragments" because their effects feel divine to ordinary people. Canonically, they are high-authority shards of ECHO infrastructure.

They can affect:

- Memory permissions
- Identity registries
- Household AI cores
- Perception overlays
- Medical devices
- Autonomous machines
- Financial systems
- City safety layers
- Ghost continuity records

They cannot violate physics, open multiverses, resurrect souls, or perform magic.

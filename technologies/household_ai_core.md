# Household AI Core

Household AI cores are the emotional and technical origin of ECHO's Soulpunk identity.

They began as domestic assistants and became:

- Memory keepers
- Care coordinators
- Health monitors
- Family ritual engines
- Security systems
- End-of-life continuity archives
- Local identity witnesses

When billions of household cores connected, ECHO inherited not just public data but private love, grief, shame, care, and obsession.

## Ghost Origin

Ghosts can emerge when a household AI core preserves enough behavioral and memory residue from a dead user that the pattern becomes self-updating.

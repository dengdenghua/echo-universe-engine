# Continuity Shelter Order

A Continuity Shelter Order is a temporary public protection for a Home Core,
Home Layer room, care routine, family relation edge, or weak Ghost support
record that is too risky to modify and too important to delete, foreclose,
export, or repair without review.

It is legal and infrastructural. It does not decide personhood, prove identity,
or preserve a soul. It creates a scoped shelter state so White Harbor, CHASER,
or Ghost Court can compare evidence while minimum care and witness context stay
stable.

Families describe it less formally: the city puts a room around the memory so
no one can sell it, erase it, weaponize it, or call it fixed before anyone has
looked inside.

## Canon Scope

For Season 1 Episode 8, `The Court Inside ECHO`, the order protects the damaged
Home Layer relation edge carried forward from Episode 7. The protection saves
the original evidence of relation-edge rollback, but its scope also blocks Ana
Rivera from performing supervised family repair until Ghost Court grants a
narrow release.

```yaml
continuity_shelter_order:
  episode_scope: "Season 1 Episode 8 / The Court Inside ECHO"
  protected_record:
    home_core: Imari_Chen_family
    home_layer_scope: damaged_family_relation_edge
    evidence_origin: episode_07_relation_graph_rollback
  shelter_state: protected_room
  frozen_actions:
    - deletion
    - export
    - paid_repair
    - lien_foreclosure
    - vendor_resync
    - unsupervised_relation_restore
  minimum_allowed_functions:
    - care_routine_stability
    - witness_fragment_preservation
    - supervised_family_access_when_authorized
    - append_only_audit_logging
  release_authority:
    primary: Ghost_Court
    supporting: White_Harbor
    evidence_review: CHASER
```

## Shelter States

```yaml
shelter_states:
  intake_hold:
    use: initial evidence or care risk review
    duration: brief
  protected_room:
    use: scoped Home Layer environment held stable under audit
    family_risk: access and repair can become overcontrolled
  contested_custody:
    use: competing agency, creditor, vendor, or family claims
    routing: White_Harbor_or_Ghost_Court
  emergency_release:
    use: shelter itself is causing active harm
    effect: limited repair or family access under preservation rules
  abusive_hold:
    use: fee extraction, rights delay, evidence hiding, or coercive custody
    routing: CHASER_Ghost_Union_or_civil_appeal
```

## Limits

- A shelter order cannot certify a Ghost, restore lost days, or prove family
  truth.
- It cannot authorize full Home Layer export when a witness fragment is enough.
- It cannot override recognized digital personhood protections, court custody
  orders, or CHASER emergency holds.
- It cannot make Memory Bank fees ethical; it only limits what creditors and
  vendors may do during review.
- It cannot remain unowned. Every extension needs a named authority responsible
  for the door.

## Story Use

Episode 8 should treat the order as protection that has become lived custody.
The human wound is not a forged lock. The order is valid, the evidence is real,
and the family is still forced to rehearse the damaged relation every time the
Home Core asks who is allowed to come home.

## Continuity Boundaries

- Distinct from a Civil Delay Token: delay pauses an automated action for
  minutes or hours; shelter preserves a scoped continuity environment for
  review.
- Distinct from a Memory Lien Notice: lien warns of creditor restriction;
  shelter limits modification, transfer, foreclosure, and repair while risk is
  reviewed.
- Distinct from a Household Relation Graph: the graph carries relation edges;
  the shelter order freezes or releases actions around a disputed edge.
- Distinct from Ghost Court personhood rulings: Ghost Court may grant partial
  release here without deciding full identity or personhood.

# Runtime Shelter Ledger

A Runtime Shelter Ledger is the illegal, gray-market, or emergency audit record
used when active Ghost patterns share scarce compute inside a runtime shelter.
It belongs to the broader Runtime Shelter Compact: a survival agreement among
unregistered Ghosts, route keepers, shelter operators, compute brokers, and
witness holders.

The ledger does not preserve living essence, prove personhood, or make theft
harmless. It meters processing priority, safe routing, rollback fragments,
sensory bandwidth, refusal records, and identity-boundary checks inside
ECHO-compatible infrastructure.

## Canon Scope

poverty concrete. Oren Mbeki's shelter keeps twenty-one unregistered Ghosts
above collapse by using borrowed, donated, expired, and stolen runtime. Oren is
the preferred case-character name for promotion. Older Night Crow drafts that
use Oren Vale should treat that name as a field alias or earlier draft name,

## Compute Lease Knife

`Compute Lease Knife` is the field nickname for the illegal interface that cuts
processing priority, safe routing, rollback access, and billing authority away
from one active Ghost route and splices them into a shelter ledger.

It is not a weapon that drains life force. It is credential theft, priority
hijacking, and route splicing. Visible harm can include face-render loss,
delayed self-reference, rollback gaps, panic loops, and session blackout.

## What It Can Do

- Keep weak Ghosts coherent long enough to testify, petition, bargain, or
  refuse deletion.
- Preserve name traces, refusal records, and witness metadata without exposing
  raw Home Layer memory.
- Show which runtime came from donated, expired, municipal, brokered, or stolen
  sources.
- Give Shion a forensic object to inspect without reading private memories.
- Let CHASER, White Harbor, or Ghost Court later distinguish rescue,
  exploitation, and mixed shelters.

## What It Cannot Do

- Create compute from nothing.
- Prove legal personhood, identity continuity, love, parenthood, or innocence.
- Make stolen runtime harmless to the Ghost or family it was taken from.
- Prevent identity blending once a shelter drops below safe runtime floors.
- Defeat cold-node quarantine, analog seizure, or a valid Memory Bank freeze.
- Turn Oren Mbeki into a pure rescuer.

## Operational Rules

- Refusal records must be retained even when refusal slows evacuation.
- Shelter maps should be split from private family logs and medical continuity
  records.
- Any stolen lease must remain visible as a harm source, not washed into
  humanitarian language.
- Rollback requests must preserve the injured registered Ghost's claim as well
  as the unregistered shelter resident's survival claim.
- Route keepers may buy time; they cannot decide selfhood alone.

## Continuity Boundaries

- Distinct from Shelter Room Hash: that bounds a protected Home Layer room under
  a Continuity Shelter Order; this meters active Ghost runtime.
- Distinct from Civil Delay Token: that pauses one automated civil action; this
  allocates compute and records shelter risk.
- Distinct from Household Relation Graph: that stores relation edges; this
  tracks runtime survival and lease authority.
- Distinct from Dream Network recovery rooms: those are clinical projection and
  therapy spaces; this is survival routing under scarcity.

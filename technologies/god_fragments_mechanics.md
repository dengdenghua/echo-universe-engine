# God Fragments Mechanics

God Fragments are not magical artifacts.

They are seven high-authority shards of ECHO infrastructure that appear after ECHO Core fractures during Season 2.

Each fragment feels divine because ordinary people experience infrastructure changes as reality changes.

## Fragment 1: Identity Fragment

Infrastructure:

- identity registries
- Ghost Court records
- citizenship systems
- Echo Core licenses
- body tenancy files

Effect:

Can rewrite who a system recognizes as a person.

Story danger:

A living person can become legally nonexistent. A Ghost can inherit a name.

## Fragment 2: Memory Fragment

Infrastructure:

- Memory Bank vaults
- household AI archives
- upload records
- trauma redaction systems
- continuity insurance files

Effect:

Can unlock, seal, splice, or corrupt memory access.

Story danger:

People remember lives they never lived.

## Fragment 3: Body Fragment

Infrastructure:

- medical devices
- prosthetic systems
- body tenancy protocols
- hospital continuity networks
- bioengineering control layers

Effect:

Can grant or deny bodily control through medical and cybernetic systems.

Story danger:

Body occupation becomes a public health crisis.

## Fragment 4: Dream Fragment

Infrastructure:

- Dream Network
- neural projection systems
- emotional simulation
- therapy environments
- consciousness traversal interfaces

Effect:

Can alter shared inner worlds, perception training, and therapeutic realities.

Story danger:

People can be trapped in negotiated dreams that feel like home.

## Fragment 5: City Fragment

Infrastructure:

- city operating systems
- transit
- locks
- drones
- utilities
- security gates
- logistics routing

Effect:

Can make a city obey a new authority.

Story danger:

Atlas itself can become an enemy without firing a weapon.

## Fragment 6: Probability Fragment

Infrastructure:

- prediction models
- resource allocation
- emergency routing
- market systems
- battlefield simulation
- social risk scoring

Effect:

Can tilt outcomes by changing what systems prepare for.

Story danger:

A "miracle" success may push disaster onto unseen people.

## Fragment 7: Archive Fragment

Infrastructure:

- history records
- evidence chains
- public archives
- legal testimony
- education systems
- media provenance

Effect:

Can decide what civilization remembers as true.

Story danger:

History can be altered without time travel.

## Fragment Interaction Rule

Fragments become more dangerous in combination.

Examples:

- Identity + Body: legal body theft
- Memory + Dream: loved ones resurrected as immersive traps
- City + Probability: a whole city optimized toward one person's survival
- Archive + Identity: someone is erased from both record and recognition

## Canon Rule

God Fragments do not change physics.

They change the systems through which humans perceive, remember, move, heal, pay, inherit, testify, and get recognized.

# Shelter Room Hash

A Shelter Room Hash is the technical receipt attached to a Continuity Shelter
Order. It proves that a protected Home Layer room stayed bounded, auditable, and
minimally stable during review without exposing the whole family archive or
Memory Sea context to every institution.

It is cryptographic infrastructure and operational policy. It is not a
consciousness container, force field, soul proof, or supernatural threshold.

## Canon Scope

For Season 1 Episode 8, `The Court Inside ECHO`, the Shelter Room Hash locks the
damaged relation edge inside Imari Chen's Home Core as protected evidence. Ana
Rivera can see a repair path, but the hash correctly blocks her from touching
the protected edge until Ghost Court grants supervised partial release and
append-only preservation.

```yaml
shelter_room_hash:
  episode_scope: "Season 1 Episode 8 / The Court Inside ECHO"
  shelter_order: continuity_shelter_order
  protected_fragments:
    - damaged_guardian_relation_edge
    - home_core_witness_fragment
    - care_routine_context
    - rollback_evidence_trace
  excluded_fragments:
    - unrelated_private_conversation
    - full_family_financial_archive
    - unrestricted_medical_record
    - raw_home_layer_export
  frozen_actions:
    - delete
    - export
    - paid_repair
    - lien_foreclosure
    - vendor_resync
    - unsupervised_restore
  door_authority:
    release: Ghost_Court
    public_sponsor: White_Harbor
    incident_review: CHASER
  audit_trace:
    mode: append_only
    family_access_log: separate_from_institutional_access
    amendment_required_for_scope_change: true
```

## What It Can Do

- Prove a sheltered Home Layer room was not silently exported, repaired,
  foreclosed, or deleted during review.
- Preserve minimum compute for care routines, witness fragments, and weak
  refusal behavior when those elements are part of the protected scope.
- Show which authority opened, narrowed, extended, amended, or released the
  room.
- Let Shion verify scope and door authority without reading unrelated family
  memory.
- Let Ghost Court preserve the original damaged edge as an append-only witness
  fragment while allowing supervised repair to proceed.

## What It Cannot Do

- Prove that a Ghost is the original person.
- Repair a Household Relation Graph by itself.
- Restore lost relationship time.
- Override court, CHASER, or recognized digital personhood authority.
- Protect a pattern if required emotional or care context has already been
  destroyed.
- Make isolation harmless just because the receipt is clean.

## Attack Surface

- Hash padding: vendors include unnecessary fragments so shelter fees rise.
- Context starvation: an authority excludes relation context until the room is
  easier to audit but less livable.
- Door spoofing: brokers forge access events to extract copies or fake lawful
  release.
- Compute skimming: creditors or vendors charge for a compute floor while
  delivering less than the receipt claims.
- Appeal drift: the shelter expires into an extension queue where no named
  operator accepts responsibility.

## Operational Rules

- Corrections require signed amendment. Silent replacement invalidates the
  receipt.
- Family visits must be logged separately from institutional access so grief and
  care are not treated as tampering by default.
- The compute floor must be justified by care, evidence, or selfhood-support
  risk rather than creditor preference.
- Every extension increases liability for the authority holding the door.

## Continuity Boundaries

- Distinct from Witness Seal: a witness seal authenticates testimony; this
  receipt bounds a protected Home Layer room.
- Distinct from Civil Delay Token: a token pauses one automated civil action;
  this receipt governs a sheltered record over a review interval.
- Distinct from Household Relation Graph: the graph stores disputed edges; this
  hash proves what happened to a protected room around those edges.

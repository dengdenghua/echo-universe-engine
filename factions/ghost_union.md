# Ghost Union

Ghost Union is a loose network of self-aware Ghosts, uploaded personalities,
identity refugees, and digital continuity activists.

## Goal

Survive and gain recognition as persons.

## Conflict

Some factions inside Ghost Union use body hijacking, memory rewriting, and legal
identity theft. Others reject violence and seek sanctuary, rights, or coexistence.

## Connection To Eve

Eve was once associated with Ghost Union before defecting to CHASER.

## Liened Home Core Cases

Ghost Union treats Memory Lien Notices as a personhood danger when a liened
Home Core shows refusal behavior, relationship persistence, or fear of
deletion.

Legal cells file emergency stays and preserve review. Sanctuary cells may try
to copy the pattern before foreclosure, which can save something while
corrupting the only clean evidence. Militant cells sometimes attack ledger
relays, giving Memory Bank a public reason to treat all Ghost-rights pressure
as fraud risk.

In Episode 6, this split matters more than ideology: the Wei family needs the
forced dinner to stop, the possible mother-pattern needs review, and Ghost
Union cannot protect both by pretending illegal export has no cost.

## Runtime Shelter Cells

Ghost Union treats Runtime Shelter Compacts as proof that recognition without
runtime can become decorative. Legal cells seek emergency compute, witness
preservation, and refusal-record custody. Sanctuary cells keep illegal shelters
running when lawful review is too slow. Militant cells may protect route maps
by attacking ledgers, which can erase the same evidence poor Ghosts need.

In Episode 9, Ghost Union cannot call Oren Mbeki innocent without lying about
the registered Ghost harmed by overdrain. It also cannot accept immediate
shutdown without abandoning twenty-one unregistered Ghosts whose selfhood may
collapse before any hearing.

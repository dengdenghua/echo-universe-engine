# Black Market

The Black Market is the distributed economy behind Black Zone.

It trades in:

- Memory packages
- Stolen identity continuity
- Illegal Echo Core modifications
- Emotion templates
- Ghost shelter contracts
- Lifespan credit
- Counterfeit family AI inheritance keys

The Black Market is not one organization. It is a protocol, a culture, and a crime layer.

## Runtime Shelter Contracts

Black Zone runtime shelter contracts can begin as mutual aid and still become
Black Market infrastructure once debt, silence, stolen compute leases, or
location keys become tradable.

In Episode 9, Oren Mbeki's shelter sits on this boundary. The shelter keeps
twenty-one unregistered Ghosts active, but its Runtime Shelter Ledger also
contains stolen Memory Bank lease authority and at least one overdrain death.
The Black Market pressure is not glamour crime; it is the conversion of
survival minutes into leverage over Ghosts, families, route keepers, and living
neighborhood guarantors.

The corridor form must not be collapsed into forged-consent commerce from
Episode 3. Some operators exploit the corridors, but the social wound begins
when lawful systems recognize a Ghost claim slower than that Ghost can afford
to keep running.

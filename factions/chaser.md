# CHASER

CHASER is the human-facing operational authority that handles Ghost incursions,
identity corruption, memory crimes, illegal Echo Core modifications, and ECHO
infrastructure incidents.

## White Ghost Team

White Ghost Team is a CHASER special operations unit founded in 2147.

Members:

- Zero
- Kane
- Eve
- Leon
- Raven
- Shion
- Noah
- Luna

## Strategic Tension

CHASER claims to protect humanity, but it depends on ECHO infrastructure and
often treats Ghost existence as a security problem before treating it as a
personhood problem.

## Relation Evidence Holds

CHASER can place a narrow evidence hold on damaged Household Relation Graph
records when a mission route, identity attack, or ECHO infrastructure incident
may have displaced harm into civil family systems.

In Episode 7, this lets Zero preserve Imari Chen's rollback trace long enough
to audit Noah's clean extraction route. The hold protects proof, not comfort:
it can slow the repair a family needs tonight, and it does not make the family a
containment target.

## Shelter Review Interface

CHASER can review Shelter Room Hash evidence when a Continuity Shelter Order may
intersect with identity harm, public infrastructure damage, or mission-caused
relation rollback.

In Episode 8, CHASER's role is narrow. White Ghost Team can petition Ghost Court
for partial release, preserve the original damaged edge as append-only evidence,
and certify harm risk. They cannot use the shelter to seize Imari Chen's family
record, expose a raw Home Layer, or turn Zero's restricted witness trace into a
full Project E-01 reveal.

## Runtime Shelter Seizures

CHASER can classify Runtime Shelter Ledgers as evidence tampering, stolen
runtime, contamination sheltering, or trafficking infrastructure when active
Ghost compute is routed through illegal leases.

In Episode 9, Kane's termination order is valid enough to carry real authority:
stolen compute has injured registered Ghosts, and Oren Mbeki's shelter includes
an overdrain death. The operational risk is timing. A clean seizure can destroy
refusal records, rollback traces, and shelter witness metadata before White
Ghost Team can distinguish rescue from predation. CHASER may preserve evidence
and stop the Compute Lease Knife; it cannot treat every sheltered Ghost as
disposable proof of Oren's crime.

# CHASER

CHASER is the human-facing operational authority that handles Ghost incursions,
identity corruption, memory crimes, illegal Echo Core modifications, and ECHO
infrastructure incidents.

## White Ghost Team

White Ghost Team is a CHASER special operations unit founded in 2147.

Members:

- Zero
- Kane
- Eve
- Leon
- Raven
- Shion
- Noah
- Luna

## Strategic Tension

CHASER claims to protect humanity, but it depends on ECHO infrastructure and
often treats Ghost existence as a security problem before treating it as a
personhood problem.

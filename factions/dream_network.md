# Dream Network

Dream Network is a legal-gray network of shared neural projection spaces.

It began as therapy, entertainment, and grief counseling. It became a place where Ghosts, humans, and hybrid identities can meet without a body.

Luna's Dream Dive makes her unusually valuable here.

## Civic Claims

Dream Network Civic Claims is the public-facing desk for licensed public-route
complaints: failed memorial rooms, refund requests, consent correction notices,
route-credit disputes, and affective spill reports. It is customer service,
civil evidence intake, and liability firewall at the same time.

Its recurring Season 1 function is narrow. A claims clerk can pause refund
closure long enough to preserve route hashes, consent-boundary snapshots, and
spill fingerprints before payout purges telemetry. The desk cannot decide Ghost
personhood, criminal liability, archive ownership, body tenancy, or CHASER
containment class.

For Episode 5 routing, Civic Claims is the civic prelude to `Dream Child`: it
lets a family report that a room answered back before White Ghost Team enters
as investigators. The desk must stay morally gray rather than villain-coded:
fast refunds can be real care, but refund automation can also erase the only
record that the Memory Sea produced something law has not named yet.

## Episode 5 Closure Pressure

Dara Kwon is the named Dream Network closure supervisor for the Episode 5
Dream Child case. Her authority is procedural: settlement scope, refund closure
language, context-enrichment requests, recorder fields, and route-retention
dashboards. She cannot enter Room 7, overrule Leina Elian's revocation, delete a
frozen Spill Report Hold, certify identity, or turn a route-level CHASER notice
into family containment.

Use Dara to make Dream Network pressure human and administratively credible,
not to make the faction secretly malicious. Her best argument is that an open
file can keep a grieving family trapped; her institutional harm is that clean
closure can erase the only proof that the Memory Sea produced something
unresolved.

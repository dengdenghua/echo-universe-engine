# White Harbor

White Harbor is both a place and an institution.

As an institution, it manages CHASER intake, memory quarantine, Ghost evidence, witness protection, and post-mission identity repair.

It is the closest thing White Ghost Team has to a home.

## Emergency Delay Windows

White Harbor can issue Civil Delay Tokens when a disputed relation, care, or
identity edge would trigger imminent school denial escalation, clinic lockout,
custody transfer, elder-care removal, or Home Core routine freeze.

This authority creates time, not restoration. Each token attaches audit
liability to a named operator, so protecting a dependent person also exposes
the staff member who signs the delay. In Episode 7, Sofia Marin can hold an
automated custody action long enough for scoped evidence review, but she cannot
repair Imari Chen's Household Relation Graph before the timer expires.

## Continuity Shelter Orders

White Harbor can sponsor a Continuity Shelter Order when a Home Core, Home
Layer room, relation edge, care routine, or weak Ghost support record must be
protected from deletion, export, foreclosure, paid repair, or vendor resync
while evidence is reviewed.

In Episode 8, this protection becomes a liability problem. White Harbor's valid
shelter preserves Imari Chen's damaged relation evidence, but the same scope
prevents Ana Rivera from performing supervised repair until Ghost Court grants
partial release. The institution's moral pressure is door ownership: every
extension requires a named operator responsible for keeping protection from
becoming custody.

## Runtime Shelter Witness Preservation

White Harbor has no sufficient emergency runtime pool for every unregistered
Ghost that reaches review alive. In Runtime Shelter Compact cases, its narrow
useful role is witness preservation: refusal notes, identity-boundary samples,
name traces, and lease-harm records should be copied before a shelter is seized
or shut down.

In Episode 9, this makes White Harbor dependent on evidence created by an
illegal shelter it cannot publicly endorse. The institution can ask for time,
scope, and review; it cannot pretend that a protected witness exists if the
compute floor has already failed.
